## Rule for adding promises

When new prose foregrounds an unexplained detail that a reader can reasonably expect to matter, add it here.

Do not turn every decorative object into a promise. Track details that carry one or more of:

- causal mystery
- character identity
- public status
- body continuity
- relationship continuity
- future mechanism
- explicit memory discrepancy
- stated historical fact
- ending pressure
