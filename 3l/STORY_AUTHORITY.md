# 3L STORY AUTHORITY

Status: active story authority for The Third Leg.

## Core shape

3L is a finite, record-framed life chronicle.

Present frame: Greg, about 59 in Life Two, has reached the ancient dragon Ithar because the western Line is failing. Greg also had reasons to seek Ithar before the Line crisis.

Ithar wants the record because Greg contains impossible knowledge with missing causal paths. The dragon is patient. Weeks or months of testimony are trivial relative to its lifespan.

The remembered story primarily follows Life Two, but Life One enters wherever Life Two depends on it. The first life is not a separate prologue novel. It is a shadow timeline and evidentiary source.

Narrative rule:

**Life Two is the path. Life One is pressure applied to the path. Ithar owns the examination.**

## Ending pressure

The late-story destination is roughly known:

- Greg and Ithar eventually pursue/reach an artifact or mechanism capable of another reset/rebeginning.
- The artifact may be usable for Greg, Ithar, or force a choice between them. Exact mechanics remain open until earned by the life story.
- The ending should confront the fact that Greg has already had two full lives.
- The second remembered timeline eventually catches the present and then moves beyond Greg's remembered future.

Do not lock a literal third reincarnation merely because the title is The Third Leg.

## Life One

Greg's first adult life runs roughly age 19 to 59.

Life One Greg eventually becomes S-class.

S-class means continental apex. In an ordinary year, roughly six or seven people on the continent qualify. They are not identical combatants, but are approximately the strongest/most capable human-scale Rankers alive.

Life One must cash this scale. Late First-Life Greg can plausibly access:

- continental threats
- ancient monsters
- restricted/dead infrastructure
- hidden geographic systems
- high political authority
- other S-class figures
- places normal Rankers cannot survive

First-Life Greg is NOT Peg-Leg Greg.

The title Peg-Leg Greg belongs to Life Two.

A major Life One continuity requirement is the provenance of Ithar's name. Greg encountered the ancient being Nhal late in Life One, roughly age 48–49, under the salt flats north of Vey. Nhal gave Greg Ithar's name and route information. In current Life Two history, that exchange has not occurred. This missing causal path is evidence for Ithar.

Nhal details beyond this are not yet locked and should be developed deliberately.

## Life Two

Greg wakes at 19 with approximately forty years of First-Life memory.

Life Two is NOT primarily an optimization run.

His first instinct is naturally to use old knowledge and possibly rebuild toward the old summit more efficiently. The story then demonstrates that he already climbed once and does not need to spend a second life proving the same thing.

Life Two must feel like a genuinely lived life:

- work
- place
- routines
- friendships
- love
- family
- money
- ordinary obligations
- aging parents
- children
- years in which nothing "plot-sized" happens

Major durable Life Two emotional arc:

**reconstruction → ordinary life / love / family → catastrophic loss → completed revenge → life after revenge → climbing again → Ithar / Line / artifact convergence**

The revenge is not merely attempted. Greg eventually completes it and must continue living afterward.

Greg later climbs again for accumulated reasons, not because 19-year-old Greg decided to min-max Life Two.

## Peg-Leg Greg

Peg-Leg Greg is a Life Two Authority-assigned named Ranker identity.

Power class and public Ranker identity are separate:

- B/A/S class = capability tier
- Ranker = Authority-recognized field actor
- Named Ranker = Authority attaches a public identity/title
- Peg-Leg Greg = Greg's Life Two named identity

At the present Dragon frame Greg is low B-class, still accomplished, publicly known, and far below the S-class capability he remembers from Life One.

The leg-loss/title event is not yet written in Records 001–010 and remains a major future Life Two threshold.

## Life Two opening run

Record 001 — THE PETITIONER
Greg reaches Ithar over the failing Line. Ithar becomes interested in Greg's impossible knowledge. Ends: "Not this time."

Record 002 — THE CLAIMANT
Greg states the impossible claim. Ithar tests memory and missing causal paths. Greg names Nhal as the First-Life source of Ithar's name. Ithar treats the memory-pattern as evidence worth examining without accepting Greg's reincarnation explanation.

Record 003 — THE BARGAINER
Greg and Ithar establish the record contract. Life Two is the narrative spine. Life One enters when Life Two depends on it. Ithar rejects Greg's right to pre-decide relevance. Greg begins remembering.

Record 004 — THE RETURNED
Greg wakes at 19. Dead family members are alive. Young body and old embodied memory conflict. He initially assumes he can rebuild the first life more efficiently.

Record 005 — THE ASPIRANT
Greg skips the remembered western ridge contract and takes a lower-paid local East Four Line maintenance job. He rationalizes this as temporary efficiency, but the human reason is that home still exists this time.

Record 006 — THE HAND
A four-week contract becomes years. Greg becomes part of East Four, family routines, and ordinary work. Nessa Vale enters as coworker/friend. Greg's drive to recreate S-class begins losing urgency.

Record 007 — THE ADVISER
Greg uses remembered outcome knowledge to prevent an East Four bypass disaster. Halden survives. This creates an external causal divergence. Greg learns that changing one event damages the reliability of later foreknowledge.

Record 008 — THE RESCUER
Greg remembers that Nessa died in a First-Life East Four pressure disaster. He attempts to prevent it, misreads the mechanism, nearly makes the situation worse, then helps uncover buried Line infrastructure and saves Nessa. Life Two now contains a person/relationship First Life did not.

Record 009 — THE STAYER
Greg receives a lucrative western field contract that could restart the old climb. He declines. He recognizes that he already paid for S-class once and that Life Two has already begun around him. Nessa, family, home, and mundane continuity matter without needing to become symbols.

Record 010 — THE STRANGER
Bren Calder, Greg's beloved First-Life friend/brother-in-practice, appears in Life Two and does not know Greg. Earlier changes have also prevented the North Vey rupture that injured Bren in Life One. Greg refuses to engineer the old friendship. This is the point where Greg stops thinking of Life Two as "going back" and accepts it as his life.

## Characters introduced in 001–010

### Ithar
Ancient dragon. Patient, observational, not omniscient. Greg's kingdom is initially uninteresting; Greg's impossible causal history is interesting.

### Nhal
Ancient nonhuman being from Life One. One legitimate holder of Ithar's hidden name. Greg met Nhal around age 48–49 in Life One under the salt flats north of Vey. Exact species/history/event remain open.

### Nessa Vale
Life Two East Four worker. In Life One, Nessa died in the East Four pressure disaster before Greg ever knew her personally. In Life Two Greg's intervention allows her to survive. Their relationship grows from coworker/friend into Greg's central ordinary-life relationship. Do not reduce her to a reward for saving her.

### Foreman Dera
East Four foreman. Practical, experienced, distrusts clever temporary workers until they earn trust.

### Halden
District engineer. Would have died in Life One East Four-related history. Greg's intervention keeps him alive; Halden later contributes to broader Line review, causing further divergence including prevention of the North Vey rupture.

### Bren Calder
Major First-Life friend, effectively Greg's chosen brother. In First Life they meet on the route Greg skipped in Life Two, become deeply bonded, and Bren eventually dies at 53 with Greg present. In Life Two Bren advances along a different route and meets Greg only as an East Four worker. Greg chooses not to manipulate him into recreating the old friendship.

## Prose authority

Record 001 on main is the style source.

3L differs from R2:

**R2: Greg lives the scene.**
**3L: Greg remembers living the scene.**

Greg's mind is the camera. Narration may contain:

- what he thought then
- what he knows now
- bodily memory
- memory uncertainty
- retrospective correction
- mundane details whose significance is unclear
- dry observational humor
- emotional avoidance

Greg should not merely report external action.

## Dragon interruption rule

After Record 003, remembered life may run for long stretches without Ithar.

Ithar returns only when the examination earns an interruption:

- Greg compresses meaningful time
- provenance is missing
- memory and interpretation diverge
- Greg contradicts himself
- a mundane detail appears unusually durable
- Ithar notices causal structure Greg does not

Do not schedule Dragon interludes mechanically.

When Ithar's voice returns after a long Greg-only stretch, audio identity should make the present frame instantly recognizable.

## Audio identity

There are two recurring audio identities only:

1. GREG VOICE
   - all narration
   - Greg dialogue
   - all remembered human/non-Ithar dialogue as Greg recounts it

2. DRAGON VOICE
   - only Ithar's actual spoken dialogue

Young and older Greg remain the same core identity.

No ensemble cast by default.

## Time-use rule

Burn real calendar time.

Greg lived a life. Do not make forty years feel like a series of adjacent quests.

Use:

- multi-year jobs
- routine accumulation
- aging relationships
- changes in home/family
- quiet years
- ordinary competence
- periods when Greg is not pursuing maximum power

Time compression must preserve distinctive texture. Ithar may challenge compression when Greg clearly remembers more than his summary admits.
