ABOUT 3L

The story starts near the end.

The Third Leg is a distinct work in the Peg-Leg Greg lineage. It opens late in Greg's second life, with an ancient dragon examining a claim that should be impossible.

The account starts with the consequence.

Greg has deliberately come to the dragon. The reader does not yet know exactly what he wants, what he is willing to trade, or why the dragon is still listening.

Then the story goes back.

The account returns to Greg at nineteen and moves forward across decades of his second life. The dragon-frame present returns only when it changes the examination, the terms, or what the reader understands.

Eventually memory catches the present.

When the lived chronology reaches the dragon encounter, the story no longer has remembered history to stand on. What follows is genuinely ahead.

Record-first. Listen-default.

The Record is the story unit. Audio and written versions are sibling renditions of the same underlying event. Listening is intended to be the default way in when audio is available, but the project is not defined by format first.

The earlier runs are optional context.

Peg-Leg Greg and R2 are part of the creative lineage. 3L is built to stand as its own work rather than requiring the reader to study the earlier versions.
