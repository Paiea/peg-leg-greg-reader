## Why this exists

This is not a conventional outline. It is a long-horizon pathfinding surface for four temporal perspectives working against the same finite life.

- **A, early Life Two / forward consequence:** What ordinary choices create the life Greg will later have something to lose?
- **B, accumulation / middle negotiation:** What work, love, reputation, institutions, and relationships become durable enough to constrain later choices?
- **C, loss / revenge / consequence:** What catastrophe can grow causally from the setting rather than arrive as arbitrary plot, and what does revenge break or reveal?
- **D, rebuilding / collapse / backward obligation:** What must already have been true for fifty-nine-year-old Greg, the failing Line, and Ithar to feel earned rather than convenient?

Each perspective reasons through **STATE IN -> trajectory -> STATE OUT**.

A pushes consequences forward. D pushes obligations backward. B and C negotiate the bridges. Rehearsal attacks weak links. Story Sync preserves only development that survives.

## Temporal density

Time is elastic.

Record 010 already proves 3L can cross years quickly when life is stable. A later month of loss, revenge, recovery, or collapse may consume many Records. Three hundred is a search budget, not a calendar and not a promise that the final book contains exactly 300 Records.

## Strong provisional through-lines from the first map

These are hypotheses to test, not directions to protect.

1. Ordinary competence compounds: station work -> contracting -> training -> standards -> reporting network -> emergency infrastructure.
2. Nessa should function as a full independent partner, not a reward or tragedy device.
3. Bren's Record-010 nonrelationship creates the possibility of a later Life-Two friendship chosen honestly rather than reconstructed through manipulation.
4. Revenge can expose rather than replace the setting plot: human wrongdoing may be real while deeper Line failure has no single villain.
5. The Life-Two ruined leg can arise through a different causal road from First Life.
6. Older Greg's leverage can shift from raw strength toward networks, judgment, teaching, records, logistics, reputation, and impossible First-Life knowledge.
7. The final dragon argument can grow from the whole life: Greg condemns civilizational replaceability while privately entertaining personal replaceability through another beginning.
