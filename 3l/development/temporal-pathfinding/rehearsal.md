**Records 161-180**

Greg objects credibly and still loses. A catastrophic loss then grows from this machine, but exact victim geography and intimate death remain open until prose earns them.

### ERA VI: REVENGE AND ITS END
**Records 181-205**

Greg uses adult resources rather than generic adventuring: investigators, contracts, records, public reputation, workers, witnesses, money, legal process, and selective pressure. Human culpability is separated from deeper Line instability. Greg eventually completes revenge by knowingly crossing a moral boundary, then discovers completion does not restore the lost world.

### ERA VII: LIFE AFTER REVENGE
**Records 206-240**

Greg rebuilds usefulness through successors, archives, cheap experiments, operator networks, disability adaptation, teaching, and distributed judgment. Body promises begin acquiring provenance. The gate-damaged hand and ruined leg remain separate causal events. Peg-Leg naming follows lived adaptation rather than injury itself.

### ERA VIII: BOTH LIVES BECOME EVIDENCE
**Records 241-260**

First-Life S-class memory becomes operationally relevant again without becoming an answer key. Ancient notation, Nhal, paired records, failed theories, human archives, and field tests make Greg's private interest in Ithar more serious while still keeping the public Line crisis below full kingdom scale.

### ERA IX: THE LINE BECOMES CIVILIZATION
**Records 261-290**

Failures cascade through trade, migration, labor, borders, parts, capacity, and settlement. Human measures can slow but not restore the deeper substrate. Greg's decades of distributed trust matter more than raw rank. The public need for Ithar finally becomes independent of Greg's older private wish.

### ERA X: THE RECORD CATCHES THE CAVE
**Records 291-300 search horizon**

Greg's account reaches the frame. Ithar separates public Line failure from Greg's private desire for another beginning. Both lives must be defended as real. Reset, branching, preservation, another beneficiary, and refusal remain live until mechanism and cost are known.
