## WHY

B and C independently reach the same underlying structure: aging infrastructure, scarce capacity, procurement incentives, acceptable-loss policy, incomplete models, distributed responsibility, and ordinary administrative routing combine into a catastrophe-capable system.

C's specific proposal that East Four bears the exposure is a useful candidate, not something the seam itself proves. What is proven by convergence is the causal machine, not the exact victim geography.

This is one of the strongest discoveries of the temporal run because neither window needs a secret conspiracy to create Greg's later grievance. Human wrongdoing and deeper Line instability can coexist.

## SURVIVING PRESSURES

- A central or regional continuity body must make scarcity decisions that are rational at system level and brutal locally.
- Procurement or contracting incentives should move hidden risk toward crews and districts with less leverage.
- Greg must object through real professional channels and lose despite being credible.
- Another qualified actor must be able to route around Greg's refusal, proving that personal virtue is not institutional control.
- Greg's distributed clients, trainees, records, and local trust must create resilience before catastrophe.
- Local evacuation/contingency planning must exist before the full failure so survival does not depend on a last-minute genius insight.
- At least one warning should be documented in a form that later makes responsibility reconstructable.
- Human culpability must remain narrower than the total Line failure.
