Window A is closest to accepted authority. It should behave most like ordinary forward story development while still being willing to replace the first map.

Start from Greg's Record-010 realization that Life Two is **my life**.

Search for the strongest causal road through ordinary accumulation.

- What parts of First-Life memory stop being predictive and remain useful only as experience?
