## PROJECTED STATE IN
- Age/time position: B begins years after Record 010, plausibly after the eleven-year East Four tenure has matured or begun to widen into a broader adult life, but no exact Greg age or exit date is assumed.
- Body/rank/skill: Greg is an established Ranker with permanent left-shoulder damage, broad practical Line competence, and embodied First-Life experience, while exact class progression remains speculative and the ruined leg, bad ribs, and damaged right-hand fingers remain unscheduled.
- Work/money/property: Greg plausibly has enough professional credibility, tools, savings, and repeat work to support broader obligations, but ownership form, company structure, property beyond the shared apartment, and wealth level remain open.
- Relationships: Greg and Nessa have years of trust and shared living; any marriage, children, household expansion, and exact family form remain open, while Bren's later Life-Two bond must still be chosen rather than reconstructed.
- Geography/social range: East Four remains a durable home base or reference point while Greg's work may plausibly begin reaching neighboring districts; the exact route of that widening is a backward obligation rather than assumed canon.
- Line/world position: forgotten older infrastructure is known to exist under East Four, Halden's survival has already influenced wider review, and no kingdom-scale Line crisis is yet assumed.
- First-Life pressure: prediction has degraded, comparison remains potent, and Greg increasingly uses First-Life memory as experience rather than a route map.
- Unresolved obligations: ordinary life must become valuable enough to constrain later choices; love/family must become fully lived; public accomplishment, named contracts, injuries, Peg-Leg identity, catastrophic loss, completed revenge, renewed climbing, Ithar's private pull, and the large Line failure all remain ahead.
- Assumptions that remain explicitly speculative: B tests a wider professional footprint, household growth, modest property or business leverage, honest Bren friendship, training institutions, and regional Line evidence without declaring any of them accepted canon.
