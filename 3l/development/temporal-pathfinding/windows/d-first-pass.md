# WINDOW D FIRST PASS

Status: FIRST-PASS EXPERIMENTAL EVIDENCE
Range: Records 241-300

## PROJECTED STATE IN
- **age/time position:** Late Life Two, plausibly early-to-mid fifties with exact age deliberately unresolved, leaving enough real calendar time for rebuilding, renewed climbing, widening Line failure, and the approach to Ithar.
- **body/rank/skill:** Greg is already diminished from his First-Life S-class memory and plausibly B-range, while the exact timing of the ruined leg, Authority naming, ribs, and hand damage remains available to earlier windows or early D if earned.
- **work/money/property:** He has accumulated durable professional leverage through work, records, standards, trainees, contracts, and reputation, but D does not require one specific business, organization, property portfolio, or leadership title.
- **relationships:** Life Two contains independent people and institutions Greg values after ordinary years, catastrophic loss, and completed revenge, while Nessa's exact fate, family shape, and Bren's later relationship remain unresolved.
- **geography/social range:** Greg is known beyond one station through accumulated work and reciprocal local history, but his reach is a network of specific places and people rather than universal celebrity.
- **Line/world position:** Western failures are becoming harder to explain through ordinary maintenance models, yet the crisis has not necessarily become kingdom-scale or publicly understood at D's entry.
- **First-Life pressure:** Greg carries lived S-class experience, the Nhal encounter, ancient notation exposure, and remembered western Line failures, but those memories remain incomplete evidence rather than a predictive archive.
- **unresolved obligations:** Present-body provenance, Peg-Leg naming, commendation, five named contracts, three-city goodwill, Nhal/Ithar provenance, lower-channel knowledge, the private Ithar search, and the reset/rebeginning pressure all still need credible causal support by the frame.
- **assumptions that remain explicitly speculative:** Exact catastrophe victim, exact revenge target, whether the leg loss precedes D, exact family structure, exact institutional form, Ithar's own stake, reset mechanics, cost, beneficiary, and final choice remain open.

## STATE OUT
- **frame-present position:** Greg's remembered Life Two has caught the cave frame with the public petition and private motive both visible, after which the examination can move beyond memory into a new shared problem.
- **body/rank/skill:** He is about fifty-nine, low B-class, permanently damaged, and capable enough to reach Ithar through judgment and experience rather than any restoration of First-Life S-class power.
- **public/material position:** Peg-Leg Greg is publicly accomplished through earned contracts, commendation, reciprocal local goodwill, and durable work that continues without him rather than through wealth or rank alone.
- **relationships carried into the frame:** Greg arrives with independent living relationships, dead or absent relationships, successors, coworkers, family history, and a possible Life-Two Bren bond whose exact composition remains available to earlier development.
- **Line/world condition:** The western Line is failing at kingdom-relevant scale after decades of visible maintenance, load, dependency, migration, and incentive problems, while human measures can mitigate but not understand or reverse the deepest failure.
- **First-Life knowledge now operationally relevant:** Nhal's name and route provenance, ancient notation, lower channels, the ninth station, the eastern sink, and lived S-class access now supply evidence and access without supplying a complete explanation.
- **private Ithar motive:** Greg had already searched for Ithar because some form of another beginning might answer a personal loss or impossible alternative, but the exact person, beneficiary, and desired restoration remain unlocked.
- **ending pressure still unresolved:** Reset versus branching versus preservation versus some other rebeginning, Ithar's stake, artifact mechanics, cost, beneficiary, and final action remain genuine forks rather than conclusions smuggled in by the map.

## OBLIGATIONS PUSHED FORWARD
- The frame must treat the public Line mission and Greg's private rebeginning desire as two real motives that overlap without becoming interchangeable.
- Ithar needs an independent reason to investigate or pursue the deeper mechanism so the dragon is neither a moral lecturer nor a convenient service provider.
- Any artifact or rebeginning mechanism must respect the story's causal-provenance logic and make preservation, erasure, memory, and identity materially different outcomes.
- Greg's institutions and relationships should continue acting while he is absent, proving that his late-life leverage is distributed rather than secretly dependent on him.
- The ending must keep at least one non-reset path and at least one non-Greg beneficiary path genuinely viable until cost and mechanics are known.
- The final replaceability argument must account for both full lives rather than using one catastrophic loss as the sole reason Life Two mattered.

- Earlier decades must repeatedly show Line infrastructure as ordinary dependence tied to settlement, trade, labor, law, maintenance, capacity, and incentives so kingdom-scale failure feels like accumulated setting consequence.
- Greg must develop recordkeeping, teaching, delegation, standards, logistics, and reciprocal trust long before the crisis so the distributed late-life network is an earned capability.

## MAP CHANGES
- **KEEP:** Preserve the original map's double-life comparison, institutional succession pressure, crisis logistics, human-alternative search, petition preparation, mountain approach, myth-versus-lived-man tension, and final replaceability examination because each can earn the frame without dictating the missing middle.
- **MOVE:** Move Greg's private Ithar search and rebeginning temptation materially earlier than the public kingdom crisis, and let succession/delegation begin before emergency conditions force it.
- **MERGE:** Compress the original run of founder/retirement beats into a smaller succession question, compress repeated crisis-command titles into dependency/logistics beats, and compress several final thesis statements into tests that can emerge through Ithar's examination.
- **SPLIT:** Separate Ithar's public relevance from Greg's private motive, separate ancient-name provenance from Line-mechanism knowledge, and separate deciding to pursue a mechanism from deciding whether or how to use it.
- **REPLACE:** Replace `THE HUSBAND TO THE DEAD` with a victim-agnostic private question, replace heroic `COMMANDER` framing with distributed civilian coordination, and replace literal `THE THIRD-LIFE MAN` certainty with multiple rebeginning mechanisms that remain live.
- **KILL:** Kill Nessa's mandated death, the assumption that Bren must be refused at the door, the idea that a failed adaptation theory should become the privileged Line explanation, and the deterministic `THE BEGINNING` ending that resolves the final choice before mechanism and cost are earned.
