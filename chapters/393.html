West Locks sounded like giants arguing under floor.

Thunk.

Pause.

Grinding hum.

Thunk-thunk.

Then water.

A lot of water.

We left road camp before sun reached first lock tower.

"Wasn't planning to."

Kel climbed driver's bench.

Sori checked seals.

Dask checked Sori checking seals.

Maro yawned into sleeve.

Vessa said, "Move."

Camp disappeared behind first rise.

One night.

Already gone.

West Locks appeared properly ten minutes later.

Not one lock.

A system.

Three channels cut through low stone hills.

Gates taller than houses.

Black iron teeth fitted into pale masonry.

Walkways crossed at several heights.

Counterweight towers stood over channels with horizontal arms and white cloth strips like the relay tower we'd passed yesterday.

Some strips moved now.

Some did not.

Water sat at different levels on different sides of gates.

One basin almost still.

Another churned brown-white below half-open spill bars.

Freight cranes moved crates over channels without touching road.

Workers in dark wax coats shouted numbers nobody repeated back because apparently Locks people trusted screaming.

I liked it immediately.

Bad sign.

Vessa said, "Eyes on route."

"They are."

"Keep them there."

Approach office was small stone box wedged between West Three and Four.

Lock clerk wore red scarf and had ink on both wrists.

She read Sori's papers while talking to someone through wall tube.

"Survey six, east gallery foot, Five cradle cargo, north landing regroup. West Three slow but running. Four still dead. Five cargo window open until second bell unless priority grain comes early."

"One before dawn. No gate movement."

"East gallery lower panel again at first light. Maintenance marked and shaded. Walkable. Don't scrape it."

Clerk looked at him.

"No."

"Yes."

Cradle.

Same logic as Basin freight cage, bigger machine.

"You need hobby."

"It makes errors less likely."

There.

Best he had.

Cradle moved.

Chains tightened.

Counterweight dropped inside tower.

Maro looked up.

So did I.

Not our decision.

Foot route tunneled through gate wall itself.

There.

Different grammar.

Not catwalk.

Then lower.

Then gone.

Because gallery moved through changing lock levels.

Floor slightly sloped.

Some warm.

Some cold.

Not magic.

Temperature.

Probably.

Yellow chalk boundary.

Not bright.

Like moonlight under bedsheet.

Useful.

There.

Meaningful.

"No movement Three!"

No cause.

Then nodded.

"Observer estimate."

Four was open only enough to expose repair chamber above waterline.

Replacement tooth.

Almost as tall as me.

Actual gate gear had gap where old tooth had been removed.

Three workers fitted pins and measured seat.

No magic visible.

Maybe everything.

Do not ask every noun.

Not difficult.

Crutches through.

Right foot.

Thunk-thunk.

Not bell.

Mechanical.

"Slow cycle."

Dask nearly died.

Nothing damaged.

Normal.

Crutches planted.
