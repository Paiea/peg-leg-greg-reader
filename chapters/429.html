Masta gave allowance, not quota.

Recorded.

Not settlement amount.

Crack geometry.

Drainage slot below was clear.

Water moving.

Unlike Section Two.

That mattered.

"B-seven is useless for elevation comparison."

"Do not enjoy yet."

"I knew there was second sentence."

"Rock controls agree with each other within acceptable repeat. Relative to them, north road crown is lower than archived quarry-face tie by between two and three fingers."

Silence.

Tera asked, "Meaning?"

Halen looked pained.

Not because question bad.

Because answer long.

"Meaning either road crown lowered, archived tie was recorded badly, quarry face itself changed, or some combination. But R-four face mark is intact, and archive sketch matches current notch geometry. So I place more confidence in actual road lowering here than at South Approach."

Jorren said, "How much confidence?"

"Enough to recommend against unrestricted heavy freight until loaded response is observed. Not enough to call whole causeway failed."

There.

Smaller claim.

Painful enough.

"One."

"Which one?"

"Lead tank wagon."

"How heavy?"

"Class ten loaded."

"Not first pass."

"Then what proves anything?"

"Class eight test wagon."

"We already did class eight south."

"Different section."

Jorren swore softly.

Enna answered carefully.

"Then we know eight passed this section under monitored conditions once."

"And clinic oil?"

"Still decision after all three sections."

Tera's jaw tightened.

"You people have sentence for everything except yes."

Enna met her eyes.

"Yes."

Tera almost laughed.

Didn't.

Class eight verified on scale blocks.

No argument this time.

Tender put sounding plate near shoulder.

"Do not move to see better."

"Yes."

"If something happens, write after you're safe."

"Yes."

Good driver.

"Hold."

Important.

Another small grind.

Then clear.

He took longer than anyone wanted.

Jorren stopped pacing.

Tera sat on stone.

I kept ledger open.

Wind moved marsh grass.

North Reach smoke ahead.

Fuel south.

Road between.

"Heavy freight hold remains."

Policy.
