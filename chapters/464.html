CHAPTER 464

THE COURIER

The sample made everything heavier.

Not physically.

Physically it was a bottle.

Padded.

Sealed.

Maybe half a pound.

Socially it weighed about six arguments.

Morning at the maintenance hut came cold and blue through the broken shutter slats.

My right shoulder had improved enough to disappoint me.

If it had been dramatically worse, decision easy.

Turn around slowly.

If magically healed, also easy.

Continue normally.

Instead I had soreness, dark bruise, full enough load-bearing, and a sharp little protest when I reached too high.

Useful body.

Annoying body.

Mine.

I ate what counted as breakfast and re-divided remaining food.

One small portion for the road to Three Sisters.

One emergency portion.

Nothing left that deserved optimism.

Water better.

I had both flasks.

The district bottle did not count.

I checked seal before anything else.

Intact.

Wax strip unbroken.

Number matched form.

Bottle padded center-pack, not outer pocket.

If I fell on it, larger problems already happening.

Good.

The spring grower was gone.

No note.

No dramatic warning.

Just fresh boot marks outside hut heading uphill.

I preferred that.

No companion.

No guide.

No convenient witness.

I left before full sun.

The lower shelf looked different from return direction.

Everything did.

Outbound travel had built a ladder in my head:

ford

steps

bench

hollow.

Now I knew where the bad plank was.

Where shelf narrowed.

Where one root caught blanket roll.

Where water channel crossed under old retaining wall.

That knowledge mattered more than confidence.

At first bend I stopped.

Stream sound louder than yesterday morning.

Not dramatically.

Enough.

I walked bank before reaching crossing.

The cracked plank remained cracked.

Good consistency.

The southern ford spread wider than yesterday afternoon.

Water maybe similar depth to yesterday morning.

No abrupt pulse while I watched.

I waited anyway.

One quarter bell.

Then another.

Food margin said move.

Shoulder said don't rush.

Sample said don't be stupid.

I crossed.

First crutch.

Second.

Right foot.

Pause.

Water cold enough to sharpen everything.

Halfway, current pressed harder on left crutch shaft.

Not surge.

Steady.

I angled shaft slightly upstream and shortened next move.

Old body knew rivers.

Current body knew compromise.

Three more placements.

Bank.

Done.

No magic.

No slip.

No heroism.

Excellent.

I sat immediately after crossing and checked seal.

Still intact.

Courier behavior.

That was what I was now.

Not investigator anymore.

Not really.

I had information.

My job was to stop adding damage to it.

Three Sisters Hollow was empty.

Morning shadows long.

The three fins did not share one shadow yet.

Story wasn't wrong.

Story was asleep.

I climbed from southern descent into hollow and took longer rest under rock lip from previous night.

Shoulder warm.

Not worse.

Good.

I considered route back.

Old lower bench still cut.

Southern bypass known.

Sleeping Stone beyond.

Then slide bypass.

Then White Reed.

Morrow after.

Could reach Sleeping Stone today easily enough if shoulder held.

White Reed maybe late.

Morrow unrealistic.

Food problem.

At minimum one more night.

Employer gave three days.

Quest had become four.

No tragedy.

But planning debt.

I ate half road portion.

Saved half.

Then started west.

Three Sisters route was easier backward because I knew gravel fan was wrong line.

I stayed upper track until old water wall.

No searching.

Faster.

Not fast.

There was difference.

By midday haze burned off.

Sleeping Stone overhang visible through trees earlier than expected.

Good.

Then I heard voices.

Two.

Human.

Not close enough for words.

I stopped.

No reason for danger assumption.

Recent twine had already shown people used route.

Still, sample changed stakes.

I moved off center trail to wider ground beside old channel wall and waited until voices resolved.

Two orchard workers came around bend carrying pruning bundles and a short-handled saw between them.

No need names.

They stopped when they saw me.

One looked at crutches.

Then pack.

Then my face.

"You came from spring?"

Question too quick.

"Why?"

Worker with saw said, "Because nobody else comes down that way with that much mud on one boot."

Fair.

"I came from above Three Sisters."

Technically true.

Other worker glanced at pack again.

"Morrow sent you?"

There.

Not random hikers.

"Who are you with?"

"North channel crews."

Not ridge growers exactly.

Not Morrow.

"Names?"

They gave them.

I did not need them yet.

I wrote roles only mentally.

Saw-worker said, "Did you sample source?"

Direct.

"Why do you ask?"

He sighed.

"Because everybody knows Morrow hired somebody. Because Garrick talks loud. Because Lakeward runner took bottle east. Because if you got one, people will spend tonight pretending bottle says whatever they already decided."

Correct.

I liked him slightly.

"Then you understand why I'm not discussing contents."

"Contents are water."

"Observations."

"Fine. Observations."

Other worker said, "Was east flap open?"

There it was.

"I'm not giving field findings on trail."

"We're not asking you to change paper."

"You're asking me to preview evidence before employer and district receive it."

Saw-worker rubbed face.

"We're asking because if Morrow thinks ridge branch is taking too much, they'll close lower exchange gate before district gets there. Then upper nursery loses pressure."

Human consequence.

"Can Morrow do that?"

"They can close their exchange gate. Whether they should is whole argument."

"Would that affect fire cistern?"

Pause.

"Maybe."

Not enough.

"How much?"

"Depends level."

"Recorded?"

He laughed without humor.

"You really are him."

"Who?"

"The sentence man."

Damn Helena.

Nickname spreading sideways now.

"That isn't improvement over Peg-Leg Greg."

Neither laughed.

Fine.

Other worker stepped closer.

Not threatening.

Urgent.

"Look, if spring is low, say spring is low. If flap is wrong, say flap is wrong. But don't walk back into Morrow with silence if they're about to close water on us."

There.

Correct procedure hurting somebody before procedure finishes.

My contract said return evidence.

Not mediate on trail.

But silence had consequence too.

What could I truthfully say without leaking findings?

"I can tell you this," I said. "Nothing I saw authorizes anyone to treat one observation as full cause. If you think Morrow may close an exchange gate before district review, send your own runner now. Tell them that consequence explicitly."

Saw-worker stared.

"That's it?"

"That's useful."

"It's annoyingly useful."

"My specialty."

He looked toward east.

"We can send one from White Reed."

"Do that."

Other worker said, "You didn't answer flap."

"Correct."

"You're protecting them."

"No."

"Then us?"

"Also no."

"Then who?"

I touched pack strap.

"Evidence."

That sounded dramatic.

I disliked it immediately.

But true.

Saw-worker finally nodded.

"Fine."

They moved past me toward east.

No confrontation.

No names needed.

But social pressure arrived before Morrow.

Good.

Bad.

I continued.

Sleeping Stone shelter showed new soot.

Very new.

Maybe those workers had used it outbound.

That solved one tiny mystery without making mystery.

I checked old twine.

Still there.

No need.

Shoulder wanted rest.

I gave it one quarter bell.

Then another.

Could stay here overnight.

Food one emergency portion plus half road portion.

White Reed reachable before dark if pace held.

Mabel had keeper house, likely water, maybe not food.

Could ask.

Hospitality not guaranteed.

But reaching maintained route reduced risk.

Continue.

Slide bypass was worst part.

Not because new danger.

Because shoulder remembered every root.

The active scar looked settled relative yesterday.

Meaningless timeframe.

I used same uphill rooted bypass.

This time descending.

Harder in different way.

I lowered pack separately twice.

Sat three times.

At one root step, right shoulder sharpened suddenly under crutch load.

I froze.

Waited.

Pain eased to strong ache.

No pop.

No weakness.

But threshold changed.

I shortened route expectations.

White Reed only.

No Morrow push even if light remained.

Good.

Below slide, old wire hazard still partly visible.

No grazer.

No need callback.

At split oak, dead trunk looked deader.

Alive trunk still yellow leaves.

Useful landmark.

By late afternoon I heard White Reed before saw it.

Reed hiss.

Water.

Then keeper house roof.

Relief landed harder than expected.

Not home.

Not even Morrow.

But maintained bridge.

Human roof.

Known person.

I crossed bridge.

Mabel Crowe was outside shaking mud from reed mat.

She looked at me.

Then shoulder.

Then pack.

"You look worse."

"I have data."

"That's usually why."

Good woman.

"Jonah?"

"Back Morrow. Came through yesterday asking whether you'd returned."

"No."

"I noticed."

She pointed at bench.

"Sit before you fall for dramatic effect."

I sat.

First thing:

sample seal.

Still intact.

Mabel watched.

"Got it?"

"Yes."

"Spring?"

"I'm returning findings to district and Morrow first."

She rolled eyes.

"Gods preserve paperwork."

"They've tried."

"Did you at least find path?"

That I could answer.

"Southern lower maintenance shelf crosses below Three Sisters slide under current conditions. North upper track remains terrible idea."

"That old shelf still there?"

"Yes."

"Huh."

"You knew?"

"Knew there was one. Didn't know it survived."

Good.

"Recent cuts. Ridge crews use it."

"That tracks."

"Why didn't you mention?"

She gave me look.

"Because I haven't been past Sleeping Stone in eleven years and didn't want to murder you with nostalgia."

Excellent.

That was exactly right.

"Thank you."

"You're welcome."

I asked about food.

Not delicately.

"I am one emergency portion away from becoming stupid. Do you sell anything?"

"No."

Damn.

"I do have stew."

"Cost?"

"You can split wood tomorrow."

"Shoulder."

"Then you can write me a route note copy after district gets original."

Fair exchange.

Not free.

"Done."

She fed me thick reed-root stew with dried meat and bread.

I did not ask magical nutritional composition.

It was hot.

Enough.

Food crisis reduced.

Not erased.

Mabel had one spare floor pallet.

"Cost?"

"Same note copy."

"You're overpaying me."

"Then make handwriting legible."

Deal.

I stayed.

Shoulder needed it.

Before dark, runner arrived from Morrow.

Jonah.

Of course.

Not secretly following quest.

Actual maintained-road runner.

He came across bridge sweaty and angry.

"You're alive."

"Barely in accounting sense."

He saw pack.

"Bottle?"

"Intact."

His face changed.

Relief.

Then urgency.

"Grandfather wants to know now."

"He gets report when I reach Morrow."

"I know."

"Then why are you here?"

"Because Helena sent me."

Ah.

"Why?"

Jonah sat across from me without asking.

"Lower exchange gate got reduced this afternoon."

There.

"By Morrow?"

"Cooperative vote. Temporary."

"Why?"

"Because Row Four dropped fruit this morning and because north crews supposedly increased draw and because everybody is furious."

"Did they know my findings?"

"No."

Important.

"Any district order?"

"No."

"How much reduction?"

"One notch."

"Effect?"

"Lower rows keep more local feed. Upper nursery gets less exchange pressure."

The workers' warning already happening.

"Fire cistern?"

Jonah shook head.

"Nobody agrees."

"Excellent."

"Not excellent."

"No."

He leaned forward.

"Mom wants you to come straight Morrow tomorrow. Grandfather wants you to tell council at dawn if spring is running. North crew sent runner saying nobody should touch another gate until district sees sample."

"They're right about last part."

"Can you tell me whether spring is running?"

There.

Jonah.

Person I knew enough to care.

Not district.

Not employer exactly.

Family.

"Visible flow was present," I said.

His shoulders dropped.

Relief too fast.

I raised hand.

"That does not mean normal flow. I do not have calibrated normal."

Relief changed into frustration.

"Greg."

"No. Listen. This is exactly where people hurt each other. `Water exists` becomes `source is fine`. `source is fine` becomes `upper gates stole it`. That's not supported."

"But it's flowing."

"Yes."

"That matters."

"Yes."

"Then why do you make every answer feel smaller?"

Good question.

Not technical.

Human.

I took time.

"Because small true answer is better than large false one."

Jonah stared at bridge boards.

"Sometimes people need larger answer before fruit falls."

There.

"I know."

"Do you?"

That one landed.

Maybe I didn't enough.

I had spent first life being right under pressure because wrong killed people.

Here, delay also hurt people.

Different problem.

"I'm coming tomorrow," I said. "Early. Sample intact. Notes intact. I'll say exactly what I saw. And exactly what I couldn't establish."

"Grandfather will hate that."

"Your mother might too."

"She will less."

Probably.

Jonah looked at my shoulder.

"What happened?"

"Rock."

"Of course."

"I won."

"You look like you lost."

"Technical draw."

He finally smiled.

Good.

He had brought no food for me because he didn't know I was here.

Good continuity.

Mabel gave him leftover bread anyway and charged him one chore tomorrow? No, don't make hospitality transactional every bite. Ordinary kindness allowed.

He ate.

Then said he had to return before full dark.

"Why come yourself?"

"Because if runner said `he's alive` I'd still wonder if runner meant mostly."

Human.

"Also Grandpa would've come if I didn't."

"Bad knee."

"Exactly."

I gave Jonah his loaned flask back.

"Finally."

He shook it.

"You drank my good water."

"Criminal."

"I'm billing you."

"Send invoice."

He left west toward Morrow maintained track.

No permanent companion.

I stayed White Reed.

Before sleep, I copied notes into two sets.

One full district sequence.

One Morrow contract summary.

Important distinction.

District gets chain-form facts.

Morrow gets contracted route/water observations.

Same underlying truth.

Different scope.

I added a third page for gate consequence because now situation changed during my absence:

**Return note: Morrow lower exchange gate reportedly reduced one notch afternoon Day 4?** Need day numbering. Day 1 to Sleeping Stone, Day2 Three Sisters, Day3 Mirror Spring, now return Day4. Yes.

**Report source: Jonah Sol at White Reed. No district order reported. North crews reportedly object and request no further gate changes pending sample review. Verify in Morrow/Lakeward.**

That mattered.

Evidence did not freeze world while I walked.

Exactly.

Cash still twelve.

Vale twenty-three.

Magic 59 / 53 / 139.

Sample sealed.

Shoulder sore.

Food no longer emergency-thin because Mabel fed me, but field provisions effectively exhausted except emergency portion.

Tomorrow Morrow.

Then district.

Maybe council.

Maybe argument.

Probably argument.

Courier.

That was what I was being.

Not because I carried bottle.

Because every person I met wanted me to open it before delivery.

Not literally.

Worse.

They wanted me to open the meaning.

Tomorrow they would all be in same room.

I suspected carrying water had been easier.
