# PLG Outward 4D Experiment Design

**Status:** approved experimental design

**Authority:** derived-only experiment. Accepted PLG canon prose remains sole story authority.

## Purpose

Use the existing Chapters 1-500 as a large evidence mass in the middle of the timeline, then reason outward in both directions:

- reconstruct plausible pre-Chapter-1 old-life histories that could have produced the Greg observed in canon;
- discover plausible post-Chapter-500 futures that the lived canon has made possible, likely, costly, or impossible;
- allow past and future hypotheses to challenge one another through the known 500-chapter middle;
- learn what the temporal/REHEARSAL architecture can do when it has far more behavioral evidence than Dragon Spotter or Gravity's Embrace.

This is not an outline generator and not a canon rewrite pass.

## Core topology

```text
DISTANT PAST -> NEAR PAST -> [ CHAPTERS 1-500 CANON ] -> NEAR FUTURE -> DISTANT FUTURE
      ^             ^                    |                    |              |
      |             |                    |                    |              |
      +---- backward requirements -------+---- forward consequences --------+
                                    |
                              REHEARSAL / PERFORMANCE
                                    |
                                STORY SYNC
```

The known manuscript is evidence. Unknown past and future are search spaces.

## Creative policy: wild but disciplined

DREAM/search may invent aggressively outside the observed canon, including entire relationships, careers, institutions, failures, enemies, loves, places, obligations, losses, successes, identities, and distant future states.

Creative permission is not epistemic permission. Every candidate must remain typed by evidence status.

Allowed derived statuses:

- `CANON_FACT` - explicit in accepted Chapters 1-500.
- `CANON_IMPLICATION` - strongly required by multiple pieces of canon evidence.
- `SUPPORTED_POSSIBILITY` - invented but explains multiple independent observations and survives challenge.
- `LIVE_POSSIBILITY` - compatible and interesting but insufficiently supported.
- `WILD_BRANCH` - deliberately distant exploration with low authority.
- `CONTRADICTED` - rejected or materially weakened by canon evidence or rehearsal.

No experimental status automatically becomes canon.

## Canon firewall

Neither prehistory nor future material may write canon during this experiment.

Even very strong derived results are capped at derived authority, e.g. `STRONG_DERIVED_HISTORY` or `STRONG_DERIVED_FUTURE`. Explicit creator promotion is required before any discovered event, relationship, fact, or future state becomes canon.

The experiment must not rewrite Chapters 1-500. A later rewrite/recomposition experiment may consume these discoveries only after the outward compilation is frozen and separately approved.

## Temporal distance and uncertainty

Constraint should generally weaken and uncertainty should generally increase with distance from observed canon.

- immediately pre-Chapter-1 candidates are heavily constrained by Chapter 1 and later behavioral evidence;
- distant old-life candidates may explore much more broadly;
- Chapter 501-near futures are heavily constrained by Chapter 500 state and current chronology;
- distant futures may diverge aggressively.

Distance does not erase durable character evidence. A far-past or far-future branch that conflicts with repeated canon behavior must explain a credible transformation rather than ignore the evidence.

## Evidence extraction from the known middle

The experiment should compile longitudinal evidence from Chapters 1-500 by persistent threads rather than treating 500 chapter summaries as the reasoning representation.

Candidate thread families include, but are not limited to:

- identity and self-conception;
- power/capability and professional competence;
- body, adaptation, aging, injury, and physical habit;
- work and attitudes toward usefulness;
- money, status, capital, and material expectations;
- authority and institutions;
- violence and risk;
- friendship, intimacy, dependence, pride, and vulnerability;
- home and belonging;
- Greg/Lyssa and other major relationships;
- social density and accumulated obligations;
- failure, loss, avoidance, and what Greg does not explain;
- reputation and how others remember him.

Thread extraction must preserve provenance back to canon evidence. The compact representation is derived memory, not replacement authority.

## Backward search: before Chapter 1

Ask from observed behavior:

> What histories could plausibly have produced this person?

Backward requirements may generate multiple competing old-life explanations. The system should prefer explanatory reach, independent evidence, behavioral compatibility, low retrofit cost, and survival under challenge. It must preserve viable alternatives rather than collapsing onto the first dramatic backstory.

Old-life candidates should be allowed to explain repeated present behavior, but should be penalized for over-explaining every trait through one event.

## Forward search: after Chapter 500

Ask:

> What person and world have Chapters 1-500 made Greg capable of becoming?

Generate multiple structurally distinct futures rather than cosmetic variants of the current trajectory. Near-future candidates inherit current chronology, obligations, economic state, relationship state, physical state, and unresolved systems. Distant futures may branch much more widely.

Do not convert unresolved lanes into scheduled plot merely because they exist.

## Bidirectional collision

Past and future search are not independent appendices.

A promising future may imply a backward question about old Greg. A strong old-life hypothesis may alter which future transformations are interesting or costly. Both directions must be tested through the known middle.

Useful collisions include:

- a future state requiring a capacity that canon has or has not earned;
- an old-life explanation that predicts current behavior across many independent scenes;
- a future that recreates an old-life failure under changed conditions;
- a distant endpoint that exposes a missing transformation in the known or near-future trajectory;
- a past hypothesis whose consequences should have left residue in Chapters 1-500 but did not.

## REHEARSAL policy

REHEARSAL remains experiment selection/orchestration, not a screenplay synonym.

Use the cheapest representation that can discriminate among meaningful candidates:

1. plausibility/constraint probe;
2. state-transition or worldline test;
3. forward-consequence/backward-prerequisite test;
4. counterfactual branch comparison;
5. temporal-distance rehearsal;
6. HIGH-HEAT PERFORMANCE only when embodied behavior is the uncertainty.

PERFORMANCE is especially valuable when testing whether an invented old-life version of Greg could plausibly become canon Greg, or whether Chapter-500 Greg could honestly cross into a proposed future relationship/identity state.

Routine structural questions should not become screenplay.

## Search pressure

Push capability rather than playing safe:

- preserve a wide search lane;
- include at least one distant/surprising candidate when generating alternatives;
- challenge semantic cousins so apparent diversity is real diversity;
- allow attractive branches to die;
- do not reward novelty that requires contradicting canon without a transformation explanation;
- keep an unknown/other escape hatch so the candidate set never pretends to be exhaustive.

## STORY SYNC

STORY SYNC evaluates derived evidence and convergence but cannot cross the canon firewall.

Signals include:

- independent recurrence across canon;
- explanatory reach;
- forward/backward agreement;
- character truth;
- survived challenge;
- contradiction;
- retrofit cost;
- causal reach;
- dramatic usefulness;
- whether predicted residue is actually present;
- whether a candidate requires hidden facts solely to save itself.

Failed hypotheses remain useful evidence.

## Hot/cold memory

Keep hot:

- compact longitudinal thread state;
- strongest competing old-life candidates;
- strongest competing future candidates;
- active forward consequences and backward requirements;
- unresolved collisions;
- compact recent rehearsal evidence.

Cool:

- routine full generation transcripts;
- dead semantic cousins;
- rejected branches whose lesson is captured compactly;
- prose-scale speculation without an active discriminating question.

Full evidence remains addressable by provenance when materially useful.

## First proving run

Do not attempt exhaustive 500-chapter prose reasoning in one giant prompt.

First run:

1. compile a bounded longitudinal evidence packet from current accepted PLG canon through Chapter 500;
2. create several old-life candidate trajectories at different temporal distances;
3. create several post-500 candidate trajectories at different temporal distances;
4. force at least one bidirectional collision where a future candidate asks something of the old-life search or vice versa;
5. select the cheapest discriminating REHEARSAL for the highest-value collision;
6. reduce evidence through STORY SYNC;
7. preserve the result as derived experiment state with zero canon writes.

The first run is successful if it produces at least one surprising but canon-compatible candidate, rejects or materially weakens at least one attractive candidate, and demonstrates that the 500-chapter middle constrains both directions more usefully than free backstory/future generation.

## Later evaluation

After the outward state is mature enough to be useful, freeze it before any rewrite experiment.

A separately approved later experiment may reread Chapters 1-500 with derived before/after context and ask whether scenes are newly legible, under-rendered, redundant, or missing earned resonance. That later stage must distinguish world/trajectory problems from rendering problems and reader-observation/curation problems.

## Non-goals

- no automatic canon promotion;
- no immediate rewrite of Chapters 1-500;
- no requirement to explain every Greg trait with backstory;
- no fixed exact number of old-life/future slabs beyond useful temporal separation;
- no requirement that future candidates become chapter outlines;
- no maximal-heat PERFORMANCE on every candidate;
- no new visible cognitive UI for the creator;
- no new general-purpose feature merely because the experiment suggests one.
