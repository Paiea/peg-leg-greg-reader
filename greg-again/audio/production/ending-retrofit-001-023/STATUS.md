# Greg, Again ending retrofit 001–023

## Current state

- Written Chapters 001–023 have already been merged to `main` with terminal `...` on the final prose sentence.
- One short `deep` landing segment has been regenerated for every Chapter 001–023 using the final three ending beats and the new terminal ellipsis.
- All 23 provider previews have crossed the durable artifact boundary and are stored under:
  - `greg-again/audio/production/ending-retrofit-001-023/audio/001.mp3`
  - through `023.mp3`
- Provider context IDs and original preview URLs are preserved in `provider-map.json`.
- These landing renders are repair artifacts only. They are **not yet routed into the published chapter MP3s**.

## Why publication is intentionally not done yet

The regenerated landing segment is smaller than many chapters' historical final take. Appending it would duplicate the existing ending. Blindly replacing the historical final take would omit earlier words from that take.

Therefore integration must first establish an exact replacement boundary, or regenerate the entire final take from exact source text in preview-safe subsegments and replace that final take wholesale.

Do not publish by simply appending the new landing clip to an existing chapter MP3.

## Cadence result

The terminal ellipsis is a useful performance nudge, not a deterministic downbeat control. Human listen-back found that it improves roughly part of the endings rather than every ending. Keep the ellipsis convention because the improvement is useful and cheap; individual continuation-like misses may receive a later tiny local cadence repair.

## Ownership / unpublished chapters

Chapters 016, 020, and 023 were not published in the audio manifest when this retrofit began. Their landing artifacts should be consumed by the active chapter production workers rather than overwriting unfinished chapter transactions.

## Pronunciation

Written `mana` remains `mana`. Provider-facing pronunciation remains `mah-nah` where needed under current audio authority.
