## Chapter-specific listening guardrail

> **TIME MUST LEAVE RESIDUE.**

The listener should hear that absence had consequences. Jorren moved farther north. Greg's body and habits changed. Sella became more efficient with him. Arlo and Ivena redesigned, tested, negotiated, sold, and financed work without waiting for Greg. Greg returns to a life that continued moving.
