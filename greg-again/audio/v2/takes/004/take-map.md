```text
A month later, Sella stopped asking for my guild token.

That was how I noticed the month.

Not calendar.

Not tally marks beside the bed.

Not the increasingly complicated page where I had attempted to track training, debt, food, mana, shale, and several financial decisions that looked much worse when arranged chronologically.

Sella looked up when I came in, reached beneath the counter, and slid the yard register toward me before I had finished reaching for the token.

Habit.

Mine and hers.

That was time.

Jorren was leaning against the end of the counter in new boots.

That was time too.

I noticed because the old pair had split inside the heel three weeks ago and he had complained about the repair cost for most of one sparring session.

"You got the escort."

He looked down at the boots.

"You remember that?"

I had forgotten his name twice during our first week.

Apparently memory had priorities.

North-road caravan left in nine days.

Jorren had spent the month earning enough contract credit to get taken on.

While I had been busy converting future knowledge into rent, training fees, loan interest, experimental ceramics, food, and a large pile of shale that remained financially committed to being stone.

His life had moved without me.

Good.

Annoying somehow.

Still good.

Sella tapped register.

Right.

I signed.
```
