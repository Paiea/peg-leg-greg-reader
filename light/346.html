TEXT READER · CHAPTER 346

THE PASSING POINT

Text-only reading · no chapter illustrations

Hessa sent no note.

She appeared at breakfast.

This was worse.

Cala opened the door, saw her, and stepped aside with the expression of someone allowing weather indoors.

Hessa looked at me.

"South Walk."

"Good morning."

"It is."

"Any reason you are physically here instead of threatening me with paper?"

"You left one question unfinished."

Lyssa looked up from the table.

"He has several."

"One I care about."

I knew which one.

GREG MOVING + EXTERNAL EFFECT.

Hessa pointed at my bowl.

"Finish eating."

"This is kind."

"This is scheduling."

Close enough.

I ate.

No artifacts.

No oval.

No holdfast.

No rest stone.

Hessa checked my bag before we left anyway.

"You don't trust me."

"Correct."

"Healthy relationship."

"We do not have a relationship."

"Healthier."

South Walk was dry today.

Same long stone arcade.

Same open arches.

No wind worth mentioning.

Hessa had already marked the floor.

Not the old pattern.

A narrow walking lane.

Three chalk lines across it.

One bead suspended at shoulder height beside the middle line.

Stationary.

"There it is," I said.

"Yes."

"You actually came to answer it."

"No."

I stared.

She pointed at the lane.

"I came to test it."

Right.

Important distinction.

"Body check."

"Right leg fine. Left knee fine. Shoulders fine. Hands fine."

"Sleep?"

"Enough."

She waited.

"Adequate."

"Better."

I hated precise people.

"Any work yesterday?"

"Mara bench observation. Mostly sitting."

"Any magic?"

"No."

"Good."

She pointed to first chalk line.

"Walk through. No mana."

I did.

Both crutches.

Right foot.

Body forward.

The bead sat just outside direct path.

I passed it.

Turned at far mark.

Came back.

Again.

Three passes.

"Where is easiest stable contact?" she asked.

"Both crutches planted. Right-foot contact after that."

"Today?"

"Today."

"Good."

Then six minimal draws while walking.

Not new idea.

Calibration.

First two clean during both-crutch plant.

Third smeared during swing.

Fourth clean enough after right-foot contact.

Fifth broke early during transition.

Sixth held better than fifth but not clean.

No revelation.

Useful baseline.

Minimal total fifty-three.

Then Hessa said, "External effect."

I stopped.

"While walking."

"Yes."

There it was.

"Stationary bead?"

"Yes."

"One grain?"

"Yes."

"Same near range?"

"Yes."

"Anything else you want to say before I make this ugly?"

"Do not correct late."

Of course.

She reset bead in tiny cloth loop.

No guide thread this time.

I noticed.

"Why no lower guide?"

"Target stationary. I want visible response if it moves."

"More swing noise."

"Small effect."

Fair.

She gave me first instruction.

"Do not cast at first pass."

"Why?"

"Because you want to."

Hessa was evil.

I walked.

Bead entered peripheral vision.

Passed.

No magic.

Turn.

Return.

No magic.

Third pass.

Still no magic.

"Good," she said.

"I did nothing."

"Correctly."

This training was hostile to narrative.

"Now attempt one."

I started.

Both crutches plant.

Right foot through.

Bead approaching peripheral field.

I drew.

External effect.

Too early.

Nothing.

"Again."

Attempt two.

I waited longer.

Contact maybe.

Bead twitched after I had already passed it.

Late.

"You threw backward," Hessa said.

"I know."

"No. You know result was late. Different."

Annoying.

Attempt three.

I tried to hold target point rather than bead.

Body moved.

Mana shape felt wrong.

I stopped before pushing.

No effect.

Hessa nodded.

"Counted."

"As failure?"

"As attempt."

Right.

Attempt four.

Stable crutch plant before bead.

I initiated draw.

Then movement carried me through.

The bead shifted.

Tiny.

Toward me.

While I was still moving.

I almost stopped walking.

Hessa said, "Keep route."

I kept route.

Turned.

Came back.

"Clean?"

"No."

Of course.

"Why?"

"Effect landed after your best contact phase. You chased with shoulder."

"But it moved."

"Yes."

I hated yes.

Attempt five.

Nothing.

Attempt six.

No effect.

But the shaping stayed cleaner through body transition.

Hessa noticed.

"Separate."

"I know."

"Say it."

"Better shaping through transition does not mean external effect succeeded."

"Good."

Attempt seven.

I deliberately chose a pass where timing felt bad and did nothing.

Hessa said nothing.

"Does skip count?"

"No attempt if no draw."

Good.

Next pass.

Attempt seven actual.

Tiny movement.

Cleaner.

I was moving past bead.

No late correction.

The effect happened near when bead crossed just behind my forward shoulder line.

Hessa stopped me after turn.

"That one."

"Clean?"

"Clean enough to keep."

I smiled.

"Do not."

Smile gone.

"Why?"

"Because now you will try to repeat it instead of observe it."

She knew me.

Unfortunately.

Attempt eight.

Failed because I tried to repeat it.

Hessa looked at me.

I looked away.

"I hate you."

"Useful."

Attempt nine.

I changed nothing intentionally.

Or tried not to.

No effect.

Attempt ten.

Contact.

Not clean.

Bead moved after my right crutch had already advanced.

Attempt eleven.

No effect.

Attempt twelve.

Clean small movement.

Better than seven.

Still not large.

Still one-grain-ish.

Still stationary target.

But I was moving.

Hessa stopped the session immediately.

"Done."

"That's two clean enough."

"Yes."

"Out of twelve."

"Yes."

"Can we do twelve more?"

"No."

"Six?"

"No."

"Three?"

"You are demonstrating the problem."

Right.

Result greed.

Again.

I sat on the low wall.

Not exhausted.

Concentrated.

Different fatigue.

Hessa untied the bead.

I counted.

External effect one hundred fifteen.

Minimal fifty-three.

Deliberate shaping still fifty-three.

No new shaping attempts as separate counted category today.

"So now I can cast while moving."

Hessa looked at me.

I sighed.

"No."

"Correct."

"I can sometimes produce a small supervised near-range external effect on a stationary target while moving through a familiar walking route."

"Better."

"Two clean-enough out of twelve."

"Yes."

"One contact-ish. One ugly success."

"Your labels are less useful than the route notes."

Of course.

She drew three small marks on the chalk lane.

First before bead.

Second beside bead.

Third after bead.

"Where did clean effects occur?"

I walked the route mentally.

"Not at both-crutch plant. Later."

"Yes."

That surprised me.

"My cleanest shaping baseline is earlier."

"Today."

"Today."

"And external contact?"

"Later in motion."

"Possibly."

"Why?"

"That is your next wrong question."

I frowned.

She tapped chalk beside bead.

"Do not ask why before you know whether it repeats."

Fair.

"Could be timing to target geometry."

"Could."

"Could be body transition changing field relation."

"Could."

"Could be visual timing."

"Could."

"Could be random."

"Yes."

"I hate that one."

"Then keep it."

I hated her professionally.

Hessa changed the task again.

Not more magic.

Walking only.

She had me pass the bead at three different lateral distances.

Close.

Normal.

Farther.

No casting.

Then three speeds.

Slow.

Normal.

Slightly quicker.

No casting.

"Why no test?"

"Because you have one session of moving-caster evidence and want to multiply axes immediately."

"Correct."

"No."

"Cruel."

She pointed at my crutches.

"You also change route geometry when speed changes."

"Yes."

"And lateral distance changes target timing."

"Yes."

"So next session can choose one."

There.

Future question without answering everything.

I looked at chalk marks.

"Distance or speed."

"Or neither."

"You enjoy withholding."

"I enjoy interpretable results."

Same thing from my perspective.

She packed bead.

"Any independent practice?"

"No."

"Any artifact activation?"

"Mara bench observation."

"You activated?"

"Mara used remote rod."

"Any mana use by you?"

"No."

"Good."

I told her the narrow result.

Less-than-one-eighth ring turn.

Internal click.

Bench edge response.

No visible base shift.

No exact function.

Hessa listened.

No excitement.

"Do not combine it with practice."

"I wasn't going to."

She looked at me.

"I wasn't currently going to."

"Better."

We left South Walk.

At the outer arch, I noticed a new public notice board.

Not because it was magical.

Because people were reading it.

NORTH SECONDARY

DAY PASSAGE REOPENED UNDER MARKED ROUTE

NIGHT CLOSURE REMAINS

REVIEW CONTINUES

There.

The long mile had changed without me.

Good.

I read once.

Kept walking.

Hessa said nothing.

"You saw that."

"Yes."

"You are not going to ask if I want to go inspect it."

"No."

"Growth everywhere."

"Don't ruin it."

At home, Lyssa asked how many times I fell.

"Zero."

"How many times did magic work?"

"Depends how insulting you want the definition."

"Normal amount."

"Two clean enough out of twelve moving external attempts."

"Is that good?"

I thought.

"It is real."

Better answer.

Cala asked, "Paid?"

"No."

"Spent?"

"No."

"Good day."

Household economics.

Tavi asked whether I could move spoons while walking now.

"Probably badly."

"Do it."

"No."

"Why?"

"Because Hessa would appear in the wall."

He looked at wall.

Reasonable.

I went upstairs.

Three copper.

Twenty-six Vale.

Holdfast high shelf.

Rest stone boxed.

Oval near bag.

I wrote:

53 MINIMAL

53 DELIBERATE SHAPING

115 EXTERNAL EFFECT

MOVING CASTER + STATIONARY TARGET: 12 SUPERVISED ATTEMPTS

2 CLEAN ENOUGH / SMALL / NEAR RANGE

DO NOT GENERALIZE

Then:

CLEANEST EXTERNAL CONTACT MAY HAVE LANDED LATER IN GAIT THAN CLEANEST SHAPING BASELINE TODAY

I stared.

Too confident.

Changed it.

TODAY: CLEAN EXTERNAL RESULTS OCCURRED LATER IN PASS THAN EASIEST SHAPING PHASE

Better.

Then:

NEXT AXIS: MAYBE DISTANCE OR SPEED / NOT BOTH

I almost added something about progress.

Did not.

The counts were enough.

Two clean effects did not make a skill.

But they made the unanswered question smaller.

That was enough for one day.
