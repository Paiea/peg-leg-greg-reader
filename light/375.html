Good.

Accumulation.

Of course.

Probably theatrical.

I blinked.

"Problem."

"Yes."

"Kills blocking."

"Different angle?"

"Tried."

"What exactly do you want from mine?"

"Mount on rated accessory rail. Face west gallery. Run lamp at rehearsal setting. If spill reduction behaves inside known family context and rear plate stays below hot limit, we use for two rehearsal blocks. You stay present. I pay two copper equipment use."

Two.

I looked at him.

"Two copper for afternoon?"

"Equipment use plus owner attendance."

"Damage?"

"Theatre-caused damage to your unit during approved mounting/use, theatre pays assessed repair or agreed replacement value if repair impossible. Existing fault not ours."

"Replacement value unknown."

"Then assessed repair first. If destroyed and value disputed, Mara or another mutually accepted assessor."

Good.

"Heat stop?"

"Our TR-6 plate limit. Warm allowed. Hot stop. Also stop for smell, sound, discoloration, vent obstruction, unstable mount, effect change, or owner request."

"Yes."

Jori had done this before.

Maybe not this object.

Equipment.

Of course.

Of course.

Accessory mount slot.

At least in this family context.

"Your consent?"

Three fingers clearance behind.

No activation yet.

Same.

"Yes."

"Whatever you're about to infer."

"I wasn't."
