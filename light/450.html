TEXT READER · CHAPTER 425

THE PASSENGER

Text-only reading · no chapter illustrations

North ferry did not cancel.

Rude.

I had half expected weather to save me six copper after I already paid it.

No such luck.

Second ring came gray and cold.

Dorrin basin bells sounded departure clearance.

I packed.

Trial leg wrapped.

Crutches.

Lot 67 nested between notebook boards.

Cash fifteen.

Ten copper floor.

Five discretionary.

Brineward ticket already hurt yesterday.

Today hurt only philosophically.

At dock, ferry was bigger than Low Lantern.

**North Crown**.

Two decks.

Low cargo waist.

Passenger cabin aft.

Not luxurious.

Just enclosed.

Mainland boat.

Different assumption.

Kelp Isles ferries expected spray.

North Crown expected distance.

Deck clerk saw crutches.

"Lower boarding?"

"Please."

"Cargo ramp after salt barrels."

No performance.

Good.

I waited.

Barrels first.

Then two wheeled crates.

Then me.

Ramp broad, ribbed, damp but not slick.

I crossed on crutches.

Trial leg remained pack cargo because wet deck plus rolling ferry remained stupid.

Inside passenger cabin, benches actually had backs.

Civilization.

Maybe decadence.

Passengers around twenty.

Salt merchants.

Woman with three children.

Two men in leather road coats.

Young priest maybe.

Old woman knitting rope cord.

One soldier asleep sitting upright with helmet in lap.

No one knew me.

Excellent.

Boat left Dorrin.

Kelp lattice receded.

Blue bleed towers became small.

Then sea.

Open sea proper.

No fields.

No lease bells.

No marker lanes except occasional black buoy.

Different scale immediately.

One road-coat man said to other, "Shelf road opened yesterday."

Other said, "Opened is generous. Passable is what board says."

Useful overheard noun.

Shelf road.

Brineward had north road.

Maybe actual route.

I did not join conversation.

Learning restraint socially too.

First bell easy.

Second bell less.

North Crown rolled deeper than island boats.

Not sharper.

Slower.

Big movement.

My stomach disliked the confidence.

I stayed seated.

Cabin windows showed gray water rising and falling.

At third bell, crew shut outer hatch.

Rain.

No drama.

Just mainland weather appearing before mainland.

Young priest looked sick.

Soldier still slept.

Talent.

I ate dry bread.

Bad decision.

Half later, regretted.

Kept it.

Victory.

I checked Lot 67 in pack.

Dry.

No experiment.

Could maybe use ferry bell intervals as external reference.

No.

Boat motion, human bell timing, unknown cadence.

Not controlled enough.

Later.

At maybe fifth bell, mainland appeared.

Not city first.

Line.

Dark long coast.

Then cliffs south.

Low marsh north.

Then towers.

Brineward had no elegant beacon field.

It had cranes.

Tall timber cranes all along harbor mouth.

Some mechanical.

Some with blue-lit counterweights.

One enormous stone gantry straddled an inner channel.

Behind that, roofs.

Many.

More than Dorrin by a lot.

Not Duskport huge maybe.

But proper port.

Smoke.

Warehouses.

Road carts.

Bell towers.

Three separate docks active at once.

North Crown waited outside harbor mouth.

Why?

Pilot boat.

Small red hull came out.

Man climbed aboard.

Harbor pilot.

Different scale rule.

He took position beside captain.

North Crown entered.

Water inside harbor thick with traffic.

Salt barges.

Timber scows.

Passenger packets.

One narrow vessel with six blue ceramic ribs down side humming faint.

No idea.

Not today.

Port smelled salt, coal-smoke, fish, horse, pitch, hot metal.

Less magical in obvious surface than Dorrin.

More everything.

Brineward didn't need magic to look impossible.

It had bureaucracy.

Dock signs everywhere.

**INBOUND SALT / QUAY TWO**

**PASSENGERS / WEST GATE**

**ANIMAL DECLARATION**

**CHARGED GOODS ABOVE THIRD GRADE / EAST HOLD**

**ROAD PERMITS / CUSTOMS ANNEX**

There.

Mainland.

People had paperwork for roads.

North Crown docked.

Crew unloaded cargo before passengers.

Salt barrels.

Crates.

Then lower ramp.

I crossed.

Dry stone quay.

First mainland step.

Nothing happened.

Correct.

I stood a moment anyway.

Not emotional.

Just orientation.

Dorrin behind water.

Carrow much farther behind that.

Brineward ahead.

Cash fifteen.

Ten floor.

Five mistakes available.

Let's not spend all at once.

West passenger gate had entry clerk.

"Name."

"Greg."

"Family."

"Don't use one."

She looked up.

"No surname?"

"No."

"Origin?"

"Carrow."

"Last port?"

"Dorrin."

"Purpose?"

Good question.

"Travel."

She waited.

"Work if useful."

She wrote something.

"Charged goods?"

"Small personal tools. Nothing above declared household/field grade as far as I know."

That was careful.

She looked at pack.

"Any wet kelp?"

"No."

"Live marine stock?"

"No."

"Caged runner?"

"No."

"Good."

Apparently kelp runners were customs problem.

Stamp.

No fee.

Excellent.

Outside gate: city.

Port city unlike Duskport.

Duskport had layers built around old waterfront and trade.

Brineward felt stretched along roads.

Everything pointed inland.

Warehouses gave way to cart yards.

Cart yards to inns.

Inns to road boards.

Salt road east.

Shelf road north.

South coast road.

Passenger coaches.

Freight caravans.

Actual mainland network.

I found public board before lodging.

Need know prices.

**SALT ROAD / EASTGATE COACH / DAILY / 3C TO MARSH CROSS / 5C TO VERN HALL**

**SHELF ROAD / NORTH COACH / WEATHER BOARD FIRST RING / 4C TO GREY POST / 7C TO HIGH SHELF**

**SOUTH COAST / 3C TO LERRIK**

Names.

Roads.

Money disappearing merely reading.

North road four copper minimum to Grey Post.

My discretionary five.

One road ticket would nearly touch floor.

Good boundary.

Not today.

Lodging board.

Cheap bunks near cart quarter:

1c common floor.

2c bed.

I chose floor.

But before paying, work board.

Need earning route first.

Brineward public labor board larger than Dorrin whole market stall.

Rows.

**SALT SACK COUNT / QUAY TWO / 2C HALF**

**CART TAG COPY / WEST YARD / 2C HALF / WRITING**

**WAREHOUSE ROLL / STRONG BACK / 6C**

No.

**ROAD LEDGER COPY / CUSTOMS ANNEX / 3C HALF / CLEAN HAND**

Maybe.

**CRANE SPOTTER / EXPERIENCE REQUIRED / 5C**

No.

**PACKET CLAIM SORT / 2C**

Too familiar maybe.

Road ledger copy interesting because inland routes.

I took tab.

Customs annex.

Stone building.

Dry.

Stairs.

Problem.

Three steps entrance.

No ramp front.

Side entrance maybe.

I asked guard.

"Ledger copy job?"

"Second room."

"Any level entrance?"

He pointed alley.

"Freight door."

Good.

Freight door had threshold but manageable.

Inside clerk looked at crutches.

"You here for copy?"

"Yes."

"Can you read route marks?"

"Probably not local ones."

"Can you copy exactly?"

"Yes."

"Better answer. Sit."

Name **Tirn Bale**.

Voice flat.

Job simple.

Three stacks road permits from previous week.

Copy route, carrier, declared load class, escort requirement, departure date into summary ledger.

No interpretation.

Ask on unclear symbols.

Perfect.

I worked.

Shelf road marks different.

Triangle with notch = upper-grade slope permit.

Blue dot = charged load declaration.

Black bar = axle-width restriction.

Double circle = overnight gate hold allowed.

I copied.

One permit had **SHELF ROAD / GREY POST / WEATHER INSTRUMENT CASE / NO ESCORT / DELICATE**.

Interesting.

Another:

**HIGH SHELF / QUARRY RESONANCE STONES / ESCORT TWO / CHARGE III**.

Another:

**MARSH CROSS / SALT MEDICINE / PRIORITY**.

Mainland routes carried different objects.

Not just kelp.

Not just road.

I copied.

Half shift.

Tirn checked random entries.

One error.

I copied carrier number 18 as 13.

He circled.

"Fix."

I did.

No protagonist perfection.

Good.

He paid three copper.

Fifteen to eighteen.

That changed day.

Now floor ten leaves eight discretionary.

Still thin.

But breathing room.

"More tomorrow?" I asked.

"Maybe. First ring board."

Everyone same answer everywhere.

Civilization unified by no promises.

I asked one question.

"Grey Post what kind place?"

Tirn shrugged.

"Road town. Shelf gate. Survey yard. Weather office. Expensive beds when road closes."

Survey yard.

Weather office.

There.

Potential.

"High Shelf?"

"Mining plateau. Quarries. Wind. People regret boots."

"Useful."

"No. Cold."

Good.

I left.

Paid **1c** common floor bunk at **Bent Nail** inn.

Eighteen to seventeen.

Bits for stew.

Current floor still ten.

Seven discretionary.

North coach to Grey Post four.

Possible.

Tomorrow maybe.

But Brineward first.

I needed stable ground.

Trial leg had rested two days now.

Bent Nail common room had dry wood floor.

Task: laundry and repack after sea.

Useful.

I asked innkeeper if okay to stand/wash near side bench.

"Don't block door."

Fine.

Trial limb on.

Fit familiar now.

That itself mattered.

Not easy.

Less ceremony.

Liner.

Socket.

Straps.

Stand with crutches.

Weight.

Pause.

Body map loaded faster.

I did laundry basin carry half-full.

Eight steps.

Turn.

Bench.

No wobble.

Then two-handed folded-clothes carry ten steps.

Turn at table.

Cleaner.

Twenty minutes passed before I realized I had not been narrating every foot placement in head.

That was new enough to stop me.

Not automatic.

Just quieter.

I continued standing.

At twenty-five minutes hip fatigue.

At twenty-seven socket heat mild.

I stopped at twenty-eight.

No push to thirty because round number stupid.

Skin check.

Upper pink mild.

Lower-front faint line.

At twenty minutes lower clear, upper mostly clear.

At thirty upper clear.

Good.

Logged:

**Brineward Bent Nail. 28 min dry wood. Laundry/repack. Half-basin carry 8 steps. Folded-clothes carry 10. Turns cleaner. Foot placement planning quieter until ~20. Hip fatigue 25, mild heat 27. Stop 28. Lower clear 20; upper mostly clear 20, clear 30.**

More hands.

More ordinary.

That was the win.

Evening I walked on crutches to road square.

No leg after session.

Road board updated weather.

**SHELF ROAD / GREY POST / OPEN WITH DELAY / HIGH WIND ABOVE MILE TEN**

Tomorrow coach still 4c.

Grey Post.

Survey yard.

Weather office.

Could work maybe.

Could also burn four, then bunk, then sit at twelve maybe.

If no work, near floor.

Need think.

Then I saw sign at customs annex side wall:

**SURVEY TRANSIT ASSISTANCE / GREY POST / ONE-DAY CLERK SEAT / DEPART FIRST RING / 5C PAY / COACH FARE EMPLOYEE RATE 2C / APPLY BEFORE NIGHT BELL**

Ah.

Danger.

Not huge payday.

Actual temporary job attached to route.

One-day clerk seat.

Five copper pay.

Coach fare employee rate two.

Net +3 before lodging.

Exactly kind of thing road could generate.

I read requirements.

**copy manifests / hold weather packets / no field authority / must travel with survey coach / return not guaranteed same day**

Interesting.

Apply before night bell.

I asked window clerk.

"What does hold weather packets mean?"

"Keep sealed packets dry and hand them to Grey Post office."

"Why one-day seat?"

"Regular clerk sick."

"Experience?"

"Writing. Route copying. Tirn endorsement."

I had that maybe.

"Mobility?"

She looked at crutches.

"Coach seats. Grey Post office has level rear entry. You don't load crates."

Good.

"Pay when?"

"Grey Post office signs five copper on delivery."

So if coach turns back? Maybe no pay.

"If weather stops coach before Grey Post?"

"Transit delay rate one copper per day after first if held at staffed gate."

Real terms.

"Return?"

"You're off contract at Grey Post. Buy return or continue."

There.

Not free round trip.

Still route-changing job.

I asked for application.

Tirn was still inside.

He came out.

"You want it?"

"Maybe."

"You copied one number wrong."

"I corrected it."

"Yes."

"Is that endorsement?"

He stared.

"Unfortunately."

Signed.

I laughed.

Could take job.

Would pay employee coach 2c tomorrow.

Current seventeen.

Pay fare: fifteen.

At Grey Post receive five: twenty if completed.

Bunk maybe expensive if road closes.

But floor protected better.

And moves me north.

No prophecy.

No quest.

Clerical temp job.

Perfect.

I signed.

Paid employee fare **2c now**.

Seventeen to fifteen.

Funny.

Back where chapter started.

But tomorrow route and earning claim changed.

Ticket chit says:

**BRINEWARD SURVEY TRANSIT / GREY POST / CLERK SEAT / FIRST RING**

Duties:

manifest copies.

sealed weather packet custody.

no field authority.

Five copper on delivery.

I liked `no field authority` enough to frame.

At inn I added note.

**Brineward. Mainland. Took one-day survey clerk seat to Grey Post tomorrow. Paid 2c employee coach. 5c on delivery if we make it. Cash 15c, Vale 23c. Ten floor intact. Trial build 28 min dry use, fine. - Greg**

Postage bits.

Maybe too many notes home.

But route farther.

Home needed crumbs.

I sat with notebook.

Passenger.

That was title.

Not traveler.

Not explorer.

Passenger.

Today I had paid to let other systems move me.

Boat.

Port gate.

Work ledger.

Inn.

Tomorrow survey coach.

Being passenger did not mean passive.

It meant knowing which part wasn't yours to steer.

Useful distinction.

Also cheaper than owning boat.

Tomorrow Grey Post.
