Hessa's note arrived while I was eating second breakfast.

Not because I had become wealthy.

Because first breakfast had been bread.

Progress had layers.

Good.

"Twelve."

"Yes."

"No."

"Speed?"

"Same shallow lift unless I tell you otherwise."

"Mass unchanged."

"Yes."

"Direction target unchanged relative to travel."

"Yes."

"Timing changes."

"Yes."

I wrote before she could accuse me of enjoying clarity.

Hessa put three cards into small cloth bag.

Shuffled.

I frowned.

"Random order?"

"Rotating predictable blocks changes attention."

"So you choose timing secretly."

"I choose card. I tell you window only after shuttle starts."

"That adds reaction delay."

"Yes."

"Then reaction delay is confound."

"Also real."

I hated good experiments.

"Could use fixed spoken cue."

"Later."

"Could use bell."

"Later."

"Could use mechanical marker."

"Later."

She stared.

I stopped.

Attempt one-forty-eight.

Hessa drew card.

Did not show me.

Lifted track.

"Early."

Nothing visible.

Pad.

She marked.

One-forty-nine.

"Late."

I waited.

The shuttle crossed middle.

Near final mark.

Attempt.

Nothing.

One-fifty.

"Middle."

Attempt.

Small tick.

Maybe.

Hessa crouched.

Looked.

"Within bad-wheel baseline spread."

Fair.

One-fifty-one.

"Middle."

Attempt.

Nothing.

First four.

No clean responses.

Excellent.

Hessa noticed.

"Your face is trying to average four trials into destiny."

"My face is private."

"No."

Not because magic strain.

Because she wanted same interval between blocks.

Of course she did.

Did not ask result.

Growth.

"Body location?"

"No."

"No."

"Unrelated. Fine."

"Fine."

"Fine."

Then turned entire track around.

Good.

Attempt one-fifty-two.

"Early."

Attempt.

Tiny.

Clean enough that charcoal edge shifted before pad.

Hessa leaned.

Marked contact.

"Yes."

"Yes."

"Good."

One-fifty-three.

"Late."

"Middle."

One-fifty-five.

"Early."

Then bad wheel wobbled back.

Pad contact landed near baseline.

"Unclear."

"Because I saw something and cannot separate it cleanly from known wobble."

Good.

Annoying.

Eight attempts total now.

One clean.

Two unclear if including one-fifty and one-fifty-five.

One-fifty-seven.

Hessa marked.

I breathed normally.

Probably.

Two.

One-fifty-eight.

"Early."

Nothing.

One-fifty-nine.

Did not say it immediately.

Shuttle rolled.

She waited.

"Middle."

Nothing.

Pad.

Done.

"Three late."

"Exactly."

"Not enough for strong claim."

"Enough to design next?"

"Maybe."

Maybe again.

"Correct."

"Correct."

"Maybe."

I said, "Late might fail because I have less time before impact."

"Yes."

"Maybe."

"Or because cue makes me rushed."

"Yes."

"Do not use momentum unless you know what you mean."

"I know what it means broadly."

"Broadly is how bad conclusions reproduce."

Fair.

"More established motion."

"Still interpretation."

"Fine. Later in travel."

"Observed."

Vocabulary mattered.

Hessa wrote:

**Candidate next question: is reduced late-window response due to target timing, Greg response latency, or simple observation limits?**

**Do not solve three variables with one experiment.**

I liked that enough to hate it.

"What would you isolate first?"

"Your response latency."

I blinked.

"Me."

"Yes."

"Not magic."

"You are part of experiment."

Terrible.

"How?"

"Non-magic cue response."

I looked at cards.

She placed shuttle aside.

Then put small wooden peg on table.

"Touch peg when I say now."

I stared.

"This is humiliating."

"Good."

She waited.

"Now."

I touched peg.

"Again."

She moved peg a handspan.

"Now."

Touch.

Again.

Different placement.

Again.

No magic.

Just reaction.

She varied delay.

Sometimes immediate after setting peg.

Sometimes several breaths.

I started anticipating.

She noticed.

"Eyes on peg. Not me."

"Your mouth creates cue."

"Eyes on peg."

Five more.

Then she had me close eyes.

"Why?"

"Auditory response without visual target."

"Now."

She stared.

"Excellent."

"Peg moved."

"Yes."

"You cheated."

"I changed target."

"That is cheating."

"It is measurement of whether you were listening to words or remembering location."

I hated her professionally.

She did not count those as magic attempts.

Good.

After several minutes she wrote something separate from protocol numbers.

"Your response timing varies more than you think."

"Everybody's does."

"Yes."

"Then why insulting me personally?"

"Because you are here."

Fair.

"Next session may use fixed cue independent of my speech."

"Bell?"

"Possibly."

"Mechanical drop?"

"Possibly."

"Second observer?"

"Possibly."

"You're becoming generous."

"No."

"Tell me current claim."

"Yes."

"Good."

"Good."
