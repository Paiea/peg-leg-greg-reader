## Theatre / illusion interface

Illusion already belongs to the world and theatre as practical craft. Prefer hybrid competence rather than making every effect one person's superpower: specialists may handle stronger effects; actors can use routine glamours; crew can use narrow repeatable projection, sound, veiling, atmosphere, or prepared effects.

Greg should remain meaningfully behind experienced theatrical specialists for a long time, especially in timing, readability, aesthetics, efficiency, cue discipline, rehearsal integration, repeatability, and recovery.
