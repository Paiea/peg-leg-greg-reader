# WORLD

Durable setting and worldbuilding memory.

Keep this file for broad truths and indexes. Split dense systems into their own files rather than letting this become a giant wiki page.

## Geography and places

_Track recurring locations, spatial relationships, travel constraints, roads, neighborhoods, institutions, theatres, workplaces, and meaningful environmental details._

## Society and culture

_Track class, customs, etiquette, food, language patterns, labor, performance culture, law, religion, education, and social assumptions only as established or deliberately designed._

## Economy and work

_Track wages, prices, scarcity, jobs, contracts, ownership, trade, transport, and practical money constraints when they affect story._

## Institutions and hidden systems

_Track guilds, government, enforcement, magic institutions, employers, powerful networks, hidden systems, and asymmetric information._

## Technology / material culture

_Track tools, infrastructure, medicine, transport, printing, lighting, weapons, construction, theatre machinery, and other limits that matter._

## Continuity risks

_Record world details most likely to drift between chats or chapters._

## Open worldbuilding questions

_Questions worth resolving when the story naturally reaches them. Do not answer them merely to fill the file._
