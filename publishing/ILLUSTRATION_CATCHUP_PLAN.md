## A. HIGH-PRIORITY FEATURE / LARGE SCENE ART

### CHAPTER 30 — C30-S01 — FOURTEEN WEST
- WHY IT DESERVES LARGE ART: The book exits the immediate Darrowmere crisis through ordinary civilian logistics. Fourteen people, two carts, three children, one injured adult, Greg and Alden escorting west. It shows the world continuing through displacement rather than turning the aftermath into heroic spectacle.
- CHARACTERS: Greg, Alden in red scarf with LIGHT USE LEFT restriction, Sava, smallest girl, mixed civilian group.
- LOCATION: West road outside Darrowmere, town receding behind them.
- ACTION: The civilian column rolls west while the smallest girl interrogates Greg about his disappointing magic; Greg glances back toward distant Darrowmere.
- COMPOSITION: Very wide road composition. Civilians and carts form the main diagonal; Greg should not dominate the frame. Darrowmere sits smaller in the far background.
- MOVEMENT / EYE FLOW: Enter on cart wheels/children → move through civilians → Greg/Alden → land on distant town/bell tower.
- IMPORTANT MANUSCRIPT DETAILS: exactly fourteen civilians; two carts; three children; one injured adult; one cart carries bedding/pots/old man with bandaged foot; other carries sacks/children; Greg has LIMITED CASTING tag; Alden has red scarf and LIGHT USE LEFT tag.
- CONTINUITY RISKS: Do not make it a refugee army or monster attack. Do not turn Greg into a powerful wizard. Any dark shape on the bell tower should remain ambiguous or omitted.
- BEST ASPECT RATIO: 2:1 or 16:9 landscape.

- ACTION: Physical gate work with hooked poles/wedge while a river lizard creates a small complication; Greg uses a coin-sized Barrier as a positioning tool rather than a weapon.

- MOVEMENT / EYE FLOW: Water/millrace → hooked poles and straining hands → small lizard/coin-sized Barrier → stopped wheel.
- IMPORTANT MANUSCRIPT DETAILS: under three miles outside city; narrow river; stone gate; stopped mill wheel; wet boots; hand labor; Barrier tiny and precise.
- CONTINUITY RISKS: Barrier must not become a blue shield. Do not turn river lizard into boss monster. Berren is still managing an injured foot/altered boot.
- BEST ASPECT RATIO: 16:9 landscape.

- IMPORTANT MANUSCRIPT DETAILS: mud in specific places; three loaded carts along wall; fourth crooked near weighing platform with left rear wheel removed; crates under two canvas sheets; active wheelwright with black mud on elbows.
- CONTINUITY RISKS: Do not make Greg foreman/owner. No dramatic magic. Preserve ordinary labor competence around him.
