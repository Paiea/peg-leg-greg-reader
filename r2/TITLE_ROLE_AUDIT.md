# R2 Chapter Title Role Audit

Status: **APPROVED THROUGH CHAPTER 26**

Rule: every selected/public title answers **Who is Greg in this chapter?**

This audit is deliberately narrow. KEEP a title when it already names a real Greg role. RENAME only when the title is a topic/event/object/place/time label or assigns somebody else's role to Greg. Duplicate roles are allowed.

Most rename targets below already existed as approved public R2 manifest titles. This audit promotes those existing judgments into selected title authority rather than inventing a parallel naming layer.
