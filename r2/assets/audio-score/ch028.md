Jorren came back while I was buying onions.

Rude for several reasons.

First, I had spent most of the morning not thinking about roads and was doing reasonably well.

Second, I had one hand full of produce when somebody behind me said:

"Those look terrible."

I knew the voice before I turned.

My body knew it too.

Everything in my chest released at once.

Too much.

Far too much.

For one ugly second I was fifty-nine again, hearing boots outside a door after a contract ran two days late.

Roads teach you `eventually` is not a promise.

People come back with scars.

People come back missing gear.

People come back furious.

Sometimes someone else comes back carrying their things.

None of that belonged to Jorren yet.

That was the problem.

Feeling arrived before current evidence could stop it.

I turned.

Jorren beside cabbage cart.

Pack over shoulder.
Road dust in everything.

Alive.

Annoyingly alive.

Same chest.
Same grin.
Different boots.

The new pair from before he left had already been resoled once. Darker leather around heel.

Sword belt higher.
Loose pack straps cut short and tied back now.

Pale line outside left hand.

Not new enough to interest anybody except me.

I stared anyway.

Then me.

"You going to say something?"
