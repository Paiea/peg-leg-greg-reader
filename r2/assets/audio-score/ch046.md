Eight days after South Four, Lessa's shin bruises had gone yellow and my sword had a better edge than before hounds damaged it.

Unfair to Lessa.

She told me so in Guild lobby.

"Your sword improved. My leg became ugly."

"You could sharpen your leg."

"I regret meeting you."

Vaska says Silver team cleared nest.

"I know. I was there."

Stop.

"You went back?"

"Healer support."

There.

Life continuing in places I left.

Silver crew trapped three more hounds. Egg clutch in old warm-water branch. City moved eggs after river ecology complained.

I had spent eight days imagining South Four complete because my part ended.

It had not.

Lessa back twice.

Vaska probably more.

"How's leg?"

Fine.

Actually?

Actually fine.

Kick boot lightly to prove it.

Then she left for another job.

I stood holding road token.

First actual road.

Two days. Maybe three.

West Spur mill hardware run.

Driver: Ossa Marel.

Only knew name because Sella said, "If Ossa tells you to move, move before asking why."

Apparently recommendation.

East Gate.

Ossa under cart.

Only boots visible.

Small boots.

Wrench on wood.

"Ossa?"

"If you're Greg, hold mule."

"How do you know?"

"You're tall and early enough to be anxious."

Carrow face-reading: regional craft.

Mule gray, patient, Murn by brass tag.

"Morning, Murn."

Ignored.

Ossa slid out.

Smaller than profanity suggested. Dark hair jaw-length. Work coat patched elbows. Hands black hub grease.

"Cart knowledge?"

Minimal.

Mule?

Also minimal.

Road?

"Some. Maybe too much in wrong direction."

Stare.

"Sella said weird."

"Sella talks too much."

"Good. We agree."

Wheel chock handed over.

"Behind left rear."

Done.

"Why?"

"Now you can ask."

Liked immediately.

Cart: wrapped mill belts. Ceramic bearings. Six sacks lamp-black pigment. Box ward pins customer counted individually out of spite.

Role: second body and Guild guard.

Fourteen copper plus food.

Road mark if Ossa signed.

West Spur began disappointingly near vegetable farms.

No mountains.

No monsters.

No mysterious horizon.

Mud.

Hedges.

Children beside goats.

Man repairing wall.

I loved it.

Not every second.

First hour pack strap rubbed collarbone.

Second, Murn farted beside me twice.

Third bell rain found gap between hood and neck.

Road desire survived all three.

Ossa drove some stretches, walked others. Put me on driver's board on flat mile after asking if I could hold reins.

"Probably."

"Bad answer."

"I can hold reins. I cannot claim I know Murn."

"Better."

Reins.

Mule immediately slowed.

"He knows."

Knows what?

New management.

"He knows you let him."

Ossa tongue click once.

Murn speeds up.

I try.

Nothing.

She laughs full minute.

Power fantasy delayed.

Noon farmhouse soup window.

Ossa knew woman by name.

Yessa.

"Ossa. Same mule?"

"Unfortunately."

Yessa gave Murn apple peel.

Suddenly personality.

"Traitor."

Road repeated people.

That was thing maps did not teach.

Not distance.

Relationship infrastructure.

Ossa knew soup farm. Flooding culvert. Which gatekeeper took exact toll and which pretended no change.

Old Greg remembered West Spur as line.

Ossa knew names.

After lunch low ridge.

Rain stronger. Ditches moving water.

South shoulder, cart stops.

Problem one heartbeat later.

Outer road edge slumped.

Long crescent crack parallel wheel rut. Twenty feet. Fresh mud below ditch where section already fell.

"Walk it."

Tie Murn. Ahead on foot.

North edge against bank. Enough room for cart if damaged shoulder held.

Maybe.

Crouch. Stick into crack.

Soft beneath.

"No."

Ossa: "You know road?"

"I know gravity."

"Useful."

Field lane uphill through gate.

"Marr."

"Person?"

"Disease."

Back cart thirty yards.

Gate chained.

Old man red cap materialized before Ossa touched it.

"Four copper."

"Two."

"Rain. Four."

"Rain is free."

"Not on my field."

Three.

Five because started at two.

Economic warfare.

Marr looks at me.

"Who's giant?"

Greg.

"He damages field, six."

"I haven't moved."

"Preventative."

Liked him against judgment.

Ossa pays three, gets route-paper signature. Marr complains while producing ink immediately.

Field lane wet grass.

Murn dislikes.

Wheels slip. Ossa bridle. I push rear rail steepest stretch.

Mud over left boot because rut hid water.

At barn barking.

Two dogs fence.

One through broken board.

Lean black dog straight at Murn.

Mule kicks. Ossa pulls bridle.

I step between before thought.

Dog changes target. Leaps.

Hand to sword.

Stop.

Not channel hound.

Farm dog.

Catch collar instead.

Bad physics.

Momentum twists shoulder. Snap near wrist. Bracer catches teeth.

"Pike!"

Marr runs from gate, surprisingly fast.

Of course dog's name Pike.

Hold collar two hands, head angled away. Paws churn mud.

Marr grabs scruff.

"You bastard."

"Your dog has poor professional boundaries."

Bite?

No.

Good leather.

Bad dog.

"Didn't cut him."

"I noticed he wasn't a drain monster."

"Some Guild don't."

Depressing.

Field lane rejoins above slump.

Rain eases late afternoon.

Left boot wet.

Murn forgives nobody.

Mill three miles west beside narrow river, two wheels under gray sky.

Unload until dark.

Belts. Bearings. Pigment. Ward pins counted individually by clerk proving Ossa honest.

One pin missing.

Clerk excited.

Ossa opens false bottom.

Pin stuck paper fold.

His disappointment beautiful.

Sleep mill loft.

Oat stew. Bread.

Wet sock by stove.

"Still want road?"

"Yes."

"First day."

"You sound like Sella."

"Insult."

"Agreed."

Thread bitten clean.

"Why Black Stair?"

I look.

"Everyone knows?"

"You checked date three times."

Paper had come out toll gate and mill office.

Secrets required better habits.

"Because I want to know if I can get there as me."

Too honest.

Ossa stops sewing.

"Who else would go?"

Complicated.

"Old version of me."

She takes that without context.

"Dead?"

"Sort of."

"Then bad competition."

Simple.

Laugh.

Outside, mill wheel steady.

Boot steaming.

Black Stair 348 days away.

Today maybe fifteen miles and three copper to Marr to avoid ditch.

Scale difference should embarrass me.
