Eleven days changed Pike's bite bruise on my forearm from purple to yellow and changed Noll's apron from brown to blue.

He bought second one himself.

That mattered to him more than I understood.

"Better," he said.

Lower Rook courtyard before dawn, tying it over work shirt.

"You paid for fabric because restaurant's free apron offended you."

"Yes."

"How much?"

"Four."

"Robbed."

"Your boots cost twenty-one."

Look down.

New boots.

He had me.

Old pair upstairs drying though no longer wet. Three road jobs and four freight shifts taught me exactly which seam failed first. Left heel worn thin enough cobbler recommended patching before summer.

Instead twenty-one copper on heavier pair. Replaceable heel caps. Better ankle wrap.

Expensive enough to resent.

Good enough I wore them around Carrow three days before trusting road.

Money came from work.

Different from simply possessing money.

Guild token now seven marks and three signed notes.

Stonehook.

South Four.

Ossa:

`LISTENS AFTER FIRST ARGUMENT. USEFUL WITH LOAD. DO NOT LET HIM DRIVE MURN ALONE.`

I objected first sentence.

Sella said second mattered more.

Objected to that too.

Spring finally stopped pretending winter.

Courtyard tree green. Rain warmer. Laundry outside without freezing stiff. Market strawberries priced for nobility.

Jorren returned six days after West Spur.

I learned two days later because he did not come find me.

That hurt more than expected.

Not because owed.

Because some part of me assumed first stop.

Instead he slept half a day, washed, delivered Beren's papers, then temporary work at Meln's factor yard because clerk broke ankle and somebody remembered Jorren could count.

I found him D024 on platform shouting numbers at flour sacks.

Happy.

Not glowing.

Not transformed.

Busy.

Current life used him before I could.

Today breakfast Lower Rook after first shift.

Noll left before him.

"Close tonight."

Again?

Three nights now.

"They trust you with close?"

Offended.

Why not?

No reason.

There was a reason. Twelve-year-old Noll still lodged in memory beside current one.

Current Noll had Brass Spoon back-door keys.

Old memories did not only lose people.

Sometimes they kept them too small.

"Good luck."

Shrug. Gone.

Upstairs to pack for nothing.

That was problem.

No road contract.

Checked Guild board yesterday and again before sunrise.

Two routes Silver.

One courier escort already claimed.

Five-day quarry wants archers.

I owned sword and opinions.

Neither counted.

So clean pack.

New boots.

Dry socks.

Oilcloth repaired after field lane.

Knife sharpened.

Sword chip gone.

I had begun accumulating habits of someone who might leave while remaining in room.

Mara's last letter on table.

Six days old.

Halden rain. Vey changing schedule three times. Sera threatening clerk with ledger. Blue cup broken on stairs.

At bottom:

**You keep writing about roads as if they are women you are trying not to admit you like. Go on another one.**

Then:

**Come west when you actually have time. Warning first.**

I had read come west more often than rest.

Selective attention healthy.

Jorren knocked once and entered before answer.

Different coat patch. Green cloth at left elbow where brown had been.

"You bought boots."

"You changed patch."

"Mine cheaper."

"Unproven."

Second chair.

Everyone used it.

"Road?"

"No."

"Factor?"

"Yes."

"How long?"

"Meln asked through summer."

There.

Eleven days assuming Jorren's next road waited around bend.

He accepted season local.

"You said yes?"

Yes.

Disappointment arrived before generosity.

Ugly.

True.

"You like it?"

Some.

More than road?

"Why choose?"

Excellent answer.

Hated it.

Factor mornings. Occasional short route if Meln releases. Likes sleeping same bed more than three nights.

Beren says boring.

Is he right?

Yes.

And?

Still doing.

Current Jorren allowed boring things.

I still treated every choice as identity evidence.

Breakfast downstairs because room contained dried oats and hard cheese rind.

Maud eggs.

Jorren ate four.

"Road starved you?"

"Factor work."

"Counting burns calories?"

"Arguing."

Fair.

Hour before yard.

Wanted conversation longer.

Instead walk east together.

Carrow greener. Vines. Open awnings. Children in drainage water with sticks.

East Square crowd around Guild demonstration yard.

Spring assessment day.

Bronze practical modules for endorsements.

Rope climb. Weighted carry. Ward recognition. Blade control. Casualty drag.

I stopped.

Jorren followed gaze.

"You want."

"I have time."

"Same."

He needed factor.

Yes.

Clap shoulder.

"Do it."

Then left.

No watching.

No validation.

I registered anyway.

Two copper.

Less glamorous than imagined.

Sandbag fifty yards.

Twelve-foot rope.

Find overloaded ward plate among four.

Weighted dummy around barrels.

Blade lane against padded swinging arms.

Body did well.

Not perfectly.

Sandbag awkward with long arms.

Rope easy until wrists remembered Stonehook near top.

Ward plate obvious.

Casualty drag easier than expected because Old Greg moved too many people.

Blade lane changed things.

Instructor sequence.

Six targets.

Step. Parry. Cut. Turn. Duck. Thrust. Disengage.

Bronze safety pace.

Watch once.

Go.

First target too slow.

Wait for it.

Second, cut inside hinge before arm finishes.

Third, duck late deliberately because remembered pace outruns apparatus.

Instructor frowns.

"Again. Full."

"Full what?"

"Your pace."

Look at wooden arms.

"They'll break."

"Don't hit hard. Move fast."

That I can do.

Second run.

Stop thinking.

Lane shortens.

Parry barely touches first arm.

Blade to second in same turn.

Duck.

Inside third.

No flourish.

Six targets before final arm completes swing.

Silence around lane.

Enough.

Instructor looks Bronze tab.

Then me.

"Where trained?"

Memory offers too many answers.

"Long story."

"Useful?"

"Probably not."

"Name."

Greg.

Writes.

"Again with live partner."

There.

Silver assistant Callen. Padded sword. Face guard.

"Control only."

Excitement.

Simple.

No shame.

Touch blades.

Callen attacks first.

Competent.

Current-world competent.

Not apparatus.

Give ground one. Catch second. Turn wrist. Tap shoulder before recovery.

One.

Blink.

Again.

Feint low, high.

I know before shoulder moves.

Old experience.

Inside. Padded edge ribs.

Two.

Callen smiles.

"Again."

Third lasts longer.

Adapts.

Good.

Crowds distance. Body instead of blade.

Nearly trip boundary rope. Bad recovery. Hit upper arm.

One him.

I laugh.

Actually laugh.

Fun.

Fourth, stop preserving mystery.

Three beats.

Bind.

Shoulder check.

Turn blade past hip.

My padded sword at throat.

Callen freezes.

"Enough."

Back immediately.

Breathing faster.

Happy.

No corrective disaster.

No floor collapse.

No arrogance lecture.

Callen removes face guard.

"Bronze?"

Yes.

"Not with blade."

Liked sentence too much.

Embarrassment later.

Assessment card:

`BLADE CONTROL: SILVER TRAINING RANGE. FIELD RECORD INSUFFICIENT FOR RANK REVIEW.`

There.

Not promotion.

Evidence.

Carry card to desk with stupid care.

Sella reads.

"You look unbearable."

"I said nothing."

"Worse."

Adds to file.

What unlocks?

Some Bronze combat jobs accept blade endorsement. Does not make Silver.

"I know."

"Do you?"

"Today yes."

Road board.

New posting:

**NORTH EDGE WAYSTATION / TWO DAYS / BEAST PRESSURE / BRONZE WITH BLADE ENDORSEMENT ACCEPTED.**

Touch assessment card.

Then posting.
