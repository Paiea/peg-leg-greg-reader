Tavin had been gone eight days when vineyard owner told me Bronze was not enough.

Would have hurt more two weeks earlier.

Now I had paperwork.

Guild endorsements spread across kitchen table.

Stonehook.

South Four.

North Edge.

Blade assessment.

Two more short marks. Warehouse watch. Road escort where nothing attacked except rain.

Tavin had spent six days in Carrow before Venn sent him south again.

Long enough to learn Maud charged extra eggs.

Long enough to get banned from touching Noll's blue apron with wax hands.

Long enough to borrow three copper and claim it reduced historical debt from four to one.

Left D047.

I did not go to gate.

Venn before dawn. I had Stonehook shift.

Considered changing it.

Tavin told me not to become dramatic.

So I worked.

Two days later his note:

**You not coming was normal. Disturbing progress.**

Kept it.

Noll closing Brass Spoon two nights, opening one morning. Jorren desk drawer now two socks because institutional growth required redundancy.

Mara's letter nine days old.

Signed `M.` instead of `Mara`, then two pages Vey complaints and one line asking when I was coming west.

I answered everything except when.

Current relationships accumulated unfinished sentences while I worked.

Vineyard owner tapped Bronze tab.

"Posting says Silver or two endorsed Bronze."

"We are two."

Point at Evin.

He stood by pantry door with spear, trying not to look amused.

Hesk looked.

"You?"

Evin endorsements.

Three fauna jobs. One boar.

"One?"

"Normal boar."

"This isn't normal."

We knew.

Ridge tusker.

Large boar-like magical scavenger. Mineral shoulder plates after feeding certain hillside ores. Usually solitary. Usually avoids farms unless food easier than forest.

This one discovered grape-root stores.

Two fences in five nights. Dog dead. Farmhand cracked pelvis.

Hesk wanted Silver.

Guild none until tomorrow.

Rain tonight.

Tracks already upper vineyard.

"We can wait," I said.

Hesk stared.

Expected salesmanship.

"You said you wanted work."

"I do. If you want Silver more, wait."

Evin looked at me.

Ignore.

Hesk rubbed beard.

"If it comes tonight, upper root shed is open wall."

"Then close it."

Door broke.

Board it.

"You want job or not?"

"Yes. I also don't need to invent urgency."

Annoyingly mature.

Missed simpler version.

Finally contract.

Kill: twenty-eight copper plus material share.

Drive: eighteen.

Damage caused by us from contract.

Damage caused by tusker: his.

Fair.

Outside Evin waited.

"You could have told him we're good."

"Are we?"

"I am. You are weird."

"Strong team."

Upper vineyard beyond north wall. Spring vines knee-high, bright green. Stone terraces. Root shed at forest edge.

Tracks obvious.

Three-toed hoof marks spread-hand wide.

Fence post snapped.

Dark hair on splinter.

Evin crouched.

"Big."

"Yes."

"Good."

Look.

"You say it too."

"What?"

"Nothing."

Two hours preparing instead of hunting.

Hesk surprised.

Workers out of upper terraces.

Root shed boarded except narrow bait opening because tusker knew food there.

Carts moved.

Lower stone gate opened for downhill flight.

Evin traded normal spear for heavy boar spear.

I kept sword.

No pit.

No elaborate trap waiting to fail creatively.

Local hunter Pela joined for four copper as tracker, not fighter.

Fresh droppings at tree line. Gray stone fragments inside.

"Eating quarry spoil."

Abandoned slate cut half mile north.

Plates likely thicker.

Useful current information.

Old Greg remembered ridge tuskers.

Memory offered clean strikes.

Most assumed stronger body and better magic.

Possibilities.

Not instructions.

Third bell. Brush above vineyard.

Pela points.

"Coming."

Positions.

Evin behind low wall, spear braced.

Me left near open lane.

Pela farther back with bow only if animal turned houses.

Hesk told stay downhill.

He stayed twenty yards instead of fifty.

Humans.

Tusker emerged.

Bigger than wanted.

Shoulder nearly chest high. Dark hide. Overlapping gray plates neck and back. One tusk broken. Other forearm-long.

Sour smell.

Walked root shed slowly.

Not enraged.

Hungry.

Could still let it take bait and drive.

I watched animal.

Stops.

Head lifts.

Wind shifts us to it.

Smells people.

Body changes.

Shoulders rise.

Hooves dig once.

Charge.

Not shed.

Me.

Excellent.

Body arrived before philosophical regret.

Sideways toward open lane.

Do not meet mass.

Stumble.

Not fall.

Shallow.

Good.

Deep.
