Eight days later, Siva still had not decided whether jumping off cart counted as useful aggression.

She had written `DOES NOT FREEZE` on interim road sheet.

I knew because Beren left it face up.

"You read private?"

"It was on table."

"Still."

"Then turn it over."

He did.

Replacement Anchor strap cost five copper at Fenner. Thicker stitching. Palm scrape from road fall already dry line. Summer made camps warmer, dustier, louder with insects. Mules drank more. Noon stops earlier, evening travel later.

Road shifted from spring mud to hard ruts.

Old Greg remembered roads as distances.

Current Greg learned seasons too.

White Fork bridge open.

Ford below closed.

Storm debris. Road wardens repairing.

Should simplify route.

Instead queue of carts at bridge.

Two hours.

Siva used wait to make me practice Anchor.

Not demonstration.

Load work.

Beren tied fifty-pound grain sack to one cart rail, another opposite. Suspended plank. Three connection points. Keep level while Siva shifts sacks.

"Why now?"

"Because you're bored."

"Cruel."

"Because if you only use it when someone is about to die, you stay bad."

Better reason.

Anchor easier to feel now.

Not stronger.

More legible.

Pressure enters one connection. Silver threads tell me where it wants to leave. Fight path, shoulders pay. Guide it, structure works.

Twenty breaths level.

Siva shifts second sack suddenly.

Left drops.

Correct too hard.

Right rises.

"Again."

"Bridge line is moving."

"Again."

Beren laughs in mule shade.

Again.

By road warden waves us forward, sweat down back and Anchor mount warm.

No adventure.

Practice inside waiting.

Mattered later.

White Fork district: low river country, reeds, wet fields, willow. Raised stone causeway.

Near sunset, bell.

Three fast rings.

Pause.

Three again.

Every road crew changed.

Beren stopped talking.

Siva stood on driver's board.

"Call ahead."

Road bell known from stories, not current experience.

Emergency.

Another three around bend.

"Stay with cart until we see."

Round slowly.

Passenger coach partly off causeway. Rear wheel over reed ditch. One horse down in traces. Driver ringing hand bell. Four passengers twenty yards away, one holding child.

No obvious attack.

Then reeds move.

Low gray shapes.

Siva swears.

"Mirewolves."

Not wolves exactly.

Lean amphibious predators. Long forelimbs. Dark wet fur. Reed channels. Usually fish, birds, livestock.

Blood from horse changed priorities.

Three shapes.

Then five.

Passenger screams.

Mirewolves look at people.

"Beren, block road with cart. Greg with me. Driver, passengers behind freight."

No hesitation.

Cart across causeway as barrier, mules clear.

Siva bow.

Me sword.

First mirewolf out of reeds.

Longer than wolf. Low shoulders. Mud feet. Mouth too wide.

Arrow chest.

Roll once, gone over edge.

Others scatter.

Not gone.

Smart enough.

"Coach first."

Downed horse alive. Front leg tangled under broken shaft.

Coach axle half over ditch. Roll farther and horse dragged.

Driver: need pull back.

Siva looks wheel.

"Anchor?"

Mine.

Crouch.

Two old iron rail eyes in causeway stones, maybe winter chains.

Third point our cart.

"Maybe."

"What do you need?"

Not can you solve.

Better.

Rope to rear frame. One line both causeway eyes. Third rear axle if Beren agrees. Spread pull, not lift.

Beren: rear axle only.

Ownership.

Rig fast.

Passengers behind wagon. Driver cuts one horse trace. Siva watches reeds while helping rope.

Blackglass center to coach line. Left to causeway eyes through bridle. Right to rear axle.

Ugly geometry.

Enough.

"Beren, slow forward when I say. Driver, push uphill side."

"You lead?" Siva asks.

For this part.

"Yes."

Nod.

Liked too much.

"Pull."

Mules move.

Rope tight.

Coach creaks.

Anchor alive.

Force split stone eyes and axle. I create little of it. Keep one line from taking all.

Rear wheel climbs inches.

Driver pushes.

"Again."

Then mirewolves attack.

Two from passenger side.

Siva shoots one. Misses when line changes. Drops bow, spear out.

I cannot leave Anchor immediately without losing load balance.

There.

Role conflict.

One clears low stone edge.

Beren shouts.

Passenger man grabs coach pole and swings wildly.

Bad.

Siva meets animal with spear.

Twists around shaft.

Second behind.

Toward passengers.

Release force.

Anchor snaps load back to physical ropes. Both hold.

Coach slides one inch toward ditch.

Acceptable for seconds.

Run.

Sword low.

Second mirewolf jumps at passenger with child.

Intercept sideways.

Cut foreleg and shoulder.

Animal hits me anyway.

Chest impact. Down.

Teeth near face.

Left forearm into throat, bracer between jaws.

Wet breath.

Rot smell.

Monster on top weighs more than descriptions suggest.

Sword under ribs one-handed.

Once.

Again.

Slack.

Shove off.

Siva killed hers with spear through neck.

Third shape stays reeds.

Another reaches horse opposite side.

Beren throws wheel chock.

Misses.

Driver shouts.

No Evin.

No extra fighter waiting.

Around coach.

Mirewolf jaws horse flank. Horse screams, kicks. One kick ribs. Animal rolls.

Arrive before footing.

Sword down behind skull.

Clean.

Still.

Siva scans reeds.

"Two more out there."

"Let them go?"

"If they go."

Wait.

Reeds move away.

Good.

Back to coach.

Wheel another two inches back, still on stone.

Passenger man now holding child because woman took coach pole.

Roles changed without permission.

Ropes.

Chest hurts. No bite. Bracer gouged. Harness twisted, intact.

"Can you?"

"Yes."

Actually yes.

Pull again.

Use morning practice.

When coach load shifts left eye, guide some into Beren axle before correction.

Less fighting.

Anchor cooler.

Wheel onto stone.

Everyone shouts once when settled.

Then horse.

Leg not broken. Harness pinned it. Shoulder cut. Free traces. Broken shaft away. Three attempts to stand.

Coach cannot safely continue.

Road problem becomes logistics.

Other freight crew takes two passengers.

We take mother, child, trunk.

Driver stays for repair wagon.

Siva incident note.

Beren checks axle after Anchor load.

"Fine."

Then bracer.

Teeth holes through outer leather, not inner plate.

"Not fine."

"Arm is."

"Gear isn't."

Right.

Camp White Fork station.

Child Arri stares at me.

Dinner:

"Are you Silver?"

Laugh.

"No."

"You killed two."

"Three with Siva."

"You killed two."

"Yes."

"Then why Bronze?"

Siva nearly chokes on soup.

Loved question from everyone except paperwork.

"Because monsters don't issue promotions."

Arri considers.

"They should."

Power-fantasy governance.

After guest room, Siva hands road sheet.

`D105. COACH RECOVERY UNDER MIREWOLF PRESSURE. GREG OWNED LOAD RIGGING, RELEASED IT TO FIGHT WHEN PRIORITY CHANGED, RETURNED AND FINISHED RECOVERY. TWO KILLS. DID NOT CHASE.`

Final words.

"You remembered."

"I was watching."

"Sponsor?"

Too direct.

Sheet back.

"Not after nine days."

"Eight."

"Worse."

"What would make you?"

Look.

"I need to know what you do when tired, bored, angry, wrong, and nobody is bleeding. Anyone can look useful for thirty seconds."

Sella's endurance point again.

"How long?"

"Work."

Road answer.

Lean back.

Chest bruise.

Sword cleaning needed.

Bracer replacement.

Anchor held coach.

Morning plank practice mattered before sunset.

Outside mirewolf carcasses hang under shed for Guild collector.

Tomorrow someone skins them.

Tonight I was tired enough not to care.

Maybe first evidence Siva wanted...
