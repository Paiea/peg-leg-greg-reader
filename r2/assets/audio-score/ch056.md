I reached Halden on the day I said I would.

Suspiciously competent.

The twelve days between Kelm and river stair were less elegant.

Return cargo cleared D117. Left before sunrise D118. Two hot days, axle repair, bridge queue long enough Siva taught me three ways to tie same load line just to remain sane.

Carrow D122.

Siva signed Silver review sheet before taking payment.

`MULTI-DAY ROAD. GREG REMAINS USEFUL AFTER NOVELTY. LOAD SPECIALTY REAL. BLADE STRONG. JUDGMENT IMPROVING. WOULD FIELD AGAIN.`

I read `WOULD FIELD AGAIN` four times.

She saw.

"Don't make weird."

"Too late."

Signature mattered more than praise because she watched me be bored.

D123 gear repair.

D124 Bell Tile because Dena had filter line issue and because returning Carrow only to leave again felt rude even if nobody asked.

"Yes."

"Correctly."

Jorren's factor rival, new counter he disliked, became friend. Bessa. Still disliked that she counted faster.

"Then practice."

"I do."

Louder. Hotter.
