The ruin contract spent thirteen days becoming real.

Different from notice appearing on Guild board and turning immediately into destiny.

Ves mentioned it D141 while signing marsh-runner report.

"West aqueduct survey. Three days minimum. Maybe five."

Danger?

Old stone. Collapsed channels. Heat pockets. Possible nest.

"Possible what nest?"

"Unknown."

"Excellent."

"That's not enthusiasm I trust."

I trusted it enough for both of us.

Municipal works owned survey. Guild supplied field bodies because abandoned aqueduct ran through old defensive cuts outside wall where maintenance crews stopped after two men disappeared last summer.

Disappeared sounded dramatic.

Records less useful.

One failed to return checking flow gate.

Second went looking and also failed.

No bodies.

No confirmed creature.

No magical event.

Just absence.

Ves lead.

Four people.

Mapper.

Healer.

Combat-capable second.

Load/ward specialist because old aqueduct drawings used charge-balancing glass.

That line tightened stomach.

"Blackglass?"

"Could be."

"You chose me because of that?"

"I chose you because you worked twelve days in Halden without making Sera ban your name."

"High standard."

"Also the rock."

Good.

Next twelve days not waiting.

Four Guild shifts. One ugly cellar killing blue-backed rats cat-sized under spice warehouse. Not magical enough to impress anyone. Still bit through glove.

Two training sessions with Kes. He swept my leg once and mentioned it every meeting afterward.

Three evenings Mara.

One good.

One argument because I called aqueduct manageable before admitting two workers vanished.

One quiet: peaches on her floor while she did Vey sums and I repaired harness clip.

That one maybe best.

Blackglass changed too.

Pell, Mara's machine-repair friend, built proper carrying plate from spring steel and leather spacers. Seven copper and enough insults to kill original mounting board.

"You bolted four-pound regulator to furniture wood."

"Scrap board."

"Furniture died before you found it."

New plate under left ribs. Three connection eyes reachable without removing harness.

Better.

Fewer spoons endangered.

D153 hot and cloudless.

West gate before first bell.

Ves, Silver lead, spear.

Mapper Lorn. Compact, older, glass eye, enough rolled paper to start fire for week.

Healer Tessa. Bronze Guild, municipal-certified. Short black hair. Heavy medical satchel. Knife.

Fourth: me.

Four-person party.

Not permanent.

Still enough something old inside woke.

Formation memory.

Spacing.

Who carried what.

Wanted optimize immediately.

Ves prevented.

"We walk two hours before you reorganize anyone."

"I haven't spoken."

"Your eyes did."

I hated everyone.

Aqueduct begins beyond Halden vineyards. Old stone channel low arches. Active nearer city. Abandoned section enters limestone ridge through municipal iron gate.

Mara packed food.

Not romantically.

She saw provisions and called them punishment.

Cheese rolls. Dried apples. Salted chicken. Leaf-wrapped thing aggressively garlic.

Runner note at west gate:

**Bring all your legs back.**

Laughed.

Then strange feeling I couldn't explain.

Folded note into inside pocket because Mara.

Gate third bell.

Municipal seal unbroken since survey attempt eleven months ago.

Cold air.

Wet stone. Old mineral.

Lamps.

Black seams after second marker.

Stop.

"Related."

Yes.

Can prove?

Hold Anchor near without activating.

"Enough for me."

Correct.

First hour boring.

Good.

Lorn maps side shafts. Tessa checks old bones. Goat, not missing men. I inspect load arches where blackglass seams cross supports.

Anchor looks more like removable component from larger civil system.

More interesting, not less.
