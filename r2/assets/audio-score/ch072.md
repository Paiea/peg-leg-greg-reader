I went to my Westreach candidacy meeting with my left hand wrapped and my best piece of equipment in somebody else's workshop.

Not how I pictured it on D001.

Good.

Probably good.

D274 arrived cold, clear, inconveniently ordinary.

Noll left Lower Rook before me because Brass Spoon had started breakfast three mornings a week and he supervised prep on two.

Jorren had inventory audit at Meln's yard and couldn't come even though he offered.

Tavin east.

Mara west.

Nobody escorted me to the important office.

After nine months of learning people had schedules, that felt appropriate.

Pell sent note at dawn:

**ANCHOR GLASS INTACT. CRADLE BENT. DO NOT TOUCH UNTIL I SAY. SIX-EYE COUPLER IS FUCKED. TECHNICAL TERM.**

I laughed harder than hand allowed.

Anchor intact.

Oh.

Relief made legs weak one second.

Coupler hurt differently.

Salvage part.

Experiment.

Anchor had become mine through months of use.

Not broken.

Good.

Actually good.

I folded Pell's note into Westreach file.

Not to show anyone initially.

Then...

No.

Accident belonged to current equipment history.

If they asked, I show it.

Now it looked like a machine already running.

Maps walls.

Crates by destination.

Rope samples.

Tent cloth swatches.

Animal lists.

Food calculations.

**52 DAYS TO WEST GATE**.

Fifty-two.

Black Stair ninety-one days away.

Departure fifty-two.

The year had stopped being large.

Not defensive version.

Six-eye coupler.

Held one second longer after clear.

Hand shocked.

Anchor inspection.

No prettier version.

"Yes."

"Not now."

"Yes."

Only record.

"Pell machine repair, Dena Bell Tile, Harl mason."

"Pell told slow. I overruled."

"Good that you said it. Bad that it happened."

"Yes."

"Ten to twenty days no load magic. Sword light sooner."

"Departure fifty-two."

"I know."

Evidence network.

"Secondary. Dorin is better at ward breaking. My useful thing is force/load across moving structures, rescues, gates, rigs, maybe old blackglass systems."

"No."

Sword was strongest obvious skill.

But Westreach value might be rarer elsewhere.

Not min-maxing damage alone.

Build had identity.

Chest tightened.

"Seven-person support unit attached to western engineering train. Rope, bridge, ward load, recovery, broken carts, bad gates, structural magic, occasional fighting because west dislikes job descriptions."

...

That sounded almost designed for me.

Dangerously satisfying.

Good.

"One wardwright already slotted, Leth Aran. Silver. You would not own wards."

Even better.

"Me."

"Candidate."

Right.

Candidate.

"Field Support Three runs outer-course trial with two other candidates. One slot."

There.

Competition.

Normal fantasy nonsense and also, annoyingly, useful.

"Who?"

"You learn at trial."

"Because you don't need three weeks inventing them."

Cruel.

Wise.

Not duel.

Good.
