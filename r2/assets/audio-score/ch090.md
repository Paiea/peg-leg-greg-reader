Mara came Carrow while I was gone.

Again.

Thirty-six hours.

I spent all thirty-six under North Quarry arguing with buried drainage.

World developed timing as weapon.

Back after eleven days.

Slate dust inside boot.
Split thumbnail.
Cloak mud enough Maud stops me front hall.

"Floor."

Floor survived worse.

"Not for this rent."

Takes cloak.

Then:

"Mara was here."

Everything stops badly.

Was?

"Morning packet west."

There.

Past tense.

Hostile grammar.

How long?

"Day and half."

Thirty-six hours.

Maybe less.

I was north dealing with stone convinced water belonged inside walls while Mara occupied my city and left.

No one did wrong.

Somehow worse.

Room.

New chair moved.

Not window chair.

Mine.

Three feet toward light.

Mara's furniture opinions survived my absence.

Folded paper seat.

Name outside.

Under:

**Your chair is acceptable. Placement remains criminal.**

Sit bed.

Room nearly same as eleven days earlier except somebody wanted passed through without me.

Open.

**Greg,**

**I had thirty-six hours in Carrow. Before you decide Vey did this specifically to hurt you, they gave me forty-eight and the north packet lost twelve to rain. I am writing this first because I know you.**

Stare.

Rude accuracy.

Route-account correction.
Three Carrow warehouses.
One Vey subcontractor who thinks numbers optional when freight changes boats.

Worked most first day.

Noll fed after sixth bell.
Jorren found second morning because Noll sent word.
Maud charged tea.

Of course.

Mara sat my room maybe hour before leaving.

Not dramatic waiting.

Read Emmet route book.
Inspected chair.
Wrote note.

**Your Anchor shelf is better than the old table. The blackglass looks worse. I assume this means better.**

Correct.

**Noll has become unbearable about soup. Jorren owns a coat he cannot afford morally. Maud says you still leave boots where people walk. Carrow appears functional without you. I assume the quarry is too.**

Laugh once.

Then paragraph coming.

**I wanted to see you. I am disappointed. I am not angry at you for being at work. Please do not turn those into the same thing because it is easier.**

There.

Maybe always knew.

Read again.

Disappointed.

Not betrayed.
Not relationship evidence.

Clocks missed.

That's all.

Still hurts.

Both true.

**512 days. Still intend to be on the steps. Still expect you not to be early.**

Then:

**Next time maybe I will arrive while you are actually in your own city. Bold plan.**

Letter down.

Bowl.
