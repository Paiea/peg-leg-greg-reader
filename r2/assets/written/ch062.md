# Year Chapter 20: Silver

Status: **TEMPORAL YEAR REHEARSAL / ZERO AUTHORITY**

`Y312 / D183 / SUMMER`

`DAYS SINCE PRIOR CHAPTER: 2`

`LOCATION: Carrow Guild`

`RANK: Bronze -> Silver review result`

---

I arrived at the Guild seven minutes after first bell.

This took effort.

Sella looked at the clock.

Then me.

"Growth."

"I was outside."

"How long?"

"Irrelevant."

"Fourteen minutes?"

"Nine."

She smiled.

I hated her.

The advancement board had posted on the west wall.

Six names.

Three promoted. Two deferred. One failed.

I did not look immediately.

This was not maturity.

It was fear with posture.

Noll stood beside me because he had decided breakfast before work could happen near the Guild today. He wore the blue apron under his coat.

Jorren had come too, straight from Meln's yard, chalk still on one sleeve.

Neither spoke.

That made it worse.

Tavin was not in Carrow. Mara was not in Carrow.

I wanted them here anyway, just not enough to pretend distance had stopped existing.

Letters later.

I stepped closer.

Found my name.

**GREG**

**ADVANCED: SILVER**

Underneath:

**FIELD SPECIALTY: LOAD / FORCE SUPPORT**

**BLADE ENDORSEMENT: SILVER**

**RESTRICTION: NO INDEPENDENT LEAD ON MUNICIPAL STRUCTURAL MAGIC PENDING FURTHER CERTIFICATION**

I read it once.

Then again.

My first thought was not profound.

Fuck.

My second was that the restriction line was longer than the promotion.

I touched the board as if the ink might move.

Noll said, "Silver."

"Yes."

Jorren said, "You look sick."

"No."

"Face."

Everyone.

Sella called from the desk.

"Come sign before you become decorative."

I went.

Promotion itself involved four forms.

Of course.

One updated Guild standing. One insurance contribution change. One equipment declaration. One acknowledgment that Silver members could be assigned liability beyond Bronze day-work thresholds.

Power fantasy had paperwork.

I loved it anyway.

Sella took my Bronze tab.

That hurt unexpectedly.

Small piece of metal. Months of sweat attached.

"Keep?" I asked.

"No."

"Why?"

"Guild property."

"I earned emotional ownership."

"Guild does not recognize."

She dropped it into a box containing other old tabs.

Cruel institution.

Then she slid a Silver tab across the desk.

Not shiny. Brushed metal with a darker edge and two stamped lines.

Heavier by maybe nothing.

My hand still felt it.

"Congratulations," Sella said.

No sarcasm for once.

I looked up.

"Thank you."

"Do not become unbearable."

Balance restored.

She added a new page to my file.

"Your promotion was not unanimous."

Of course.

"Why?"

"One reviewer thinks your current blade is ahead of your team judgment. One thinks your magic specialization is useful enough to justify advancement. One thinks you are annoying but trainable."

"Which one are you?"

"Not reviewer. I already know you're annoying."

"What was deciding factor?"

Sella flipped the notes.

"Siva's road endorsement. Ves and Orra. Halden retaining review. Your field record showing you keep working after the interesting part."

Not the white drake alone. Not the sword duel. Not the Anchor.

The boring parts counted too.

I liked that more than expected.

"What changes?"

"Pay bands. Contract access. Liability. Expectations."

"Westreach?"

"Makes candidacy simpler. Does not guarantee slot."

"Sponsor?"

"Still need field sponsor or role lead by D274. Silver standing means you can apply directly to trials, but somebody still has to own where you fit."

"Load specialist."

"Maybe."

"Ressa noticed."

"Provisional."

"You enjoy ruining things."

"It's why desk gave me mug."

Noll and Jorren waited outside.

I clipped the Silver tab to my coat.

Noll touched it.

"Same."

"It is visibly different."

"Small."

"You bought blue apron and made me inspect stitching."

"Apron bigger."

Jorren laughed.

We went back to Lower Rook for breakfast even though Noll had already eaten, because apparently promotion required a second meal.

The walk felt strange.

Nobody knew.

A fish seller shouted the same price. A cart nearly splashed us. A child ran into my leg and apologized without checking rank.

Silver did not alter street physics.

At Lower Rook, Maud looked at the tab once.

"More rent?"

"Why?"

"Silver makes more."

"Predatory."

"Business."

She did not raise the rent.

Probably.

Maud joined us for coffee without invitation. Noll insisted I buy eggs.

I did.

Jorren asked what contracts Silver unlocked.

I had not checked the board yet.

That surprised him.

"You waited half a year for rank and didn't look?"

"Not yet."

"Why?"

I looked at the eggs.

"Wanted one meal first."

Jorren stared at me.

"You sick?"

"Apparently Silver is dangerous."

After breakfast, Noll went to Brass Spoon and Jorren to the factor yard.

I went upstairs alone.

My room had changed while I was gone fifty-four days.

Not much.

A dust line where the burned seven sat. Noll had moved the second chair closer to the window. One nail had been added near the door, probably Maud, holding spare cloth.

The carved river bird from Mara sat on the table now.

I unwrapped it.

Small wooden thing with a ridiculous beak.

No use.

Exactly as intended.

I placed it beside the Blackglass Anchor.

One useless object. One increasingly useful object.

Both from Halden.

Then I wrote letters.

Tavin first.

**Silver. Apparently paperwork surrendered.**

Then three lines explaining the result.

Mara second.

I stared at the paper longer.

Wanted clever.

Ended with:

**I got it. Silver.**

Then:

**You were right that the boring jobs counted. I dislike this.**

I almost wrote *wish you were here*.

Stopped.

Then wrote it anyway.

**Wish you were here. Not as a complaint.**

That felt exposed enough to fold immediately.

I took both letters to the packet office before I could change my mind.

Outside, summer Carrow had become hot.

D183.

Half the year.

I stopped in the street.

D001 felt both close and stupidly far.

Back then the Black Stair had been one sentence on cheap paper.

Now I had a Silver tab, road endorsements, field-positive money, a real magical specialty, a strange artifact with known limits, two cities where people knew my name, and enough marks on my body that nineteen no longer felt untouched.

Still no Westreach slot.

Still no sponsor.

Still no winter kit.

Still no proof the Anchor would matter west.

The deadline felt closer now.

I went back to the Guild.

This time I checked the Silver board.

Different wall. Different rates. Different problems.

One posting caught immediately.

**NORTH QUARRY ROAD / SIX DAYS / SILVER FIELD LEAD OR SECOND / STONEBACK PREDATION / TWO CONFIRMED LOSSES**

The pay was high enough to make me reread it.

Monster contract.

Real one.

Silver accepted.

I touched the new tab.

Then stopped.

Old pattern would have taken the token immediately because new access existed.

I read the full posting first.

Team already had lead. Needed second. Departure D190.

Seven days.

Enough time to repair gear, work Bell Tile, see Noll and Jorren, train my shoulder, maybe receive Mara's reply, and decide instead of react.

I copied the posting number.

Did not claim it yet.

Sella saw.

"Growth again."

"Stop watching me."

"Desk faces board."

I left.

Outside, the Silver tab felt heavy for another block.

Then a wagon splashed mud onto my trousers.

Rank normalized...
