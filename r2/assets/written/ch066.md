# Year Chapter 24: The Delver

Status: **TEMPORAL YEAR REHEARSAL / ZERO AUTHORITY**

`Y312 / D226 / AUTUMN`

`DAYS SINCE PRIOR CHAPTER: 12`

`LOCATION: Old Watchfort`

`RANK: Silver`

---

By D226 the trees north of Carrow had gone half gold, Tavin had moved his arrival estimate twice, and Mara's eastbound freight had acquired enough paperwork to become a religion.

Neither had arrived yet.

Good.

Life refused neat convergence.

I had spent twelve days learning the glassmaw dampener was useful enough to keep and irritating enough to matter.

Direct control gave me sharper Anchor feel but punished shoulders.

Dampened control let me manage larger shifting loads but blurred fine changes.

Pell made a little brass lever so I could switch without opening harness.

I loved it.

She hated that I called it a mode switch.

"It is a bypass."

"Mode."

"You deserve equipment failure."

"Hostile engineer."

"Correct."

The old watchfort contract came from Silver board with a red wax mark.

**THREE TO FIVE DAYS. OLD MILITARY SITE. HOSTILE FAUNA OR ACTIVE CONSTRUCTS POSSIBLE. RECOVER SURVEY CACHE. MAP LOWER WORKS. NO SOLO ENTRY.**

Then the line that got me:

**PRE-CHARTER LOAD-WARD SYSTEM REPORTED.**

Blackglass maybe.

I hated how easy I was becoming to bait.

Still, this was exactly the kind of work Westreach cared about.

Ruins.

Old systems.

Team pressure.

Unknown magic.

I took it.

Lead was a woman named **Ressa Holt**, Silver for eleven years, not the Westreach Ressa from registry desk. Carrow apparently had too many Ressas. She had a round shield, curved sword, and the calm expression of someone who had seen many people discover that old forts contained floors.

Scout: **Mett Var**, Silver, narrow build, short bow, climbing hooks.

Mage: **Dorin**, Bronze by Guild but formally certified in ward breaking, which meant rank and specialty again refused simple hierarchy.

Healer: **Sava**, no Guild rank, temple field license, heavy pack, terrible singing voice discovered before noon.

Me: Silver second and load/force specialist.

That title still felt fake enough to enjoy.

Old Watchfort stood a day's walk north-east on low basalt ridge overlooking abandoned road.

Not castle.

A squat stone fort with two broken towers and central yard full of grass.

Old defensive ditch nearly vanished under leaves.

Current goats had more claim than soldiers.

The survey cache had been left six months earlier by a mapping crew that abandoned the lower works after "animated armor incident."

I reread that phrase.

"Animated armor?"

Ressa said, "Maybe construct. Maybe frightened mapper."

"Either good."

"You say that until armor hits you."

"Probably."

We reached outer wall late D226.

No dramatic weather.

Cold clear sky.

Wind across ridge.

The fort gate had fallen inward years ago.

Mett stopped before crossing.

"Tracks."

Goat.

Fox.

Something pawed with four long toes.

Fresh.

Sava said, "Hounds?"

I crouched.

Not channel hounds.

Larger.

Claws deeper.

Dorin held ward rod near stone.

"Active charge inside."

There.

We entered formation.

Mett first ten paces.

Ressa center-front.

Me right.

Dorin and Sava middle.

No one left rear for old memory ghost.

I noticed and stayed where current party needed.

The central yard was empty except collapsed well cover and rusted training posts.

One tower door closed.

Main hall open.

Survey cache should be in north barracks.

We took barracks first.

No monsters.

Two bed frames.

Rotten shelves.

One chest with municipal seal.

Cache intact.

Maps, chalk, rope, three ration tins spoiled, one silver measuring chain, and notes.

Dorin read while Ressa watched door.

**LOWER STAIR ACTIVE AFTER SUNSET.**

**DO NOT REMOVE BLACK PLATE FROM THIRD LANDING.**

**ARMOR MOVED WHEN LOWER SEAL BROKEN.**

Good notes.

"Sunset?" I asked.

Mett looked through window.

Two hours.

Ressa said, "We secure upper fort and camp. Lower tomorrow daylight."

"If active after sunset, shouldn't we observe?"

"From upstairs."

Right.

We cleared upper rooms.

The first fight happened in west tower.

Not armor.

Grave hounds.

Three.

I knew name from Guild bestiary, not personal experience.

Lean black canine things with pale bone ridges growing through skin along shoulders, drawn to old battlefield charge residue.

One lay asleep in old stairwell until Mett's boot kicked loose stone.

It woke badly.

Snarl.

Then two more above.

Ressa did not yell strategy.

"Door!"

We backed through narrow tower doorway so only one could reach at time.

Simple.

First hound charged.

Ressa shield caught.

I cut over rim into neck.

Not kill.

Hound twisted.

Bone ridge scraped blade.

Mett shot from side through doorway gap.

Arrow entered chest.

Down.

Second leapt over body.

Ressa stepped back.

My turn front.

I planted force plane knee-height across doorway, not wall.

Hound hit invisible edge mid-stride.

Forelegs stopped.

Body flipped.

It landed on back.

I stabbed through softer belly.

Clean.

Third did not charge.

It stayed stairs, growling.

Good animal.

Ressa said, "Leave exit."

We shifted away from door and opened yard route.

Mett threw stone past hound toward tower opening.

Creature followed sound? Not enough.

Dorin sparked small blue light against wall opposite us.

The hound looked.

Then ran down stairs, through doorway, across yard, out broken gate.

No need kill all.

First Silver fight ended in maybe forty seconds.

Sava checked us.

No injuries.

Mett retrieved arrow from first hound and complained shaft cracked.

Loot? Bone ridges had minor ward-reactive value but Guild required carcass mark only. We cut one sample plate for Dorin, then dragged bodies out of tower because sleeping near dead hounds smelled stupid.

Upper fort secured by dusk.

We camped in main hall with one door barred and small fire venting through roof hole.

At sunset, lower stair activated.

We heard it before seeing.

A low bell tone from beneath floor.

One note.

Then another.

Blackglass Anchor warmed.

I stood.

Ressa pointed.

"Observe."

Not descend.

We went to lower stair entrance in east hall.

Stone steps went down into darkness.

At third landing, exactly where survey notes warned, a black plate set into wall began glowing with silver threads.

Blackglass.

My Anchor responded.

Dorin whispered, "Same family."

"Yes."

Then footsteps below.

Metal.

Slow.

One.

Two.

Three.

Something climbed.

Ressa drew sword.

I did too.

A helmet emerged around stair bend.

Empty eye slit.

Old iron armor, rust dark, no body visible through gaps.

Not floating loosely.

Moving as if worn.

Construct.

Normal fantasy finally stopped pretending.

I grinned.

Ressa saw.

"Don't."

"I said nothing."

The armor reached third landing.

Stopped beside black plate.

Silver light ran from wall into its gauntlet.

Then chest.

It turned toward us.

Sword came up.

Dorin said, "Ward-powered sentinel."

Ressa: "Can disable?"

"Maybe plate."

Survey note said do not remove plate.

Good reason unknown.

Sentinel advanced.

Ressa stepped down one stair.

"Test."

She struck shield with sword.

The sentinel attacked.

Fast enough to matter.

Old sword came down.

Ressa caught shield.

Impact rang hall.

I felt charge through black plate spike.

The armor was not self-powered.

It borrowed load from fort system.

Interesting.

I clipped Anchor one line to stair rail, second to wall ring, dampener on.

"What are you doing?" Dorin asked.

"Listening."

Sentinel struck again.

The Anchor felt the system pulse.

Every attack drew a packet of force through blackglass network into armor joints.

Not mana battery.

Distributed mechanical assistance.

Like my regulator, scaled.

"It loads before swing."

Ressa shouted, "Useful when?"

"Now."

Third strike.

I felt charge build half breath before blade moved.

"High!"

Ressa ducked before sword came.

The old blade hit wall.

Opening.

She cut armor knee joint.

Metal split but did not stop.

Sentinel backhanded shield.

Ressa retreated two stairs.

"Can you starve it?" Dorin asked.

Maybe.

Blackglass Anchor could redistribute load if connected to same network.

My line was only wall ring, not black plate.

Need connect without removing plate.

Third landing had three bronze studs around blackglass.

Maintenance interface?

I had seen similar on Anchor arms.

"Dorin. Studs. Can you identify safe?"

He leaned from hall, rod out.

"All active. Middle control maybe."

Maybe.

Sentinel climbed.

Ressa held.

Mett arrow into eye slit bounced inside armor, doing little.

Sava stayed back.

I needed ten seconds.

"Ressa, can you give me landing?"

"Five."

She shield-rushed down stairs.

Hard.

Sentinel staggered.

I moved behind her, clipped Anchor line to middle stud.

The moment metal touched, everything became loud in my senses.

Fort.

Walls.

Stair.

Sentinel.

Multiple dormant nodes below.

More armor maybe.

Fuck.

"More below!"

Dorin swore.

I switched dampener direct for precision.

Feedback hit shoulder.

The sentinel drew force from network.

I redirected that packet sideways into stair wall instead of its sword arm.

Silver threads flared.

The armor tried to swing.

Nothing.

Not nothing.

Sluggish.

Ressa saw.

She cut elbow joint.

Sword arm fell down steps.

Sentinel kept moving with shieldless left hand.

I diverted next pulse from hip.

Its right leg failed.

Ressa shoved.

Armor tumbled backward down stairs in pieces.

Metal crashing.

Silence.

My shoulder ached.

Anchor hot.

Dorin stared at me.

"You hijacked it."

"Borrowed its load path."

"That is hijacking with more syllables."

Maybe.

Ressa looked down stair.

The black plate still glowed.

Then another metal footstep below.

Then another.

Multiple sentinels waking.

Ressa said, "Up. Bar stair."

No greed.

We withdrew to hall and blocked entrance with old table plus chain, not because it could stop constructs forever but because warning mattered.

Through night, metal moved below but nothing climbed past third landing.

Maybe sentinel only activated when we crossed something.

Maybe black plate boundary.

We did not discover that in dark.

Around fire, Dorin said, "Tomorrow we can disconnect plate."

I said, "Survey note says don't."

"They were frightened."

"They also knew something we don't."

Ressa looked at me.

"Good."

I glared.

"Stop."

She smiled.

We slept in shifts.

I dreamed of empty armor using city bones like muscles.

Morning came cold and ordinary.

Breakfast was oats.

The lower works waited.

There might be more constructs.

There might be a cache worth the contract.

There might be blackglass hardware older than my Anchor.

The difference from Spring was not that I was fearless.

It was that I now had enough experience to be excited without pretending excitement was a plan.
