# Chapter 66: The Release

I used the brace ring badly on the third try.

Not third test. Third actual job.

Meren had called me for a warehouse lintel ward replacement.

The ring made sense because the temporary support points were three ugly iron brackets somebody had installed in different decades.

First cycle clean. Second clean.

On the third, one bracket shifted.

I saw the bracket move, the hanging ceramic channel, and Meren's left hand occupied with the replacement clamp all at once.

I tried to keep the channel aligned, compensate for the shifting bracket, and preserve the original support plane.

My forehead tightened instantly.

Kesra's diagnosis arrived too late to prevent the mistake and early enough to prevent the second one.

"RELEASING," I called.

Meren looked up.

"Release."

I dropped the plane.

The ring rotated harmlessly and the ceramic channel sagged into the padded sling Meren had installed before we began.

Nothing broke.

I sat down for two minutes with my eyes closed while Meren replaced the bad bracket.

"You saw too much?" he asked.

"Yes."

"Good problem."

"Kesra disagrees."

"Good problem if you stop. Bad problem if you prove something."

We finished the job.

The ring worked on the next two cycles without ceremony. Equipment used correctly after being used incorrectly.

Meren paid me full rate.

"You stopped before I told you," he said.

"Progress."

"Some."

I went home with a mild headache, ate bread, and put the ring back on the shelf.
