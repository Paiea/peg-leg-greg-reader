# Chapter 68: The Room

Mara came to Lower Rook because the Vey guest room had bedbugs.

Romance remained structurally dependent on sanitation.

"You're certain?" I asked.

"I saw one."

"Could be a beetle."

"It drank from my arm."

"Strong evidence."

She arrived after seventh bell with one travel bag and the expression of a woman betrayed by company lodging.

Noll opened his door as we reached the third floor.

He looked at Mara.

Then me.

"Freight administration?"

I closed my eyes.

Mara said, "Hot."

Noll smiled.

Disaster.

My room passed inspection better than Vey's.

Mara noticed the second chair.

The sword rack.

The drying alcove.

Letters on the shelf.

The repaired east window.

"You moved."

"Upstairs."

"I know how stairs work."

"I wanted to be clear."

She put her bag down.

"It's bigger."

"Costs more."

"That sounds like you."

I did not know whether it was an insult.

We sat.

Talked too late.

Not about history.

Mostly.

Mara told me Vey's Bracken office wanted her to stay through spring planning if the audit went well.

She had not decided whether she wanted that.

"More money?" I asked.

"Probably."

"More work?"

"Definitely."

"Then the traditional problem remains."

She threw a pillow at me.

Later, when the room went quiet, she touched the edge of the shelf holding my field gear.

"You actually go now."

"Yes."

"Before, you talked about going."

Young Greg.

Not accusation.

Observation.

"I know."

Mara looked at me.

"Do you?"

I did not answer quickly.

Good.

She took the bed.

I took my bedroll on the floor beside it.

Nothing needed to become a declaration.

At dawn Noll knocked once and left tea outside the door because apparently boundaries could be learned under threat...
