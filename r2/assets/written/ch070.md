# Year Chapter 28: Hookfin

Status: **TEMPORAL YEAR REHEARSAL / ZERO AUTHORITY**

`Y312 / D267 / AUTUMN`

`DAYS SINCE PRIOR CHAPTER: 7`

`LOCATION: East floodworks / lower river`

`RANK: Silver`

---

Seven days after the Gold team left, East Floodworks called me back without them.

That felt better than I expected.

Aren's team had gone north on D262.

No ceremony.

They finished their report, repaired Bram's shield strip, argued about breakfast money, and left Carrow because Gold people had other work.

I kept Aren's endorsement folded inside Westreach file.

Pell kept the broken glassmaw dampener and told me she might salvage one usable strip from the uncracked end.

Until then, I trained direct control shorter and cleaner.

No long load holds.

No heroics with city gates.

That lasted seven days.

D267 started cold and bright.

First frost stayed white on roofs after sunrise.

I was at Guild signing a warehouse escort closeout when Vaska entered.

She did not greet.

"Floodworks."

"Again?"

"Different."

"Better?"

"Animal."

There.

I followed.

Gate Three had been repaired enough for limited operation. Old blackglass node remained isolated behind temporary steel brace. City had reopened lower fish channel that morning to equalize river levels.

Something came through.

A **hookfin**.

I knew the name from Guild board drawings and Old Greg memory both.

Large river predator, twelve to fifteen feet, long armored body, two hooked forefins strong enough to climb low banks, jaws narrower than pale drake but faster.

Usually deep river.

Not city sluice.

This one had entered the lower maintenance basin and could not find way back because fish channel gate closed behind it automatically after pressure shift.

Problem was not immediately kill.

Open river route if possible.

Then hookfin ate a maintenance dog.

That changed public patience.

At basin, three city workers stood behind railing with poles.

The hookfin circled brown water below.

Back broke surface occasionally, dark blue plates with silver underside flashes.

One fin hook scraped stone.

Vaska said, "Water crew wants gate open."

Nema, operations lead, nodded.

"Fish gate can reopen if debris clear. But chain release is basin-side."

Meaning someone had to reach lever near water.

"Other access?"

"Walkway."

Narrow stone walkway crossed basin ten feet above water to gate lever.

The hookfin could jump perhaps six feet according bestiary.

Old Greg remembered eight.

Maybe.

"Who leads hazard?"

Vaska pointed at me.

I looked behind.

No one.

"Me?"

"You are Silver. Guild sent no one else yet. We need decision now before upper level rises."

There.

Not second.

Lead.

My stomach lifted and dropped.

Current team available:

Me.

Two city guards with spears, Dall and Merr.

Nema water operator.

Rigger Ilan, because of course he existed anywhere rope threatened.

No healer on site yet, one running.

I could wait for more Guild.

Upper water would rise and possibly flood lower mill channel within hour.

Could still wait twenty minutes perhaps.

The hookfin surfaced and rammed gate.

Chain housing shook.

Decision.

"We open route now. Nobody goes onto walkway until we control animal position."

Vaska nodded.

Mine.

I asked Nema basin geometry.

Deep center eight feet.

Shallow north shelf four.

Gate east.

Maintenance stairs west.

Hookfin tended to circle counterclockwise.

Can we bait?

Fish offal from market.

Vaska sent runner.

Ten minutes.

Meanwhile I inspected walkway anchors.

Two iron rings along rail, sound.

One overhead crane beam used for gate maintenance.

The Blackglass Anchor could make safety line better.

Not fight animal directly.

Ilan rigged a traveler line from overhead beam to my harness.

"You?" he asked.

"I know lever?"

Nema said, "I do."

Right.

Operator should operate.

"Then Nema on traveler. I go walkway with him. Guards west rail."

Why me?

Protect operator if hookfin jumps.

Sword useful.

Nema looked unhappy.

"I don't fight."

"Good. Don't. Pull lever when I say."

He nodded.

Fish offal arrived in bucket, smelling like punishment.

We tied sack to rope and lowered at north shelf.

Hookfin noticed immediately.

Water turned.

Dark body surged north.

It struck sack, jaws closing.

Dall and Merr hauled line, keeping it occupied.

"Now."

Nema and I moved onto walkway.

I clipped Anchor first line to overhead traveler frame, second to far walkway ring through running rope, third direct control on harness.

No dampener.

But load would mostly be safety if someone fell.

Halfway.

Hookfin thrashed at bait.

Seventy feet away.

Good.

Nema reached lever.

It was stuck.

Of course.

"Rust."

He pulled.

Nothing.

I looked back.

The offal sack tore.

Fish guts exploded into basin.

Hookfin swallowed half and lost interest in empty rope.

Its head turned.

Toward walkway vibration.

"Fast."

Nema put both hands on lever.

I joined? He knows mechanism but force okay.

"Together."

We pulled.

Lever moved one inch.

Chain clanked.

Gate began opening.

Water current changed.

Hookfin felt it.

Instead of attacking us, it turned toward gate.

Good.

Then gate jammed at three feet.

Too narrow for hookfin body maybe.

"Debris," Nema said.

From here I could see log wedged in track below water.

Need more gate lift or remove log.

Hookfin approached gate.

It shoved snout into opening.

Could maybe squeeze.

No.

Plates caught.

Animal panicked.

Tail slammed basin.

Water sprayed walkway.

Nema slipped.

Traveler line caught him.

Anchor took shock through frame.

I felt load, held stable.

"Up!"

He grabbed rail.

The hookfin backed from gate.

Now angry.

It looked up.

Or seemed to.

Then launched.

Old Greg remembered eight feet correctly.

Twelve-foot predator came half out of water, hooked fin catching walkway edge.

Its jaws snapped at Nema's boot.

I moved before fear.

Sword out.

Cut down across snout.

Blade skidded armor, opened softer nostril seam.

Hookfin roared and pulled itself higher.

One hooked fin over rail.

The walkway shook.

Nema crawled toward west.

Good.

I had line.

No one else on walkway.

Animal and me.

This was stupid and perfect.

The hookfin lunged.

I stepped inside bite line, not back.

Old Greg move.

Current body trusted it now.

Sword into mouth corner.

Deep.

The animal jerked sideways.

I ripped blade free.

Tail below slammed support.

Walkway cracked under one stone.

Ilan shouted from shore.

"Greg!"

"Nema clear?"

"Almost!"

The hookfin shifted weight to climb fully.

If it reached walkway, maybe collapse.

Could retreat.

Could let animal fall.

Need get it off.

I clipped Anchor second line from far ring to railing section under hooked fin.

Set force plane not against animal, but under rail.

Lift outward.

The Anchor redistributed into overhead traveler.

Rail flexed.

Hookfin's fin lost purchase by inches.

It scrabbled.

Jaws came again.

I ducked and cut underside of jaw.

Blood hot over coat.

No apology for coolness.

The animal slipped.

One fin still caught.

I kicked the hook joint.

Stupid against armor.

Force pulse at same time.

Joint opened.

Hookfin fell backward into basin.

Huge splash.

Nema reached shore.

Everyone shouted me back.

I could go.

But gate still jammed.

Animal now bleeding, more dangerous.

And I could see the log.

Three feet below walkway, wedged diagonally.

Could cut with sword? No.

Pull with line maybe.

Ilan understood when I pointed.

He threw hook rope.

I caught.

Hookfin circling below.

"Thirty seconds!" I shouted.

Maybe less.

I lowered hook into water, caught log branch on second try.

Ilan and both guards hauled.

Log shifted.

Gate rose another six inches from water pressure.

Hookfin saw opening.

Turned.

It charged gate instead of me.

Body hit gap.

Plates scraped stone.

Stuck for one second.

Then squeezed through.

Gone into river channel.

I laughed.

Too soon.

Blood trail moved east.

Animal injured but alive.

Potential problem downstream.

Vaska shouted, "Get off walkway!"

Right.

I went.

Halfway back, hookfin returned.

Of course.

Not through gate.

It had circled outside and surfaced in river side below lower quay, where current brought it alongside basin wall.

Maybe confused.

Maybe angry.

It launched again, this time from exterior at me.

No warning enough.

I turned.

Jaws open.

There was no operator to protect now.

No reason to preserve animal after it came again injured into populated works.

Decision clean.

I anchored feet and used everything together.

Anchor line overhead.

Force plane against left side of snout, not to stop mass, to rotate.

Sword low.

The hookfin hit the plane and turned six inches.

Enough.

Its head passed my right shoulder.

I stepped alongside neck as body momentum carried over edge.

Saw pale seam behind gill plate.

Old Greg knew.

Current Greg executed.

Sword drove in to hilt.

I held with both hands and let animal's own weight pull blade through seam.

The cut opened deep along neck.

Hookfin crashed partly onto walkway, partly against wall.

I jumped back before tail.

It thrashed.

Once.

Twice.

Then slid into river.

Blood spread dark.

Body floated against gate wall.

Silence.

My breathing loud.

Nobody corrected me.

Nobody said I should have waited.

Nema stared from shore.

Ilan started laughing.

Vaska put both hands on hips.

"You killed it."

"Yes."

Dall said, "On walkway."

"Yes."

Merr said something obscene about the cut.

I smiled.

Not modest.

Not ashamed.

I had just killed a twelve-foot river predator while hanging over a flood gate and kept the operator alive.

It felt fucking good.

Healer arrived six minutes later and found no one to heal except a scrape on Nema's elbow and my bruised hip from rail.

The rest became work.

We winched hookfin carcass onto quay.

Research Guild paid bounty.

Vaska filed gate incident.

Nema reopened fish channel fully after debris cleared.

Water levels normalized.

Ilan inspected walkway and closed it because cracked stone from animal weight.

My sword edge needed repair but had not broken.

Anchor showed no new damage.

Direct control hurt shoulders, but only minutes, not city-gate duration.

Manageable.

Hookfin hide had two useful fin hooks. One went research. One released as salvage.

I sold mine.

There.

Growth.

I did not turn every monster into equipment.

The money went straight into Westreach fund.

Thirty-eight silver after share and fees.

Enough to make winter kit suddenly plausible.

"Lead?"

"Yes."

"No."

"Gate opened. Nema safe. Hookfin dead after second attack."

No surprise.

That felt even better.

"Fast."

"Yes."

For once I did not need another victory before it.
