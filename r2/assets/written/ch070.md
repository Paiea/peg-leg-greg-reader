# Chapter 70: The Cistern

The city cistern job belonged to a plumber named Rell, not the Guild.

Meral made that clear before we climbed down.

"He says stop, we stop."

Rell was sixty if he was a day, had forearms like pipe sections, and called every magical fitting "the shiny part" regardless of material.

The south cistern overflow gate had frozen partly open. Water still moved, too much of it.

Meral led safety. I carried tools and provided small support if called.

Different team from the people I usually worked beside.

At the lower platform, Rell pointed at the valve bar.

"Need that held six inches off seat while I clean the ice from the guide."

Meral looked at me.

"Can you?"

I checked the load. Small enough if the water pressure did not spike.

"Yes."

"Call it."

"BAR."

I put a five-inch plane under the iron lever, directed up.

Rell worked.

He did not care what school the plane belonged to. He cared that the bar stayed where he needed it.

A minute later the water pressure changed and I felt the load rise.

"RELEASING."

Rell pulled his hand out before I dropped the plane.

"Pressure?" Meral asked.

"Up."

Rell spat into the drain.

"North feed opened."

Someone above had changed city flow. Not our mystery.

We waited for the pressure to settle and tried again.

The second cycle held. Rell cleared the guide and the gate seated.

He looked at where my plane had been.

"Shiny part?"

"No."

"Still worked."
