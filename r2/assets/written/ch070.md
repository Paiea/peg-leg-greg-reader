# Chapter 70: The Cistern

The city cistern job belonged to a plumber named Rell, not the Guild.

Meral made that clear before we climbed down.

"He says stop, we stop."

Rell was sixty if he was a day, had forearms like pipe sections, and called every magical fitting "the shiny part" regardless of material.

The south cistern overflow gate had frozen partly open.

Water still moved.

Too much.

Meral led safety.

I carried tools and provided small support if called.

No Jorren.

No Neris.

Different team.

At the lower platform, Rell pointed at the valve bar.

"Need that held six inches off seat while I clean the ice from the guide."

Meral looked at me.

"Can you?"

I checked load.

Small enough if the water pressure did not spike.

"Yes."

"Call it."

"BAR."

Plane under the iron lever.

Five inches.

Direction up.

Rell worked.

He did not care what school the plane belonged to.

He cared that the bar stayed where he needed it.

A minute later water pressure changed.

I felt the load rise.

"RELEASING."

Rell pulled his hand out before I dropped it.

Good team magic as language.

"Pressure?" Meral asked.

"Up."

Rell spat into the drain.

"North feed opened."

Someone above had changed city flow.

Not our mystery.

We waited.

Tried again.

Second cycle held.

Rell cleared the guide.

Gate seated.

Job done.

He looked at my plane after it was gone.

"Shiny part?"

"No."

"Still worked."

Professional taxonomy complete...
