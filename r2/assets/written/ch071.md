# Chapter 71: The Overflow

The cistern overflowed two days later for a different reason.

Rell sent word to Meral, not me.

Meral sent word to the Guild.

The Guild sent two Bronze workers, and one happened to be me.

This mattered because recurrence did not have to mean destiny.

The thaw had arrived early for one afternoon.

Roof snow across the south quarter turned into runoff faster than the lower channels could take it.

The repaired valve worked perfectly.

The system around it did not.

Rell stood ankle-deep in water and looked pleased to be proven right about something nobody had asked.

"Drain throat," he said. "Not gate."

The second Bronze, Sera Lin, carried sandbags like she had been born angry at water.

My job was not magic at first.

We moved bags.

Redirected a doorway.

Cleared leaves from a grate.

Then one timber stop began to slide.

"STOP!" Rell shouted.

I knew which one because he pointed.

Plane.

Small.

Enough to slow the timber while Sera wedged a bag beneath it.

"GONE!"

Release.

No headache.

No cleverness.

The overflow peaked, then dropped.

Rell inspected the gate afterward.

"Repair holds."

He sounded offended that success had not solved every future water problem.

"Good," Meral said.

"Drain's too small."

"Different work order."

"Still too small."

"Write it."

Rell did.

The city gained another piece of deferred infrastructure.

I gained wet socks.

Both were real consequences...
