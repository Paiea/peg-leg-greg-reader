# Chapter 72: The Quiet Day

I spent a day doing almost nothing and discovered I had become bad at it.

Not incapable, just bad.

I woke without work, business note, packet, scheduled instruction, or Jorren knocking on my door.

The Guild board could wait.

So I washed clothes, oiled the scabbard, checked the brace ring latch, and sharpened nothing because the sword did not need it.

When I moved the bed to sweep underneath, I found one copper I had not known I owned.

Profit.

At midday Noll asked whether I wanted lunch.

"Yes."

We ate fried potatoes downstairs.

He told me a woman at the bakery had asked if he was available Fifthday evening.

I looked at him.

"For work?"

"No."

"Oh."

He stared.

"That took you too long."

"I was being careful."

"Don't."

"You going?"

"Maybe."

"Who?"

"None of your business."

Fair enough.

After lunch I walked to East Market without needing anything and managed to buy nothing.

Back home I practiced one short sword sequence, stopped before it became training by anxiety, read half of Mara's old packet, and put it back.

By evening the laundry had dried.

I folded it badly and went to bed.
