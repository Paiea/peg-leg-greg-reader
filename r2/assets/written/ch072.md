I went to my Westreach candidacy meeting with my left hand wrapped and my best piece of equipment in somebody else's workshop.

This was not how I had pictured it on D001.

Good.

D274 arrived cold, clear, and inconveniently ordinary.

Noll left Lower Rook before me because Brass Spoon had begun serving breakfast three mornings a week and he now supervised prep on two of them.

Jorren had an inventory audit at Meln's yard and could not come even though he offered.

Tavin was east.

Mara was west.

Nobody escorted me to the important office.

That felt appropriate after nine months of learning people had schedules.

Pell sent a note at dawn:

**ANCHOR GLASS INTACT. CRADLE BENT. DO NOT TOUCH UNTIL I SAY. SIX-EYE COUPLER IS FUCKED. TECHNICAL TERM.**

I laughed harder than hand allowed.

Anchor intact.

Relief made legs weak for one second.

The coupler hurt, but differently.

A salvage part.

An experiment.

The Anchor had become mine through months of use.

Not broken.

Good.

I folded Pell's note into Westreach file.

Not to show anyone initially.

Then reconsidered.

The accident belonged to current equipment history.

If they asked, I would show.

Westreach office occupied Guild annex that had seemed boring on D001.

Now it looked like a machine already running.

Maps covered walls.

Crates labeled by destination.

Rope samples.

Tent cloth swatches.

Lists of animals.

Food calculations.

A chalk board reading **52 DAYS TO WEST GATE**.

D326.

Fifty-two.

That number hit harder than D365.

Black Stair was ninety-one days away.

Departure was fifty-two.

The year had stopped being large.

"Excellent."

"You haven't said hello."

"What happened?"

Not defensive version.

Held one second longer after clear.

Hand shocked.

Anchor under inspection.

"So deadline changed engineering judgment."

There.

"Yes."

"Not now."

"That's easy answer after injury."

"Yes."

Only record.

"Pell machine repair, Dena Bell Tile, Harl mason."

"Pell told slow. I overruled."

No need ask more.

"Good that you said it. Bad that it happened."

"Yes."

"Ten to twenty days no load magic. Sword light sooner."

"Departure fifty-two."

"I know."

Not min-maxing damage alone.

Dangerously satisfying.

Good.
