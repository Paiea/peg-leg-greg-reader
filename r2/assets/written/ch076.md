# Chapter 76: The Pool

We saw the pond again the next morning from the ridge.

The reflection was wrong in a different direction.

Lin wanted to measure it.

Esta said no.

Lin accepted no after asking exactly once.

Civilization.

We stayed at the orchard because one of our boundary markers sat on the far side of the closed causeway and Harl decided losing half a day was cheaper than adding six more miles through frozen fields.

The orchard did not stop working because the road did.

Esta's brothers pruned pear limbs.

Two hired hands hauled compost.

A girl of maybe twelve checked rabbit guards around young trees.

Pate discovered fallen pears under straw and became impossible.

I helped move three sacks because standing still made me cold.

Esta watched me lift.

"Guild?"

"Bronze."

"For road office?"

"Today."

She nodded as if jobs changing shape was ordinary.

"Pool ever hurt anyone?" I asked.

"Yes."

"How?"

"People walked when the path wasn't where it looked."

That was enough.

Old Greg had fought creatures whose teeth were easier to understand.

At midday the horizon in the pond straightened.

Esta turned an hourglass on a fence post.

Nobody hurried it.

One full glass later she unlocked the chain and walked the first ten stones herself.

Slow.

Looking at her feet, not the water.

Then back.

"Open foot and pack. No carts until morning."

Harl nodded.

We crossed.

I kept my eyes on stone.

Nothing attacked.

The local woman who understood the place better than me decided when the road existed again.

We continued east with one more thing I could not fit into later magical theory...
