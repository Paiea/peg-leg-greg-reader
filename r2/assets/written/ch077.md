# Chapter 77: The Marker

The disputed marker was under a pig shed.

This improved the entire survey.

Lin's historical sketch put it beside a road bend. The current bend sat behind a farm, and the farmer remembered his father moving "an old useless stone" when they expanded the pen.

Harl closed his eyes.

"Moved where?"

The farmer pointed.

Pig shed.

We found the marker built into the foundation, its East District seal facing inward beneath a feed trough.

Lin laughed until he had to sit down.

Harl did not.

"Can we verify original position?"

The farmer remembered a stump. The stump remained, and the distances matched the old sketch.

Administrative truth recovered through pigs.

I stood outside the shed because somebody needed to watch Pate.

Later road memory had no pig shed, farm, or marker. History would clean all of this into a district line on a map.

Current evidence smelled worse.
