# Chapter 79: The Return

Eight days became nine because rain found us on the return.

Not dramatic rain.

Persistent rain.

The kind that turned every sock into a personal grievance.

We reached Carrow late on Ninthday evening.

North Gate admitted us under lantern inspection.

Pate looked cleaner than I did because mud respected hierarchy.

Harl closed the survey the next morning.

District line corrected around three bridges.

One moved marker documented.

One farm access dispute flagged for legal review.

Orchard pond closure procedure added as route note.

No explanation of the pond.

Correct.

I was paid for nine days.

At Lower Rook my room smelled like dust and stale air.

Noll had pushed two letters under the door.

Neither urgent.

The filtration workshop had not burned.

The Guild had not collapsed.

My bed remained exactly as comfortable as memory had made it.

I slept eleven hours.

Travel widened the map and mostly increased laundry...
