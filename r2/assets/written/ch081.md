# Chapter 81: The Moving Plane

I finally bought another support block from Neris after the first lesson had spent enough time becoming habit.

She made me move the plane without rebuilding it.

One inch.

This was terrible.

"Again," she said.

We worked in a borrowed yard with a hanging sandbag and chalk marks on a timber frame.

Plane formed.

Five inches.

Stable.

"Right one inch."

I tried.

The plane vanished.

"That was a new plane."

"Yes."

"I said move."

"I noticed."

"Again."

Old Greg could alter support geometry under load.

Current Greg's magic preferred discrete decisions.

On.

Off.

Form.

Release.

Moving an existing construct required a different kind of attention.

Not more reserve exactly.

Continuous control.

I failed seven times.

On the eighth, the plane shifted enough to smear the chalk edge.

Half inch.

Neris looked.

"Again."

No celebration.

By the end I could move it roughly one inch under no meaningful load.

Under load, it still failed.

Good boundary.

"Practice unloaded only," Neris said.

"How long?"

"Until it stops feeling like holding your breath with your forehead."

Technical language improving...
