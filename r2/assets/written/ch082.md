# Chapter 82: The Handrail

The moving plane first mattered on a staircase.

Not combat.

Better.

Meren had a hotel handrail ward half-disassembled when a guest ignored the rope barrier and tried to come down anyway.

Old woman.

Cane.

Determined.

"Stairs closed," Meren said.

"My room is upstairs."

"Then remain in it."

She continued descending.

Current authority failed.

One temporary rail support had been removed.

Meren's hands were occupied.

I formed a plane under the loose rail segment as the woman reached it.

"RAIL."

She grabbed higher than I expected.

Load shifted.

The plane sat an inch wrong.

I moved it.

Not much.

Enough.

The rail settled into her grip instead of rolling away.

She descended.

"Thank you," she said.

Meren waited until she was gone.

"That new?"

"One inch."

"Useful inch."

Then he made me release because the actual job remained unfinished.

No bigger magic.

One inch entering the world...
