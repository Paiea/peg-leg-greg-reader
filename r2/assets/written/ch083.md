# Chapter 83: The Scabbard

The sword was fine.

The scabbard was not.

Lera noticed before I did.

She drew my sword halfway, stopped, and looked at the mouth lining.

"Replace."

"Blade?"

"Scabbard."

Relief arrived embarrassingly fast.

The inner leather had worn rough enough to begin polishing one side of the blade unevenly.

Not damage yet.

Future damage if ignored.

I had spent enough attention on owning a good sword that I had started treating the container as scenery.

Expensive mistake waiting politely.

A leatherworker near East Market quoted me.

More than socks.

Less than sword.

Still irritating.

I ordered a new inner liner and throat rather than a whole decorative scabbard.

No silver fittings.

No upgraded weapon.

Maintenance purchased before failure.

Arlo would approve.

Probably too much...
