# Chapter 84: The Caravan

Jorren asked me to take six days north-east with a three-cart caravan.

He had already accepted.

That mattered.

"Need second Bronze?"

"Client wants two."

"Why me?"

"Because I like knowing which mistakes I'm getting."

Friendship.

The caravan carried lamp oil, nails, flour, and two crates of ceramic drain fittings.

No treasure.

No secret passenger.

Winter had loosened enough that roads moved again and merchants were filling every delayed order at once.

We left on a wet morning.

Jorren took rear cart.

I took front.

By evening we had already developed arguments with two drivers and one axle.

Normal travel.

The second day carried us toward a river town I remembered only after it had a stone bridge.

Current map showed ferry.

Good.

Different world waiting...
