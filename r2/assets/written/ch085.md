# Chapter 85: The River Camp

The ferry stopped at dusk because the river had turned silver.

Not color.

Behavior.

Thin bright lines moved across the current against the wind.

The ferryman took one look and tied the boat higher.

"No crossing tonight."

One merchant objected.

The ferryman ignored him.

Jorren asked, "Ice?"

"No."

"What?"

"Spawning lightfin."

I knew lightfin as food in later towns.

Not this.

Current river filled with magical fish whose migration changed surface tension enough to make loaded ferry cables unpredictable.

Of course.

The ferryman did not explain theory.

He moved the mooring.

We camped above the bank.

Hundreds of silver lines passed in darkness.

Nobody fought them.

Nobody harvested them because local rule prohibited nets during spawn.

The merchants complained about delay.

The ferryman charged nothing extra.

River owned the schedule...
