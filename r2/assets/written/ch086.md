# Chapter 86: The Ferry

We crossed after sunrise when the lightfin had moved upstream.

The ferryman tested with an empty boat first.

Then one cart.

Then two.

Our three carts crossed separately.

No one argued by then.

Current evidence had become expensive enough to respect.

Halfway across with the second cart, the ferry yawed when a residual silver line broke near the upstream cable.

The ferryman corrected before I understood the motion.

One pole.

One shouted order.

Driver braked.

Jorren shifted weight.

Boat straightened.

I did nothing.

Useful restraint again.

On the far bank the ferryman said, "Good crossing."

The merchant who had complained overnight said, "That was good?"

"You arrived."

Professional standard.

We continued north-east.

I looked back once at the river.

Later bridge memory had erased an entire seasonal profession.

Current world kept restoring people history had made unnecessary...
