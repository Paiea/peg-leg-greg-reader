# Chapter 91: The Practitioner

I did not replace Lera.

This annoyed three people who had not been asked.

The Guild posted an instructor list beside the reopened yard: two Silver fighters, one retired watch sergeant, and a Gold assistant passing through for less than a month. Rates sat beside their names.

The Gold rate made my ribs hurt without physical contact.

I read the list and put it back.

Neris found me doing footwork alone two days later.

"No instructor?"

"Gone."

"Dead?"

"Dorrin."

"Worse."

Professional rivalry between cities, apparently.

I practiced ten minutes.

Cut. Arrive. Recover.

Outside step only when the position earned it.

Again.

Stop.

Neris leaned against the fence through most of this because her support block started after mine.

"You always count?"

"Only when I don't trust myself."

"So always."

Fair.

Her student arrived, a Bronze woman I had seen at East Annex, and Neris immediately stopped caring about me.

I packed the practice blade away.

Jorren appeared on Fourthday with a bruise under one eye.

"Work?" I asked.

"Crate."

"Crate hit you?"

"Crate owner did."

"Different contract."

"Resolved."

He picked up a padded sword.

"Lera gone?"

"Six weeks."

"You worse already."

"Thank you."

We sparred.

Jorren was not Lera, which was useful. He did not pressure the same angles or punish the same habits. He also cheated with his shoulder more often and called it "being where the sword isn't."

I lost two exchanges and won one.

Then stopped.

Jorren frowned.

"Done?"

"Yes."

"You're winning."

"Exactly."

He stared.

"Lera broke you."

"Probably."

We went for food.

Six weeks did not need to be filled because a teacher left. I still had work to practice, and other people were allowed to remain other people.
