# Chapter 91: The Practitioner

I did not replace Lera.

This annoyed three people who had not been asked.

The Guild posted an instructor list beside the reopened yard.

Two Silver fighters.

One retired watch sergeant.

A Gold assistant passing through for less than a month.

Rates beside names.

The Gold rate made my ribs hurt without physical contact.

I read the list.

Then put it back.

Neris found me doing footwork alone two days later.

"No instructor?"

"Gone."

"Dead?"

"Dorrin."

"Worse."

Professional rivalry between cities, apparently.

I practiced ten minutes.

CUT.

ARRIVE.

RECOVER.

Outside step only when the position earned it.

Again.

Stop.

Neris leaned against the fence through most of this because her support block started after mine.

"You always count?"

"Only when I don't trust myself."

"So always."

Fair.

Her student arrived.

A Bronze woman I had seen at East Annex.

Neris immediately stopped caring about me.

Good boundary.

I packed the practice blade away.

Jorren appeared on Fourthday with a bruise under one eye.

"Work?" I asked.

"Crate."

"Crate hit you?"

"Crate owner did."

"Different contract."

"Resolved."

He picked up a padded sword.

"Lera gone?"

"Six weeks."

"You worse already."

"Thank you."

We sparred.

Jorren was not Lera.

Useful.

He did not pressure the same angles or punish the same habits.

He also cheated with his shoulder more often and called it "being where the sword isn't."

I lost two exchanges.

Won one.

Then stopped.

Jorren frowned.

"Done?"

"Yes."

"You're winning."

"Exactly."

He stared.

"Lera broke you."

"Probably."

We went for food.

No new technique.

No substitute instructor.

Six weeks did not need to be filled because a teacher left.

Practice could remain practice...
