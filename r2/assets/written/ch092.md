# Chapter 92: The Cook

Noll got a real kitchen job because somebody ate his dumplings.

This seemed more reasonable than several systems of government I remembered.

He told me while carrying a pot downstairs.

"The Brass Spoon."

I knew the place.

Not well.

East Market dining room with six front tables, four back tables, and prices that implied chairs were part of the meal.

"Pay?"

Noll told me.

Better than his current collection of kitchen shifts and tenant suppers.

Longer evenings.

Two meals included.

"You taking it?"

"Trial week."

"Good."

He stopped on the stair.

"That's all?"

"Do you want a business analysis?"

"Absolutely not."

Correct friendship.

I went to The Brass Spoon on his third trial night.

Not to inspect.

To eat.

This distinction lasted until I saw him through the open kitchen hatch.

Noll moved differently at work.

Faster.

Less talking.

Knife down.

Pan up.

Plate pass.

A head cook named Vessa controlled the room with three words and one terrifying eyebrow.

The chef Noll had described as spoon-throwing was apparently named Domm.

I learned this when a spoon struck the wall beside the wash station.

Not a person.

Technically.

Noll did not react.

Vessa did.

"Domm."

One word.

He stopped throwing spoons.

Management.

My dumplings arrived six minutes later.

Better than Lower Rook dumplings.

I resented this personally.

Noll had changed the filling.

More onion.

Less pork.

Sharper vinegar.

Current professional growth occurring without a lecture.

He came out after the rush.

"Well?"

"Traitor."

"Good?"

"Very."

He smiled.

"Vessa taught me the fold."

"You already knew folds."

"Not that fast."

There.

A person could acquire a teacher by getting hired.

No silver instruction block required.

"Domm?" I asked.

Noll looked toward the kitchen.

"He throws things when he loses count."

"Still employed?"

"His sauces are excellent."

Complicated institution.

"You staying?"

Noll looked at his hands.

Small burn on one knuckle.

Flour near his cuff.

"If they keep me."

Not destiny.

Trial.

I paid for dinner.

Noll did not comp it.

Professional boundary again.

Back at Lower Rook, the hallway felt quieter before he came home.

Not empty.

Just missing one person's ordinary noise.

Belonging had acquired a work schedule...
