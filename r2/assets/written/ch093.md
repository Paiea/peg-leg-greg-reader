# Chapter 93: The Tenant

Noll's new job changed my room before it changed his.

Three evenings a week he left before dinner and returned after I was asleep.

My second chair stayed empty more often.

I noticed because I had bought the larger room partly to stop living as if objects were the only things that needed space.

Now I had space. People had schedules.

Bad planning.

Jorren filled the chair one night.

Then nobody for two.

On the third I ate alone and repaired a loose pack strap.

Not loneliness exactly. Schedule.

Noll came home early on Fifthday carrying half a loaf under one arm.

"Fired?" I asked.

"No."

"Injured?"

"No."

"Spoon?"

"Domm finally hit someone."

I looked at him.

"With spoon?"

"Shoulder."

"Still employed?"

"Sent home two days."

"And you?"

"Vessa closed early."

Noll stole my tea, sat in the second chair, and put both feet on the wall.

"Don't."

"Rent?"

"Mine."

"Chair?"

"Mine."

"Wall?"

"Maud's."

He removed his feet.

Property law succeeded.

"They keeping you?" I asked.

"Vessa says yes after trial if I want it."

"Do you?"

Noll stared into the tea.

"I think so."

Enough.

"Then good."

He looked at me.

"Still no analysis?"

"I am growing."

"Terrible."

He fell asleep in the chair twenty minutes later.

I put a blanket over him because waking a cook after a trial week seemed professionally irresponsible.

The second chair was occupied again.

Tomorrow it might not be.
