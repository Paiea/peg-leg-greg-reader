# Chapter 98: The Card Player

The burned seven rule remained unsolved because Tavin remembered it differently from everyone else.

This improved the game.

"Red seven burns," Tavin said.

"Any seven," Noll said.

"Only if played after a pair," Jorren said.

I looked at him.

"You invented that."

"You cannot prove it."

He had found the central weakness in damaged memory.

We played three hands under three rule systems and declared each other criminals.

Tavin lost four copper.

He accused Jorren and me of collusion.

Correctly.

Noll fell asleep in the second chair before midnight.

Work schedule had finally caught him.

Jorren left soon after.

Tavin stayed at the window with the burned seven between two fingers.

"You really don't remember?"

No accusation.

"No."

"We burned it behind Mara's mother's place."

I waited.

Nothing.

"Why?"

"You said the deck cheated."

"Did it?"

"No. You were losing."

Credible.

He smiled.

"Mara said it was stupid."

"Also credible."

The memory did not return.

The story did not become less true because I lacked it.

Tavin put the card down.

"You coming south sometime?"

There it was.

Not job offer.

Not deadline.

Not now.

"Probably."

"That sounds older than you."

"Everything does."

He looked toward my sword rack.

Then the shelves.

Then Noll asleep in the chair.

"You staying?"

Different question.

"Tonight?"

"Carrow."

I thought about it honestly.

Mara had gone west.

Tavin worked south.

Jorren took roads in every direction.

Lera had left temporarily because another city paid her more.

I had gone north-east and come back.

Carrow was no longer the place I had failed to leave.

It was the place I currently returned to.

"For now."

Tavin nodded.

No disappointment.

"Good enough."

We talked until the room went cold.

Not about memory.

Mostly freight.

At dawn I walked him to North Gate.

His crew had stacked a sack of grain on a crate marked **GLASS / DO NOT STACK**.

I pointed.

Tavin swore.

Future knowledge remained unnecessary.

He moved the grain himself...
