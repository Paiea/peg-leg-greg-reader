# Chapter 105: The Recommendation

West Cut did not get repaired because we surveyed it.

This offended the part of me trained by stories and pleased the part trained by cities.

Senna presented the four-day report at East Annex.

Harl stood beside her.

I stood farther back because Bronze field hands did not become public works directors by proximity.

The recommendation had three parts:

Clear the blocked lower culvert immediately.

Excavate the suspected old upper drain line before wall reconstruction.

Restrict heavy thaw carts until seep behavior was understood.

No new retaining wall authorization yet.

One merchant objected.

"We paid for a repair survey."

Senna answered, "You paid to find out what repair is needed."

Good distinction.

"And?"

"We need more information."

He hated that.

The city works clerk did not care.

Harl's field authority ended when the report did.

Mine had ended earlier.

We were paid.

The road remained partly bad.

The survey had ended. The road had not.

Outside, Harl gave Pate an apple.

"Next?" I asked.

"Other roads."

"West Cut?"

"When they fund excavation."

Could be weeks.

Could be next year.

Could disappear into city paper forever.
