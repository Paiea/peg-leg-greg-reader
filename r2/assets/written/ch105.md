# D512: Their Specialist

Status: **TEMPORAL REHEARSAL / ZERO AUTHORITY**

---

The Gold party did not ask whether I could keep up.

That made me suspicious immediately.

Sella put the contract on the counter.

Heavy paper. Blue cord. Private commission mark.

"Third line," she said.

I read it.

**Contracted specialist: Greg, Silver, static load and ward-interaction support.**

Not escort.

Not additional hand.

Specialist.

"Why me?"

"Their support mage can hold more force than you. Their warder reads deeper systems than Meren. Their scout has two working legs."

"Cruel."

"You notice load changes before other people know there is a question. They need that."

There.

Different, not best.

Useful category.

The party called itself Hearthline.

Four Golds.

Venn Aric led. Compact man, collapsible spear strapped along his pack. Nesha Pell scouted. Ilyra Tonn handled healing. Daro Mesk handled wards and looked fifteen until he spoke like disappointed masonry had personally raised him.

Their job was an old cistern east of Pell Road.

Surveyed approach. Cleared entrance. Unknown lower chamber.

My restrictions disliked the last part.

"You stay in the known upper ring," Venn said. "We need the roof to remain there."

Reasonable.

The upper chamber was thirty feet across, half original stone and half later timber repair. Two columns stood. One was broken. One had vanished into the lower opening years ago.

I stopped at the threshold.

Everyone stopped because I stopped.

That felt good again.

I ignored the feeling and looked.

Roof load. Ring wall. Timber shore. Crack lines.

Weight did not care what year it was.

"Don't walk center yet."

Daro asked, "Ward?"

"I don't know."

Good answer.

I tested paving stones with the short staff. One sounded tighter than the others.

Loaded.

I crouched and set a small horizontal plane against it.

The stone barely moved.

Not because it was supported.

Because something beneath was already pushing up.

Daro knelt beside me and put two fingers to the floor.

"Ward line. Under pressure."

Venn asked, "Bad?"

Daro said, "Unknown."

Venn nodded. "Then bad until promoted."

Correct management.

We mapped the outer ring first.

My load map and Daro's ward map disagreed in three places.

At the fourth they disagreed violently.

A wall segment that should have been carrying roof shifted toward center under almost no pressure from my plane. Daro's chalk mark beside it brightened.

"The ward is holding the wall," he said.

"The wall is holding the ward."

"Same problem."

"Different failure order."

He smiled.

Professional pleasure.

Ilyra watched me more than the wall.

After fifteen minutes she said, "Sit."

"I'm fine."

"Socket edge is wet."

Damn Gold healer.

I sat.

No argument.

Skin damage cost days. Pride paid badly.

Venn waited until I reset the liner.

Actually waited.

No discussion about continuing without me.

When I stood again he asked, "Ready?"

"Yes."

Then we solved it.

Daro isolated the trapped ward segment. I held the ring wall against its current load path while Venn and Nesha installed two temporary braces.

At one point Venn asked, "More brace or enough?"

"Enough. More changes the path."

He stopped immediately.

Gold leader.

Asked the Silver specialist.

Stopped when answered.

My old life remembered powerful people who needed rank explained through consequences.

Current Venn simply wanted the ceiling above us.

Better system.

The dangerous part lasted eleven minutes.

Nobody shouted.

Nobody reached outside role.

Once the braces took weight, Daro bled charge from the trapped line. The floor settled a fraction.

Nothing collapsed.

Excellent adventure.

Nesha checked only enough of the lower opening to confirm the missing column had fallen across the old channel.

Venn called the job there.

"We come back with excavation authority."

No push because Gold could probably survive more.

Enough.

On the wagon home, Ilyra handed me salve for the socket edge.

"Thin layer tonight."

"Ingredients?"

"No."

"Why?"

"Because you will ask Bera to improve it."

I considered lying.

Did not.

"Fair."

Venn sat across from me.

"You available again?"

There.

The question I wanted.

"Prepared sites."

"For now."

I disliked the phrase less than I used to.

"For now."

He nodded.

"Good. Daro hates doing two jobs."

Daro said, "I hate doing one."

At Lower Rook, Noll looked up from the cookroom.

"Good?"

I thought about Gold pay in my pocket and a Gold leader stopping because I said enough.

"Very."

Noll nodded.

"You eat?"

Apparently rank had limits.
