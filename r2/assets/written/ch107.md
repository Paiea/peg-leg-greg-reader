# Chapter 107: The Second Cook

The first time I saw Noll supervising someone, he was telling a boy not to salt soup from shoulder height.

"Why are you doing that?"

The boy froze with salt between two fingers.

"Chef does it."

"Chef knows how much is in his hand. You do not."

Noll took the salt, measured, and returned it.

The Brass Spoon had moved him from trial cook to regular second cook weeks earlier. Now Vessa had given him a kitchen runner for two evening shifts.

I was eating at the back table with Jorren.

"He looks angry," Jorren said.

"Professional."

"Same face."

Noll heard.

"I can poison both of you legally."

"Restaurant category?" I asked.

"Chef discretion."

We ate anyway.

The soup had correct salt.

The runner dropped one plate and did not die. Vessa charged breakage to nobody because accidents happened.

Noll finished after us and sat for exactly three minutes before standing again.

"Inventory."

"You do inventory?"

"Now I do."

He disappeared back through the kitchen door before I could ask whether he liked that part.

The runner followed carrying a chalk board twice the width of his chest.
