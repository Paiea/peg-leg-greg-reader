# Chapter 107: The Second Cook

The first time I saw Noll supervising someone, he was telling a boy not to salt soup from shoulder height.

"Why are you doing that?"

The boy froze with salt between two fingers.

"Chef does it."

"Chef knows how much is in his hand. You do not."

Noll took the salt.

Measured.

Returned it.

The Brass Spoon had moved him from trial cook to regular second cook weeks earlier.

Now Vessa had given him a kitchen runner for two evening shifts.

Not promotion ceremony.

More responsibility because there was work.

I was eating at the back table with Jorren.

"He looks angry," Jorren said.

"Professional."

"Same face."

Noll heard.

"I can poison both of you legally."

"Restaurant category?" I asked.

"Chef discretion."

We ate anyway.

The soup had correct salt.

The runner dropped one plate and did not die.

Vessa charged breakage to nobody because accidents happened.

I liked her more every visit.

Noll finished after us.

He sat for exactly three minutes before standing again.

"Inventory."

"You do inventory?"

"Now I do."

Independent life kept adding verbs...
