# Chapter 109: The Moving Load

Neris made me walk while holding the plane under a suspended crate.

Not supporting the whole crate.

One corner.

One inch of plane.

Small enough that failure remained educational rather than expensive.

"Call first," she said.

"RIGHT CORNER."

The porter heard me.

Shifted grip.

I formed.

One inch.

Under the crate corner.

Then we walked three steps.

The plane wanted to remain where I had made it.

I wanted it to move with me.

Different skill.

It lagged.

Crate dipped.

"GONE."

I released before turning bad support into surprise.

The porter reset.

Neris nodded.

"Again."

No lecture.

Ten attempts.

Three useful.

Two clean.

My forehead tightened before reserve became pain.

"Stop."

I stopped.

Old Greg could have moved support under a running fighter without thinking about one inch.

Current Greg had moved a crate corner three steps.

Both facts existed.

Only one paid today's instructor...
