# Chapter 110: The Caller

The moving-plane practice became useful two weeks later in a place with no crate.

Meren had me on a dye-house ward inspection.

One worker carried a ceramic channel section down a narrow stair while another guided the lower end.

The upper worker slipped.

Not fall.

One foot.

Enough.

"LOW END!"

Call before magic.

The lower worker understood.

I formed under the ceramic edge and moved the plane down one stair as they recovered.

One inch.

Then another.

"GONE."

Released.

Nobody dropped the channel.

Meren looked at me.

"New?"

"Practicing."

"Useful."

That was all.

No new rank.

No reserve jump.

Instruction had become work without requiring a staged exam.

At closeout Meren paid ordinary second-hand rate.

No tool fee.

No specialist premium for using my own magic.

I considered arguing.

Did not.

Growth could remain salaried sometimes...
