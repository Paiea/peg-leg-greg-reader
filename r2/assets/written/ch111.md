# Chapter 111: The Pairing

A client asked for Jorren and me together before either of us knew the job existed.

Sella told us separately.

This was how I learned our informal pair had become client memory.

Hollen Brick needed two Bronze for a three-day lime-road escort after losing a load to ditch thieves the previous spring.

Their yard foreman had remembered Jorren from culvert work and me from a closeout verification conversation after the Guild fire.

Neither memory involved heroism.

Useful reputation.

Jorren had not accepted yet.

Neither had I.

"Rate?" he asked.

Sella gave it.

We looked at each other.

"I can," Jorren said.

"I can."

"Then yes," he said.

Not party declaration.

Calendar alignment.

Three days...
