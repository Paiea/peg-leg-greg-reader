# D699: The Betrayer

Status: **TEMPORAL REHEARSAL / ZERO AUTHORITY**

---

Venn canceled my retainer without raising his voice.

That hurt more than if he had hit me.

Hearthline used the back room at The Brass Spoon because apparently Noll's employer had become neutral diplomatic territory.

Noll knew enough to be elsewhere.

Good man.

Venn sat across from me.

Nesha beside him.

Daro standing near the window because sitting while angry was apparently beyond his current technique.

Ilyra was not there.

That somehow made the room worse.

Venn put one folded contract on the table.

My retainer.

Then a second paper.

Harrow's notice.

**Artifact missing following contractor inventory access. Private recovery requested before formal city complaint.**

No accusation by name.

Yet.

"You have it," Venn said.

"Yes."

Daro swore softly.

Venn nodded once.

"Return it."

"No."

There.

Again.

Still ugly.

Daro pushed away from the window.

"We got you into that room."

"I know."

"My name is on the inspection."

"I know."

"Then why the fuck are you saying it like weather?"

Good question.

I looked at him.

"Because I don't have an answer that makes it better."

"Try worse."

Fine.

"I wanted it more than I valued not doing this to you."

Silence.

Nesha looked down at the table.

Daro stared.

Venn's expression did not change.

That was the truth.

Not complete forever.

Complete enough for this room.

Daro said, "Fuck you."

"Yes."

"Stop agreeing."

"I don't know what else to do."

"Give it back."

Right.

There was always that.

Venn tapped the retainer contract.

"Hearthline will not employ you while you possess property taken through access we provided."

I felt the loss immediately.

Not money first.

Work.

Cistern.

Daro's ward questions.

Gold sites.

The feeling of a room full of very capable people asking me what I saw.

Gone.

Because I chose glass.

Good.

Costs should arrive.

"Understood."

Nesha looked at me.

"Do you?"

"Yes."

"You sound like you planned for it."

I had.

Not exactly this sentence.

Relationship cost.

Professional access loss.

Reputation risk.

I had written them all.

The categories had been cleaner than Daro's face.

"I considered it."

That was the wrong answer.

Daro laughed once.

"Of course you did."

Venn folded the retainer paper in half.

"Did you leave anything that clears Harrow's staff?"

"A note."

Nesha's head snapped toward me.

"Signed?"

"Yes."

All three stared.

"What?"

Venn asked, "You admitted it?"

"I said the warehouse staff did not take Faultglass. I did. I asked Harrow to contact me directly."

Daro sat down finally.

"You are the strangest thief alive."

"Limited sample."

"Greg."

Right.

Venn rubbed his forehead.

"Why?"

"Because Nesha was right. I wasn't letting Harrow frighten clerks because I wanted an artifact."

Nesha closed her eyes.

"That was not permission to become an honorable criminal."

"I know."

"Do you?"

Apparently not convincingly.

Venn asked, "Harrow responded?"

I took a third paper from my coat.

Put it on the table.

Harrow's hand, surprisingly neat.

**Return Faultglass within ten days undamaged and I will treat the matter as a private breach of contract. Refuse, damage, conceal, or transfer it and I file with city authority and Guild.**

Daro read it.

"Ten days."

"Yes."

"You're returning it."

Not question.

I said nothing.

Daro stood again.

"Oh, fuck you."

"Daro," Venn said.

"No. He is sitting there calculating whether ten days is enough to learn everything he wants first."

I looked at him.

He knew me too well now.

"Yes," I said.

Daro left.

Door shut hard enough to disturb the wall picture.

No artifact required.

Nesha watched it swing.

Then looked at me.

"You lost him."

"For now."

"There."

I stopped.

For now.

Old phrase.

Everything temporary if enough leverage existed.

People included.

That was bad.

Venn stood.

"Return the glass and we can discuss work later. Not immediately."

"If I don't?"

"Then don't use my name to get into another private collection."

Fair.

"Will you report me?"

"Harrow already has your admission. I am not adding a complaint. I am also not hiding anything if asked."

Clean boundary.

I respected it.

Hated it.

Respected it more.

Venn left.

Nesha stayed.

She pushed the canceled retainer toward me.

"You knew this might happen."

"Yes."

"Worth it?"

I thought about Faultglass wrapped in cloth under a loose floorboard in my room.

Not because floorboards were secure.

Because Noll touched everything else.

Ten days.

One stolen artifact.

One Gold party currently unavailable.

Daro angry enough to stop being curious.

Harrow waiting.

"I don't know yet."

Nesha nodded.

"Good answer."

She stood.

At the door she turned.

"If you decide people are replaceable because future you has met more of them, I will break your good leg."

I looked at her.

"That seems counterproductive."

"Wanted to phrase it in a language you respect."

Then she left.

I sat alone in the back room.

Noll came in five minutes later with two plates.

He looked around.

"Bad?"

"Yes."

He put one plate in front of me.

"Eat."

No strategic value.

No moral instruction.

Just dumplings.

I ate.
