# Chapter 119: The Trial Route

Mara left Carrow on a northbound packet with two route clerks and a box of copied toll records.

Vey had decided to test one part of the east handoff farther north.

Not because Mara chose it. She had helped count why.

One wagon transfer disappeared. One local cart leg became longer. Carrow merchants complained before the trial began.

Merchants elsewhere probably celebrated in private.

At North Gate, Mara adjusted the strap on her ledger case.

"Back west after?" I asked.

"Maybe. They want three weeks of trial numbers first."

"Halden?"

"Unless Vey discovers another city needs me more."

Her life kept acquiring directions without consulting my memory.

She climbed aboard.

The packet driver checked his clock.

Mara leaned down once.

"Write before I have to complain."

"Threat received."

Then the packet left because schedules had no interest in emotional timing.

I watched until the road bent.

Then I went to Pell Orst's shop to have my brace ring inspected...
