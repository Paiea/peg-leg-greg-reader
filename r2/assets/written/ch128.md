# Chapter 128: The Baker

Noll's new room smelled like bread at dawn and onions after midnight.

"Improvement?" I asked.

"Depends which direction time is moving."

One bed.

One chair.

One narrow shelf.

The Brass Spoon was five minutes away if he cut through the produce alley.

That mattered more than square footage.

I brought two bowls.

He stared at them.

"You own enough bowls to give bowls now."

"Wealth changes men."

"Terrible."

We ate bread from the bakery downstairs because the baker had discovered that Noll worked at the Brass Spoon and was already trying to trade breakfast for kitchen favors.

Local economies formed quickly.

Noll moving out had not ended anything.

It had added a place I could go.

On the walk back to Lower Rook, I caught myself turning toward his old door before remembering.

Tiny error.

Current life required updates too...
