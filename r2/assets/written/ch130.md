# Chapter 130: The Long Job

Jorren asked whether I could disappear east for twelve days.

"Disappear?"

"Professionally."

The job was a survey escort.

Two cargo carts carrying markers and tools.

One records clerk.

Two Bronze requested because the route crossed several lightly maintained stretches and one district boundary whose paperwork had apparently become a feud.

Twelve days.

Not three.

I checked my life before answering.

Rent paid ahead enough.

No filtration meeting I had promised.

Noll no longer depended on our hallway for friendship.

No letter required a body in Carrow.

The Guild could survive my absence.

That last one had always been true.

My life could tolerate twelve days away.

That felt larger than the job.

"Yes."

Jorren nodded.

"Good. I already said probably."

"Professional."

"I learn from you."

We left three mornings later...
