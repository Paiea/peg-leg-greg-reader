# Chapter 132: The Drying House

We slept beside a salt store where the rafters dripped on purpose.

This required explanation.

Braided reed cords hung from the ceiling in long loops. Workers ran their hands down them every few hours and squeezed clear water into buckets.

The storage air stayed dry enough that the salt did not cake.

Not a ward like Meren's.

Not any support method I knew.

"What do you call it?" I asked the forewoman.

"Drying."

"The magic."

She looked at me.

"Drying."

Corrected.

One worker replaced a frayed cord while another checked the wall vents.

Magic was part of the building's maintenance, not a lecture waiting for an adventurer.

I helped carry buckets because that required no theory...
