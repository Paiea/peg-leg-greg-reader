# Chapter 137: The Notice

The Guild had sent a note while I was east.

Not urgent.

That was the first line.

I appreciated it.

The rest concerned Silver assessment scheduling.

I had filed the application before the long job. The Guild had found a window while I was east.

The assessment would not be a duel.

It would not be a spell demonstration arranged for convenience.

One assessor would review current work history, then observe me during ordinary eligible field work selected from available contracts.

No guarantee Jorren would be present.

No guarantee support magic would matter.

No guarantee sword work would matter.

Assessment through life rather than stagecraft.

I liked the institution more immediately.

The proposed window began in nine days.

I was tired enough from twelve days east that nine sounded excellent...
