# Chapter 138: The Window

The assessment packet had three pages.

Two were administrative.

The third was worse because it was useful.

**Candidate is responsible for ordinary contract obligations. Assessment does not replace client authority, site safety, or lead-worker ownership.**

Good.

**Assessor may decline to evaluate a situation that becomes unsuitable for assessment conditions.**

Better.

**Passing requires sufficient evidence. Lack of dramatic incident is not grounds for automatic rescheduling.**

Best.

I could apparently become Silver while nothing exciting happened.

Or fail while nothing exciting happened.

Professional advancement had escaped theatre.

I put the packet beside my field book.

Then spent the afternoon cleaning road grit from my sword belt because the assessment window had not changed the fact that leather cracked if neglected...
