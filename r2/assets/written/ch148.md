# Chapter 148: The Two Bronze

Pevi looked at my Silver cord for a long time.

"Fast."

"Not from inside."

"Still fast from here."

Miko Serr was younger than both of us and had the alarming posture of someone prepared to obey instructions before understanding them.

South Market Warehouse Two needed three damaged lane barriers reset after a delivery cart clipped the old posts.

The client clerk wanted freight moving around us.

Pevi wanted the west lane closed entirely.

I thought one lane could stay open.

We measured.

I was wrong.

The first regional wagon that came through had a rear axle wider than the local carts I had been picturing.

Pevi caught it before I did.

"That wheel takes your chalk."

It would.

"Close the west lane."

He looked at my cord again.

"Silver."

"Yes."

"Excellent work."

Sarcasm survived rank advancement.

We moved the chalk line and closed the lane.

Miko started carrying the first barrier post before the warehouse carpenter had cleared the broken base.

"Wait."

He stopped mid-lift.

"Put it down."

He did.

Pevi watched me.

"You can explain why."

Right.

"Broken iron in the socket. Carpenter clears it first."

Miko nodded.

Better.

We reset two posts before lunch.

The third needed a new sleeve, which the warehouse did not have.

The clerk wanted us to improvise wood packing.

Pevi said it would last a week.

The carpenter said maybe two.

I refused to sign a permanent barrier on temporary packing.

The clerk called me expensive.

Pevi called me something quieter.

We left the third lane blocked and ordered the sleeve...
