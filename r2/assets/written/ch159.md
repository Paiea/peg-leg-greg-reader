# Chapter 159: The Warm Wall

The warm wall did not hide a room.

It hid older masonry.

After the barrels moved, Issen found a vertical seam where newer cellar stone had been built against a broader older arch.

Not an opening we could use.

A shape.

The mineral bloom tested slightly salty.

Issen thought old drainage or heat-return infrastructure.

A city clerk named Renn thought the arch was an abandoned service conduit from a pre-fire district plan.

I remembered a later tunnel farther east.

Everyone disagreed by several yards.

"You remember an opening?" Issen asked.

"Later. East of here. Maybe."

"How much later?"

"Decades."

She snorted.

"Useful like weather next year."

Fair.

We measured the arch.

Then the soap merchant announced we had used our authorized hour and wanted everyone out.

Renn argued the order allowed two.

The merchant produced his own copy.

One hour.

Renn's copy was wrong.

We left.

The wall remained warm behind barrels and paperwork...
