# Chapter 160: The Wrong Lead

Issen let me be wrong without being polite about it.

The next access order opened a second cellar east of the mill.

I expected the old masonry to continue there.

It did not.

Newer foundation. Cold stone. No seam.

"Your later tunnel?" Issen asked.

"Apparently not here."

"Historic."

Lera would have liked her.

Renn wanted to dig a test pit anyway because the district plan showed a line under the lane.

Issen refused without utility clearance.

They argued for fifteen minutes.

Both knew more than I did.

Neither convinced the other.

The work stopped pending records from the water office.

I had wanted the thread to become visible because I remembered enough to be curious.

It did not.

Issen handed me the measuring rod.

"Carry this upstairs. Your future can wait."

I carried it...
