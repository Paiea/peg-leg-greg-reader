# Chapter 163: The Silver Board

The Silver board contained better pay and worse signatures.

I learned that after three weeks of reading it as if the numbers were the important part.

Some jobs wanted force I did not have.

Some wanted a lead who could coordinate Bronze hands through ordinary work.

Some wanted someone willing to sign that a site had been left safe enough for the client to resume operations.

That last sentence paid more because it could become expensive later.

Sella saw me reading.

"You wanted Silver."

"I wanted the word. I am discovering the verbs."

"Too late."

I took a two-day warehouse access job with two Bronze and a client mechanic.

Not because it paid the most.

Because the mechanic had already written the work plan and the responsibility boundary was clear.

Sella stamped it.

"Dinner plans?"

"Noll after close tomorrow."

"Then finish on time."

I took the stamped contract home...
