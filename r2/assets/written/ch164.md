# Chapter 164: The Missed Dinner

The warehouse job finished on time.

The closeout did not.

The client mechanic found a cracked anchor plate during final inspection and refused to reopen the access lane.

Correctly.

The warehouse manager wanted it reopened anyway because night freight was already waiting.

Also understandably.

My signature sat on the line that would say the Guild portion was complete.

If I signed before the anchor issue was resolved, the manager would treat the lane as open.

If I refused, two night carts would wait until morning.

Noll's dinner waited somewhere else entirely.

I refused to sign.

The manager called a senior clerk.

The clerk called the Guild.

The Guild called nobody because the Guild office was closing.

We spent two hours writing an interim hold the manager hated and the mechanic accepted.

By the time I reached The Brass Spoon, chairs were already upside down.

Noll was counting spoons.

"You missed food."

"I know."

"I saved some."

Relief arrived too early.

"Then Domm ate it."

"Of course he did."

Noll kept counting.

I stood there waiting for him to say it was fine.

He did not.

"I should have sent word."

"Yes."

"I couldn't leave."

"You could send a runner."

Also yes.

No professional defense fixed the social mistake.

I helped stack the last chairs while he stayed annoyed with me...
