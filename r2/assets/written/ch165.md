# Chapter 165: The Inventory

Noll stayed annoyed for two days.

This was inconvenient because I had apologized correctly.

Apparently correctness was not the full transaction.

On the third night I went to The Brass Spoon before close instead of after.

Noll looked at me, looked at the dining room, and handed me a crate of onions.

"Those go down."

"Hello to you too."

"You know where down is."

I did.

The cellar smelled like vinegar, onions, old wood, and one sack of potatoes that had begun negotiating with death.

When I came back up, a junior kitchen hand was trying to slide a chipped bowl behind the clean stack.

Noll saw it.

"No."

The boy froze.

"It still holds soup."

"Then it can hold soup at your house. Not here. Put it in breakage."

The boy looked unhappy.

Noll looked more tired than angry.

He had a ring of three keys clipped to his belt now.

Closing lead.

Inventory.

Breakage.

The boring parts of authority had found him.

"You want help?" I asked.

"You are helping. Onions."

"I meant with the count."

"I know what you meant."

He gave me a slate anyway.

We counted oil, vinegar, dried beans, salt, flour, smoked fish, and spoons.

The spoon count was wrong.

Again.

"Domm," Noll said.

"You know that?"

"I know him."

At the end of the night he locked the rear door, checked it twice, and handed the key ring back to the owner.

"Next Thursday," he said.

"Dinner?"

"If you can manage it."

"I can send a runner if the Guild traps me in a warehouse."

"Excellent. Civilization advances."

He was still a little annoyed.

I preferred that to being forgiven on schedule...
