# Chapter 168: The Ditch Hand

Doss believed three things about drainage.

Water went downhill.

People lied about where they had put it.

And the north ditch beside Marker Seventeen hated iron after rain.

The first two were engineering.

The third sounded like superstition until Mira put a probe rod into the wet bank and blue-white sparks crawled three inches up the shaft.

She let go.

"That," Doss said, "is why we use wood there."

Varin swore.

"The map says no charged bed within forty yards."

Doss shrugged.

"Map doesn't sleep here."

Varin did not enjoy this answer.

He also did not put the iron rod back.

Competence survived irritation.

We switched to ash probes.

The bank held old low charge after storms, probably from a buried line or mineral seam Varin's records did not include.

I could feel the load shift against a small plane if I held one near the wet cut.

Not dangerous by itself.

Potentially unpleasant if somebody drove a long iron stake through the wrong place.

Doss showed us three dark notches on an older wooden marker.

"Those mean don't be clever."

"Official city notation?" I asked.

"Official me notation."

Varin made a noise through his nose.

"You cannot maintain municipal records by scratching warnings into rotten posts."

"Been working."

"That does not make it a record system."

Both men were right enough to remain angry.

I marked the location on the Guild sheet and let Varin argue Doss into drawing it properly.

Doss's drawing looked like a dying spider.

Varin stared at it.

"This is why literacy was invented."

"Water can read it."

Mira had to turn away so they would not see her laugh...
