# Chapter 169: The Long Way

On the second day the road split around a low ridge.

I knew the right fork.

Or I knew it later.

Stone causeway.

Good drainage.

Fast enough that old Greg had used it without thinking.

Current Varin stopped at the fork and swore.

The right road was open.

The left road had a toll post.

"Right is shorter," he said.

Doss shook his head.

"Not with the survey cart."

"It is dry."

"Top is dry."

Varin looked at me.

Silver cord had become an invitation to disappoint people.

I walked thirty yards down the right fork.

The packed gravel I remembered was there in pieces.

So was a wagon rut deep enough to hide my boot.

The road had not received the later stone bed yet.

I came back.

"Left."

Varin's jaw tightened.

"Toll is six copper for the cart."

"Yes."

"City will not reimburse it without a closure notice."

"Then don't ask me. Ask Doss."

Doss spat into the ditch.

"Left."

Varin paid the toll from the field purse.

He wrote my name beside it.

"If the right road is fine on return, I'm charging this to Guild caution."

"Fair."

It was not a threat.

It was accounting.

We took the longer road.

Nothing collapsed behind us to prove me wise.

No wagon appeared overturned in the rut.

No divine hand reimbursed six copper.

The left fork was simply slower, drier underneath, and expensive...
