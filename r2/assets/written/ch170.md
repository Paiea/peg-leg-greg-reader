# Chapter 170: The Watch List

That night I opened my old notebook beside the inn stove.

Most of it had become current-life work.

Rates.

Names.

A sketch of Lera's foot placement.

Filter dimensions.

Mara's route marks.

Jorren's terrible spelling where he had borrowed the page.

Old future notes lived farther back.

I found the west road.

**RIGHT FORK STONED. RELIABLE.**

No date.

Of course no date.

Old me had known things the way people knew where their own kitchen was.

Nobody wrote down when the kitchen became a kitchen.

I added one word beneath it.

**WATCH.**

Doss came downstairs carrying two mugs.

He set one beside me and looked at the page.

"Gambling?"

"Poorly."

"You write down losses?"

"Sometimes."

He pointed at WATCH.

"Good system."

"You don't know what it means."

"Then I can't disagree."

He drank half his beer standing up.

"Knee bothering you?"

"Not yet."

"That means yes tomorrow."

He went back upstairs.

I closed the notebook before he could become correct twice...
