# Chapter 175: The Freight Word

Tavin's letter arrived folded around a freight receipt.

Not mine.

His.

**Rell Ford now has me managing outbound yard two days a week because apparently competence is punished with chairs and signatures.**

I approved of the diagnosis.

He wrote about three damaged axle boxes, a merchant who believed wet canvas was a moral failure, and one driver who had tried to declare a mule as empty cargo.

Then:

**Saw your name on a Guild hold form attached to a Carrow warehouse packet. Not our yard. Yet. Your people use too many words. If work ever drags you here, I intend to complain professionally.**

Tavin had acquired a yard and paperwork.

I wrote back:

**Congratulations on the chair. Sorry about the signatures. The mule was correct.**

Then I added:

**I also use too many words now. They pay me more for some of them.**

I almost wrote that I missed him.

Instead I wrote:

**Try not to become important. Jorren already failed.**

That was close enough for the mail...
