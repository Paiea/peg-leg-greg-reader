# Chapter 176: The Return

Lera came back from Dorrin because Dorrin stopped paying her.

This was more satisfying than fate.

I found her in the Guild yard at seven in the morning arguing with the rack steward about practice blades.

"These are soft," she said.

"They are training steel."

"They are decorative disappointment."

The steward saw me.

"Silver Greg. Tell her."

Lera turned.

Her eyes went to the cord at my shoulder.

Then back to my face.

"You got promoted while I was getting rained on."

"Apparently."

"Annoying."

"Welcome home."

She hugged me once, hard, then pulled back and pinched the Silver cord between two fingers.

"This mean you're expensive now?"

"Sometimes."

"Good. Lesson price went up."

"That's not how promotion works."

"Worked immediately."

Dorrin had paid her better than Carrow.

Dorrin watch training had also taught her to hate clean drills.

"People shout," she said. "Doors open. Civilians walk where they're stupid. Rain exists. Carrow yard teaches as if everyone fights in a dry box."

"You sound happy."

"I made money. Different thing."

She picked up one of the soft practice swords.

"Tomorrow. Same hour. Bring bruises you don't mind replacing."

Then she resumed insulting the rack steward...
