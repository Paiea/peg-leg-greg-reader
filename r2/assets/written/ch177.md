# Chapter 177: The Interruption

Lera's new drill had rules until it started.

Then other people were allowed to ruin them.

I cut.

Recovered.

Reset.

A junior yard hand walked across the edge of my line carrying three helmets.

I stopped.

Lera hit my forearm.

"Dead."

"There was a person."

"Yes. People are famously present."

Second run.

Cut.

Recover.

Someone behind me shouted, "LEFT!"

I turned.

Lera hit my shoulder.

"Dead."

"That was a call."

"From a man watching the wrong pair."

Third run.

A bucket dropped.

I ignored it.

Wrong choice.

Water spread under my lead foot and the next recovery slid six inches.

Lera did not hit me.

She watched me catch balance.

"Again."

By the sixth run my old instincts were angry because none of the interruptions respected combat grammar.

That was probably the point.

Probably.

Lera had not asked my opinion.

At the end she checked the bruise on my forearm with one thumb.

"Silver didn't fix that."

"Deeply disappointing."

"Tomorrow."

"You just got back."

"And already you're whining. Carrow feels normal."

She walked away before I could turn the morning into a lesson...
