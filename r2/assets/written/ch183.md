# Chapter 183: Rell Ford

Work took me to Rell Ford before friendship did.

A freight-yard brace had twisted under a loaded transfer frame.

The yard wanted Guild support while their mechanic unloaded tension and replaced two iron pins.

Three-day job.

Silver lead.

Two Bronze.

Client authority over freight sequencing.

I accepted before I knew which yard.

Tavin was standing under the gate when we arrived.

He had a clipboard too.

Apparently this disease was spreading.

"Absolutely not," he said.

"Hello."

"You are the Guild Silver?"

"Apparently."

"I specifically requested someone reasonable."

The client mechanic looked between us.

"You know each other?"

"Unfortunately," Tavin said.

Then he hugged me.

Once.

Hard.

Then stepped back and pointed at the freight frame.

"If your people write HOLD across outbound two, I lose half a day."

"If outbound two is unsafe, you lose half a day."

"You haven't looked yet."

"Then why are we arguing?"

"Practice."
