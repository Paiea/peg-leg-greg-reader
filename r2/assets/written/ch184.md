# Chapter 184: The Wording

The frame was safe unloaded.

Unsafe under full transfer weight.

Tavin wanted the closeout to say **restricted use**.

I wanted **no loaded transfer until pin replacement and retest**.

The mechanic wanted everyone to stop talking and let her work.

She won for forty minutes.

Afterward Tavin read my draft.

"This blocks the whole lane."

"Only loaded transfer."

"Yard clerk will read the first four words and block the whole lane."

"Then train your clerk."

His face changed.

Small change.

Bad change.

"You don't manage this yard."

"I manage the Guild condition."

"And I manage twelve carts waiting behind your condition."

Both of us had become professionally correct in ways that annoyed the other.

The mechanic leaned against the frame.

"Can I suggest a sentence before the childhood friendship becomes a transport delay?"

"We met as adults," I said.

"Worse."

She rewrote the line:

**LANE OPEN FOR EMPTY PASSAGE. LOADED TRANSFER PROHIBITED UNTIL PIN REPLACEMENT AND RETEST.**

Tavin liked it.

I liked it.

This irritated me.

"Fine," I said.

"Fine," Tavin said.

We signed without forgiving each other for the previous ten minutes...
