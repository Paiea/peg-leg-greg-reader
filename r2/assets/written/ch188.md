# Chapter 188: The Keys

Noll had his own key ring now.

Not borrowed at close.

His.

Three keys.

Rear door.

Dry store.

Waste cabinet.

The last one amused me until I watched him use it.

A junior cook had been hiding spoiled greens under fresh stock to avoid recording waste.

Noll found them.

The cook was sixteen and nearly cried.

"I thought Domm would dock me."

"He might," Noll said.

"Then why would I write it?"

Noll rubbed both hands over his face.

There was no correct answer that made the boy happier.

"Because if rotten food goes into service, somebody gets sick and all of us lose more than greens. Write the waste. I'll talk to Domm."

"You'll tell him?"

"Yes."

The boy hated that too.

Noll wrote the entry himself.

Later he told me, "Authority is mostly having the key to the cabinet everybody wishes didn't exist."

"Guild has those."

"Of course it does."

He locked the waste cabinet and went back to work...
