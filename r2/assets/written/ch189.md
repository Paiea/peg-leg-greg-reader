# Chapter 189: The Offer

Near the end of Jorren's original eight-week appointment, the road office offered him another twelve weeks.

More pay.

Same field-coordinator title.

One additional crew.

Authority to approve routine material substitutions inside city spec.

Still no Guild Silver.

Still no city-engineer signature.

He brought the letter to my room and put it beside his unsigned Silver application.

"Thoughts?"

"You want Guild work or road work?"

"Helpful."

"You asked."

He read the road offer again.

"I like the work."

"Then that's information."

"I also like not being owned by the city."

"Also information."

"You became Silver and got worse at advice."

"Costs more now too."

He laughed.

Then stopped.

The city offer expired in three days.

The Guild application expired whenever he got tired of carrying it.

For once there was no clean ladder in front of him.

Just two kinds of work and one person who had to choose...
