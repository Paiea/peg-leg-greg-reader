# Chapter 191: The Heating File

The water office found East Mill under heating.

Issen brought the folder to the Guild Annex and dropped it on the table hard enough to move Renn's inkpot.

"Municipal scholarship," she said.

Renn opened the cover.

**EAST MILL AUXILIARY SERVICE. HEAT / WATER / RETURN.**

Three pages had mildew.

One had been eaten along the corner by something with administrative taste.

The plan beneath them was older than the mill's current cellar walls.

A narrow service line crossed under the east foundation, turned beneath what was now a lane, and continued through three parcels that had not existed as separate properties when the plan was drawn.

"Tunnel," Renn said.

Issen pointed at the scale.

"Conduit."

"People can crawl."

"Small people."

"Useful people."

"No."

Renn looked at me.

Silver had apparently made me eligible for bad alliances.

"I prefer not crawling into municipal infrastructure that has spent seventy years being forgotten."

"Coward."

"Alive coward."

Issen tapped three current parcel numbers she had written in the margin.

"Before either of you discovers heroism, we need access permission."

One parcel belonged to East Mill.

One belonged to a cooper's warehouse.

One belonged to a woman named Hara Venn who had already sent the city a written complaint about surveyors leaning ladders against her wall.

Renn closed the folder.

"How long?"

Issen smiled without warmth.

"Property law is not measured in useful units."

The old warm wall had finally produced an answer.

The answer was paperwork...
