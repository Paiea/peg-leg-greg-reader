# Chapter 193: The Warm Joint

Hara's cellar was cold.

One stone joint was not.

Not hot.

Not glowing.

Warm enough that wet cloth dried there first.

Renn tested that twice because he enjoyed irritating property owners professionally.

Hara stood with her arms crossed.

"If that cloth stains my floor, I charge you."

"It's water."

"Water stains everything eventually."

Issen had marked the old conduit line from the plan.

The warm joint sat almost exactly above it.

Almost.

Renn used a wooden mallet on the adjacent masonry while I held a small plane across the exposed seam.

Tap.

Nothing.

Tap.

A faint sideways load pressed along the plane instead of into it.

I shifted the angle.

Tap.

There again.

Not force traveling through open air.

Something in the joint was carrying pressure along connection.

Kesra's tension sink came to mind.

That meant almost nothing.

Kesra had shown me one professional effect months ago. Similar behavior did not make this the same mechanism, any more than two wheels made every cart the same vehicle.

Renn watched my hand.

"You felt something."

"Yes."

"What?"

"Load moving sideways."

"Magic?"

"Probably."

He frowned.

"Useful?"

"Not yet."

Hara snorted.

"Excellent. You people have discovered warm stone."

She checked the clock on the shelf.

"Seventy-three minutes left."

Renn looked offended by time...
