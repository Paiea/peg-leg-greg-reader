# Chapter 196: The Four Batches

Arlo found a kiln that could be rented without buying one.

This made him unbearable for half a morning.

"Four firing slots," he said. "Hesk Street pottery yard. Same clay range. We move green chambers there, fire, cool, bring them back."

Pellin looked at him.

"Move green chambers."

"Carefully."

"Across two streets."

"Three."

"Better."

Ivena took the paper from Arlo.

Three-month arrangement.

Pay by batch.

No equipment purchase.

No minimum after the first four firings.

She tapped the number four.

"This many. Then we decide whether we learned anything worth paying for."

Arlo opened his mouth.

"Four," she repeated.

Pellin wanted transport trays before the first load.

Dena wanted to know who was carrying them.

Senn wanted the outside yard to use our batch numbers.

The pottery owner wanted nobody to touch her cooling shelves before she said so.

I wanted Arlo to stop smiling like he had won.

"This isn't the kiln," I said.

"It's more kiln."

"Temporary kiln."

"Still hot."
