# Chapter 197: The Other Cooling

The first outside batch came back perfect.

Arlo behaved badly about this.

Not cruelly.

Triumphantly.

Pellin ignored him.

The second batch cracked two lower collars.

Pellin behaved badly about that.

The problem was not drying time.

Both damaged chambers had passed Pellin's hold marks.

The outside pottery yard cooled faster than ours because their kiln room vented straight into an alley.

Arlo blamed the other yard.

The other yard owner blamed our clay thickness.

Pellin blamed Arlo's face.

Ivena asked what changed if we slowed the first two hours of cooling.

The pottery owner said it would cost one copper extra per batch because the shelf stayed occupied longer.

Arlo objected.

Pellin offered to pay the copper personally if Arlo promised silence.

Arlo almost accepted.

Ivena approved the slower cool for batch three...
