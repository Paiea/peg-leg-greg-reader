# Chapter 198: The Missing Empty

Senn found the missing crate before anyone found the crate.

North mill said Hesk had collected it.

Hesk had a signed receipt saying he had collected six empties.

The shop had five.

Dena accused him of inventing arithmetic.

Hesk produced the receipt and sat down to enjoy himself.

Senn traced the handoff marks.

Six left the mill.

Six reached North Exchange yard.

Five were loaded onto Hesk's return cart.

One had been stacked with flour boxes during a rain shuffle.

"So the receipt is wrong," Dena said.

"The receipt is accurate at the first handoff," Senn said.

"Then useless."

"Incomplete."

"Fancy useless."

Recovery cost six copper because the exchange yard had already moved the crate into bonded storage.

Hesk refused to pay.

Correctly.

The mill refused to pay.

Understandably.

We paid because the crate was ours and six copper was cheaper than spending three days discovering a moral principle.

Senn added a second carrier-count line to the transfer sheet.

Paperwork improved after failing in a new way...
