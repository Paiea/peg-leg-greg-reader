# Chapter 199: The Junior Close

Noll gave Miko the dry-store key for one close.

Miko was the same junior cook who had hidden spoiled greens months ago.

He had stopped hiding waste.

He had not stopped being sixteen.

"You're sure?" I asked.

Noll looked at me.

"No."

That was better than a speech about trust.

Miko counted sacks, checked the lower vent, locked the rear shelf, and forgot the upper vent.

Noll found it before they left.

"Again."

Miko groaned.

"Everything else is done."

"Upper vent."

"It's cold tonight."

"Upper vent."

"Domm leaves it closed sometimes."

Noll's mouth tightened.

"Then Domm can sign your close."

Miko opened the vent.

He was angry enough to do it loudly.

Noll gave him the key again the next night.

"Still sure?" I asked.

"Still no."

Miko heard us.

"I hate both of you."

"Check the vent," Noll said.

The key stayed in Miko's hand...
