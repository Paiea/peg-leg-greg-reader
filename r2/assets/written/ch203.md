# Chapter 203: The South Reach

The South Reach ferry contract was two days of boring support work.

This made me suspicious only because I had become professionally damaged.

The ferry company was replacing an approach rail and checking two low-charge guide posts before autumn freight increased.

Silver support.

One Bronze.

Local ferry engineer in charge of everything I did not own.

Her name was Tessa Coil.

She had forearms like rope and no patience for Guild people who called the river predictable.

"It isn't," she said before I had said anything.

"I agree."

"Good. Saves time."

The Bronze was a nineteen-year-old named Henn who had never worked a ferry and admitted it immediately.

Tessa liked him more for that.

We replaced the first guide-post collar before noon.

At lunch I looked upstream.

A spill channel cut through the north bank farther than I remembered.

Newer work.

Jorren had mentioned similar drainage politics west of Carrow.

"When did they cut that?" I asked.

Tessa followed my gaze.

"Two summers back. After the petition."

"Helps?"

"Sometimes."

"With flood approach?"

She gave me a look.

"That is what channels usually hope to do."

I accepted the correction...
