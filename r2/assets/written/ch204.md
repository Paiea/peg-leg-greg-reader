# Chapter 204: The Open Ferry

Rain came that night.

Then more.

By dawn the South Reach was brown and loud enough that conversation required commitment.

Old Greg remembered this ferry closing in late-summer flood.

Not maybe.

I remembered sleeping in a freight shed because the landing had been underwater.

I remembered paying too much for bad stew.

I remembered the ferryman's daughter cheating three adventurers at cards while we waited two days.

Current Tessa walked the north approach at first light.

Water had filled the spill channel.

The main landing was wet.

Not underwater.

She drove one marker deeper, checked the guide posts, and opened passenger crossing on reduced load.

Freight waited until noon.

Then freight crossed too.

I stood beside the rail longer than I needed to.

Henn noticed.

"You hate ferries?"

"Historically."

"That's specific."

A cart rolled aboard behind him.

No closure.

No freight shed.

No bad stew.

Maybe the flood I remembered came another year.

Maybe another storm.

Maybe this channel had changed it.

Maybe the story I remembered as my life had already stopped being a clean comparison.

Tessa shouted for me to stop staring at water and help Henn with the south guide.

I went...
