# Chapter 207: The Wet Return

The south linen carrier refused a used chamber at the bathhouse.

Not because it was broken.

Because it was wet.

Dena held the wrapped ceramic under one arm.

"It's not leaking."

The carrier, a woman named Pera Sol, looked at the linen bundles behind her.

"Wet."

"Wrapped."

"Wet wrapped."

"That is more wrapped."

Pera did not move.

The bathhouse manager watched from the doorway with the expression of a man who had successfully made a problem belong to three other people.

Senn checked the route sheet.

Outbound condition.

Customer signature.

Return count.

No return-state rule.

"We can add one," he said.

Pera pointed at the cart.

"You can add twelve. The wet thing is not going beside clean linen."

Dena looked at me.

I had ownership.

I also had no cart.

"She's right about the linen."

"Traitor."

"Professionally."

We carried the chamber back ourselves.

Halfway home Dena announced that this was proof the company needed a horse.

"It proves we need a dry return."

"Horse can be dry."

"Horse can be wet."

"Bad horse."

The next morning Pellin built a cheap slatted draining stand for the bathhouse service room.

Pera approved nothing.

She said she would reconsider after the first dry return.

Dena wrote **PERSUADE LINEN TYRANT** on the route sheet.

Senn crossed out **TYRANT**.

Pera saw both versions the following week...
