# Chapter 217: The New Arrow

Mara's next letter came from east of the place I expected.

Not Halden.

Not west.

East.

A damaged bridge had pushed one Vey account onto river packet work, and Mara had followed the paperwork because apparently that was a profession now.

She hated the packet-office chair.

She liked the clerk who knew which captains lied about departure times.

She had learned enough river tariffs to become dangerous.

**You would hate this desk. It moves every time somebody drags the rain bucket underneath it.**

I put the letter beside the current map.

There was no arrow waiting for her.

I drew one after reading.

Then corrected it because the river bend on the current atlas was farther south than memory wanted.

The map looked worse...
