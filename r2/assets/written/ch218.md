# Chapter 218: The Ridge Contract

The contract wanted five days and one Silver.

That was enough to make me suspicious of the noun.

**NORTH RIDGE IRRIGATION DISTRICT — GATE BRACE INSPECTION / TEMPORARY SUPPORT / WATER-TURN CONTINUITY.**

Sella tapped the second line.

"They have engineers."

"Comforting."

"You are not the engineer."

"More comforting."

The district had six hillside reservoirs feeding orchards and two grain terraces north of Carrow.

Old Greg remembered riding through the district later when half the upper channels had been stone-lined.

Current map showed dirt cuts, timber gates, and one maintenance road that disappeared beneath a coffee stain in the Guild copy.

The district engineer was Sava Kern.

She met me at the lower reservoir with mud to both knees and a pencil behind each ear.

"Silver?"

"Greg."

"Good. Silver is paperwork. Greg can carry that."

She handed me a coil of brace cord.

A canal tender named Ollo laughed into his sleeve.

I carried the cord.

The first gate had a charged timber brace that needed unloading while two carpenters replaced the lower shoe.

Sava owned the gate.

Ollo owned the water turn.

The carpenters owned the timber.

I owned a five-inch plane under one ugly corner while everybody else argued above me.

At noon Sava checked the temporary brace and said, "Fine. Tomorrow we do the west gate."

"Anything interesting there?"

"Peaches."

"The gate grows peaches?"

"The farmer does. He thinks that makes him an engineer."

Ollo laughed again.

Five days had begun...
