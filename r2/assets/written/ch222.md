# Chapter 222: The Stable Room

The district lodging was above a stable.

The horses had opinions about sleep.

One kicked a stall wall at irregular intervals all night.

Another breathed directly beneath my floorboards like a large damp bellows.

By morning my left knee had stiffened from two days of hillside work and one mattress designed by a carpenter with unresolved anger.

Nothing injured.

Everything old enough to complain.

Sava noticed me taking the stairs sideways.

"Problem?"

"Stairs."

"They were here yesterday."

"I liked them less today."

She handed my field bag to Ollo.

I reached for it.

Ollo moved it away.

"Engineer said."

"Engineer owns water."

Sava said, "Engineer also owns whether the Silver hand arrives at reservoir six before lunch."

"That feels jurisdictionally ambitious."

"File a complaint."

I carried the lighter brace coil instead.

Nobody discussed accepting help.

Ollo complained that my bag contained too many pencils for a man who wrote so little.

I told him two belonged to Sava.

She checked behind both ears.

One pencil was missing.

Ollo had it.

The morning improved...
