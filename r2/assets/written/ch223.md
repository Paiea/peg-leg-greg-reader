# Chapter 223: The Gate That Stayed Shut

Reservoir six did not need me.

The gate shoe was cracked.

That part was true.

The charged brace was old.

Also true.

But the replacement timber had not arrived.

A cart had thrown a wheel three miles south.

Sava looked at the empty road.

Then at the gate.

Then at the water board.

"We close the east turn early."

A grain farmer named Pell Darr objected before Ollo finished carrying the message.

"My lower terrace gets another hour."

"Not today."

"Contract says sunset."

"Gate says no."

"Gate doesn't sign contracts."

"Neither does a flood."

Pell looked at me.

I remained beautifully irrelevant.

The replacement cart did not arrive by noon.

Or two.

At three Sava cancelled the repair.

Ollo shifted the remaining water turns.

Pell lost forty minutes of irrigation.

He wrote a complaint to the district office while sitting on an overturned bucket.

Sava helped him spell the engineer's name correctly at the top.

"You are assisting?" I asked.

"I want them to know exactly who ruined his afternoon."

Pell heard her.

"You did."

"See? Agreement."

We spent the rest of the day securing a gate we had not fixed.

My Silver contract still paid because remaining available had been part of the work.

Pell's barley did not care...
