# Chapter 226: The Quiet Work

For six days nothing I remembered tried to become uncertain.

This was restful.

I did two Silver support jobs.

One warehouse brace replacement.

One mill intake inspection.

Both used old experience in boring ways.

A pulley rope showed interior weathering before the client loaded it.

A mill apprentice had set a temporary anchor too close to a wet return trench.

Neither discovery changed history.

Neither required prophecy.

The warehouse bought new rope.

The mill moved the anchor.

I ate lunch with Noll twice.

Lost three copper to Jorren at cards once.

Read Mara's last letter again without drawing anything new on the map.

WATCH stayed closed in my field book.

That felt important enough that I refused to make it important...
