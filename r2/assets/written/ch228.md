# Chapter 228: The Use Letter

Bessa wanted the city to answer a smaller question.

Not what the old line was.

Whether she could keep hanging wet laundry on it.

Issen arrived with a one-page temporary-use letter.

Bessa read it twice beside the warm strip.

**CURRENT ORDINARY CONTACT USE MAY CONTINUE. NO CUTTING. NO DRILLING. NO NEW FIXTURES PENDING SERVICE-LINE REVIEW.**

"Aprons yes."

"Yes."

"Existing hook?"

Issen looked at the iron hook already mortared into the wall.

"Existing hook stays."

"Shelf?"

"New shelf?"

"New shelf."

"No."

Bessa folded the letter.

"Annoying."

"That means you understood it."

Renn was standing three feet away with two measuring rods and the expression of a man watching civilization waste an ancient mystery on laundry.

"We still don't know what the line does," he said.

Bessa handed him a wet apron.

"Then use the mystery."

He hung it on the existing hook.

The apron began drying faster than the one beside it.

The city had not explained the wall.

It had explained the hook...
