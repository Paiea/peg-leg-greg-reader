# Chapter 230: The Transfer Line

The six-line sheet failed without gaining a seventh line.

North Exchange signed a crate out dry and whole.

Then rain started.

A loader moved the crate after signing.

The next carrier received it with one crushed corner under a tarp.

Nobody had lied.

That was the irritating part.

Senn laid the sheet on Ivena's table.

"Damage happened after transfer sign."

Dena leaned over his shoulder.

"Add **AFTER THEY SWORE IT WAS FINE**."

"No."

"Large letters."

"No."

Senn picked up a pen.

Ivena took it out of his hand.

"If you add a seventh line, I burn the form."

"I wasn't adding."

"You were thinking in ink."

They changed one boundary instead.

**TRANSFER COUNT / SIGN** became:

**TRANSFER OUT**

**RECEIVED NEXT**

Same space.

Two moments.

The next sheet came back with both initials and a coffee stain through the customer line.

Senn pinned it beside the first ugly sheet.

"Better?" I asked.

"Different failure surface."

Dena groaned.

"He learned that phrase from you people."

I denied responsibility...
