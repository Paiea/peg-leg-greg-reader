# Chapter 231: The Fever Close

Noll had a fever and still tried to stand in the kitchen.

Domm solved this by pointing upstairs.

"Bed."

"Lunch order."

"Bed."

"Dry store."

"Miko."

Noll looked at Miko.

Miko looked offended before anyone assigned him anything.

"I can close."

"You forgot oil."

"Once."

"Last month."

"Which is not today."

Domm coughed theatrically into Noll's direction.

Noll went upstairs.

That night Miko closed the dry store without him.

Salt counted.

Oil counted.

Upper vent open.

Rear shelf locked.

Waste total missing.

The kitchen still existed in the morning.

Noll discovered the missing number from bed because apparently fever did not reduce his ability to be irritated by ledgers.

Miko carried the book upstairs himself.

"I forgot it."

"Yes."

"I can go fix it."

"Yes."

Miko waited.

Noll did not give a speech.

Miko went back downstairs.

Twenty minutes later the waste total existed.

Noll remained sick...
