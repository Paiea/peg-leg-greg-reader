# Chapter 234: The Service Margin

The old heating file had maintenance notes.

Renn had been reading the diagrams.

Olin read the margins.

That was why he found them first.

**AUTUMN FLUSH COMPLETE.**

**RETURN WARMTH BELOW MINIMUM. CHECK EAST VALVE.**

**PRESSURE HOLD PASSED AFTER SEAL REPACK.**

Maintenance handwriting.

Renn read the second line twice.

"Return warmth."

Issen said, "Words."

"Useful words."

"Potentially."

Olin put a finger on the seasonal flush note.

"Whatever this was, they expected it to clog or settle."

"Heat-transfer loop," Renn said.

"Maybe."

"Charged heat-transfer loop."

"Still maybe."

I looked at the old dates.

Somebody had once known what failing service looked like.

Renn smiled.

"Now we find the east valve."

Issen closed the file.

"Now we find who owns the parcel where the east valve might be."

Renn stopped smiling...
