# Chapter 235: The Open Spur

Jorren reopened a road without telling me.

I found out from a fruit seller who was angry about it.

"West spur is open again."

"Good?"

"No."

"Why?"

"People stopped using my market shortcut."

Apparently infrastructure improvement had enemies.

The city notice board said:

**WEST SPUR — FULL LOAD RESTORED.**

Engineer signature.

Below it:

**FIELD COORDINATION — JORREN VELL.**

I had not known the repair was scheduled.

That afternoon Jorren came into the Annex with mud dried up one sleeve.

"Your road opened."

"City's road."

"You ruined a fruit seller's commerce."

"Which one?"

I described her.

"She owes the road office two copper for a barrier cone."

"That explains her politics."

He took my water without asking.

"What did you do?"

"Listened to a bridge pin."

"Did it say anything?"

"Not to me."

"Good pin."

Neither of us mentioned the folded Silver application...
