# Chapter 239: The Friday No

Miko told Noll no.

Not angrily.

That made it harder.

"Can't close Friday."

Noll looked up from the order book.

"Why?"

"My sister's naming dinner."

"You knew that yesterday?"

"Yes."

"And today?"

"Also yes."

Noll closed his eyes.

"When were you planning to tell me?"

"Before Friday."

"Technically excellent."

Miko looked at the floor.

"I can close Saturday."

Noll wanted Friday off.

I knew because he had mentioned it twice and complained about it three times.

"Go," he said.

Miko looked up.

"You sure?"

"No. Go anyway."

Friday night Noll closed the dry store himself.

Then came to my room with two dumplings and a professional grievance.

"Delegation is terrible."

"You wanted him to have a life."

"Not on Friday."

He ate one dumpling.

I reached for the other.

He moved it away.

"This one is compensation."

Apparently authority had a catering budget...
