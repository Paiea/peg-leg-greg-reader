# Chapter 240: The Missed Slot

Pellin lost the firing slot on purpose.

The greenware arrived late again.

Different cart.

Different reason.

Same clock.

Hesk Street's owner looked at the sun mark on the yard wall.

"Load now or next cycle."

Arlo was across town.

Ivena was with a supplier.

Pellin stood beside six chambers and did not ask anyone to decide for him.

"Next cycle."

Dena stared.

"That's six days."

"I know."

"Customer wants four before then."

"I know."

"Arlo is going to become weather."

"I know."

We rolled the greenware back.

Arlo became weather.

Not because Pellin had made the wrong call.

Because the right call still moved three customer dates.

"Could've saved two," Arlo said.

Pellin's jaw tightened.

"By shortening the hold."

Arlo stopped.

Dena looked between them.

"Oh good. Growth. I hate it."

Arlo rubbed both hands over his face.

"No. You lose the slot."

"I did."

"And we move the orders."

"I did."

"You tell Marris."

Pellin looked less certain.

"Fine."

"That part is also yours."

Pellin picked up the customer ledger.

The chambers remained unfired...
