# Chapter 241: The Archive Wagon

The Guild job carried paper instead of freight.

Six lockboxes of municipal copies.

Three villages east of Carrow.

Seven days.

One archivist who treated rain as a hostile profession.

My role existed because several copied records involved old charged boundary markers and the city wanted a Silver support hand available if anyone needed one exposed or stabilized.

The archivist was Pel Aro.

She inspected my pack before departure.

"Oilskin?"

"Yes."

"For the copies?"

"For me."

She stared.

I added another sheet.

The driver was named Venn.

The Bronze guard was a woman named Kess who had done two warehouse escorts and no archive work.

"What attacks paper?" she asked.

Pel said, "Water. Ignorance. Poor shelving."

"Bandits?"

"Less frequently than mildew."

We left by the east road with six boxes of dead people's handwriting and no expectation of adventure.

Pel still checked the clouds every ten minutes...
