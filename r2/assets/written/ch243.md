# Chapter 243: The Toll Seal

The toll house did not exist in my memory.

Current Greg still had to stop there.

Pel said municipal archive wagons were exempt.

The toll keeper said municipal archive wagons were exempt on official seal days.

"Today is an official seal day."

"Seal?"

Pel became very still.

The seal was in one of the six lockboxes.

She knew which village packet contained it.

She did not know which box.

We opened three under oilskin while the toll keeper watched with professional pleasure.

The seal was in the fourth.

He stamped the exemption sheet.

"No charge."

Pel looked at him.

"You knew the seal would be there."

"I knew you would eventually find out."

Kess covered a laugh with a cough.

We rolled through without paying two copper.

I marked the toll house on my current map.

Pel noticed.

"It will still exist tomorrow."

"Probably."

She disliked that word enough to check the lockboxes again...
