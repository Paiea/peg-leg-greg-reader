# Chapter 255: The Half Day

Ivena found Senn working after lunch because his handwriting kept showing up after lunch.

Three route entries.

Two carrier notes.

One transfer dispute.

All dated after the hours we actually paid him.

She put the ledger on the packing table.

"How much?"

Senn knew the question.

"Usually three hours. Sometimes five."

"A week?"

"Yes."

"Paid?"

He became interested in a crate label.

That was an answer.

Arlo asked what another half day cost.

Dena asked what a lost chamber cost.

Pellin asked whether they could decide after the drying check because the current batch was becoming more urgent than labor law.

Ivena waited for the trays to come off the rack.

Then we decided.

One paid half day each week.

Six weeks.

No new clerk.

No permanent promise.

Senn wrote **ROUTE ADMIN TRIAL** into the labor ledger.

Dena leaned over his shoulder.

"You enjoy that phrase too much."

"Trial?"

"Admin."

Senn considered this seriously.

"Somebody has to."

"No. Somebody has to do it. Enjoying it is a private illness."

Ivena signed the change.

Then made Senn back-date three unpaid hours into the current week's adjustment.

He objected that he had volunteered them.

"You are not donating route administration to owners," she said.

Arlo looked at me.

"Can employees forbid generosity now?"

"Apparently."

Dena said, "Good. I have been generous enough."

Nobody knew what she meant...
