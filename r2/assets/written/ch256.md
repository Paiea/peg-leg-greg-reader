# Chapter 256: The Winter Packet

Jorren brought a city packet to my room because his table was covered in road grit and mine was merely covered in maps.

The packet listed three winter crews.

Two recurring closure windows.

Material substitutions he could approve inside written spec.

Engineer sign-off still required for structural changes.

His name sat under **FIELD COORDINATION**.

I read that twice.

"You have people now."

"I have three crews who already had people."

"You tell them what to do."

"Sometimes. Mostly I tell them where not to put gravel."

"Leadership."

"Terrifying."

The folded Guild Silver application was still in his field book.

It had become a bookmark for a drainage schedule.

I pointed at it.

"Still alive?"

"Paper doesn't die quickly."

"You signing it?"

"Maybe."

"When?"

He looked at me.

"When maybe becomes yes."

Fair.

He spread the crew map across my old-route copy and covered Widow Sar's bridge with a salt-delivery note.

"Your city is on my roads."

"Your roads are on my work."

It was my table.

Noll arrived with soup and moved both sets of paper six inches toward the wall.

"My bowls are on your future."

"Present," I said.

"Worse. Eat before it cools."

We did...
