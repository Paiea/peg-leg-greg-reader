# Chapter 258: The Housing

The housing was warmer than the floor around it.

Barely.

Enough that Olin checked the thermometer twice.

No current pipe entered from the visible side.

No wire.

No obvious charged line.

The iron had one packed seam and a square recess beneath the scale.

Renn wanted the recess cleaned.

Olin wanted the housing photographed before anybody changed its surface.

Issen wanted both men to remember the order said no disassembly.

I held a narrow plane above the exposed edge while Olin brushed mineral crust from the stamp.

Faint sideways load.

Same family of sensation as the laundry wall.

Maybe.

That word had become useful.

Renn crouched close enough that Mila put a flour scoop between him and the opening.

"Boundary."

"I am not touching it."

"Your face has plans."

The stamped mark resolved into three short cuts and one small circle.

Olin copied it.

Then Mila's carpenter put the boards back.

Renn objected.

"We just found it."

Mila said, "And I just found tomorrow's bread."

The floor closed.

The housing remained underneath it.

Evidence had acquired office hours...
