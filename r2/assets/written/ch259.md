# Chapter 259: The Repack Line

The stamp matched a maintenance entry two days later.

**EAST SERVICE HOUSING - REPACK - YEAR 43.**

Below it:

**RETURN WARMTH RESTORED AFTER FLUSH.**

Renn leaned back from the copied ledger.

"It moved heat."

Olin said, "Probably."

"Through charge."

"Maybe."

"You people are exhausting."

Issen smiled.

"Accuracy often is."

The line interested me for a different reason.

Repack.

Flush.

Restored.

Those were maintenance words.

People had expected the system to degrade.

Expected somebody to notice.

Expected somebody else to fix it.

Renn traced the route across the current property sheet.

Laundry.

Bakery.

Two rentals.

A narrow municipal lane.

"If the return still carries something-"

Issen tapped the owner column.

"Next owner first."

He stopped...
