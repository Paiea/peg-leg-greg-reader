# Chapter 260: The Bread Invoice

Mila billed the city for one hour and forty minutes of lost flour-room use.

Renn called the number aggressive.

Mila called it arithmetic.

The argument happened in the bakery doorway while I ate a roll I had paid for separately.

"You still mixed dough," Renn said.

"In the back room."

"So production continued."

"Slower."

"By how much?"

Mila handed him the sheet.

She had written batch times.

Normal output.

Actual output.

One delayed oven load.

Two staff shifted.

Renn stared at the page.

"You documented this?"

"I own a bakery. Flour becomes bread by schedule, not prayer."

Issen approved the invoice.

Renn looked betrayed by government.

Mila looked disappointed he had expected less paperwork from a business owner.

I finished the roll.

"Good?" she asked.

"Very."

"That was not free evidence."

I paid for another...
