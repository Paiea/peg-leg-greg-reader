# Chapter 261: The Cold Room

The fish market cold room had stopped being cold in one corner.

That sounded magical until I saw it.

Then it sounded expensive.

South Market Cooperative used six blue-glazed chill tiles along the north wall.

Low-charge infrastructure.

Common enough that Old Greg had ignored thousands of them.

Current Greg got paid because one tile housing needed unloading while the market technician opened it.

Her name was Esi Marr.

She had two missing fingertips on her left hand and no interest in explaining them.

"Plane here," she said.

I put the plane where she pointed.

"Smaller."

I made it smaller.

"Good."

The tile came free.

Behind it, the copper backing was green along one edge.

Renn would have called this fascinating.

Esi called it salt creep.

Fish brine had worked through a cracked wall seal and reached the backing plate.

"Tile bad?" I asked.

"Tile fine. Wall stupid."

The cooperative manager asked whether they needed six new tiles.

Esi said no so fast he looked offended.

One backing plate.

One seal strip.

Dry the wall.

Move the rinse barrel two feet.

I held the load for twelve minutes.

The corner began cooling again before lunch.

Old experience had not predicted the failure.

Current maintenance had found it anyway...
