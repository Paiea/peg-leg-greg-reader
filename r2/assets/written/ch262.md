# Chapter 262: The White Salt

The cold-room wall had a second problem that did not need Guild work.

White crystals kept forming along the lower grout line.

I assumed more brine.

Esi scraped one with her thumbnail.

"Packing salt."

"Difference?"

"Taste it."

I looked at her.

She smiled for the first time.

"Don't."

The crystals came from a cleaning powder the market used on fish tables.

Workers rinsed toward the wall.

Water carried the powder into the grout.

Then heat from the room dried it back out.

The cooperative manager looked almost disappointed.

Esi made him change the rinse direction and buy a longer floor squeegee.

"That fixes magic?" he asked.

"Fixes people. Harder."

I had nothing to add.

She charged for the visit anyway...
