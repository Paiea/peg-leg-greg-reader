# Chapter 263: The Door Seal

Three days later Esi requested me again.

Not for the tiles.

The cold-room door.

A replacement seal had arrived too thick.

Closing the door compressed the lower edge unevenly and pushed the frame against one charged tile mount.

Tiny load.

Wrong direction.

"Could trim seal," Esi said.

The carpenter said, "Could reset hinge."

The manager said, "Which is cheaper?"

Everyone looked at him.

"I am allowed to ask."

He was.

The carpenter reset the lower hinge.

Esi checked contact.

I held a two-inch plane behind the mount for one test close.

No load.

Second close.

No load.

The thick seal stayed.

The hinge moved.

The manager saved three copper and became unbearable about it.

Esi told him the savings belonged to maintenance because maintenance had found the problem.

He stopped celebrating...
