# Chapter 264: The Empty Afternoon

I had an afternoon with no contract.

This should not have been remarkable.

It felt suspicious anyway.

I cleaned my sword.

Washed two shirts.

Re-stitched the loose corner of my field bag.

Then stopped before reorganizing the map because the map had not done anything wrong.

Noll came in after lunch carrying half a cabbage cake.

"You home?"

"Apparently."

"Sick?"

"No."

"Fired?"

"Not yet."

He ate the rest of the cake.

My left knee felt normal.

WATCH stayed closed.

No letters arrived.

No ancient housing warmed itself dramatically beneath anyone's floor.

At four I went downstairs and helped Domm move a flour sack because he asked.

He paid me with dinner.

This was not Guild rate.

I accepted anyway...
