# Chapter 265: The Customer Line

Senn moved one label on the route sheet.

This caused more argument than adding three fields would have.

A bathhouse manager kept signing the carrier line because he wrote left-handed and the customer box sat beneath his wrist.

Four returns.

Four wrong signatures.

Senn called this user error.

Ivena called four identical errors a design problem.

Dena called Senn a design problem.

The meeting improved from there.

They moved **CUSTOMER** above the condition block and **CARRIER** below the transfer line.

No new boxes.

No seventh field.

The next sheet came back correct.

The bathhouse manager wrote **FINALLY** beside his name.

Senn pinned it under the older muddy sheet.

"Success?" I asked.

"Evidence."

"Of what?"

He looked at Dena.

"That users can occasionally be trained by moving ink."

Dena threw a scrap of wrapping paper at him...
