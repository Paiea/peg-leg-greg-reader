# Chapter 266: The Closure Window

Jorren closed a road before breakfast.

Not permanently.

Four hours.

A winter-drain crew needed the west spur empty while they cut a blocked side ditch and reset two stone lips.

I learned this from the notice board because Jorren had not told me.

**WEST SPUR - CLOSED 6 TO 10 - FIELD COORDINATION: JORREN VALE.**

A fruit seller beside me read the sign and swore.

"Know him?" she asked.

"Unfortunately."

"Tell him this is stupid."

"Is it?"

"I have pears."

This was apparently enough engineering.

At eleven I found Jorren at the Guild Annex eating something that had once been warm.

"Your closure ruined pears."

"Which seller?"

I described her.

"She used the south lane."

"How do you know?"

"She complained to me at seven."

"Then I bring no new information."

"Rare for you."

He drank my water.

The west spur reopened at ten twelve.
