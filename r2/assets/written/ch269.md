# Chapter 269: The Drift Line

The legal road and the winter road disagreed by twelve feet.

Nothing was wrong.

Marker nine sat exactly where the district survey said it should.

The village snow stakes curved east of it.

Tamar noticed.

A boy carrying firewood noticed us noticing.

"Drift moved."

"Snow drift?" Fenn asked.

The boy looked at the stakes.

"What other kind?"

His aunt set them each autumn.

We found her behind the cooperage repairing a sled runner.

Two years earlier a farmer uphill had cut half a windbreak.

Winter wind now laid the deep snow farther east.

The road boundary had not moved.

The useful winter line had.

Tamar copied the change onto the route sheet.

She did not move the stone.

"Different systems," she said.

The aunt shrugged.

"Different snow."
