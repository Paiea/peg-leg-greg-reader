# Chapter 270: The Closed Cut

Old Greg remembered using the east cut in winter.

Current road had a red gate across it before the first real snow.

**CLOSED AFTER FIRST FREEZE.**

Water seeped from a dark seam above the road.

Soli pointed at the rock shelf.

"Freezes under. Drops pieces."

"Later they roofed this," I said.

Tamar looked at me.

I should have kept that sentence internal.

"Current roof?"

"No."

"Useful current advice?"

"Take the lower road."

"Good."

The lower road added six miles.

It also passed two farms, one shrine, and a blacksmith where Fenn replaced a boot buckle for less than Carrow charged.

We bought apples at the second farm...
