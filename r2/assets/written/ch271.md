# Chapter 271: The Wayhouse Bench

The district wayhouse had six beds and five mattresses.

Fenn arrived last.

He claimed the bench beside the stove by choice.

At midnight the bench won the argument.

My mattress had a hollow shaped like a smaller man with worse posture.

Three days climbing in and out of the marker cart left my left knee stiff enough that I sat on the edge before standing.

Tamar noticed.

"Need the cart?"

"I have the cart."

"Need to stay in it for the first hill?"

That was a better question.

"Yes."

Soli gave me the front seat in the morning without discussion.

Fenn complained that Silver rank had finally found a practical use.

Tamar handed him the paint bucket...
