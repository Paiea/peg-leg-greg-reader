# Chapter 272: The Seal Eaters

Marker twelve had insects under the lamp hood.

Clear wings.

Copper bodies.

Fenn reached toward one.

The local road woman slapped his hand away.

"Seal eaters."

They were chewing the waxed gasket around the low-charge lamp cup.

Not the lamp.

Not the marker.

The gasket.

Once the seal opened, rain would reach the contact plate and turn a small maintenance problem into a wet one.

The road woman warmed bitter resin in a spoon.

"Kills them?" I asked.

"Keeps most off."

"Glass beetles?"

She shrugged.

"That's what children call them."

Old Greg knew charge pests from rope stores.

None of that helped more than the woman already holding the correct spoon.

I held the hood steady.

She resealed it.

Fenn inspected his slapped hand.

It remained attached...
