# Chapter 274: The Orchard Curve

The district road went around an orchard because a man seventy years ago had refused to sell.

His great-granddaughter wanted it straightened.

Tamar laughed before she could stop herself.

The woman pointed at three loaded carts waiting for each other on the curve.

"My dead relatives did not own wagons like these."

Fair.

Current road office had no approved straightening project.

Current compensation had not been negotiated.

Current apple trees were full.

We replaced two marker caps where wagon mud kept burying the numbers.

I remembered the later straight road.

The owner sold us cider.

Then complained about her ancestors until Soli agreed with everything because the cider was free...
