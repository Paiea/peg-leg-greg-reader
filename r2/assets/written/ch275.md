# Chapter 275: The Early Gate

Tamar closed the ridge road while the road was still dry.

The sky looked bad.

Not closed-road bad.

Soli said the cloud sat wrong.

A shepherd at the gate said the wind had changed at noon.

Tamar checked the district forecast.

Then the snow poles.

Then the ridge.

"Lower road."

A merchant behind us objected.

"I can reach Vale Cross before dark."

"Maybe," Soli said.

The merchant disliked maybe more than no.

We took the lower road.

Snow started three hours later.

Enough to whiten the upper turn.

Not enough to prove the merchant would have been trapped.

Tamar did not celebrate.

She ate stew at the wayhouse and fell asleep before Fenn finished complaining about room prices...
