# Chapter 276: The Locked Gate

The twenty-fourth marker sat behind a locked farm gate.

The owner was away.

His hired man had a key and no permission to admit district crews because the previous crew had broken a latch and nobody had paid for it.

Tamar had a service order.

The hired man had a grievance.

Both were real.

She read the order twice.

Then wrote:

**ACCESS DEFERRED - OWNER NOTICE REQUIRED.**

Fenn looked at the marker visible beyond the field.

"We came eight days for twenty-four."

"We serviced twenty-three."

"Finished?"

"Accurate."

Nobody climbed the fence.

The district would send notice and another crew later.

My travel pay remained full.

The service units did not.

A job could end with one thing still undone and no one pretending otherwise...
