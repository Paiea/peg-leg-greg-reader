# Chapter 277: The Corrected Sheet

Tamar's return sheet had more handwriting than road.

Bridge restriction.

Frost lace at seven.

Moved drift stakes at nine.

East cut closed after freeze.

Market Bend wheel repair.

Orchard curve.

Ridge closure.

One deferred property access.

I copied three village names onto my map after we reached Carrow.

Noll leaned over my shoulder.

"More paper."

"More places."

"Same table."

He put down soup and pushed Tamar's copy aside with one finger.
