# Chapter 278: The Current Direction

Two days later a delivery driver stopped beside the east-road board and asked me how to reach Market Bend.

I knew.

That was new in a boring way.

"North fork. Lower road if the ridge gate is closed."

"Closed today?"

I looked at the board.

"Check the winter notice."

He did.

Open.

He thanked me and left.

I did not tell him where the later bypass would run.

I did not tell him the east cut would eventually have a roof.

I did not explain the orchard curve's future.

He needed Market Bend today.

I knew the road there was...
