# Chapter 279: The Tuesday Line

Six weeks ended on a Tuesday.

Senn knew because he had written **TRIAL ENDS** in the labor ledger and underlined it twice.

Ivena knew because she had been paying him.

Dena knew because Senn had mentioned it every Tuesday for six weeks.

Arlo did not know.

This improved the meeting.

Ivena put six weeks of route entries on the table.

Four regular outside customers.

Two transfer carriers.

One wet-return restriction.

Three to four hours of route work most weeks.

No unpaid afternoons since the half day started.

"Still there," she said.

Senn nodded.

Arlo looked at the numbers.

"Could shrink again."

"Could."

"Then why make it permanent?"

Ivena tapped the ledger.

"Because permanent means we schedule the work that exists. Not that the universe signed a contract."

Dena said, "Put that over the door."

Pellin shouted from the drying room that nothing else was going over the door until somebody fixed the hinge.

We kept Tuesday afternoon.

No new clerk.

No extra hours.

Senn crossed out **TRIAL**.

The line now read:

**ROUTE ADMIN - TUESDAY PM.**

He stared at it longer than necessary.

"Happy?" I asked.

"Accurate."

Close enough...
