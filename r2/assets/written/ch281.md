# Chapter 281: The Green Cover

East Mill crossed a tailor's storage room next.

The tailor was Vara Pell.

No relation to Pellin, which disappointed Renn for reasons he did not explain.

Vara owned the building and the business.

That made the permission chain shorter.

It did not make permission automatic.

Issen showed her the old route sheet.

A square service mark sat beneath the rear wall.

Vara looked at the wall.

An iron cover had been painted green to match the shelving.

Thread cards hung from two hooks on its upper edge.

"That?" Renn asked.

"That has held blue thread since before I bought the shop."

"Warm?"

"Usually wall temperature."

"After rain?"

Vara looked at him.

"Do you ask every wall about weather?"

"Recently."

She allowed visual inspection.

No screws removed.

No cards moved without asking.

Olin photographed the cover and copied the old screw spacing.

I had nothing magical to do.

That was fine.

Vara watched us for twenty minutes.

"You still don't know what this line is?"

Issen said, "We know more than we did."

"City answer."

"Yes."

Vara respected that more than certainty...
