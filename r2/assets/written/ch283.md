# Chapter 283: The Packing Strip

The materials clerk did not care that the sample came from East Mill.

This improved her usefulness.

The city had removed one loose fragment from the old wrapping after Vara agreed.

Flax fiber.

Mineral binder.

Resin.

Maybe traces of old charge treatment.

The clerk, Ossa Den, had seen similar packing around older bathhouse steam lines and two brewery heat runs.

Renn looked offended.

"Same material?"

"Similar."

"Used for heat?"

"Used around things people did not want leaking."

"Charge?"

"Sometimes."

"You are doing this deliberately."

Ossa looked at him.

"Doing what?"

"Refusing nouns."

"I gave you three. Flax. Mineral. Resin."

Issen coughed into her hand.

Probably laughter.

The sample did not solve the old line.

It did something better.

It made part of the line ordinary enough that a current materials clerk had comparisons.

Vara's wall remained closed.

The city now had to decide whether old packing condition mattered to current use.

Another office gained a problem...
