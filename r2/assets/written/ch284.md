# Chapter 284: The Smaller Share

My owner distribution was six copper.

Last time it had been nine.

Nobody died.

This also disappointed Arlo.

Route wages.

Two-copper refund.

Outside firing fees.

One cracked spare collar.

The business had spent money while continuing to exist.

Ivena read the numbers aloud.

Pellin asked whether the firing slot was losing money.

"No."

"Then why less?"

"Because four other things cost money."

Dena said, "Put that over the door too."

Pellin shouted that the hinge was fixed now and they had lost the excuse.

Arlo wanted to hold one copper of each distribution in extra reserve.

Ivena made him propose an actual reserve target instead.

He did not have one.

The copper stayed ours.

I took six.

Bought boot laces on the way home.

They were better than the old ones immediately.

Investment accomplished...
