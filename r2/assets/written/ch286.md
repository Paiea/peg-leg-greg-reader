# Chapter 286: The Clinic Board

The Guild already had a support clinic.

Of course it did.

Second Saturday each month.

Bronze and new Silver support workers.

Two paid instructors.

Rotating demonstration hands when needed.

Sella showed me the notice as if I had personally hidden it from myself.

"You're not an instructor."

"Excellent."

"You could demonstrate release timing for one block next month."

"Less excellent."

"Paid."

"Recovering."

Pell Arin had apparently asked the correct person.

The clinic lead was Kesra Dain's former assistant, a woman named Omi Tress who had no interest in my history and wanted to know whether I could repeat the same small-plane release six times without turning the demonstration into autobiography.

"Probably."

"That is not yes."

"Then yes."

She booked one forty-minute demonstration block next month...
