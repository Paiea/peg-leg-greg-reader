# Chapter 287: The Silver Request

Jorren requested a Silver hand.

Not me.

The city road office sent the request through the Guild because a winter-drain crew had exposed an old charged retaining plate beside a culvert mouth.

Sella assigned me because I was available.

I enjoyed this more than I should have.

When I reached the west district, Jorren was already under the road arguing with a mason about whether half the ditch could stay open during the reset.

He looked up.

"Oh. You."

"Heartwarming."

"Silver?"

"Still."

"Stand there."

The retaining plate carried a faint outward load through one stone lip.

Jorren owned the closure.

The mason owned the stone.

I held a small plane while she reset the lip.

A city inspector checked the ditch line.

Jorren reopened half flow.

He signed **FIELD COORDINATION**.

I signed **GUILD SUPPORT**.

Different boxes.

Same mud.

On the walk back he said, "I didn't ask for you."

"I know."

"Good."
