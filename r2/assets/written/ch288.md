# Chapter 288: The Oil Count

Miko gave the dry-store key to Tessa for the oil count.

Tessa was seventeen, fast with numbers, and terrified of Noll.

Noll saw the key in her hand from the kitchen door.

"Why does she have that?"

Tessa stopped breathing.

Miko did not.

"Oil count."

"You gave her the store key."

"For oil."

"I gave you the store key."

"Yes."

"To close."

"Oil count is part of close."

Noll opened his mouth.

Closed it.

Tessa remained between shelves holding a tally slate and the future of kitchen government.

Miko said, "I am standing right here."

Noll looked at the key.

Then at him.

"Next time tell me first."

"Fine."

Not permission exactly.

Not refusal.

Tessa counted oil correctly and handed the key back.

Noll checked the number later.

He did not retake the key from Miko.

That seemed worth not mentioning...
