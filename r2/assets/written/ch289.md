# Chapter 289: The Wrong Winter

Mara's letter arrived from the same river region and a different office.

A customer bankruptcy had turned three tidy accounts into one recovery problem.

Vey sent her north because that was where the paperwork had gone.

She hated the boarding house.

Liked the baker.

Had discovered one ferry captain accepted advance payment only when he trusted the weather.

**I think this is accounting. I may be doing anthropology by accident.**

I laughed at the table.

Then unfolded the map.

No arrow already waited north.

Good.

I added one after reading.

Noll looked over from the window.

"You know where she is now?"

"Approximately."

"Progress."

"Low standard."

"Still progress."

I wrote back about Tessa surviving the oil count.

Mara did not need my theories about where she would go next...
