# Chapter 291: The Tuesday Bell

Tuesday afternoon became ordinary.

That was how I knew the route-admin decision had taken.

Senn closed the packing ledger after lunch and opened the route ledger without anyone holding a meeting.

Dena left one transfer receipt on his desk.

Pellin asked him about a carrier time.

Arlo complained that nobody had told him one bathhouse moved its winter pickup.

Senn pointed at the route board.

It had been there three days.

"I do not read walls," Arlo said.

Dena said, "Explains the door problem."

The hinge had already been fixed.

This did not stop her.

I spent twenty minutes helping Pellin move finished chambers off a drying rack.

Then left...
