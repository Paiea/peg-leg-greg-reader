# Chapter 302: The Warm Bank

Steam rose from the riverbank in freezing air.

A small patch beneath Landing Three.

Enough to keep the stones dark while the rest were white.

Moro said, "Spring seep."

Warm groundwater entered below the bank.

Tiny silver insects collected where the warm trickle met cold river.

A fisherman had set a woven bait trap downstream of the seep.

"Good bait?" I asked.

"Very."

Yara pointed at the bank marker.

"Protected edge. No digging."

Nobody had intended to dig.

Old Greg had crossed this reach later without storing any of it.

Current Moro knew where fishermen bought warmflies by the handful...
