# Chapter 305: The Saturday Block

The support clinic had twelve chairs and nine people.

Omi Tress considered this adequate.

She considered most things adequate only after checking them herself.

Three weighted frames stood along one wall.

Two had old repair marks.

One had a new leather sling and a chalk note that said **DO NOT TRUST LEFT BUCKLE**.

I liked the room immediately.

Pell Arin sat in the second row.

She lifted one hand when I came in.

Not a student greeting.

More an acknowledgment that the person she had bothered in the stairwell had actually appeared.

Omi handed me a wooden frame with a twenty-pound sand sack suspended inside it.

"Forty minutes," she said.

"Sella promised forty."

"I promise thirty-eight if you talk too much."

"Good system."

She faced the room.

"Greg. Silver. Barrier support. Low reserve. Clean short release."

A young man in the back asked, "How low?"

"Low enough that wasting it is educational," Omi said.

I objected to none of this because she had already started the clock.

My demonstration was simple.

Plane under the sack.

Omi shifted the sling.

Load moved to the frame.

Release.

Again.

Again.

By the fifth cycle the room had stopped watching my hands.

Omi noticed.

"What changed?"

Pell answered first.

"The path."

Omi nodded.

"Good. Greg is not the lesson."

I had been promoted to equipment.

This was probably healthy...
