# Chapter 309: The Second Name

The rope warehouse came back three weeks later.

Not because the treatment failed.

They wanted the high rack inspected before spring damp.

The request card said:

**PREFERRED: NERIS HAL IF AVAILABLE.**

I read it twice.

Sella noticed.

"Problem?"

"No."

There was a small, stupid pinch somewhere behind my ribs.

I had declined the first job.

Neris had done it.

The client now wanted the person who had actually shown up.

This was offensively reasonable.

Neris came in ten minutes later eating a pear.

Sella handed him the card.

"Your admirers."

He read it.

"Terrible people."

"They pay on time."

"Excellent people."

He took the job.

I took a stairwell brace inspection on the south side.

By lunch the pinch had become embarrassing enough to stop having.

Jobs could belong to whoever did them.

Apparently this remained true even when mine had been the first name on the card.

I did not write that down...
