# Chapter 311: The Shelf

My room needed another shelf.

Not urgently.

That was how I knew the problem was real.

The existing shelf held Mara's letters, maintenance cards, three route copies, and two books I had somehow acquired without becoming a scholarly person.

My second map lived rolled beside the bed.

My winter gloves lived on the chair when the chair was not pretending to be furniture.

Maud Pell inspected the wall above the gear alcove.

"Three copper installed."

"Two if I install it."

"Four if you install it and split plaster."

"You have little faith."

"I manage rentals. Faith is not a maintenance category."

I paid three.

Her carpenter put up a narrow shelf in twenty minutes.

I placed the letters there.

Then moved them because steam from the wash basin reached that wall.

The route cards fit instead.

The letters moved to the old shelf.

The rolled map moved off the floor.

Nothing important had happened.

I had simply accumulated enough present to require another board.

Noll came in, looked at the new shelf, and said, "More paper."

"Vertical paper."

"Advanced."

He put dinner on the table the shelf had failed to replace.
