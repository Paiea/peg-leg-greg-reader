# Chapter 313: The Tuesday Off

Noll took the next Tuesday off.

He informed Miko on Wednesday.

Miko said, "Next Wednesday?"

"This Wednesday."

"For next Tuesday."

"Correct."

Miko wrote it down with unnecessary force.

"Good communication," Noll said.

"I hate you."

"Also clear."

On Tuesday Noll did not go to the Brass Spoon.

This created a second problem.

He had nowhere he particularly wanted to go.

He ended up in my room at noon while I repaired a scabbard strap.

"Day off," I said.

"Apparently."

"Enjoying it?"

"No."

He lay on the floor for twenty minutes and then went to buy onions because the room made him feel unproductive.

He came back with onions and a cheap sweet bun.

"Better?"

"Slightly."

At four he started to stand because the Brass Spoon would be entering supper prep.

Then stopped.

"Miko has it."

"Probably."

"Do not say probably."

He stayed seated.

The restaurant did not send anyone running for him.

At six he looked offended by this success.
