# Chapter 314: The River Letter

Mara's next letter came from the river again.

Not the same account.

The bankruptcy recovery had ended.

Vey moved her back because two regular customers needed someone who already knew which packet captains would accept advance payment in winter.

She hated one landing clerk.

Liked another landing's fried fish.

Had learned that Cenn Varo would carry paperwork in the mug rack if no one stopped him.

That caught me.

**You know Cenn?** I wrote.

Then stopped.

Of course she might know Cenn.

The river existed when I was not on it.

I crossed the line out.

My actual reply said:

**I spent six days on the packet inspection and finally learned why some schedules are decorative. If Vey makes you argue with Middle Landing, bring warmer socks.**

I told her about Noll's unsuccessful day off.

She wrote in the margin of her next sheet that this was the most alarming part of the letter.

Different lives kept discovering the same river without needing a meeting...
