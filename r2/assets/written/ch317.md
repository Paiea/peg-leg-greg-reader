# Chapter 317: The Uneven Stair

Lera made me work stairs.

Not sword forms on stairs.

That would have been stupid.

She stood at the bottom of the Guild service stair and told me to go up and down six times without hurrying.

"This is training?"

"This is you pretending your left leg arrives at the same time after long jobs."

I disliked her diagnostic style.

First pass fine.

Second fine.

Fourth, my left foot landed a fraction late on the descent.

No pain.

No buckle.

Just timing.

Lera heard it.

"Again slower."

"I am already walking."

"Then walk better."

I did.

She moved the sword lesson to flat footwork and cut the final sparring block.

I still paid full price.

"You removed work."

"I removed stupid work. Premium service."

At the end she made me climb the stairs once more.

The timing stayed even.

She did not praise it.

I did not need her to.

Outside, I chose the longer street home because it was flatter and immediately resented the wisdom of this choice.
