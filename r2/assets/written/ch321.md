# Chapter 321: The Red Cart

One quarry cart was painted red.

I assumed danger.

Jass said, "Training."

Worse in a different way.

The rail from upper cut to dressing yard ran downhill.

Gravity provided motive power.

A brake arm and drag shoe provided civilization.

The red cart carried half load and a new brakeman named Ors.

An older rail hand walked beside him.

"Post."

Ors eased the arm.

"More."

He pulled harder.

The wheels locked and skidded two feet.

"Release."

Ors stared at him.

"It stopped."

"Locked wheel doesn't steer."

The older hand made him reset from the previous mark.

No praise for stopping too hard.

No humiliation either.

Again.

This time the cart slowed without locking.

At the bottom Ors looked pleased.

The rail hand said, "Tomorrow full half load."

"That was half load."

"Tomorrow you'll know it."

Old Greg remembered powered quarry winches.

Current Blackcut painted one cart red and taught gravity by repetition.

I stayed out of the lesson because no one had invited me into it.
