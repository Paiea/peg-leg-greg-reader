# Chapter 323: The Blue Dust

Everyone stopped before I understood why.

A cutter on the east face raised one hand.

Fine blue powder spilled from a hairline seam above his work line.

Not much.

Enough.

Jass cleared the face.

Ressa climbed the side stair and checked two chalk marks from the morning.

The waiting client wagon below immediately developed opinions.

"How long?" the driver called.

Jass ignored him.

Ressa measured again.

"Cold pull. Move cut west six feet. Leave this until sun."

The driver said, "My slot is now."

Jass pointed at the dust line.

"You can have that block now if you stand under it."

The driver discovered flexibility.

Tools moved.

The east face went quiet.

No magic.

No Guild action.

Twelve minutes of professional caution cost the quarry more than my entire morning reserve.

Later I asked the cutter what the blue dust meant exactly.

"Could mean nothing."

"Then why stop?"

"Could mean stone."

He seemed to believe this explained everything.

In context, it did.
