# Chapter 330: The Blue Chip

Six days produced five inspected wedges.

Three passed without argument.

One chipped wedge left active service.

One bad packing ring returned after repair.

The quarry paid travel, lodging adjustment, and five support units.

Ressa signed the Guild sheet.

"Useful?" I asked.

"You kept charge off tools while we opened them."

"Glowing praise."

"Want poetry, ask sales."

Before I left she handed me the original blue-gray sample.

"Guild doesn't need this back."

"Neither do I."

"Then throw it away."

I put it in my bag.

At home Noll found it beside the map.

"Rock."

"Stone."

"Difference?"

"Six days of people correcting me."

He used it to hold one corner of the map flat while setting dinner down.

Blackcut had six classification categories for stone.

Lower Rook found the seventh.
