# R2 Character-First Rebuild — Later-R2 Quarry

Status: **QUARRY / REHEARSAL / EVIDENCE ONLY**

This file deliberately destroys chronological authority for Chapters 27+.

Historical later R2 remains valuable because it discovered people, relationship languages, work textures, money pressures, magic evidence, places, jokes, objects, and consequences. None of the chapter numbers below are protected for the rebuild. A discovery may move earlier, arrive later, merge with another scene, change causal owner, or die.

The exact historical Chapters 27–31 were fresh-read first. Existing high-fidelity audits through Chapter 190 were then used to identify later candidates cheaply, with exact prose checked for the strongest relationship and social discoveries.

## Things worth saving

### SAVE 1 — Tavin proves young Greg already wanted roads

Historical 29 reveals that young Greg had talked about leaving Carrow enough for Tavin to send him a real freight lead. `Halden first if Mara goes` exists beside a broader desire to get on the road.

**Portable discovery:** Greg's adventurous impulse does not belong exclusively to Old Greg. The nineteen-year-old was already moving toward something.

**Why it matters:** this complicates Greg's fear that the older consciousness is simply replacing a blank boy. It also gives young Greg a relationship and ambition not routed through Mara.

Fresh-perform if used. Do not preserve the historical chapter's tidy explanatory arc automatically.

### SAVE 2 — The burned seven is communal disputed history

Tavin, Mara, Noll, Jorren, and Greg can all carry different versions of the burned-card rule or incident.

**Portable discovery:** damaged memory can create comedy and intimacy without requiring a recovered answer. A disputed ordinary object is better social continuity than a lore dump.

### SAVE 3 — Jorren returns visibly changed by work Greg did not witness

Historical 30 and later 57 both succeed when Jorren comes back with evidence on his body and gear: resoled boots, patched sleeve, scar, flour in his hair, sore shoulder, changed card tell, different work contacts.

**Portable discovery:** absence should leave physical and behavioral evidence. Greg should have to meet the new Jorren rather than merely resume a paused friendship.

### SAVE 4 — Jorren owns contacts and asks Greg selectively

Later Jorren receives job leads through grain factors and road work, then considers whether Greg fits.

**Portable discovery:** friendship can generate work without making Greg the dispatcher. Jorren may own the opportunity, terms, and decision.

### SAVE 5 — Two chairs / borrowed bowls / floor space can carry friendship

Later rooms become socially meaningful because another chair suddenly has a user, four people expose a two-bowl inventory problem, and someone needs the floor for one night.

**Portable discovery:** material household growth should be tested by people, not narrated as progression.

### SAVE 6 — Noll has a work-self Greg does not know

Historical 92 works because Greg visits The Brass Spoon to eat and discovers that Noll moves differently in a real kitchen, has teachers, a terrifying manager, a spoon-throwing coworker whose sauces are excellent, and improved dumplings.

**Portable discovery:** enter another person's world as a visitor. Let Greg discover that his friend has status, competence, irritation, and relationships elsewhere.

This exact Brass Spoon timing is not protected.

### SAVE 7 — Noll and Greg can have ordinary social failures that professional correctness cannot fix

Historical 164: Greg makes the correct warehouse decision, misses dinner, fails to send word, and Noll stays annoyed. `I couldn't leave` does not answer `You could send a runner.`

**Portable discovery:** a good professional choice can coexist with a selfish or careless social choice. Do not let competence absolve the relationship mistake.

### SAVE 8 — Mara intimacy works best under work clocks and ordinary inconvenience

Later Mara scenes succeed through bedbugs, ledgers, employer lodging, one-hour dinners, room inspections, cold lamps, work at first bell, and kisses that do not erase scheduling.

**Portable discovery:** romance and desire should coexist with jobs rather than suspend them. Greg may want her to stay and still walk her back because her work is real.

Exact later visits are not protected.

### SAVE 9 — Mara notices Greg's life through objects and room changes

The later second chair, sword rack, drying alcove, letters, repaired window, bowls, and bigger room let Mara see that Greg has actually accumulated a life.

**Portable discovery:** let a returning person read material evidence and notice something Greg did not plan to explain.

### SAVE 10 — Current Tavin can choose intimacy before memory returns

Historical 97 works because Tavin insults Greg's coat, opens his arms, and hugs him. Greg does not need recovered precedent to respond.

**Portable discovery:** old relationships can become new present relationships through the other person's current choices. Lost memory does not have to be solved first.

### SAVE 11 — People from different Greg-circles can form their own relationships

Later Tavin, Noll, and Jorren share dinner/cards and become a room Greg does not fully control. Mara and Noll also recognize each other through their own old-Carrow history.

**Portable discovery:** social density grows fastest when supporting characters gain direct edges to one another. Greg should sometimes become the least important connection in the room.

### SAVE 12 — Filtration can pay owners without becoming solved

Historical 108: a real distribution happens only after wages, reserve, route costs, firing, maintenance, and future needs. Greg gets actual owner money and spends it on reserve, socks, maintenance, and dinner.

**Portable discovery:** material improvement may stick. Real money should create new choices rather than resetting Greg to scarcity.

### SAVE 13 — A junior worker can puncture owner logic accidentally

Pellin asks Arlo why they need a kiln if Bell Tile currently works. The question lands harder because it is not a strategic performance.

**Portable discovery:** hierarchy should not monopolize insight. A worker can embarrass an owner simply by asking the obvious question from another position.

### SAVE 14 — Filtration failures can have distributed blame and incompatible priorities

Later audits identify multi-owner failures with no single culprit, capacity limits, carrier friction, breakage, scheduling, and customer pressure.

**Portable discovery:** business scenes become alive when nobody owns the clean explanation and good choices still conflict.

### SAVE 15 — Greg can be useful and still violate another worker's preferred control path

Later Silver-era work includes a hoist incident where Greg's intervention helps but the rigger dislikes how he altered the control path.

**Portable discovery:** `useful` and `welcome` are different. Another professional may remain angry after Greg prevents a worse outcome.

### SAVE 16 — Jorren can choose authority without choosing Greg's imagined ladder

Later Jorren accumulates leadership evidence, accepts city road/coordinator work, and can leave a Silver application unsigned.

**Portable discovery:** Greg may misunderstand what status or identity another person wants. Jorren can value authority, money, place, or work without wanting the future shape Greg projects onto him.

### SAVE 17 — Unknown magic can remain unknown

Historical 31's dry warm wall and limebacks are excellent magic quarry because Old Greg does not recognize the mechanism.

**Portable discovery:** Greg's old experience should sometimes deepen wonder instead of removing it. Current specialists, local creatures, records, or time may know things he does not.

Do not preserve the old subcellar contract merely to preserve this mystery.

### SAVE 18 — Mundane environmental phenomena can alter work without becoming fights

Glasshorn migration, lightfin/ferry restrictions, orchard water behavior, weather-soft roads, drainage, animal corridors, and seasonal route use all widen the world while letting local people own the practical response.

**Portable discovery:** the world can obstruct, reroute, inconvenience, and surprise without escalating into combat or becoming a lesson every time.

## Habits not to reimport

### DO NOT REIMPORT 1 — Paid lesson as default next-chapter engine

Historical 27 immediately converts the frontier into a sword lesson: correction, repetition, visible improvement, Gold comparison, drill coda.

Lera is worth saving. `Furniture` is worth saving. The automatic training chapter is not.

### DO NOT REIMPORT 2 — Requested-by-name as automatic competence chapter

Historical 28 has a good social beat in Meren specifically asking for Greg, then spends the chapter proving he is faster at a bounded ward action.

Keep recognition if earned. Do not force the recognition to cash out as another skill demonstration.

### DO NOT REIMPORT 3 — Reunion immediately consumed by next contract

Historical 30 gives Jorren a strong return, then converts the reunion into a subcellar intake.

When an important person returns, let the person own enough page time to change the relationship before machinery claims the scene.

### DO NOT REIMPORT 4 — `present evidence beats future memory` as the only use of Old Greg

Keep empirical humility. Stop using it as the universal dramatic move.

Old memory should also create false familiarity, nostalgia, grief, premature affection, dread, temptation, resentment, sexual awkwardness, inappropriate certainty about a younger person, or mourning for something currently alive.

### DO NOT REIMPORT 5 — The competent-professional carousel

Do not repeatedly introduce a new adult whose job is to be dry, capable, correct, and better calibrated than Greg.

Professionals may be territorial, vain, angry, superstitious, impatient, commercially ugly, generous, inconsistent, socially stupid, or wrong outside their domain.

### DO NOT REIMPORT 6 — `Greg knows his boundary` as a climax

Restraint is good craft and good ethics. It cannot remain the primary proof of growth.

A scene needs wants, costs, misunderstandings, social ownership, or discovery beyond Greg correctly declining to overreach.

### DO NOT REIMPORT 7 — Evaluator fragments as connective tissue

Later audits repeatedly identify isolated `Good`, `Useful`, `Correct`, `Different`, `There`, and similar labels.

Keep them when they are truly Greg and funny. Stop using them as the default punctuation after every piece of competence.

### DO NOT REIMPORT 8 — `Not X. Y.` as automatic wisdom cadence

The construction is part of Greg's voice but became a machine. It loses force when every distinction is framed as a neat reversal.

### DO NOT REIMPORT 9 — Dialogue followed by explanation of what the dialogue proved

If Mara refuses reassurance, Noll stays annoyed, Arlo says no, a worker names a practical need, or Jorren chooses something, trust the action.

Do not stand Greg beside the scene and translate it into theme.

### DO NOT REIMPORT 10 — Tidy Greg-principle chapter endings

Stop ending every chapter with the polished sentence explaining the chapter's governing truth.

Use rooms, dishes, boots, another person's line, work still waiting, a bad night's sleep, an unanswered letter, a missing bowl, a bill, a smell, or silence when those land better.

### DO NOT REIMPORT 11 — Distributed agency explained immediately after it happens

If Arlo and Ivena decide something without Greg, or Jorren gets his own work, or Noll has a teacher, do not add `the world moved without me` every time.

Let the accumulated behavior become the reader's knowledge.

### DO NOT REIMPORT 12 — Mundane inventory as progression receipt

Socks, shelves, gloves, bowls, room size, tools, and food matter when they alter a life or relationship.

Do not add another object solely to prove Greg has accumulated ordinary capability.

### DO NOT REIMPORT 13 — Work scene after work scene because work is reliably good

Work is one of R2's greatest strengths and therefore one of its easiest addictions.

Do not let `specific interesting profession` substitute for story movement. Relationship, home, sex, friendship, resentment, boredom, desire, and social mistakes deserve chapter-selection authority too.

### DO NOT REIMPORT 14 — Every adult becoming emotionally optimized

Reasonable boundaries do not require reasonable personalities.

Allow grudges, class irritation, bad humor, pettiness, irrational attachments, vanity, jealousy, favoritism, and inconsistent generosity.

### DO NOT REIMPORT 15 — Anti-escalation as its own formula

`Nothing attacked us`, `no rank`, `no destiny`, `no dramatic result` can become as predictable as spectacle.

Do not perform anti-power-fantasy signaling. Simply let ordinary stakes be ordinary when that is what happens.

### DO NOT REIMPORT 16 — Compression that removes the promised experience

Later speculative tails sometimes preserve event identity while skipping work, waiting, awkwardness, failed attempts, bodies, argument, or aftermath.

If the synopsis contains the most interesting part, the prose has failed even if the causal state is correct.

### DO NOT REIMPORT 17 — Chronological sunk cost

A historically later discovery does not deserve its old approach sequence. The dry wall does not need the old subcellar job. Tavin does not need to wait until historical 29. Noll's work-self does not need to wait until 92.

Move only what current life earns.

### DO NOT REIMPORT 18 — Making Greg the best connector among every circle

Supporting characters should know, choose, hire, teach, annoy, date, help, and remember one another without Greg being the reason every edge exists.

A room becomes more alive when Greg is occasionally the least informed person in it.

## Quarry priorities for the first reconstructed run

The strongest immediate quarry is **not** the dry wall, another ward job, or a sword lesson.

The strongest immediate human material is:

1. Tavin's late letter proving young Greg already wanted roads.
2. Jorren returning changed by life Greg did not witness.
3. Noll becoming socially legible as more than `neighbor` when another Greg-circle intersects him.
4. Mara correspondence continuing on her own work clock rather than providing reassurance on demand.
5. Greg wanting other people to notice and validate the current life he has built, while also resisting being trapped inside young Greg's old relationships.

Those discoveries can create a five-chapter run with no need to open a new subsystem.
