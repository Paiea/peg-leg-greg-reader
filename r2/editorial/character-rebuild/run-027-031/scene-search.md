## What is live at the end of Chapter 26

Greg has:

- a repaired sword, boots, pack, and field-worn body
- enough cash to choose a day off without immediate panic
- a support assessment still weeks away
- a filtration venture that works without requiring his daily presence
- Noll next door as a current relationship built from fire, soap, food, and noise
- Jorren away on his own road clock
- Mara in Halden on her own work clock, with correspondence active but not guaranteed on Greg's schedule
- a real desire for roads/adventure
- a growing attachment to Lower Rook and Carrow
- no urgent reason to choose another Guild subsystem tomorrow

The strongest available pressure is therefore social, not procedural.

## Primary human pressure

**Greg wants people from young Greg's life to recognize the current life he has built, while also resisting the idea that those people get to define him by a version of Greg he cannot remember.**

He wants continuity and freedom at the same time.

This should not be narrated as the thesis of the run. Let it emerge through a letter, a returning friend, a room, food, cards, the filtration workshop, and a departure.

## Greg's incompatible wants

### Want A — Be recognized

Greg wants Jorren, Tavin, and eventually Mara to see that he is not merely confused, broken, or waiting. He has work, a sword, road experience, money, a business interest, a neighbor/friend, and choices.

This makes him vulnerable to showing off.

### Want B — Not be trapped inside young Greg

Greg does not want every old person to hand him another obligation, intimacy, promise, or ambition that he must perform because nineteen-year-old Greg once meant it.

This makes him defensive when old evidence surprises him.

### Want C — Roads

He wants adventure, distance, field work, novelty, and the old identity of `adventurer`.

### Want D — Home

He also wants the room, familiar cookroom, Noll through the wall, a place to return to, people who know where to find him, and enough money to leave tomorrow undecided.

The run should not resolve roads versus home.

## Old Greg emotional pressure

Do not use Old Greg mainly as a technical comparison in this run.

When Jorren returns from the road, let fifty-nine-year-old Greg's decades of departures and people not always coming back make the relief arrive too hard and too fast. He does not know Jorren's future. He knows what `eventually` can mean after enough roads.

That is emotional memory without prophecy.

Greg may cover the relief with a joke because the current friendship does not yet have a practiced language for saying `I was afraid you might not come back.`

## Other people's independent choices

### Tavin

The late letter already exists in quarry. Young Greg had discussed roads enough for Tavin to send a freight lead. The job deadline has passed.

Tavin chose to write. Greg did not ask for the evidence.

### Jorren

Jorren returns because his own work brings him back, not because Greg summoned him.

He has changed in small ways Greg did not cause.

He also already has a likely next road opportunity / continuation through his own contacts. He does not need Greg to make it viable.

Greg dislikes this more than he believes he should.

### Noll

Noll does not wait to be formally integrated into `Greg's friend group`.

When Jorren arrives, Noll can help with a blanket, food, a joke, or a card rule directly. He and Jorren may like each other without Greg managing the introduction.

This should make Greg weirdly territorial or left behind for a beat.

### Arlo / Ivena / Dena

The filtration venture has made at least one ordinary decision since Greg left for the West Cut. The decision should be defensible and not require his approval.

Greg brings Jorren to the workshop partly because he wants Jorren to see something impressive. The workshop should refuse to stage-manage itself for Greg's ego.

## Five-chapter shape

### Reconstructed 27 — `The Late Letter`

**Scene grammar:** solitary material discovery + interrupted attempt at correspondence.

Greg is cleaning/reordering the remains of the day-off accounting when he finds the second old letter that has been sitting under an ordinary receipt since before he woke.

Tavin's letter proves:

- Tavin and Greg had a rude, easy relationship
- young Greg had talked about roads seriously
- `Halden first if Mara goes` was part of the plan, not the whole plan
- the freight opportunity has already passed
- the burned-seven debt remains disputed

Do not make Greg instantly grateful for the discovery.

Let him be irritated that young Greg had more plans than he can remember and irritated that he wants to claim the road desire as his own.

He starts a reply. He should not successfully produce another Mara-style emotionally correct letter.

Possible physical landing: two copper placed under the burned seven; the unfinished reply remains unsent because Greg cannot decide whether Tavin deserves the complicated truth yet.

**Discovery:** young Greg was not blank, and Greg is not sure whether that comforts or threatens him.

**Residue:** no reply sent yet.

### Reconstructed 28 — `Eventually`

**Scene grammar:** reunion + household logistics.

Jorren returns to Lower Rook / Greg after closing his own road work.

Greg notices physical changes before Jorren explains them: repaired gear, altered straps, a new small scar or wear pattern, road dust, whatever exact details survive fresh performance.

Relief hits Greg harder than current social language can comfortably carry because Old Greg has lived through too many departures. He covers it with teasing.

Jorren needs a floor for one night because his normal lodging is unavailable / full. Keep the logistics ordinary.

Noll intersects them before the chapter can become a Greg/Jorren private reunion. Greg introduces Noll too narrowly, likely as `the neighbor` or `next door`, and Noll's response makes the lag visible without a speech.

Noll and Jorren establish their own first edge.

**Discovery:** Greg has already let Noll become more important than the category he uses for him.

**Residue:** Jorren is back but not `back for Greg`. He has his own next steps.

### Reconstructed 29 — `The Seven`

**Scene grammar:** group social scene / game / food / small emotional misfire.

Noll, Jorren, and Greg eat in Greg's room or shared Lower Rook space. The limited room inventory should matter materially: bowls, chair/floor, food, cards.

The burned seven comes out because Tavin's letter has made it newly salient.

Noll and Jorren begin inventing / disputing rules without deference to Greg's lost history.

Greg enjoys this until he suddenly does not.

The emotional misunderstanding should be small but real. Possible pressure:

- Greg gets sharp when a joke makes his missing memory feel like public property.
- Jorren thinks he is joking back.
- Noll notices the tonal shift first because he knows current Greg through walls and ordinary days rather than old history.

Do not resolve the discomfort with a clean apology in the same scene.

Let someone keep playing, change subject badly, leave, or wash dishes.

**Discovery:** Greg wants current people to inherit old Greg's life, but he also resents losing ownership of a history he cannot remember.

**Residue:** somebody knows he was bothered even if Greg refuses to name why.

### Reconstructed 30 — `The Workshop`

**Scene grammar:** social performance inside existing work, not a work problem.

Greg brings Jorren to the filtration workshop because he wants to show him what happened while he was gone.

Greg does not admit this is showing off.

Arlo, Ivena, and Dena are working. The shop has changed in one or two small material ways since Greg last looked. Do not invent a major expansion.

A modest business decision has been made without Greg, such as service scheduling, another ordinary customer, a spare allocation, or a delivery routine. It is defensible. Greg has thoughts and was not consulted.

Jorren asks questions from Jorren's attention hierarchy, not Greg's. He may be more interested in Arlo's craft, Dena's route reality, or Ivena's customer logic than in the part Greg expected to impress him.

At least one supporting character says or does something that punctures Greg's self-presentation without deliberately humiliating him.

Greg should be useful only incidentally if at all. No filtration crisis. No technical lesson.

**Discovery:** Greg wants admiration. He has been pretending he only wants other people to own their domains.

**Residue:** the workshop is proud of Greg in a different way than Greg wanted Jorren to see.

### Reconstructed 31 — `The Next Road`

**Scene grammar:** departure / choice / aftermath.

Jorren's next road opportunity becomes concrete. It came through his own work/contact, not Greg or Sella.

Greg assumed, without admitting it, that Jorren's return meant time together or the possibility of a shared next contract.

Jorren has already made or now makes a defensible choice that does not include Greg.

Greg reacts imperfectly. He may overadvise packing, rates, route risk, or physical recovery because Old Greg's fear and current Greg's disappointment combine into control.

Jorren should push back in Jorren language, not deliver a therapeutic diagnosis.

Do not make Greg wrong about every practical concern. The social mistake can be using practical concern to disguise `I wanted you to stay / ask me.`

Jorren leaves or goes to complete his own preparation without resolving Greg's feelings.

The chapter ends on ordinary residue in Lower Rook: borrowed blanket, one fewer bowl, cards still out, Noll's wall noise, the unsent Tavin letter, no Mara packet yet, or another concrete object.

**Discovery:** Greg can understand another person's right to choose and still dislike the choice.

**Residue:** roads and home remain in conflict; Tavin's reply is still unsent or only now ready; Mara remains off-camera on her own clock.
