## Overall verdict before light finish

**MIXED, strongly leaning PASS.**

The temporal engine did not immediately recreate the old forward conveyor. The four chapters use elapsed time and independent clocks to create a connected human sequence:

- Noll acquires a kitchen opportunity while Jorren is away.
- Dena's workday reveals that filtration occupies only part of her economic life, while Greg's Tavin letter finally leaves the room.
- Mara's own Vey/packet clock creates a near-future visit and Greg makes a binding choice before knowing its cost.
- Jorren returns and does exactly what Greg asked for: he asks Greg onto the next road. Greg now has to refuse something he genuinely wants because he already told Mara he would be in Carrow.

That accumulation is temporal causality serving character rather than replacing it.

The run does contain several explicit anti-doctrine/editorial leaks and one continuity-breaking reference to a removed draft scene. These are LIGHT FINISH problems, not scene failures.

## Character challenger

### Greg — PASS

Across the four chapters, Greg remains specifically Greg because he:

- turns two copper into a chance to over-help
- listens for Noll's room and dislikes the quiet
- follows Dena partly because his other people are absent

This is not generic competent restraint.
