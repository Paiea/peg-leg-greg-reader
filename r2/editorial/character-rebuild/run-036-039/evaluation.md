# R2 Character-First Temporal A — Chapters 36–39 Evaluation

Status: **CONNECTED READER / CHARACTER / ANONYMOUS / REPETITION / TEMPORAL CHALLENGE**

Scope: numbered experimental A Chapters 36–39 after temporal reconciliation 001.

## Overall verdict before light finish

**MIXED, leaning PASS.**

The four-chapter run is materially alive and should not be re-outlined.

Its strongest achievement is temporal rather than structural: Greg's decision to stay in Carrow does not freeze the people around him. Noll acquires work that changes home. Mara arrives late because work and transport do not obey Greg. Greg and Mara become physically closer without the relationship becoming permanently available. Mara's second evening is mostly reclaimed by the job that brought her east in the first place. Jorren's road continues without Greg. Tavin's letter remains elsewhere.

That is the engine working.

The run still contains several small lines where the narrator explains why a scene is healthy, current, non-doctrinal, or sufficiently messy. Those are clear LIGHT FINISH losers. The underlying scenes survive.

## Chapter 36 — Four Nights

**PASS with one clear cut.**

What wins:

- Greg goes to The Brass Spoon partly because the quiet wall bothers him and partly because Mara's uncertain arrival leaves him restless.
- Noll is visibly different at work through movement, timing, mistakes, burns, and kitchen language rather than a new formal status.
- Vessa and Domm own the kitchen. Greg is a paying customer, not the problem-solver.
- Greg nearly stands when Noll burns a finger and has to tolerate the fact that this workplace does not require his intervention.
- Noll genuinely wants the better job and better skill.
- He still chooses four nights instead of five even though Brass Spoon pays more because he also cooks the Lower Rook carter supper and lives there.
- Vessa accommodates four for her own staffing reasons rather than delivering a lesson about work-life balance.
- the two copper physically return to Greg.
- ending on the scheduled quiet through the wall is strong ordinary residue.

Clear loser:

- after Noll says `Because I live there`, the prose explains the significance with `No speech about community. No declaration that Lower Rook was home.` The line already won. The explanation makes the engine visible.

Revision: trust Noll's sentence and move on.

## Chapter 37 — One Day Late

**MIXED -> LIGHT FINISH.**

What wins:

- Greg hears Mara working before he sees her.
- Mara has also asked for Greg twice. Wanting is reciprocal without becoming symmetrical perfection.
- the first reunion hug is initiated naturally and does not recover the missing kisses.
- Emmet corrects a Halden/Carrow date convention. Mara is competent without being omniscient.
- Mara bargains Emmet down from first bell to second because he has children. Her work-self remains human rather than optimized.
- the blue cup arrives as a ridiculous current object rather than a symbol explained to the reader.
- Mara and Noll recognize each other directly.
- Greg's declined Doss road becomes uncomfortable relationship information.
- Mara refuses to become the moral accounting that makes the missed road `worth it`.
- Greg admits resentment at timing and Beren without blaming Mara cleanly.
- dinner continues after the awkwardness.
- Mara chooses the Lower Rook turn herself.

Clear losers:

- `Current professional training.` after Emmet's correction is an evaluator label.
- `I am discovering everyone in my life had a separate life while I was busy forgetting mine.` states the rebuild thesis too directly in the middle of an otherwise specific Noll/Mara recognition beat.
- the immediate `Not broken glass` exchange exists mainly to instruct the scene how to handle memory damage and can be removed without losing the human beat.

Revision: keep Mara/Noll's apple-core history and Greg's discomfort; remove the abstract explanation.

## Chapter 38 — Two Keys

**MIXED -> LIGHT FINISH.**

What wins:

- Mara is wrong about the squeaky stair, or the building changed. Either way, she is allowed imperfect memory too.
- the two keys give a strong physical bridge between old belonging and current room life.
- Mara sees Greg's current room through objects rather than a guided tour.
- the Doss socks/half-packed bag expose how close Greg came to leaving more effectively than another argument could.
- Mara admits she would have been angry if he left while still refusing to become the reason he had to stay.
- Greg admits the reciprocal ugly reaction he would have had if she never arrived.
- neither person repairs the contradiction into a rule.
- Mara initiates the kiss.
- Old Greg creates a brief embodied interruption from another room/woman, but Greg gives Mara the only answer she actually needs: it was not from her.
- Mara wants to stay and still chooses sleep/work.
- Mara correctly catches Greg converting disappointment into approving `makes sense` language.
- Noll returns on his own Brass Spoon clock and intersects the ending without becoming an emotional referee.
- hand-holding on the walk back is a better landing than the B rehearsal's thesis sentence.

Clear losers:

- `At least I recognized the disease now.` after Greg realizes he wants Mara to admire his room explicitly turns character vanity into diagnosis.
- `There was no attempt to fix the contradiction.` explains the very messiness the dialogue already demonstrates.
- `Current evidence.` after the cheek-biting observation is another evaluator tag.
- `This kiss belonged to a room with one chair, two keys on a ring, a half-packed field bag, and Noll absent through the wall. I let that be enough.` is thematically accurate but over-authored. The kiss and room details already exist physically.

Revision: remove the diagnostic/evaluator statements and trust the objects, pauses, and dialogue.

## Chapter 39 — The East Desk

**PASS with small light finish.**

What wins:

- Mara cancels the second dinner because the work that brought her to Carrow actually claims the evening.
- Greg does ordinary preexisting work before going to Vey, so waiting for Mara does not suspend his city life.
- a low-pay road job still catches his attention. The road want survived the romance choice.
- Mara is corrected by Emmet on the local East Quay mark and accepts the evidence.
- Greg automatically tries to help with the ledger stack and is stopped because he does not know the local filing system.
- Greg becomes genuinely bored watching Mara work. This is excellent anti-romanticization: her life remains hers even when he is attracted to her.
- Mara and Emmet disagree and negotiate like coworkers rather than optimized adults.
- Greg states disappointment about the canceled dinner instead of disguising it as approval.
- Mara is disappointed too, but that does not restore the lost evening.
- Greg asks to remain in the office simply to be near her and then reads while she works.
- Mara initiates hand contact and the short goodnight kiss.
- Mara leaves at first bell, one day earlier than Greg wanted.
- the final room still contains Noll's scheduled absence, Jorren's separate road, Tavin's moving letter, and Mara's note without turning those facts into a choice-of-home thesis.

Clear losers:

- `Good. No. I was going to stop doing that eventually. Not today.` is now visibly arguing with the house habit rather than being Greg.
- `Important distinction.` after Greg becomes bored is an evaluator fragment. The boredom itself is the important discovery.
- `Mara did not make the loss smaller. Good. No. Still a bad word.` repeats the same anti-`good` meta machinery.

Revision: remove those self-conscious labels. Preserve the boredom, disappointment, and missed second evening.

## Character challenger

Name-stripped, he remains specific:

- checks a packet office too often and lies badly about coincidence
- wants roads, Mara, Noll's closeness, recognition, and to be chosen first

She:

- is wrong about a local ledger mark/date convention
- wants Greg
- wants her work done
- does not want to owe him the road
- would still have been angry if he left
- wants to stay in his room
- chooses not to
- cancels the next dinner
- gives Greg small current intimacy on her own timing
- remembers some old details and forgets others

He remains recognizable through food, noise, blunt economics, household ownership, kitchen ambition, soap, and refusal to maximize income at the cost of everything else.

### Emmet / Vessa — PASS AS LOCAL PEOPLE

They have domain authority without becoming interchangeable system designers. Keep them local and limited.
