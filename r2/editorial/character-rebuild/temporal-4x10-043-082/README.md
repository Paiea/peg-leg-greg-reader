## Purpose

Run four simultaneous ten-chapter future rehearsals from the same accepted Chapter-42 state. Each horizon aims for roughly 30,000 words, about 3,000 words per chapter-sized performance, so the experiment can discover character behavior, physical action, setting pressure, plot possibilities, pacing, and prose problems that outlines miss.

Total target: roughly 120,000 words of rehearsal evidence.

## Independence rule

During first rehearsal:

- A may use accepted truth through 42 and A-local consequences only.
- B may use accepted truth through 42 and B-local consequences only.
- C may use accepted truth through 42 and C-local consequences only.
- D may use accepted truth through 42 and D-local consequences only.

No horizon imports another horizon's invented events.

After all forty performances exist, the audit may compare them and harvest portable discoveries.

## Fidelity rule

Aim around 3,000 prose words per chapter-sized performance. Healthy variation is roughly 2,500–3,500. Do not pad dead scenes, but repeated shortfall is evidence the engine failed to create chapter-scale life.

Rehearsal prose should be real enough to judge:

- voice and cadence
- embodied Greg POV
- thought/action mismatch
- dialogue
- character autonomy
- relationship language
- setting and sensory detail
- physical movement
- work and money
- magic and danger
- scene transitions
- chapter endings
- causal momentum

## Physical-action rule

Increase verbs, movement, bodily consequence, and geographic change.

Action is not synonymous with combat. Carrying, climbing, hauling, cooking, walking, riding, packing, crossing water, working, repairing, getting wet, being tired, touching, avoiding touch, changing clothes, physical embarrassment, dangerous labor, sword work, magic under pressure, accidents, pursuit, and fights can all count when they materially change the scene.

Greg may think at length, but thought should usually remain attached to a body doing something in a place.

## Greg contradiction rule

Greg's internal explanation and behavior may disagree.

Do not immediately translate the contradiction for the reader.

He can claim not to care while rearranging his day. He can decide not to interfere and interfere. He can want a road and physically build a home. He can understand a boundary and still resent it. He can know the correct interpretation and emotionally behave according to the wrong one.

## Anti-conveyor rule

Do not default to:

`problem -> professional -> boundary -> narrow useful action -> sensible result -> lesson`

Do not turn every chapter into work, training, restraint, or progression proof.

Each ten-chapter rehearsal should contain different scene grammars and enough causal connection to feel like story rather than ten unrelated demonstrations.
