# Horizon A Rehearsal Brief — Coordinates 43–52

Status: **REHEARSAL ONLY / ZERO AUTHORITY**

## Pressure question

What does Greg actually do in the immediate days after Chapter 42 when several futures are open but none is scheduled?

Mara is west. Tavin's truthful reply is outbound. Jorren is still on the Doss/Kelm road. Noll's work has changed the rhythm of Lower Rook. The Guild still exists. Greg wants roads and has a packed pair of socks without a road.

Do not answer this by giving Greg the obvious next contract in the first paragraph and letting work own ten chapters.

## Rehearsal purpose

Test whether immediate post-42 R2 can become more physical and plot-active without abandoning the character-first rebuild.

Greg should be forced to move through Carrow, work with his body, make choices while distracted by people, and confront the fact that he says he wants a road while repeatedly attaching himself to current life.

## Useful live clocks

- Jorren's six-to-ten-day road clock may plausibly bring him back during this rehearsal, but not on demand.
- Tavin's reply is outbound. A second packet or freight arrival is possible but not required.
- Noll has regular Brass Spoon shifts and Lower Rook obligations.
- Guild work is available.
- Filtration exists independently and may intersect Greg without needing rescue.
- Halden is an invitation, not a scheduled destination.

## Action palette

Prefer scenes that require motion:

- Guild board and actual city navigation
- river warehouses, loading, lifting, ropes, hoists, wet stone, carts, crowds
- a job where magic or sword skill is used under pressure rather than demonstrated in a lesson
- a physical mistake or overreach Greg cannot narrate away
- Jorren returning with bodily evidence of the road
- Greg helping Noll or Lower Rook while resenting the time cost
- Greg preparing for a road before knowing whether one exists
- weather or urban infrastructure changing a plan

At least one dangerous sequence should be genuinely dangerous if the run earns it.

## Character pressure

Greg can think he is choosing freedom while behaving possessively toward home, friends, or opportunities.

Let him be vain about being useful, annoyed when someone does not need him, jealous of someone else's road, and tempted to use Old Greg knowledge too confidently.

Supporting people should interrupt his self-story through their own decisions.

## Setting pressure

Deepen Carrow beyond Lower Rook and the familiar workshop. Use the river, Guild approaches, freight districts, bridges, markets, poorer streets, richer commercial surfaces, weather, smells, noise, and magic infrastructure as constraints.

## Prohibitions

- no automatic Halden trip
- no automatic Tavin arrival
- no instant Jorren return in the first chapter
- no ten-chapter work carousel
- no support-assessment chapter merely because it remains pending
- no tidy lesson endings

## Success

By the end of ten rehearsal chapters, A should feel like a plausible near future but remain easy to kill. It should discover what immediate story pressure has the most life, not prove a preselected arc.
