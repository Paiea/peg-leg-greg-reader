# Horizon B Rehearsal Brief — Coordinates 53–62

Status: **REHEARSAL ONLY / ZERO AUTHORITY**

## Pressure question

What does R2 feel like if Greg actually gets out of Carrow and spends enough time on a road for the fantasy of `wanting roads` to encounter weather, labor, danger, boredom, other people's authority, and the limits of Old Greg's map?

This is a local rehearsal possibility, not a promise that A sends him away.

## Rehearsal purpose

Stress-test Greg away from his increasingly comfortable Carrow support network. Give him real physical travel, practical discomfort, and situations where he cannot solve social or magical uncertainty by consulting future memory.

The road should not be an adventure montage. Make travel expensive in time, body, sleep, food, wet gear, horse/cart problems, route decisions, and relationships.

## Local assumptions allowed only inside B

B may rehearse a plausible future in which Greg accepts a road opportunity after Chapter 42. It may use Jorren as a road-connected person if useful, but Jorren must have his own employer/contact logic and must not exist merely to invite Greg.

No B-local event becomes true outside B.

## Action palette

- loading and balancing a cart
- walking or riding for hours
- wet road, mud, cold, bad footing, damaged bridge, ford, ferry, or route closure
- sleeping badly in shared space
- animal or nonhuman ecology that changes practical decisions
- magic used while moving or under time pressure
- sword work that is ugly, short, tiring, and consequential if violence occurs
- pursuit, rescue, accident, or dangerous labor where another worker owns the control path
- physical aftereffects that continue into later B chapters

## Character pressure

Greg wants the road before he has it. Once on it, let him discover that wanting movement and enjoying every part of travel are different things.

Old Greg may recognize a place and still be wrong about what exists there now. That should create emotional and practical distortion, not simply an information puzzle.

Greg can think he is independent while checking packet opportunities, missing Lower Rook noises, thinking about Mara, comparing current companions to future people, or hoarding a small object from home.

Another person should make at least one major route/work choice Greg dislikes but cannot legitimately own.

## Setting pressure

Move through multiple distinct physical environments. Roads should have surfaces, grades, drainage, farms, inns, roadside labor, tolls, ferries, small settlements, vegetation, smells, insects, animals, weather, and local magic practices.

At least one setting should surprise Old Greg rather than merely confirm his map.

## Plot pressure

The ten chapters should accumulate a movement: departure or commitment, road reality, complication, a meaningful reversal, consequence, and a changed relationship to travel. Do not make the ending `Greg learns roads are hard`.

## Prohibitions

- no training montage
- no generic bandit sequence solely to add action
- no Old Greg instantly solving local magic
- no Jorren reduced to sidekick
- no automatic return to Carrow as a tidy final beat
- no chapter that is only route exposition

## Success

B succeeds if it discovers whether R2 can support sustained physical travel and danger while remaining intimate, funny, specific, and socially messy.
