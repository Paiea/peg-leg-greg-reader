Carrow appeared one day later than planned.

Nobody apologized.

The last leg was boring enough to feel earned.

Dry road. Two tolls. One broken strap on salt wagon repaired before it became interesting. Lunch under elm. Brin returned my oversized sock washed badly and still damp.

"You too."

Unfair.

Accidental team gone.

Road quieter.

"Good people."

"Yes."

No need explain.

East Gate queue long.

Base rate. Delay day. Held pay Kelm. Extra four from timber rescue split. Costs deducted from route purse not our wages.

My share larger than expected.

Thirty-nine copper.

I counted twice.

Road had become financially real.

There.

Invitation.

Not Gold.

Not rank.

"Yes."

Too fast.

"Ask job first."

"Depends."

"Better."

Not same party as A because horizons independent, though writer pressure intentionally tests same concept. I forced myself to build different people.

Gold coordination.

Shield carrier.

Embarrassing.
