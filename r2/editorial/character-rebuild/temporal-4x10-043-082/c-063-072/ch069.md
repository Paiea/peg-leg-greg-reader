# Rehearsal C69: River Fire

The warehouse caught fire while Mara was explaining that I needed to leave her office.

Timing rescued us both from argument.

I had gone to Vey after breakfast for a bad reason.

Mara had not asked.

I wanted to know her schedule before deciding whether to take another Guild job.

This was a small form of organizing myself around her while insisting I was independent.

She noticed immediately.

"You don't need my day plan."

"I need to know when you're free."

"Ask when I'm free."

"That is the same information."

"No. One is about us. One is my whole workday."

There.

Boundary precise enough to make me defensive.

"I'm not monitoring you."

"You came to my office at first bell."

"Gate let me."

"Gate regrets it."

Sera, at next desk, said without looking up, "Gate absolutely regrets."

I was preparing an excellent response when bell outside began ringing.

Not city bell.

Vey yard bell rapid, continuous.

Everyone moved.

Mara's chair hit wall as she stood.

Sera grabbed red ledger.

"Fire."

Smoke already visible through window.

Black column from river warehouse north side.

We ran.

Office clerks became organized chaos in yard.

Workers rolling barrels away. Someone unhooking horses from carts. Fire crew dragging hose line from river pump.

Warehouse three, according to Sera.

Stored cloth, paper, lamp oil? Mara shouted to clerk.

"What section?"

"East half cloth. West sealed mixed."

"Oil?"

"Two barrels lamp, south wall."

Bad.

Flame visible beneath roof edge.

I stopped near yard line.

No role.

Mara ran toward manifest station.

Sera to evacuation count.

I looked for authority.

I went to her.

"Guild Bronze, visiting. Magic capable. Need hands?"

She looked once.

"Can you hold hose?"

"Yes."

"Then hold hose. No magic until I ask."

Perfect.

I joined line.

River pump drove water through leather-reinforced hose. Pressure surged irregularly with hand crew on piston.

Three of us held nozzle line aimed at warehouse door.

Heat immediate.

Smoke turned eyes wet.

Fire inside ceiling beams, cloth bales burning bright.

"Low!"

"Roof!"

Lamp barrel maybe.

Glass and wood fragments.

Blood.

Danger clarity flooded me.

Clean necessity again.
