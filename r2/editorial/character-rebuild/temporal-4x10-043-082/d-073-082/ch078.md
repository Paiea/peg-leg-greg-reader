# Rehearsal D78: Gold

Status: **TEMPORAL 4×10 REHEARSAL / ZERO AUTHORITY**

The Gold party arrived carrying breakfast.

This ruined my expectations usefully.

Four of them entered Bell Tile courtyard just after first bell while I was helping Pellin move filter frames away from the warm wall.

No dramatic cloaks.

No crowd.

One paper sack of fried bread between them.

Gold tabs still unmistakable.

Guild had sent them as city-contracted hazard survey, not combat rescue.

Names were rehearsal-only and owned nothing beyond D.

Veyra led, a compact woman in dark scale coat with a short spear collapsed into three locking sections along pack.

Tomas? Avoid existing Tomas elsewhere but D independent, still generic. Use Renn: broad man with two narrow shields strapped one over another and heavy bracers scored from use.

Seli? Already B used Seli, cross horizon name contamination maybe not fact but better unique. Mage Orra, thin older woman with copper-thread gloves and a wand case containing six rods rather than one staff.

Fourth was Pell? Established Mara friend only in C, avoid. Nesk, small man with soft boots, hooked knife, coils, chalk, and more pockets than dignity.

They ate while Vaska briefed.

This impressed me.

Efficient chewing.

Vaska showed old plans.

Veyra asked only factual questions.

"Confirmed creature count?"

"Two observed signals. One body seen."

"Aggression?"

"Defensive thrash only."

"Injury?"

"Greg bruised knee. Not creature."

Everyone looked at me.

"Ladder."

Vaska continued.

"One bakery flood. One washhouse surge. Heat pulses. Old copper-glass mortar network."

Orra held flake Harl had collected inside glass vial.

"Conductive. Low storage. Probably transfer medium."

I wanted to ask how she knew.

Did not interrupt.

Nesk did.

"Probably?"

Orra looked at him.

"I have one flake."

"Good."

Their language was sparse without being robotic because history filled gaps I did not know.

Renn ate last fried bread piece.

Veyra noticed.

"That was mine."

"You talked."

"I lead."

"Then lead hungry."

Elite people remained thieves.

I liked them more.

Veyra looked at me.

"You first sensed wall?"

"Yes."

"Show."

Not ask for biography.

We went below Bell Tile.

Warm wall still cool after creature release.

I explained dry patch, first pulse, lower gate, Old Salt flood.

Veyra listened while physically checking path.

Nesk marked distances with chalk.

Orra sampled blue mortar at three points without touching bare.

Renn tested every handhold before anyone else used it.

Coordination almost irritating.

At crawl gate, Veyra said, "Greg, behind Orra. Vaska behind Greg. Ilan last."

"Why?"

"If your sensing changes, Orra hears. If you panic, Vaska knows route. If ceiling drops, Ilan owns line."

"I don't panic."

Five people stared.

"Fine."

We crawled.

Nesk went first alone on thin safety cord, moving quietly enough that water barely splashed.

He reached lower shaft and signaled with fingers.

Two.

Veyra repeated to Renn.

No spoken relay.

They had grammar.

Old Greg recognized shapes.

Not this exact system.

Enough to feel ache.

At shaft, Renn built anchor with Ilan.

No dominance fight.

Renn asked, "Your line standard?"

Ilan showed knots.

Renn said, "Use yours."

Gold did not automatically overwrite local professional.

Important.

We descended.

Lower gate now partly torn from frame after Old Salt surge.

Water level lower.

Creature gone from visible side.

Blue mortar glowed faintly.

Orra opened one copper rod and passed over line.

It vibrated.

"Flow east-west," she said.

"Mana?" I asked.

"Heat carrying charge. Not same thing."

"Could creature create?"

"Could. Could use. Could follow."

Three possibilities.

No answer fetish.

Veyra looked at bent gate.

"We go west side?"

Vaska said, "Old Salt access flooded but pumped."

"Not through here."

Veyra pointed at damaged frame.

"Structural unknown."

I wanted to argue opening now gave path.

Did not.

Then signal.

Three pulses through wall.

Pause.

Two.

Stronger.

Orra's rod lit red at tip.

Nesk crouched near channel, held fingers in air above water, not touching.

"Coming upstream."

"How know?" I asked.

"Ripples."

Of course.

Veyra said, "Up."

Immediate.

"We just got here."

She looked at me.

Not offended.

"And pressure is changing."

"Could observe from platform."

"We can observe from higher platform."

Only geometry.

Everyone climbed.

I did too, but last half second enough to see red light moving beneath brown water.

One.

Two creatures.

Maybe three.

If we had stayed, platform might still hold.

Might.

Outside, she ate remaining crumb from paper sack.

"Old Salt next."

"That's it?"

No denial.
