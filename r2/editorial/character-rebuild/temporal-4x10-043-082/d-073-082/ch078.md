# Rehearsal D78: Gold

Status: **TEMPORAL 4×10 REHEARSAL / ZERO AUTHORITY**

---

The Gold party arrived carrying breakfast.

This ruined my expectations usefully.

Four of them entered Bell Tile courtyard just after first bell while I was helping Pellin move filter frames away from the warm wall.

No dramatic cloaks.

No crowd.

One paper sack of fried bread between them.

Gold tabs still unmistakable.

Guild had sent them as city-contracted hazard survey, not combat rescue.

Veyra led, a compact woman in a dark scale coat with a short spear collapsed into three locking sections along her pack.

Renn was broad through the shoulders and carried two narrow shields strapped one over another, his heavy bracers scored from use.

Orra was older and thin, with copper-thread gloves and a wand case holding six separate rods instead of one staff.

Nesk was small, soft-booted, and equipped with a hooked knife, coils, chalk, and more pockets than dignity.

They ate while Vaska briefed.

This impressed me.

Efficient chewing.

Vaska showed old plans.

Veyra asked only factual questions.

"Confirmed creature count?"

"Two observed signals. One body seen."

"Aggression?"

"Defensive thrash only."

"Injury?"

"Greg bruised knee. Not creature."

Everyone looked at me.

"Ladder."

Vaska continued.

"One bakery flood. One washhouse surge. Heat pulses. Old copper-glass mortar network."

Orra held the flake Harl had collected inside a glass vial.

"Conductive. Low storage. Probably transfer medium."

I wanted to ask how she knew.

Did not interrupt.

Nesk did.

"Probably?"

Orra looked at him.

"I have one flake."

"Good."

Their language was sparse without being robotic because history filled gaps I did not know.

Renn ate the last fried bread piece.

Veyra noticed.

"That was mine."

"You talked."

"I lead."

"Then lead hungry."

Elite people remained thieves.

I liked them more.

Veyra looked at me.

"You first sensed wall?"

"Yes."

"Show."

No biography requested.

We went below Bell Tile.

The warm wall remained cool after the creature release.

I explained the dry patch, first pulse, lower gate, and Old Salt flood.

Veyra listened while physically checking the path.

Nesk marked distances with chalk.

Orra sampled blue mortar at three points without touching bare skin to it.

Renn tested every handhold before anyone else used it.

Coordination almost irritating.

At the crawl gate, Veyra said, "Greg, behind Orra. Vaska behind Greg. Ilan last."

"Why?"

"If your sensing changes, Orra hears. If you panic, Vaska knows route. If ceiling drops, Ilan owns line."

"I don't panic."

Five people stared.

"Fine."

We crawled.

Nesk went first alone on a thin safety cord, moving quietly enough that water barely splashed.

He reached the lower shaft and signaled with fingers.

Two.

Veyra repeated it to Renn.

No spoken relay.

They had grammar.

Old Greg recognized shapes.

Not this exact system.

Enough to feel ache.

At the shaft, Renn built anchor with Ilan.

No dominance fight.

Renn asked, "Your line standard?"

Ilan showed knots.

Renn said, "Use yours."

Gold did not automatically overwrite the local professional.

Important.

We descended.

The lower gate was now partly torn from its frame after the Old Salt surge.

Water level lower.

Creature gone from the visible side.

Blue mortar glowed faintly.

Orra opened one copper rod and passed it over the line.

It vibrated.

"Flow east-west," she said.

"Mana?" I asked.

"Heat carrying charge. Not same thing."

"Could creature create it?"

"Could. Could use it. Could follow it."

Three possibilities.

No answer fetish.

Veyra looked at the bent gate.

"We go west side?"

Vaska said, "Old Salt access flooded but pumped."

"Not through here."

Veyra pointed at the damaged frame.

"Structural unknown."

I wanted to argue that the opening now gave us a path.

I did not.

Then the signal came.

Three pulses through the wall.

Pause.

Two.

Stronger.

Orra's rod lit red at the tip.

Nesk crouched near the channel, holding fingers in the air above the water without touching it.

"Coming upstream."

"How know?" I asked.

"Ripples."

Of course.

Veyra said, "Up."

Immediate.

"We just got here."

She looked at me.

Not offended.

"And pressure is changing."

"Could observe from platform."

"We can observe from a higher platform."

There was no ego in retreat.

Only geometry.

Everyone climbed.

I did too, but a last half second was enough to see red light moving beneath brown water.

One.

Then another.

Two creatures.

Maybe three.

At crawl level, water surged through the lower channel.

The gate frame below broke completely.

Iron slammed the wall.

If we had stayed, the platform might still have held.

Might.

Veyra did not look pleased.

She simply kept moving.

We exited Bell Tile's service passage.

Outside, she ate the remaining crumb from the paper sack.

"Old Salt next."

I stared.

"That's it?"

"For this access."

"You didn't see them."

"I saw enough to not put six people below a failing gate."

"Could have learned more."

"Yes."

No denial.

"Then why not?"

Veyra tightened her spear strap.

"Because information is not free just because you're curious."

That line hit harder because she was Gold and I wanted her to validate the curiosity.

Instead she recognized it and refused.

Old Greg memories supplied several Gold leaders who would have stayed longer.

Some died.

Names remained fog.

That was not an argument either.

At Old Salt, the Gold party did not enter immediately.

Nesk walked the entire building exterior, counted drains, and found one old vent hidden behind a trash shed.

Orra tested warm runoff from the pump.

Renn inspected the brick stair opening.

Veyra talked to Ema about evacuation routes.

Ema made them move flour first.

Golds moved flour.

All four.

Renn carried two sacks.

Ema told him one.

He obeyed.

I smiled.

"What?" he asked.

"Nothing."

"Good."

At lunch they sat in the bakery eating fresh bread because Ema charged the city account.

I sat nearby, not part of the party but part of the local survey.

No one offered recruitment.

Nesk asked about my magic.

"What can you actually do now?"

I listed bounded supports, sensing, small force, ward work.

He stopped me when I drifted into Old Greg capabilities.

"Now."

"That's what I said."

"You said later version twice."

I had.

Annoying.

Orra looked over.

"Old knowledge useful?"

"Sometimes."

"Then label confidence."

"I do."

Vaska coughed loudly.

Traitor.

Veyra smiled for the first time.

The Gold party was not mystical.

They were four very practiced people who had built systems around each other's bad habits.

That was somehow more aspirational.
