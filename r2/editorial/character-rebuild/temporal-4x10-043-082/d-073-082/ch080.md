# Rehearsal D80: Open Water

Status: **TEMPORAL 4×10 REHEARSAL / ZERO AUTHORITY**

---

The creatures came to the river because the city accidentally gave them a road.

Two days after cistern abort, drainage crews opened an old relief channel downstream to reduce pressure beneath Old Salt.

Vaska had warned everyone this might move water.

Nobody knew it would move animals.

I was at Bell Tile when river bells started.

Not emergency fire bells.

Three long, two short.

The same rhythm.

Pellin looked at me.

"Three, two."

"Yes."

We ran to street.

Other people did too.

River quay north of washhouse had become chaos.

Warm water poured from old stone outlet normally dry. Red light pulsed beneath surface. Three translucent creatures circled in shallow berth, one much larger than others.

Boats trying clear.

People crowding railings.

City guards pushing back.

Gold party already there because they had been inspecting relief outlet.

Veyra stood on quay edge issuing commands.

Renn had one shield deployed, not against creature but as moving barrier keeping workers away from water.

Orra's copper rods planted in stone, threads forming faint lattice over outlet.

Nesk was waist-deep? No, he would not casually enter charged water. He moved along moored barge using overhead line to inspect from above.

I reported to Vaska first.

"Need me?"

She pointed at western stairs.

"Keep civilians off."

Not glamorous.

I did.

A fish seller tried pass because her stall was inside closure.

"My knives."

"Not now."

"They'll steal."

"City guard there."

"City guard steals worse."

She had point perhaps.

I still held line.

Then first creature surged onto quay.

Not attack exactly.

Water under it pushed, body slid over low edge like seal. It thrashed among rope coils.

Crowd screamed.

My civilians moved backward except fish seller who leaned forward to see.

I physically turned her by shoulders.

"Move."

She slapped my hand.

"Don't touch me."

"Then move yourself."

She did while insulting ancestry.

Creature's red ridge flashed.

Quay ward stones answered with blue light.

Interaction violent.

A discharge cracked across wet stone.

One dockworker fell.

Renn moved before sound finished.

Shield down between worker and creature, second shield unfolded from back in one motion. Orra pulled one copper rod and grounded it against dry stone. Veyra signaled two fingers.

Nesk cut a mooring rope.

Why?

Barge swung outward with current, creating open water lane beside creature.

Seamless.

Creature saw lane? Felt flow. It turned toward river.

Renn did not strike.

He used shields to narrow visual path, advancing sideways.

Veyra tapped spear butt on stone at controlled rhythm, not threatening.

Creature moved.

Back into water.

No fight.

I was impressed enough to become useless for two seconds.

Then dockworker groaned.

I ran because Vaska shouted, "Greg!"

Worker on back, conscious, burn across one forearm from discharge.

"Don't touch wet skin," Orra called.

Right.

Charge possible.

I pulled dry coat from nearby crate and used it to grip shoulder fabric, dragging worker six feet from wet line with another guard.

Orra checked charge with rod before healer touched.

The second creature surfaced near moored skiff.

Smaller.

It struck hull repeatedly.

Three.

Pause.

Two.

Not rage.

Signal to larger maybe.

Then water beneath outlet bulged.

Third creature, enormous, came partly through relief arch and stuck.

Body too wide for stone opening.

Pressure behind increased.

Red ridge blazed.

Warm water volume doubled.

"Gate it?" Vaska shouted.

"No!" Orra and Veyra together.

Closing could trap it and recreate pressure.

Need widen outlet or reverse flow.

Stone arch ancient.

Breaking dangerous but possible.

Veyra looked at city mason Harl.

"Can we remove side block?"

Harl examined from dry flank.

"One. Not top."

"How fast?"

"Ten minutes if it doesn't move."

Creature moved.

Arch cracked.

"Five," Harl revised.

Everyone worked.

This became action without combat.

Harl and two masons hammered mortar at lower side block.

Renn shielded them from water discharge, copper-lined shield edge sparking faint.

Orra managed charge with grounding rods, calling when wet stone became unsafe.

Nesk used barge line to keep floating debris from trapping creature.

Veyra coordinated timing.

I carried stone wedges and moved civilians farther when outlet spray increased.

Then top of arch shed brick.

One struck mason shoulder.

He fell toward water.

I caught belt.

Weight dragged me to one knee.

Bruised knee exploded pain.

Renn caught my collar with shield hand, pulled both back.

No time to thank.

Harl took mason's hammer himself.

"Block!"

Side stone finally loosened.

We pried.

It dropped into river.

Opening widened maybe eight inches.

Creature compressed body, pushed.

For one terrifying moment stuck harder.

Then slid free with rush of hot water.

Ten-foot body launched into berth.

Renn braced shields.

I drew sword.

Veyra shouted, "No blades!"

I stopped.

Creature did not attack.

It rolled, red ridge flashing, then dove.

Two smaller followed.

Three sets of red pulse moved downstream.

Pause.

Two.

River swallowed them.

Quay remained steaming wreck.

Everyone breathed.

Then fish seller shouted from behind barrier, "My knives!"

Reality resumed.

Aftermath took longer than event.

Burned worker to healer.

Mason shoulder wrapped.

Quay stones inspected.

Outlet temporarily braced wider.

Barge retied.

One skiff hull cracked by creature impacts.

Insurance people arrived before floor dried.

Vaska recorded every decision.

Gold party sat on curb eating cold bread.

Renn's shield edge had a melted copper streak.

I stared.

He saw.

"Want it?"

"What?"

"Damage. You keep looking like collector."

"No. How did shield take discharge?"

"Copper sacrificial strip. Replaceable."

He unlatched blackened strip with tool.

Modified gear.

Not magic artifact destiny.

Maintenance.

"Expensive?"

"Cheaper than arm."

He tossed ruined strip into scrap bucket.

I wanted that level of equipment competence.

Not strip specifically.

Integration.

Veyra sat beside me.

"You drew sword."

"Yes."

"Why?"

"Large animal came out fast."

"Reasonable."

I waited for correction.

"And you stopped when told. Also reasonable."

That mattered.

Not every impulse flaw.

Sometimes sword was appropriate backup.

"Would you have killed if it charged?"

"Yes."

No anti-action purity.

Gold party would fight when needed.

They simply did not need to this time.

I liked that more.
