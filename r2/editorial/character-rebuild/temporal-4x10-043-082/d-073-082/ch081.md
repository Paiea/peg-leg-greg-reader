# Rehearsal D81: Seven Seconds

Status: **TEMPORAL 4×10 REHEARSAL / ZERO AUTHORITY**

---

The fight lasted seven seconds.

The work around it took nine hours.

That ratio felt correct.

After river release, city closed warm-water branches and sent survey teams to downstream outlets. Gold party remained one more day to confirm creatures had not entered occupied cellars.

We found one beneath a dye warehouse.

Not trapped.

Cornered.

Different.

Warehouse manager had ignored closure because warm floor dried cloth faster.

He reopened an old valve overnight.

Current commerce tried to kill us with efficiency.

By morning workers heard impacts under floor.

Vaska arrived furious enough to heat building without infrastructure.

Gold party with her.

I came because Bell Tile owners wanted confirmation network branch separated from their service line and because Vaska had explicitly requested my sensing.

Actual role.

Dye warehouse cellar held shallow stone basin connected to warm return channel. Creature lay half out of water behind stacked dye barrels.

Medium size, eight feet.

Red ridge dim.

One forelimb bleeding from broken glass.

Workers had surrounded it with poles before city arrived.

Bad.

Animal had nowhere to move.

Veyra cleared cellar.

"Only survey team."

Manager objected about barrels.

Renn physically escorted him upstairs by standing where manager wanted to go until manager chose elsewhere.

Elegant.

We planned exit path.

Open rear channel gate to river branch. Move barrels. Give creature corridor.

Simple.

Then manager's foreman dropped a barrel on stairs.

Not intentionally.

Barrel rolled into cellar, hit stone, split.

Purple dye and sharp alcohol smell flooded floor.

Creature panicked.

Seven seconds began.

Second one: red ridge flashed white.

Orra shouted, "Charge!"

Second two: creature lunged toward nearest movement, which was Nesk shifting barrel.

Veyra's spear unfolded with metallic snap.

Renn shield hit floor between them.

Second three: creature struck shield with forelimb. Discharge blew copper strip sparks. Renn slid backward two feet.

I drew sword.

Second four: Nesk was already over barrel stack, not retreating straight, opening corridor behind creature by cutting retaining rope.

Barrels rolled aside.

Second five: Veyra drove spear butt, not blade, into floor beside creature's head, redirecting lunge. Orra grounded charge through two rods and shouted "Now!"

Second six: creature twisted toward open rear channel.

Second seven: I saw its tail coming at Vaska.

No time to ask.

I used force.

A short hard plane between tail and her knees.

Tail struck mana support, deflected upward enough to miss.

My shoulder snapped with recoil.

Creature dove into rear channel.

Fight over.

Seven seconds.

Then everyone stood breathing in purple dye.

Vaska looked at me.

"Thanks."

Simple.

No boundary lecture because action needed and worked.

My shoulder hurt.

Orra checked immediately.

"Move arm."

I did.

"Pain?"

"Yes."

"Joint?"

"No. Muscle."

"Stop magic today."

I wanted to argue.

Did not.

Seven seconds had enough evidence.

Nesk looked at Renn's shield.

New copper strip already scorched.

"Two in two days."

Renn said, "Invoice city."

Vaska said, "No. Warehouse manager."

Everyone smiled.

Economic justice.

Creature moved through rear channel toward river according to Orra's fading charge trace.

We did not chase.

Important.

The remaining nine hours:

We isolated valve manager reopened.

Harl sealed access with removable plate rather than permanent brick.

Warehouse workers moved forty-three barrels because purple spill made lower room unsafe.

I carried with one arm less useful.

Veyra told me to stop twice.

Third time Renn simply took barrel from me.

"I can carry."

"You can also be annoying empty-handed."

Gold humor.

Manager argued lost production.

Vaska documented unauthorized valve opening.

Ivena appeared somehow because Bell Tile liability touched same line and spent an hour dismantling manager's attempt to shift blame.

Pellin brought lunch and asked Gold party eleven questions before Dena limited him to three.

He chose badly.

"How much does shield cost?"

Renn answered.

Pellin's eyes widened.

"How many monsters you kill?"

"This month? None."

He looked disappointed.

Third question:

"Why Gold then?"

Silence.

Renn considered.

"Because Guild pays me to make expensive problems smaller."

Better than rank sermon.

Pellin accepted.

I did too.

Later Veyra inspected my force technique.

Not lesson formally.

She asked me recreate static plane unloaded.

I did.

Then she pushed against it with spear shaft.

Support failed sideways.

"Your plane is strong normal to surface, weak shear."

"I know."

She looked.

"You know now?"

"Yes."

Fair.

"Tail hit mostly normal. Lucky geometry."

Lucky.

Word struck old memory.

Rock.

Mangled leg.

Someone saying Lucky.

A friend knocked unconscious maybe.

My stomach dropped.

Plane vanished.

Veyra saw.

"What?"

"Memory."

"Bad?"

"Probably."

She did not demand.

"Sit."

I sat on dye-stained crate.

Old Greg memory flickered harder.

Two party members trapped under collapsed stone.

A horde approaching.

One couldn't walk.

Someone arguing to stay.

Me saying we leave them.

Or maybe someone else.

Then a blow.

Lucky.

No full names.

No clean context.

I hated it.

Seven-second fight clarity disappeared into old-life ambiguity.

Veyra handed water.

"You don't have to solve it here."

I looked at her.

"You don't know what it is."

"Correct."

"Then how know?"

"Because we're standing in purple dye and you look like you're fifty miles away. Neither improves by guessing."

Practical.

I drank.

By dusk cellar smelled less dangerous.

Creature gone.

Valve sealed.

Barrels relocated.

Manager fined pending review.

Gold party had fought for seven seconds.

Most of what made them Gold happened before and after it.
