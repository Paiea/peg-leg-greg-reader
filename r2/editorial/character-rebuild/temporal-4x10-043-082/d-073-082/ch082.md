The Gold party left Carrow arguing about breakfast money.

No recruitment.

No secret test.

No one told me I was destined for anything.

Renn owed Veyra two copper because he had eaten her fried bread twice.

This became the final dispute.

I met them at Guild because Vaska wanted one last joint summary before city released contract.

My shoulder was sore from tail deflection. Knee still bruised. Boots finally dry.

Physical receipt of D horizon.

Veyra's team looked less impressive indoors.

Gear spread across bench for maintenance.

Renn replacing scorched copper shield strip.

Orra polishing corrosion from rods.

Nesk sewing torn pocket.

Veyra oiling spear locks.

Gold competence included chores.

I liked that.

Vaska read findings:

Old warm-return network exists beneath at least six blocks.

Copper-glass mortar transfers heat and low mana charge.

Unknown translucent river fauna uses or responds to network.

At least three individuals observed, probably more.

No evidence of deliberate aggression except cornered response.

Closing old channels can trap animals and raise local pressure.

Reopening without survey can create occupied-building hazard.

City should map endpoints before permanent work.

Bell Tile service pipe should be physically separated from old line.

Old Salt access closed pending structural repair.

Dye warehouse unauthorized valve sealed.

That was the revelation.

A maintenance report.

Not species name.

Not ancient civilization.

Enough to change city behavior.

I asked Orra, "What do you think creatures are?"

She shrugged.

"River animals."

"No classification?"

"Eventually."

"You aren't curious?"

She looked offended.

"I'm very curious. That's why I don't want first answer to become permanent."

There.

I wrote that mentally, then decided not to because borrowing Gold woman's line as personal doctrine felt cheap.

Nesk asked Vaska whether city wanted downstream ecology survey.

"Different contract."

"We available?"

Veyra answered, "No. North job."

Their life moved elsewhere.

Not around Greg.

Guild clerk brought payment sheets.

Gold contract amount made my eyes hurt.

Not absurd wealth. Enough to explain gear maintenance and why city had hesitated calling them.

Vaska signed hazard extension.

I received local specialist pay for two days where formally requested, less than Gold by large factor.

Correct.

Pellin would have asked exact ratio.

I did not.

Almost.

Veyra caught me looking.

"Ask."

"How much per person after Guild?"

She told me.

I whistled.

"Still want Gold?"

Question direct.

"Yes."

"For money?"

"Some."

"Good."

She laughed at herself.

"Sorry. Apparently Carrow says that too much."

I smiled.

"For what else?" she asked.

I considered.

"The way you move together."

Renn looked over from shield.

"We argue constantly."

"I noticed bread."

"Not bread. Everything."

Veyra said, "He means during work."

"Still argue. Faster."

Nesk pulled thread with teeth.

"You saw final version. We practiced ugly."

That mattered.

"How long?"

"Us four? Three years. Orra joined later."

Orra said, "Two and half."

"Feels longer."

She threw cleaning rag at him.

No mystique.

Practice, relationships, equipment, shared corrections, enough fights to stop needing some words.

Old Greg had remembered formations without people.

Current Greg wanted people enough that formations might eventually have names.

Dangerous sentimental thought.

I let it remain anyway.

Veyra packed spear.

"Your force plane."

"What about it?"

"Useful. Don't make it moving until you can hold shear."

"I have practiced moving."

"Not in this horizon's facts."

Obviously she could not say that. Rehearsal contamination. Remove. Better line:

"Useful. Don't make it carry what your shoulder can't correct."

"How know shoulder?"

"You keep touching it."

Body again revealing thought.

"Fair."

"Train with someone who can hit plane sideways."

Specific recommendation.

No prophecy.

Renn finished shield.

Team stood almost together without cue.

Not magical.

Habits aligned.

I walked with them partway because my route to Bell Tile overlapped.

Veyra paid because breakfast debt accounting had become too complicated.

Renn stole one.

Gold party disappeared into ordinary crowd before gate.

"Accurate enough."

City reimbursement uncertain.

Bakery claim separate.

Dye warehouse likely fined.

Rigger time.

Drainage overtime.

Materials.
