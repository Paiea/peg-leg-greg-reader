# D320: Mail Day

Status: **TEMPORAL REHEARSAL / ZERO AUTHORITY**

---

Rain trapped us in a roadside inn on D320.

Not dramatic rain.

Persistent rain.

The kind that made every road argument stupid.

Merel declared travel delayed before breakfast.

Orris complained once.

Then found cards.

I got mail.

Three letters.

Mara.

Renn.

Vessa Krail.

I put Vessa last on principle.

Mara's was six pages.

Two pages were about Northbank grain records.

One was about Sera.

Ash Crown had sent formal notice that she remained alive and offended by paperwork.

Mara had somehow received this before I did.

Of course.

Then:

**South vote rebuild is uglier than first vote. I think we can still get two firms back. Do not send names. I know you know people now. This is not invitation.**

I smiled.

Continued.

**Also, I miss you. This is invitation, just not useful one.**

Better category.

I wrote answer later.

Renn's letter began:

**Your Krail problem is contagious.**

I stopped.

Read again.

Orris looked up from cards.

"Bad?"

"Probably funny."

"Worse."

Correct.

Renn had reached Carrow D318.

Noll gave him spare key.

My chair survived first night.

Apparently this required reporting.

Then:

**I needed temporary quarry-yard access for two sample blocks I brought back from Dunbarrow. Sella told me city permit would take six days because south yard is technically bonded storage now. Krail heard me complain at Spoon.**

I closed eyes.

Continued.

**She said two days and three copper. I told her Greg says you are dangerous. She said Greg is observant.**

That sounded exact.

**I said no.**

Good.

**Then Sella said six days again.**

Of course.

**I hired Krail.**

I rubbed forehead.

Merel looked over from window.

"Renn?"

"Yes."

"Town alive?"

"He left it."

"Progress."

I kept reading.

**Permit took one day. She charged three copper. Receipt says city filing fee was one. I asked where other two went. She said into preserving my youthful belief that government has process.**

I laughed.

Orris held out hand.

"Read."

"No."

"Cruel."

Renn continued:

**She asked nothing about Dunbarrow. Nothing about song. Nothing about you except whether I had your spare room key because apparently she booked someone else into building across street and wanted to know if landlord still fixed roof leak. I said ask landlord. She did.**

There.

Boundary kept.

Not because Greg present.

Not because immediate reward.

Maybe because long-term client value.

Maybe because she had decided that rule mattered.

Maybe because Renn was less interesting to her than permits.

All possible.

Then:

**I do not trust her. I may use her again. This is apparently your fault.**

I put letter down.

Merel said, "What?"

"Nothing."

"Face says something."

"Renn hired Krail."

Orris slapped table.

"Excellent."

"No."

"Why not?"

I opened mouth.

Closed it.

Why not?

Renn knew warning.

Renn negotiated own money.

Renn chose his own risk.

Vessa kept boundary.

I disliked all of this because nobody required my opinion.

Healthy.

Annoying.

"Because she charges too much," I said.

Merel smiled.

"Coward."

I opened Vessa's letter.

One page.

No greeting.

**Calder paid promptly. Good client.**

Below that:

**Your North Gate inn tried to reverse my referral commission after checkout. I corrected them. No action needed.**

Then:

**Potential conflict: Pell Road private survey D336 asks for you by name. Client is cousin to warehouse owner from D292. I have not accepted, discussed rate, or confirmed your availability. If you want details, two copper.**

She had remembered conflict rule.

Also monetized remembering it.

Vessa.

At bottom:

**No, I did not ask Calder about Dunbarrow. You told me not to investigate him. He is also less mysterious in person than your contract implied.**

I stared at that line.

She had met Renn.

She had formed opinion.

She had not turned him into data product.

I did not know whether to be relieved or insulted for him.

I wrote back:

**Decline Pell Road survey. Payable one copper for conflict notice. Two is extortion.**

Stopped.

Added:

**Thank you for leaving Renn alone.**

Then crossed out **Thank you**.

Wrote it again.

Left it.

Merel watched from window.

"Hard letter?"

"Embarrassing."

"Better."

"Why?"

"Hard letters usually matter less than embarrassing ones."

I considered asking what that meant.

Did not.

Rain kept falling.

Orris dealt cards.

Merel joined.

I wrote Mara back before playing.

No names.

No strategy.

Mostly that I missed her too.

Then lost four copper to an S-class Ranker who cheated badly and a Platinum who cheated well.

I could not prove either.

Untrustworthy circle expanding.
