## Surviving block shape

### D334 `Same Chair`

Greg returns to Carrow after the autumn attachment and discovers Renn has already been living in his room/social orbit for more than two weeks.

The Brass Spoon circle now moves independently:

### D343 `Three Inches`

A routine Hearthline prepared-site demolition goes well.

After paid work ends, Bronze support Pell asks Greg about an intentionally tilted force plane.

Greg stays unpaid for roughly forty extra minutes helping Pell discover how stance and load direction matter more than geometric neatness.

The scene does not resolve Greg's identity, but it establishes that he enjoys teaching enough to remain after the contract when nobody needs him to.

### D351 `Worth More Quiet`

Vessa reveals that private-history broker Pel Ardin offered her five silver to confirm a rumor that Greg remembers previously being an S-class Ranker.

She declined.

Her reasons are mixed and explicitly not noble:

- five silver was cheap;
- public exposure would degrade her private access to Greg;
- a public future-memory story would attract Guild, Ranker, collector, and rumor attention;
- she also likes Greg.

Vessa tells Greg only after Ardin leaves Carrow and the immediate offer window closes, preserving her own optionality until the decision is no longer live.

She is therefore simultaneously safer than expected and still untrustworthy.

Greg does not buy a silence agreement.

The rumor remains partially leaked in Carrow but unconfirmed.

### D362 `Green Coat`

Greg visits Mara in Northbank during a genuinely free afternoon.

Nothing needs fixing.

He sees an expensive dark-green winter coat, likes it for no strategic reason, and buys it at fair price without negotiating.

This produces a small current-self discovery:

> Greg likes green.

Neither Greg nor Mara knew that beforehand.

The visit also includes honey nuts, bad Northbank wind, ordinary office stories, and no relationship crisis.

This is a direct answer to the run's slow question about what Greg wants for himself without turning the answer into an identity thesis.

### D374 `No Ledger`

Merel asks whether Greg can build a list of currently living historically important people he remembers dying before his later active period.

Greg, Renn, and Sella all resist a broad death ledger for different reasons.

Renn points out that learning the Dunbarrow song already contaminated his perception of ordinary current life.

Sella refuses any Guild-held future death registry based on Greg's impossible memory.

Greg refuses Merel's broad list but agrees that specific future hazard information may still need disclosure.

Merel pushes back on Greg's procedural comfort:

> withholding uncertain information is still agency.

They do not derive a perfect rule.

No broad ledger is created.

### D383 `Jessa`

Dunbarrow quarry worker Jessa visits Carrow and demonstrates a resonance read near iron that is better and cheaper than Renn's version of the same technique.

Renn becomes petty and jealous.

He interrupts her explanation, gives a bad congratulations when she receives more paid work and a possible spring Silver evaluation, and spends time sulking.

He does not become Future Red Bell ominous.

Two days later he apologizes without Greg arranging it and asks Jessa to teach him.

Jessa charges dinner as tuition.

The future S-class person is allowed to be young, competitive, embarrassed, and capable of learning from someone history does not remember.

### D393 `Eligible`

Sella informs Greg that his accumulated file now qualifies him to attempt Gold review.

He has five supporting evaluators, including Daro, Kessa, Nesha, and Tessa Brin / Last Door.

Earliest review is D401, five days before the midwinter Far Lantern attachment.

Greg could plausibly compress both.

He instead schedules review for D447, forty-six days later, because he wants the two-week attachment more than he wants to accelerate the badge.

This is not framed as indifference to time. Greg genuinely wants Gold.

He simply chooses something else over getting it six weeks sooner.

### D401 `Three Hollow Fords`

Sera sends Greg a postcard announcing that there are three places called Hollow Ford, possibly four depending on local naming.

She remains alive.

This further degrades Greg's confidence in future historical precision without making the memory useless.

Renn has independently told Jessa a limited version of Greg's future-memory situation. Greg initially feels ownership pressure, then accepts that Renn has the right to disclose his own involvement.

Vessa sees the postcard front, asks whether it is private, and does not read the back after Greg's answer.

### D406 `Midwinter`

Greg begins the first midwinter two-week Far Lantern attachment.

He wears the unnecessary green coat.

His Gold review remains scheduled for D447.

The route includes ordinary municipal work, a winter bridge review, a frozen aqueduct dispute, caravan support through Rime Cut, and possible joint work with Sera / Ash Crown.

Merel establishes one new boundary for the attachment:

> no future questions unless a current event makes them relevant.

After spending weeks knowing that Greg remembers her dying young, Merel herself limits future-memory inquiry.

Greg agrees.

The block ends as they leave Carrow northward.

- Greg cannot say she failed him;

- Greg knows this.

The relationship remains per-task and socially entangled.
