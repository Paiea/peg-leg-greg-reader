# D351: Worth More Quiet

Status: **TEMPORAL REHEARSAL / ZERO AUTHORITY**

---

Vessa told me the secret because market closed.

That was how she phrased it.

We were at Brass Spoon before lunch rush.

Noll counting onions.

Renn gone to Guild quarry yard.

Jorren somewhere with route papers and whatever human he refused to name.

Vessa sat across from me with coffee she had not paid for yet.

"Market closed," she said.

"What market?"

"Yours."

I put spoon down.

"No."

"Probably correct response."

"Krail."

She slid folded card across table.

Not letter.

Calling card.

**PEL ARDIN**

**HISTORIES, ACCOUNTS, PRIVATE COLLECTIONS**

I looked up.

"What is this?"

"Man who offered me five silver to confirm a rumor."

Cold moved through stomach before thought caught up.

"Which rumor?"

Vessa drank coffee.

"That Greg Vale claims to remember being an S-class Ranker before waking young."

Silence.

Kitchen knife struck board behind counter.

Three times.

Noll did not look over.

Maybe did not hear.

Maybe Noll heard everything and chose survival.

"Who told him?"

"He didn't say."

"You know."

"I know three possible paths."

"Cedi."

"One."

"Jorren."

"Less likely."

"Renn."

Her face flattened.

"No."

Immediate.

Interesting.

"How do you know?"

"Because Renn is terrible at lying and very good at not talking when he decides not to. Also I did not ask him."

I believed that.

Which was annoying.

"Other paths?"

"Guild room. Someone heard part of conversation. Brass Spoon table. Someone heard part of conversation. Cedi told someone after cards. Someone told someone. Carrow exists."

Fair.

Terrible city.

"Five silver for confirmation?"

"Five to me. More if I had documentation."

"Do you?"

"No."

"Did you sell?"

"No."

Answer too quick to comfort me.

"Why?"

Vessa smiled slightly.

There.

Wrong question if I wanted morality.

"Because five silver was cheap."

I stared.

"Excellent."

"You asked."

"How much?"

"Not money."

She tapped table.

"If rumor becomes public enough to be interesting, you stop being normal Silver support with weird friends. You become story. Guild starts asking questions. Ranker offices ask questions. Collectors ask questions. People follow you. Merel becomes evidence. Renn becomes evidence if anyone connects him. Future knowledge becomes commodity."

She took coffee.

"My access gets worse."

There.

"So you protected me because I'm more valuable private."

"Partly."

"Other part?"

"I like you."

"Terrible judgment."

"Known."

I looked at card again.

"What did you tell him?"

"Nothing useful."

"Exact words."

Vessa sighed.

"I said I had heard three versions of same tavern story, none matched, and if Ardin wanted to buy drunken Carrow nonsense he should negotiate with drunk people."

"True?"

"Yes."

"You didn't deny it."

"No."

"Why not?"

"Because hard denial from someone known to work near you creates value."

I hated that she was right.

"When?"

"Nine days ago."

I looked up.

"Nine."

"Yes."

"Why tell me now?"

"He left Carrow yesterday. Offer window closed."

There it was.

"You waited until you could no longer change your mind."

Vessa met eyes.

"Yes."

"So I didn't get choice about whether you sold."

"You never hired me to keep rumor private."

"No."

"You also never told me rumor."

"No."

"I chose."

"For yourself."

"Mostly."

I leaned back.

Chair not same repaired one.

Brass Spoon chair.

More trustworthy furniture.

Probably.

"If he offered twenty?"

Vessa thought.

Actually thought.

That mattered more than answer.

"Still probably no."

"Fifty?"

"Different conversation."

"One gold?"

"Greg."

"What?"

"You are trying to find price so you can know whether secret is safe."

"Yes."

"There is no number because price changes with circumstance."

I knew that.

Hated hearing it.

"So not safe."

"Correct."

"But unsold."

"Also correct."

Noll appeared beside table with bowl of chopped onions.

"Coffee."

Vessa handed him copper.

"Thank you."

Noll looked at me.

"You eating?"

"Apparently not."

"Bad."

He left.

Vessa watched him.

"Noll knows?"

"Enough."

"Would he sell?"

"No."

"Certain?"

I almost said yes.

Stopped.

"As certain as I get."

Vessa nodded.

"Different relationship."

"Obviously."

"You trust Noll."

"Yes."

"And you trust me to what?"

Mara's question.

What am I willing to put in her hands?

Except this time I had not put anything there.

World had.

"Routes," I said.

Vessa smiled.

"Permits."

"Sometimes."

"Rooms."

"With disclosed commission."

"Naturally."

"Not secrets."

"Good policy."

"Yet now you have one."

"Rumor."

"You believe it."

Vessa tilted head.

"I believe you believe something strange happened to you. I believe Renn believes it. I believe Merel Dane keeps inviting you places for reasons not explained by Silver badge. Exact metaphysics remain low priority."

Fair.

"Will you sell it later?"

"Maybe."

No pause.

There.

I should have been angrier.

Was angry enough.

But she had told me truth after incentive shifted.

Not before.

Not because honorable.

Because market closed.

I picked up Pel Ardin's card.

"Can I keep this?"

"Yes."

"Why?"

"Because if he asks someone else, you should know name."

Useful.

Annoying.

"Fee?"

"No."

"Favor?"

"Memory."

Merel's phrase.

Every favor creates memory even without debt.

I looked at Vessa.

"You know she said that about you."

"Merel?"

"Yes."

"Accurate woman."

"You don't know what she said."

"Still."

I laughed despite myself.

Vessa finished coffee.

"Anything else?"

"No."

She stood.

Then stopped.

"Greg."

"What?"

"If you want me to keep that specific rumor quiet, ask."

I stared.

"Would you agree?"

"Depends on terms."

Of course.

I thought about buying silence from Vessa.

Wrong shape.

Maybe right shape.

Couldn't tell.

"Not today," I said.

"Fine."

She left.

Pel Ardin's card stayed on table.

Five silver had not been enough.

That was not trust.

It was still information.
