# D180: Opposite Roads

Status: **SIX-MONTH TEMPORAL REHEARSAL / ZERO AUTHORITY**

---

Three days before Renn left for Dunbarrow, I paid Harrow the last installment.

Three days before I left with Merel Dane.

Same morning.

This felt like somebody else's symbolism.

Harrow counted coins.

Signed receipt.

Stamped settlement closed.

Then looked at me over desk.

"Try not to steal anything from an S-class Ranker."

I stared.

"How do you know?"

"Carrow."

Fair.

No privacy.

"I wasn't planning to."

"You weren't planning to steal Faultglass when you first saw it either."

Also fair.

"Improved reputation," I said.

"Moderately."

"I'll take it."

"You already did. That's why there were three payments."

I hated him less than before.

Not enough to mention.

I left with receipt in coat.

At Guild, Sella had two tokens for me.

One was Merel's route attachment.

Paid.

Silver scope.

My name printed under **field support observer**.

The other was a prepared-site Hearthline job dated after I returned.

Daro's approval mark sat at bottom.

No note.

No retainer.

Work.

I touched the paper once.

Then put both away.

Sella watched.

"Nothing to say?"

"Apparently I'm developing restraint."

"Concerning."

"I know."

At Brass Spoon, Noll was teaching Pela how to order winter onions early enough to avoid river prices.

Summer had barely arrived.

Owners apparently lived in future too.

Different kind.

Jorren sat at our table with Cedi.

I stopped.

"Why are you here?"

Cedi held up cards.

"Taking his money."

Jorren said, "She's cheating."

"You're bad."

"Both."

Renn sat across from them wearing a clean dark shirt.

I stared.

He noticed.

"Wedding."

"You bought it already?"

"Mam threatened death."

"Reasonable procurement."

He pointed at empty chair.

I sat.

Somehow game became five people.

Noll refused because owner.

Then joined because Pela told him she could handle onions without supervision.

Ownership had limits.

We played for coppers.

I lost three.

Renn lost six.

Jorren lost enough to accuse table.

Cedi won because Cedi always won.

Nothing about this mattered.

That made it difficult to leave.

After second game, Jorren asked Merel route.

I told him.

"Six weeks?"

"Approximately."

"You hate approximately."

"Merel travels."

"You scared?"

The question came so casually I almost answered casually.

"Yes."

Table quieted slightly.

Jorren looked surprised.

"Of what?"

Good question.

Not Merel's power.

Not roads.

Not leg.

"Liking it too much," I said.

Cedi put cards down.

Renn watched.

Jorren frowned.

"Training?"

"Being around people operating at that level."

Old Greg.

Command rooms.

S-class.

People who changed maps through competence and force.

I had spent months deciding that old headspace was useful and dangerous.

Now someone from that ceiling had opened a door.

"I know what that life feels like," I said. "Or I remember knowing. I don't know how much of wanting it again is me and how much is habit."

Jorren considered.

Then shrugged.

"Could be both."

Mara had said basically same thing with better taxonomy.

Annoying consensus.

Cedi dealt next hand.

"If you hate it, come back."

"It's six weeks."

"People leave contracts."

That sentence felt illegal.

"Ranker contract."

"Still contract."

Sella would agree.

Probably.

I hated probably less than six months ago.

Not much.

Renn picked up cards.

"What if you love it?"

There.

I looked at him.

"Then problem gets harder."

"Why?"

"Because I still have to decide whether I want to become that kind of person again."

Renn frowned.

"You were S-class."

Jorren's head turned.

Cedi stopped dealing.

Right.

Not everyone at table knew exact future biography.

Renn looked at faces.

"Oh."

I closed eyes.

"Excellent discretion."

"You told me."

"Privately."

"There were walls."

"Walls imply privacy."

Cedi leaned forward.

"What?"

Jorren said, "Long story."

"He was S-class?"

"Longer now."

Noll took one card from deck, looked at it, put it back.

"Greg old."

Cedi stared at him.

"That did not help."

"No."

I rubbed forehead.

Renn looked guilty.

Then started laughing.

I tried not to.

Failed.

Jorren laughed because everyone else did.

Cedi demanded explanation.

She did not get full one.

We played another hand instead.

Later, after Cedi left with too much of our money and Jorren went to meet someone he still refused to name, Renn and I walked toward Lower Rook.

Summer evening stayed light past sixth bell.

Six months ago Halden had been cold enough for Mara's gray coat.

Now Carrow windows stood open.

Children still shouted in courtyard after dinner.

Time had moved without asking whether I valued it.

At my building Renn stopped.

"Three days."

"Yes."

"Opposite directions."

Merel west-north toward Morn coast.

Dunbarrow southeast.

"Yes."

"You going to write weird reports?"

"No reports."

"Merel rule?"

"Mara rule. Continental conspiracy."

Renn nodded seriously.

"Good."

I pointed at him.

"Stop using that word."

He smiled.

Then went quieter.

"If I get angry in Dunbarrow, I'm not going to destroy it."

My stomach tightened.

"I know."

"Do you?"

There was the question.

Current Renn.

Future song.

No certainty between.

"I know you don't want to," I said.

He accepted that.

Better than promise.

"If something feels wrong, I'll write."

"Okay."

"Don't come unless I ask."

Harder.

"Okay."

He looked at me for a second.

"You're getting less annoying."

"Temporary condition."

"Hope so."

He left.

I went upstairs.

My room had two travel piles.

Walking leg supplies.

Field peg repair kit.

Merel route papers.

Mara's latest Northbank letter under room key.

Harrow's final receipt in coat.

A glassback plate Renn had bought from Guild split and then abandoned here because it was too heavy to carry to Brass Spoon.

His problem now.

Maybe mine while he traveled.

I sat at table.

Notebook waited.

For months I had used it for campaigns.

Leg.

Halden.

Faultglass.

Work.

I opened to blank page.

Wrote:

**WHO IS GREG?**

Stared.

Too large.

Under it:

**WHAT DO I WANT?**

Worse.

I almost started categories.

Work.

Mara.

Rank.

Magic.

Renn.

Trust.

Stopped.

Closed book.

Not every question improved when decomposed.

Three days until road.

Six weeks with Far Lantern if nothing changed.

Renn going home to a wedding in a town I remembered broken.

Mara in Northbank doing work she chose.

Noll owning one quarter of a restaurant without needing me.

Daro willing to put my name on one job again.

Six months ago I had sat on Halden stone steps thinking I had more life than I knew what to do with.

Apparently life had taken that as a challenge.

I packed socks.
