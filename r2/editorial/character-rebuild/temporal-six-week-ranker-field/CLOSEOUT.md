## Corrected Ranker model

This run supersedes the earlier mistaken implication that `Ranker` is simply above Platinum.

Two systems coexist:

### Guild operating rank

Bronze -> Silver -> Gold -> Platinum

This measures practical current capability and contract scope: raw power, field judgment, mobility, survivability, role independence, team integration, and what work the Guild can safely trust the person to carry.

### Titled Ranker registry

A person becomes a Ranker only after a durable public title attaches to consequential verified results.

No title, no Ranker.

Titled Rankers are then classified:

C -> B -> A -> S

The active registry shown at Morn Coast contains **147** people across all four letters.

Letter class is not a second straight power ladder. It tracks the accumulated consequence, scale, repeatability, trust, and planning significance of the result history attached to a title.

Important edge cases established in rehearsal:

- Tessa Brin / **Last Door** is Gold by operating rank and C-class as a titled Ranker after the Briar Mine rescue.
- Miko Tan / **Blue Thread** is only Silver by operating rank but C-class because the Grey Fever courier system became consequential enough for a durable title.
- Orris Hale is Platinum and untitled. He is directly stronger than some C-class Rankers and remains completely outside the Ranker registry.
- Merel Dane / **Far Lantern** is Platinum by operating rank and S-class among titled Rankers.
- Sera Vell / **Ash Crown** is Platinum and A-class. Her direct heat output may be more oppressive than Merel's, while Merel's accumulated S-class result history has broader strategic planning significance.

A titled person may climb C -> B -> A -> S through later result history. Raw power can create access to larger opportunities but does not itself grant or advance a letter.

The title is reputation formalized after other people get involved.

- Greg asks for teaching rather than demanding the identity word `master`.

Merel's line `You said time was cheap` becomes the useful counterpoint: Greg's advancement may take a year while still moving faster in meaningful access and capability than theft-based acceleration did.
