# D218: Not Needed

Status: **SIX-WEEK TEMPORAL REHEARSAL / ZERO AUTHORITY**

---

On day thirty-six Merel did not need me.

Not metaphorically.

Contractually.

The coastal ward vault had collapsed access, unknown lower geometry, and enough old military charge that Silver field support observer belonged nowhere near it.

Merel went.

Orris went.

Two local Platinums went.

I stayed at Guild station with laundry.

This felt personal.

"It's not," Merel said.

"Didn't ask."

"Face did."

Orris had stolen that phrase.

Bad influence.

"What am I supposed to do?"

"Whatever you do when not needed."

Cruel woman.

Then they left.

I washed liners.

Repaired one strap on field peg.

Read half a bad adventure serial someone had abandoned under bunk.

Hero entered cursed tower alone despite four separate warnings.

He deserved tower.

At lunch I helped station cook carry flour because sack was on floor and I was tall.

This did not become identity.

Afterward I sat outside with left leg off because socket edge had rubbed raw the day before.

Sun on residual limb felt strange.

Not bad.

Just private body in public yard.

Nobody cared.

Good.

Mail cart arrived at second bell.

Renn.

I recognized handwriting before opening.

**Wedding done. Tali still married. Oran still bad at cards. Mam has decided this proves stability.**

Then:

**Quarry job turned into municipal survey because old retaining runs under bell foundry row. Before you become weird: they make bells here. This is a normal industry. There are three foundries. I am being paid ten copper now because I asked. Do not claim credit.**

I smiled.

Ten.

Excellent.

Not credit.

He continued:

**I'm staying another week. Maybe two. Bess says I hear voids under old stone better than their rod crew. I like it here more than I expected. That should not surprise me because it is home. It surprised me anyway.**

No request.

No alarm.

No reason to go.

I folded letter.

Sera Vell sat down beside me.

Of course.

Ash Crown had remained at station waiting on convoy south.

Hollow Ford route tomorrow.

She carried two cups.

Handed one over.

Tea.

Not enough sugar.

"Your leg supposed to be off?"

"Yes."

"Good. Wasn't going to ask again."

We sat.

There were probably elegant ways to tell a person history said they died somewhere.

I knew none.

"Sera."

"Greg."

"I need to tell you something and I don't know if it's useful."

She drank tea.

"Best category."

I hated Rankers.

"I remember your death."

Cup stopped halfway.

No dramatic drop.

No flare of heat.

Just stillness.

"Mine?"

"Yes."

"From your old-life thing Merel half explained?"

"Yes."

"She said you were weird."

"Accurate."

Sera put cup down.

"Tell me exactly what you know."

There.

Not what should I do.

What do you know.

I breathed.

"You die at Hollow Ford. Before old me reaches Gold. You are already historical by then."

"When?"

"I don't know."

"Cause?"

"I don't know."

"Which Hollow Ford campaign?"

"I don't know."

"Was there more than one?"

"Yes."

"How many?"

"At least three major actions I remember. Probably smaller operations too."

Sera rubbed thumb along cup rim.

"And tomorrow I go there."

"Yes."

"Do you think tomorrow kills me?"

Worst question.

"I think I would be lying if I said no. I would also be lying if I said yes."

She nodded.

No comfort requested.

"Why tell me?"

I looked at Renn's folded letter.

"Because I spent too much of this year deciding future knowledge gave me right to arrange people. It doesn't. But hiding relevant risk because I'm scared of affecting you is also arranging you."

Sera considered.

"Better."

I pointed at her.

"Merel infection."

She laughed once.

Then serious again.

"Did you tell Merel?"

"She heard me react when you said Hollow Ford. She knows something. Not details."

"Good."

No.

I let word pass.

Sera said, "Write it."

"What?"

"Everything you know. Separate what you remember from what you infer. Dates if any. Names. Campaigns. Don't improve it."

Reasonable.

I went inside.

Wrote one page.

It was embarrassingly short.

History had made Ash Crown enormous and left me almost nothing useful about her death.

I wrote:

**KNOWN WITH MODERATE CONFIDENCE:** Sera Vell / Ash Crown dies at Hollow Ford before Greg first reaches Gold.

**UNKNOWN:** date, operation, cause, whether death preventable, whether warning changes location/cause, whether memory itself is complete.

Then listed three Hollow Ford actions I remembered, none with dates I trusted.

Sera read page twice.

Folded it.

Put it inside coat.

"I'm still going."

My stomach tightened.

"Okay."

She looked at me.

"That all?"

"Your decision."

"You hate it."

"Very much."

"Good."

I closed eyes.

She smiled.

"Sorry. Merel infection."

"Terminal."

Sera picked up tea.

"I am changing some things."

I looked over.

"What?"

"Mine."

Fair.

"Will you tell Merel?"

"Some. My command. My family. Maybe nobody else."

"Okay."

"If I live through this trip, you buy breakfast next time."

"If?"

"Don't start."

I stopped.

She leaned back against wall.

"For what it's worth, I'd rather know badly than be managed perfectly."

That landed harder than intended.

Renn's rules.

Mara's job.

Faultglass.

Same disease in different forms.

Sera left next morning.

No prophecy music.

No final look.

She complained convoy horse was too narrow and rode south.

Merel returned from ward vault that evening covered in dust.

Orris had a cut over eyebrow.

"Good day?" I asked.

"No," he said.

Merel saw empty bunk where Sera had slept.

Then looked at me.

"You told her."

"Yes."

"How'd she take it?"

"Went anyway."

Merel nodded.

I waited for judgment.

None came.

"Vault?" I asked.

"Boring."

Orris touched eyebrow.

"Liar."

"Mostly boring."

They ate.

I had already eaten.

For once, nothing needed me.

I survived.
