# D258: Alive

Status: **TEMPORAL REHEARSAL / ZERO AUTHORITY**

---

Sera Vell was alive.

Sella told me while stamping a sewer contract.

Apparently life-changing information respected office sequence.

"Ash Crown returned from Hollow Ford yesterday," she said.

Stamp.

Turn page.

"Wounded. Walking. Two dead on operation."

Stamp.

I stood at counter.

"What?"

Sella looked up.

"Which part?"

"All."

"She returned. Hollow Ford containment failed at south bridge. Operation completed. Two casualties. Ash Crown has burns and a broken wrist."

"Which wrist?"

Sella stared.

"Greg."

"Relevant to heat shaping."

"Left."

I breathed.

Had apparently not been doing that correctly.

Sella pushed sealed letter across counter.

"For you."

Ash-gray wax.

No title mark.

Just S.V.

I opened it at side table because standing in front of Sella while having feelings felt administratively vulnerable.

The letter was short.

**Greg,**

**I survived this Hollow Ford.**

Good start.

**Primary north vent failed exactly where our current survey said it might. Your warning did not tell me that. It did make me spend an extra day forcing everyone to rehearse secondary release positions because I was unwilling to die from laziness after being told I eventually die there.**

I sat down.

**That mattered. Maybe. We will never know how much. South bridge still went. Neral and Vett died getting civilians off it. I am not turning their deaths into evidence for or against your memory. Neither should you.**

There.

Sera understood future knowledge better than I did.

Annoying pattern among powerful women.

**I remain scheduled for Hollow Ford again in winter if repairs require it. Do not begin screaming yet.**

Too late internally.

**If you remember anything new, send it. If you remember nothing new, do not invent meaning because you are frightened.**

**Ash Crown**

Then, under signature:

**Still alive. Obviously.**

I read it twice.

Sella kept working.

No audience.

Good office.

"Two dead," I said.

She looked over.

"Yes."

"Would there have been more?"

"Don't know."

"Could have been zero."

"Could."

"Could still be the wrong Hollow Ford."

"Greg."

I stopped.

Sella pointed at letter.

"She told you what she knows."

Right.

I folded it.

Put it inside coat.

Outside Guild, Vessa Krail sat on low wall eating an apple.

I nearly walked past.

She said, "Ash Crown lived."

I stopped.

Of course.

"How do you know?"

"Route board."

"Sella just got confirmation."

"Sella gets official confirmation."

"And you get?"

"People who want to sell horses before wounded Rankers arrive and make every stable expensive."

I looked at apple.

"You were trading on injury news."

"Stable space."

"Same sentence with cleaner shoes."

Vessa smiled.

Then saw my face properly.

Smile went away.

"You knew her?"

"Not really."

"That answer means yes in complicated people."

I sat on wall two feet away.

Vessa finished apple.

Did not ask another question.

That surprised me.

After a minute she held out folded paper.

"What?"

"South road casualty list."

My hand stopped.

"Why do you have that?"

"Because I bought route packet."

"For stable space?"

"For three things."

"Do I want to know?"

"Probably not."

I took list.

Neral Senn.

Vett Arlow.

Seven injured.

Forty-three civilians cleared before bridge failure.

Sera Vell, burns left arm, fracture left wrist.

I read names again.

Folded paper.

Held it back.

Vessa did not take it.

"Keep."

"Why?"

"You care."

"What's price?"

She looked offended.

Not fake this time.

I thought.

Maybe.

Hard to tell.

"No price," she said.

"You bought it."

"Bundle. I needed other pages."

"Still value."

"Greg."

"What?"

"Sometimes I give people paper."

I stared at her.

Vessa stood.

"This is why people find you exhausting."

"You lie for money."

"And yet somehow you're difficult one."

She walked away.

I kept casualty list.

At home that night I put Sera's letter beside notebook.

Did not open notebook.

Two dead.

Maybe fewer because warning.

Maybe same.

Maybe future already different.

Maybe this was not future event at all.

No answer.

There was one thing I knew.

Sera had received information.

Sera had chosen.

Sera had survived.

For now.

That should have been enough.

It almost was.
