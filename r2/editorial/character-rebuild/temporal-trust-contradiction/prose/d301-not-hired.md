# D301: Not Hired

Status: **TEMPORAL REHEARSAL / ZERO AUTHORITY**

---

I called Vessa that afternoon.

Not to rehire her.

Important distinction.

Probably dishonest distinction.

North Gate permit had been sitting three days between Streets and Municipal Works because each office claimed other needed to sign first.

I spent two hours learning this.

Then another hour confirming both offices knew exactly where paper was.

At fourth bell, I stood outside Streets holding duplicate forms and thinking about murder.

There was public call box across square.

I stared at it.

Did not move.

Five minutes.

Seven.

Then walked over.

Vessa answered at contract office on second ring.

"Krail."

"Greg."

Pause.

Not long.

"You rehiring me?"

"No."

"Then this is social?"

"Worse."

"Permit?"

I hated her.

"North Gate. Streets and Municipal Works. Temporary access extension for prepared-site load survey."

"File number?"

I told her.

Paper rustled.

"Fee is four copper."

"For what?"

"Knowing who is lying."

"They're both saying other office signs first."

"Yes."

"Which is lying?"

"Neither."

I closed eyes.

"Vessa."

"Old process requires Streets first. Revised process requires Municipal first. Revision never reached Streets manual because clerk who updates it is out with fever. Ask Municipal clerk whether application entered before or after D286 policy date."

I looked at papers.

Entered D284.

"Before."

"Then old process. Streets first. Tell them docket predates revision and ask for Kellen, not front desk."

"Why Kellen?"

"Because front desk hates admitting manual wrong."

"Four copper?"

"Five now."

"Why?"

"You asked follow-up."

I almost laughed.

Did not give satisfaction.

"Fine."

"Cash."

"Fine."

She hung up.

Kellen signed in six minutes.

Municipal Works signed twenty later.

I sent Vessa five copper by runner.

No note.

She sent one copper back.

Also no note.

That was first call.

Second was eight days later.

A private client wanted me Firstday morning.

Hearthline had provisional hold Firstday afternoon.

No conflict on paper.

Travel made conflict obvious.

I called Vessa to ask route time.

"Three hours if bridge open," she said.

"Is bridge open?"

"Today."

"Firstday?"

"Maybe."

"Useful."

"Weather exists."

"Can you find safer route?"

"Yes. Two copper."

She did.

Longer by forty minutes.

Guaranteed crossing unless river flooded enough that nobody should be working anyway.

I took it.

Third call was not mine.

Vessa found me outside Guild on D297.

"Don't take Mel Orrow job."

I stopped.

"Why?"

"Conflict."

"Whose?"

"Mine."

"Meaning?"

"I represent somebody else bidding same property problem."

"You don't represent me."

"Not currently."

"Then why warn me?"

Vessa looked annoyed.

"Because if you take it and later learn I knew their bid position, you will assume I used you."

"Did you?"

"No."

"Would you?"

"Not without pricing it."

I stared.

She smiled.

"Joke. Mostly."

"You are terrible at rebuilding trust."

"I am not rebuilding trust."

"What are you doing?"

"Preserving future options."

There.

She left.

I declined Orrow job.

Not because Vessa told me.

Because Sella confirmed client had two competing structural bids and contract language allowed broad information claims after inspection.

Bad job.

Vessa had been right.

Again.

By D301, she was not hired.

She had also:

fixed one permit,

planned one route,

flagged one conflict,

and charged me nine copper total.

I had called her twice.

She had called me once.

No standing authority.

No calendar.

No commission.

No contract.

This should have been cleaner.

Instead it felt like I had removed railing and kept using stairs because I knew where broken step was.

At Brass Spoon, Jorren asked, "Krail still fired?"

"Yes."

Vessa walked through door behind him.

Jorren looked at me.

I looked at Vessa.

She held up hands.

"Dinner."

Noll shouted from kitchen, "Pay first."

"I know!"

Jorren started laughing.

"Very fired."

"She can eat."

"In your restaurant?"

"Not my restaurant."

Noll leaned out kitchen.

"Quarter mine."

"Exactly."

Vessa paid.

Sat with us because only open chair was ours.

Maybe.

I suspected she had waited for it.

Did not ask.

Cedi came later.

Cards appeared.

At some point Vessa told story about city clerk locking himself inside records room and refusing rescue until somebody agreed not to report missing whiskey.

Noll laughed so hard he had to sit.

I did too.

That was problem.

Not permit.

Not supplier commission.

Not payment risk.

I liked her.

People could be useful without becoming friends.

People could be friends without becoming trustworthy.

Apparently.

I did not know rules for that.

Vessa won two coppers off Jorren.

Lost one to Cedi.

At end she stood.

"Autumn notice came for you."

I looked up.

"What?"

"Far Lantern. Guild board this afternoon. D315 departure. Two weeks."

"Why are you telling me?"

"Saw it."

"Incidental information."

"You're learning."

She put coat on.

"You want me to book North Gate room?"

There.

Simple offer.

No standing authority.

I should have said no.

"Rate?"

"One copper plus room cost."

"No referral commission."

"If there is one, disclosed."

"Amount."

"Amount."

I thought about it.

Then nodded.

"Do it."

Vessa smiled.

Not victory.

Maybe just money.

Hard to know.

She left.

Jorren watched door close.

"Still fired?"

I looked at him.

"Yes."

"Sure."

"Shut up."
