# D315: Autumn

Status: **TEMPORAL REHEARSAL / ZERO AUTHORITY**

---

Vessa Krail had booked me a room beside North Gate with clean floor, wide stair, and terrible mattress.

Two out of three.

Receipt showed:

Room: six copper.

Booking fee: one copper.

Referral commission from inn: two copper.

Disclosed.

I hated how pleased that made me.

Morning of D315 was cold enough that breath showed briefly before sun reached gate wall.

Autumn attachment.

Two weeks.

Merel Dane waited beside three pack horses and one wagon.

Far Lantern wore no dramatic coat.

Brown travel wool.

Gloves.

Hair braided badly.

Orris stood nearby arguing with stable boy about apples.

Platinum problem.

Merel looked at my bag.

Then my field peg case.

Then receipt sticking from coat pocket.

"Krail?"

I stopped.

"How do you know?"

"That inn only writes referral commission on customer receipt when she makes them."

Of course.

"You know Vessa."

Merel made face.

"Unfortunately."

"Everybody says that."

"Because everybody knows her."

Orris looked over.

"Krail?"

"Apparently," Merel said.

Orris laughed.

"She still alive?"

"Yes."

"Good. She owes me four silver."

I stared.

"Why?"

"She doesn't."

Merel sighed.

Orris smiled.

"Old joke."

"Bad joke."

"Long joke."

I looked between them.

"You worked with her?"

Merel said, "Many times."

"Trust her?"

Both answered.

"No."

Same time.

I hated relief.

Merel noticed.

Of course.

"What happened?"

I told her while stable boy finished straps.

Not every detail.

Enough.

Warehouse.

Late-payment history.

Vessa withheld because she thought I would decline.

Fired.

Receivable bought at face.

Permit call.

Route call.

Conflict warning.

Room booking.

Merel listened.

At end she checked saddle girth.

"And?"

"And what?"

"What do you think?"

"She's useful."

"Obviously."

"Untrustworthy."

"Often."

"Keeps some promises exactly."

"Yes."

"Sometimes does kind things without obvious advantage."

"Also yes."

"This is not helping."

Merel climbed onto horse.

"You asked wrong person if you wanted moral sorting."

I put bag in wagon.

"How do you use her?"

Merel looked down at me.

"I don't give Krail secrets whose sale would improve her life more than keeping me as client."

There.

Cold.

Practical.

"That's it?"

"No. I don't let her define my choices. I pay promptly. I assume every favor creates memory even when it creates no debt. I verify anything where truth matters more than speed."

"Sounds exhausting."

"Then don't use her."

I frowned.

"You keep using her."

"Yes."

"Why?"

Merel pointed at North Gate.

"Because once, during Red Marsh evacuation, I needed sixty carts from four towns in one afternoon and Krail found fifty-eight."

"How?"

"I never asked."

"That seems dangerous."

"It was evacuation."

Different threshold.

There.

Orris mounted.

"She lied to a mayor."

Merel looked at him.

"We don't know that."

"Mayor says she did."

"Mayor lies."

"Fair."

I laughed.

Merel looked at me.

"Greg."

"What?"

"Do not turn this into permission."

That killed laugh.

"I wasn't."

"You were almost."

Damn.

She adjusted rein.

"Useful people can be unsafe. Unsafe people can be worth using. None of that transfers responsibility away from person choosing what to hand them."

I thought about Vessa holding my calendar.

Then not.

Renn's name.

Mara's office.

Warehouse risk.

A disclosed two-copper room commission.

"What do you hand her?" I asked.

Merel smiled slightly.

"Carts."

Fair.

We left North Gate at first bell.

Road north-west.

Two weeks.

No title waiting.

No Gold review.

No master.

Merel had first stop at a village reservoir and spent first hour discussing grain storage with local engineer.

Orris rode ahead.

I sat wagon bench because morning socket felt stiff and current body had vote.

At second milestone I checked mail pouch.

One letter from Mara.

One from Bera.

Nothing from Renn yet.

He was due Carrow three days from now.

We would miss each other.

Again.

I thought about writing ahead.

Then did.

**Renn,**

**I'm with Merel until D329. You will arrive after I leave because time hates coordination. Noll has room key if you need my spare chair. Do not break it.**

I stopped.

Added:

**Krail is not working for me. She may claim otherwise because language has failed. Do not give her information about yourself unless you want her to have it. She is useful. She is also Krail.**

I read that.

Terrible description.

Probably accurate.

Folded letter.

Merel glanced over from horse.

"Who?"

"Renn."

"The bell?"

"Renn."

Merel nodded once.

Correction accepted.

Good.

Road climbed.

Cold burned off slowly.

I had written three months ago that I wanted to be worth trusting.

Apparently next question was whether I could want that without demanding everyone around me be same thing.

I did not write that down.

Too neat.

Instead I checked wagon straps.

One loose.

Fixed it.

Then watched road.
