## What this is testing

The run begins immediately after current R2 Chapter 39 and advances exactly two fictional years. The fixed endpoint is Greg meeting Mara in Halden on the final morning of her two-year Vey term. Everything between those points was rehearsed speculatively.

The experiment intentionally lets time leave residue through work, money, roads, rank, relationships, body, magic, prosthetic use, reputation, and mistakes. It also tests whether later-life S-class thinking can be useful without becoming automatic moral or narrative authority.
