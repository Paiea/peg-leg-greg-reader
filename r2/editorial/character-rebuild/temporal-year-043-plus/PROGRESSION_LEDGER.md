## Opening state, D001

- **Rank:** Bronze.
- **Money:** stable, not expedition-ready.

- **Magic:** sensing, ward reading, bounded/static force, uneven current-body execution.
- **Blackglass Anchor:** not acquired.
- **Body:** current nineteen-year-old body, no peg-leg foreshadowing or future injury requirement.
- **Gear:** functional Bronze travel kit, sword, field pack, ordinary clothing.
- **Reputation:** local and narrow.
- **Mara:** west in Halden, relationship active by letters and invitation.
- **Noll:** Brass Spoon work continues independently.
- **Jorren:** road/factor choices remain his own.
- **Tavin:** reconnected by letter after Greg's memory disclosure.
- **Westreach:** no slot, sponsor, party, winter kit, or qualification path.

## Progression bands used

**Rank:** Bronze -> Silver pressure -> Silver. Gold is not a one-year target.

**Sword:** mismatch -> field-proven -> integrated -> dangerous at Silver scale.

**Magic:** bounded -> load-aware -> moving -> integrated -> expedition-useful.

**Anchor:** unknown -> acquired -> characterized -> field-tool -> integrated -> Westreach-grade.

The Anchor is not sentient, not a stat screen, not prophecy, and not designed around a future amputation.

## Final state, D365

### Greg

Greg is Silver, not Gold. His sword is dangerous at Silver scale. His force/load magic is integrated enough to be expedition-useful, but it still depends on geometry, limits, equipment, and other people. He has learned to stop more reliably, although curiosity remains part of him rather than a solved flaw.

His current body now knows roads through calluses, cold, wet wool, injuries, recovery, repetitive labor, camps, wagons, animals, and team movement. That current bodily knowledge is qualitatively different from simply remembering what Old Greg once knew.

### Blackglass Anchor

The Anchor ends as a scarred, repaired, de-rated field tool. It has:

1. been legally salvaged and purchased;
2. been tested badly and well;
3. gained monster-material dampening and a hard-limited splitter;
4. been damaged by Greg's overreach;
5. bridged a larger old blackglass system at Storm Keep;
6. survived Nine Teeth with a bent primary eye;
7. finally fit a related old regulator socket at the Black Stair approach.

The D365 fit is evidence that the Anchor belongs to a lost or uncommon tool family. It is not evidence that the object or Stair chose Greg.

### Relationships and independent lives

Mara, Noll, Jorren, and Tavin all accumulate off-page life during the year. Mara becomes more Halden and later experiences Greg's Carrow rather than living in a romance waiting room. Noll gains genuine Brass Spoon responsibility. Jorren develops a durable factor/ledger identity while keeping road competence. Tavin remains mobile, current, and sometimes physically absent when Greg wishes he were present.

### Westreach

The expedition reaches the Black Stair with Greg as one member of Field Support Three. The expedition got him there, and he materially helped get the expedition there. The target is therefore fulfilled without turning the promise into protagonist privilege.
