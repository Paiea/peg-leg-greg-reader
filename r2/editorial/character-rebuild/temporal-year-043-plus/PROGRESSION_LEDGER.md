## Spring endpoint inherited by Summer

Greg remains Bronze at D091, but he is no longer generic Bronze. His sword is already in Silver training range; his field record now includes multiple fauna contacts, roads, freight failure response, salvage, and a glasshorn redirect. The Blackglass Anchor is an owned, bounded field tool with a reinforced harness. He has a provisional Westreach specialist path but no sponsor. Silver review after Summer is the cleaner route if he can prove sustained multi-day judgment.

The year has also changed his social world: Tavin is physically current again but south, Mara has a D128 Halden visit planned, Noll has recurring open/close responsibility at Brass Spoon, and Jorren has accepted stable factor work. None of them are waiting for Greg's progression.

## Summer endpoint inherited by Autumn

At D183 Greg is Silver, a little over halfway through the one-year clock. The promotion rests on sustained road work, live rescue, disciplined retreat, a decisive pale-drake kill, structural-load judgment, and multiple Silver endorsements. His sword now cleanly belongs at Silver level. His magic has progressed from static support into field-integrated force/load work, and the Blackglass Anchor has moved from odd salvage to a repeatable tool with a plausible historical role as a portable maintenance regulator.

Socially, the time experiment has also accumulated real residue. Greg spent fifty-four days away from Carrow, built an actual Halden work reputation, and deepened a relationship with Mara without moving there. Returning to Lower Rook still feels like home. Noll and Jorren have continued changing in Carrow, while Tavin remains mobile and current by packet. Autumn must not reset these relationships to their Spring versions.

Westreach is closer but not solved. Silver standing opens direct trial access, but Greg still needs a clear expedition role, a sponsor or role lead who owns his placement, expedition-ready money, winter kit, and evidence that his specialty scales beyond the small and medium failures seen so far.
