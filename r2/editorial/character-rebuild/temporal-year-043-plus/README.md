## Folder contract

- `SPEC.md` defines the experiment.
- `YEAR_CLOCK.md` owns the forty dated checkpoints and elapsed-time accounting.
- `PROGRESSION_LEDGER.md` owns experiment-only state after each rehearsal chapter.
- `SOURCE_MAP.md` records which old 4x10 discoveries are being used as quarry.
- `READER_SCRUB.md` is the mandatory prose-cleanliness and continuity check.
- `spring/`, `summer/`, `autumn/`, and `winter/` hold the forty reader-facing rehearsal chapters.
- `AUDIT.md` will judge the completed year.

## Authority rule

Accepted R2 story truth through Chapter 42 is the only story authority entering the experiment.

Nothing in this folder becomes canon because it is written, numbered, polished, dated, or liked. If a discovery survives, later accepted prose must be freshly re-performed from current authority.

## Time rule

This experiment is specifically designed to prevent speculative prose from collapsing into one long week.

The story clock is editorial authority inside the experiment. Chapters may skip days or weeks. Those gaps must leave visible evidence in bodies, money, weather, work, relationships, gear, rank, and the world.

## Genre rule

This run intentionally permits more conventional fantasy and isekai pleasures than the prior character-first rebuild normally foregrounds:

- stronger Guild progression;
- monsters and materials;
- ruins and old magical sites;
- rare equipment and loot;
- rank pressure;
- party tactics;
- increasingly dangerous roads;
- decisive Greg wins;
- visible magic growth;
- power-fantasy payoff.

R2 remains character-first. Power does not exempt Greg from other people's authority, needs, schedules, or independent lives.

## Blackglass Anchor rule

The unique artifact is provisional experiment material. It must earn its place during this year as a useful force/charge-balancing object even if Greg never loses a leg.

Do not write toward the future peg-leg event. If later canon discovers a second use for the artifact after an amputation, that is future work, not this experiment's design target.
