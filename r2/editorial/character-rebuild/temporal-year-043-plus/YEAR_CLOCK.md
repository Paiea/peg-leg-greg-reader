## Gap enforcement result

Every chapter with a gap greater than two days was written to establish elapsed-time consequences through at least two domains such as body, money, weather, work, relationship, gear, rank/reputation, ecology, or location change. The mechanism is intentionally visible in this experiment and should be varied more aggressively if promoted into a future engine.

## Westreach clock result

- D274: candidacy becomes concrete. Greg is Silver, sponsored, and competing for the Field Support Three specialist slot.
- D304: Greg earns the specialist slot in the crew trial.
- D316: final role and kit are resolved. Role: `load/force specialist, reserve blade`.
- D326: Westreach physically departs Carrow.
- D326 to D365: 39 elapsed days of westward expedition time.
- D353: Nine Teeth closes and the planned route fails.
- D360: Greg recommends the safer slower route even though it likely misses his personal deadline; later evidence makes Load Road the better expedition choice.
- D365: the expedition reaches the Black Stair on Firstday of Spring, Year 313. Greg physically touches the first black step.

The final date did not move.
