# Year Chapter 22: Glassmaw

Eleven days later, my coat had a new side panel and the quarry had a worse problem.

The stonebacks stayed north.

That part worked.

Osa sent a one-line note through Guild:

**Mother alive. Young alive. Waste blocks holding. Foreman still cheap.**

I kept it.

The worse problem came from below.

Lower Gray opened a new cut on D198.

Three charges into limestone face exposed a hollow chamber behind the quarry wall.

Workers heard something moving inside.

They did what workers always did when confronted by unknown darkness containing expensive stone.

They put a lantern near it.

The lantern disappeared.

Then a mule.

Not entirely.

What remained of the mule caused the Guild posting.

**LOWER GRAY QUARRY / SUBSURFACE PREDATOR / SILVER TEAM / HIGH HAZARD / MINERAL INTERFERENCE REPORTED**

Pay enough that I stopped pretending money was secondary.

Field-positive could become expedition-ready if Autumn went well.

Westreach kit share still sat like threat on paper.

I took the contract under Sile again.

Not because he had become my party leader.

Because he had worked with me, liked my judgment enough, and the quarry asked for someone familiar with site.

Osa joined.

Fourth was a mage named Reth, Silver, younger than Sile and older than me, whose entire personality seemed built around disliking physical labor.

"I am here for resonance," he said while I loaded rope.

"Rope resonates if you drop it."

"You're annoying."

"Documented."

He had heard.

The eleven days between contracts had done more than repair coat.

I trained three times with Callen.

Worked Bell Tile twice.

Bought two winter undershirts after first cold morning and immediately felt robbed by wool.

Mara's reply arrived D197.

**Wish you were here too. Not as a complaint.**

**East load likely around D249. Do not write it on wall.**

Calendar only.

Tavin sent nothing.

That worried me enough to write one extra packet and not enough to invent disaster.

Current friendship included silence sometimes.

At Lower Gray, new quarry cut looked like giant had bitten hill.

Fresh white stone face thirty feet high.

A dark opening near base where blast revealed chamber.

Blood trail from mule stopped at opening.

No tracks outside.

Reth tested air with crystal rod.

The crystal flickered and went dark.

"Interference," he said.

"Useful."

"I already knew."

"Now we paid for demonstration."

He hated me more.

Sile assigned roles.

Osa outer high line.

Reth magic survey.

Me second and load support.

Sile front.

Quarry crew stayed out.

We entered.

The chamber had been natural cavern once, then modified.

Old cut marks on floor.

Stone niches.

Bronze hooks green with age.

Not full ruin yet.

Industrial cavity older than current quarry.

The walls glittered with thin black crystal veins.

Not Blackglass.

Different.

Reth touched one with insulated probe.

His crystal rod died entirely.

"Mana sink."

That got my attention.

"Absorbs?"

"Maybe scatters. My reading collapses."

I opened sense.

The chamber felt wrong.

Not empty.

Flat.

Like trying to hear through thick cloth.

The Blackglass Anchor went dull against ribs.

Interesting and bad.

"My regulator too."

Sile said, "Then assume no magic."

Clean.

I drew sword.

The cave narrowed after fifty feet.

Mule remains appeared in pieces.

No need detail.

Teeth had crushed bone.

Large predator.

Then we found lantern.

Whole.

Bent metal, glass missing.

Reth crouched.

"Heat."

Burn discoloration on frame.

Not fire from oil alone.

Osa whispered from rear, "Movement high."

I looked ceiling.

Nothing.

Then stone blinked.

Not actual eye.

A facet rotated.

The creature had been against ceiling, hide matching mineral wall.

It dropped.

Massive.

Long-bodied, six limbs, head broad and wedge-shaped. Plates translucent gray with black crystal embedded. Mouth opened sideways farther than anatomy should allow.

Glassmaw was name quarry men invented later.

At the moment it was just fucking large.

Sile raised shield.

Impact drove him to one knee.

Reth cast a force bolt.

It hit creature and scattered in black veins like light through broken mirror.

Almost nothing.

"No magic!" Sile shouted.

Already obvious.

I went blade.

Creature snapped toward Sile.

I cut rear joint.

Sword sparked off crystal plate.

No penetration.

Osa arrow hit softer mouth tissue.

Glassmaw screamed.

Good target.

It spun.

Tail struck wall hard enough chips flew.

I ducked.

Reth did not.

Tail caught him chest and threw him six feet.

He hit stone.

Air gone.

Now second mattered.

Sile still up but shield arm numb maybe.

He looked at me.

"Call."

There.

My call.

Current room:

Magic unreliable.

Creature armored except mouth, underside maybe.

Reth down but conscious.

Osa bow useful if angle.

Sile shield can hold one hit, maybe not three.

Exit behind.

Could retreat.

Need recover first.

"Osa high mouth. Sile draw head left. I get Reth."

No poetry.

Sound.

Creature turned.

Osa arrow into open mouth.

Glassmaw lunged toward her instead.

Wrong.

"Can move?"

"Yes."

Creature changed again, sensing floor vibration maybe.

It came toward us.

Sile intercepted shield-first.

Impact sent both backward.

Shield edge cracked.

He shouted.

"Go!"

Not ceiling this time.

Fast.

Maybe disaster.

"Black veins stop at entrance."

Natural chamber with sink crystal only interior.

Outside, magic returns.

"Get him out!"

Osa took Reth.

"Move!"

Five.

Silver threads bright.

"What?"

"Now magic."

Good.

Terrible.

Not elegant.

Sideways plane at shoulder, Anchor distributing into two iron eyes.

Osa arrow first.

Then me.

Deep.

Hot blood.

Good habit.
