# Year Chapter 20: Silver

Status: **TEMPORAL YEAR REHEARSAL / ZERO AUTHORITY**

`Y312 / D183 / SUMMER`

`DAYS SINCE PRIOR CHAPTER: 2`

`LOCATION: Carrow Guild`

`RANK: Bronze -> Silver review result`

---

I arrived at the Guild seven minutes after first bell.

This took effort.

Sella looked at the clock.

Then me.

"Growth."

"I was outside."

"How long?"

"Irrelevant."

"Fourteen minutes?"

"Nine."

She smiled.

I hated her.

The advancement board had posted on the west wall.

Six names.

Three promoted.

Two deferred.

One failed.

I did not look immediately.

This was not maturity.

It was fear with posture.

Noll stood beside me because he had decided breakfast before work could happen near Guild today. He had worn the blue apron under coat.

Jorren had come too, straight from Meln's yard, chalk still on one sleeve.

Neither spoke.

That made it worse.

Tavin was not in Carrow.

Mara was not in Carrow.

Good.

My life did not need everyone summoned for a rank board.

Still, I wanted them here in smaller ways.

Letters later.

Current people current distances.

I stepped closer.

Found my name.

**GREG VALE?** No. Careful. Greg's surname not established here. Just **GREG** if Guild file uses single name. Better.

**GREG**

**ADVANCED: SILVER**

Underneath:

**FIELD SPECIALTY: LOAD / FORCE SUPPORT**

**BLADE ENDORSEMENT: SILVER**

**RESTRICTION: NO INDEPENDENT LEAD ON MUNICIPAL STRUCTURAL MAGIC PENDING FURTHER CERTIFICATION**

I read it once.

Then again.

My first thought was not profound.

It was:

Fuck.

My second was that the restriction line was longer than promotion.

My third was that I had wanted this badly enough for six months? No, 183 days since D001, almost half a year, but Silver pressure started earlier. Still.

I touched the board as if ink might move.

Noll said, "Silver."

"Yes."

Jorren said, "You look sick."

"No."

"Face."

Everyone.

Sella called from desk.

"Come sign before you become decorative."

I went.

Promotion itself involved four forms.

Of course.

One updated Guild standing.

One insurance contribution change.

One equipment declaration.

One acknowledgment that Silver members could be assigned liability beyond Bronze day-work thresholds.

Power fantasy had paperwork.

I loved it anyway.

Sella took my Bronze tab.

That hurt unexpectedly.

Small piece of metal.

Months of sweat attached.

"Keep?" I asked.

"No."

"Why?"

"Guild property."

"I earned emotional ownership."

"Guild does not recognize."

She dropped it into a box containing other old tabs.

Cruel institution.

Then she slid a Silver tab across desk.

Not shiny.

Brushed metal with darker edge and two stamped lines.

Heavier by maybe nothing.

My hand still felt it.

"Congratulations," Sella said.

There.

No sarcasm for once.

I looked up.

"Thank you."

"Do not become unbearable."

Balance restored.

She added a new page to my file.

"Your promotion was not unanimous."

Of course.

"Why?"

"One reviewer thinks your current blade is ahead of your team judgment. One thinks your magic specialization is useful enough to justify advancement. One thinks you are annoying but trainable."

"Which one are you?"

"Not reviewer. I already know you're annoying."

"What was deciding factor?"

Sella flipped notes.

"Siva's road endorsement. Ves and Orra. Halden retaining review. Your field record showing you keep working after the interesting part."

There.

Not white drake alone.

Not sword duel.

Not Anchor.

The boring parts counted.

I liked that more than expected.

"What changes?"

"Pay bands. Contract access. Liability. Expectations."

"Westreach?"

"Makes candidacy simpler. Does not guarantee slot."

"Sponsor?"

"Still need field sponsor or role lead by D274. Silver standing means you can apply directly to trials, but somebody still has to own where you fit."

"Load specialist."

"Maybe."

"Ressa noticed."

"Provisional."

"You enjoy ruining things."

"It's why desk gave me mug."

Noll and Jorren waited outside.

I clipped Silver tab to coat.

Noll touched it.

"Same."

"It is visibly different."

"Small."

"You bought blue apron and made me inspect stitching."

"Apron bigger."

Jorren laughed.

We went to breakfast even though Noll had already eaten because apparently promotion required second meal.

At Maud's? Lower Rook breakfast. We returned through city.

The walk felt strange.

Nobody knew.

Fish seller shouted same price.

Cart nearly splashed us.

A child ran into my leg and apologized without checking rank.

Silver did not alter street physics.

Good.

At Lower Rook, Maud looked at tab once.

"More rent?"

"Why?"

"Silver makes more."

"Predatory."

"Business."

She did not raise rent.

Probably.

Breakfast became four people because Jorren sent runner to Noll's kitchen? No, Noll was already there. Tavin absent. Maud joined for coffee without invitation.

Noll insisted I buy eggs.

I did.

Jorren asked what contracts Silver unlocked.

I had not checked board yet.

That surprised him.

"You waited six months for rank and didn't look?"

"Half year."

"Same."

"Not same."

"Close."

He was right enough.

I wanted to sit with it for one meal.

Not optimize immediately.

This was perhaps the most suspicious sign of growth so far.

After breakfast, Noll went to Brass Spoon.

Jorren to factor yard.

I went upstairs alone.

My room had changed while I was gone fifty-four days.

Not much.

Dust line where burned seven sat.

Noll had moved second chair closer to window.

One nail had been added near door, probably Maud, holding spare cloth.

The carved river bird from Mara sat on table now.

I unwrapped it.

Small wooden thing with ridiculous beak.

No use.

Exactly as intended.

I placed it beside Blackglass Anchor.

One useless object.

One increasingly useful object.

Both from Halden.

Then I wrote letters.

Tavin first.

**Silver. Apparently paperwork surrendered.**

Then three lines explaining result.

No grand speech.

Mara second.

I stared at paper longer.

Wanted clever.

Ended with:

**I got it. Silver.**

Then:

**You were right that the boring jobs counted. I dislike this.**

I almost wrote wish you were here.

Stopped.

Then wrote it anyway.

**Wish you were here. Not as a complaint.**

That felt exposed enough to fold immediately.

I took both to packet office before changing mind.

Outside, summer Carrow had become hot. The season was turning. D183. Just over half? 183/365 is just over half exactly, 50.1%. The year crossed midpoint.

I stopped in street.

Halfway.

D001 felt both close and stupidly far.

At D001, Black Stair had been a sentence.

Now I had Silver tab, field-positive money, road endorsements, a real magical specialty, a strange artifact with known limits, two cities where people knew my name, a relationship living across packet schedules, and enough scars that old mirror photos? No cameras. Enough marks that my body no longer felt like the untouched nineteen-year-old I had started with.

Still no Westreach slot.

Still no sponsor.

Still no winter kit.

Still no proof the Anchor would matter west.

Half a year gone.

The deadline felt closer now.

Good.

I went back to Guild.

Now I checked the Silver board.

Different wall.

Different rates.

Different problems.

One posting caught immediately.

**NORTH QUARRY ROAD / SIX DAYS / SILVER FIELD LEAD OR SECOND / STONEBACK PREDATION / TWO CONFIRMED LOSSES**

Pay high enough to make me reread.

Monster contract.

Real one.

Silver accepted.

I touched new tab.

Then stopped.

Old pattern would have taken token immediately because new access existed.

I read full posting first.

Team already had lead.

Needed second.

Departure D190.

Seven days.

Enough time to repair gear, work Bell Tile, see Noll and Jorren, wait for Mara's reply maybe impossible by packet, train shoulder, and decide rather than react.

I copied posting number.

Did not claim yet.

Sella saw.

"Growth again."

"Stop watching me."

"Desk faces board."

I left.

Outside, Silver tab felt heavy for another block.

Then a wagon splashed mud onto my trousers.

Rank normalized.
