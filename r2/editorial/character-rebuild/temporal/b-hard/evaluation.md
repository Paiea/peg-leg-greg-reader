1. Noll's outside work changes Lower Rook before it changes his status.

That is character-first temporal causality.

## Character challenger

### Greg — PASS

He is recognizable through wants that conflict rather than through doctrine:

- wants Mara
- wants roads
- wants to keep promises
- wants credit for keeping them
- wants Noll close while supporting Noll's job
- wants other people to choose him first
- hates being replaceable
- monitors competence automatically
- can redirect practical intelligence into emotional control or self-protection

### Mara — PASS

She does not collapse into the correct relationship referee.

She:

- has work she cares about
- is irritated and tired on arrival
- wants dinner
- dislikes becoming Greg's moral justification
- wants Greg to have his own life
- would still have been angry if he left
- wants to stay in his room
- still leaves for work
- remembers ordinary history imperfectly herself

The contradiction is a major survivor.

### Noll — PASS

He:

- has a distinct work body at Brass Spoon
- wants skill improvement
- has money motives
- also values Lower Rook beyond maximizing pay
- knows Mara independently
- carries work back home through bones/dumplings/hours
- never becomes Greg's emotional interpreter

### Vessa — PASS AS LOCAL CHARACTER

She owns the kitchen and her labor calculation. Preserve sparingly. Do not turn her into another hyper-optimized institution designer.
