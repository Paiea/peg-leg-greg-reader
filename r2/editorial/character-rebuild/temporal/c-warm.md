# C WARM — Tavin Clock

Status: **SPECULATIVE / UNNUMBERED / DISCOVERY REHEARSAL ONLY**

This is not chronology. It scouts the next Tavin pressure after Greg's Chapter 33 reply leaves Carrow.

## Why this clock is warm

Greg finally sent Tavin:

- the freight lead arrived late
- the job is probably gone
- Greg is still in Carrow
- Greg has been out on the road anyway
- Tavin was right that Greg wanted roads
- Greg is less sure what he means by that now
- Greg still disputes the two copper

Greg did **not** explain the memory discontinuity directly.

Tavin therefore has legitimate reasons to think the letter is strange without automatically knowing why.

Tavin also owns a river/freight life independent of Greg. Any return contact should happen because that life creates a packet, route, layover, or freight movement, not because the engine decides `friendship arc now`.

## Portable pressure

Greg wants Tavin to recognize that he became someone who actually goes on roads.

Greg also fears being recognized too accurately by a person who knew young Greg better than he knows himself.

Those wants conflict.

Tavin's job is not to explain Greg's youth. His job is to arrive with his own current body, work habits, irritations, jokes, money problems, and expectations.

## Variant A — Paper gets there first

A South Latch reply reaches Carrow before Tavin does.

Useful shape:

- Tavin opens by mocking Greg's strangely formal letter rather than treating it as a confession.
- He confirms the old freight job is gone without tragedy.
- He disputes the burned-seven debt again.
- He gives one current work fact Greg did not ask for because it matters to Tavin, not to history.
- He notices Greg's sentence about not being sure what `wanting roads` means and asks a blunt question that Greg cannot answer with competence language.
- He may mention a Venn movement through Carrow or nearby without promising he will personally be on it.

What this variant tests:

- whether a friendship can become current through correspondence without Mara teaching Tavin how to handle Greg
- whether Greg can write something that sounds like himself rather than a report
- whether Tavin's ordinary work life has enough gravity to resist becoming memory delivery

Do not let the reply become `Tavin explains young Greg`.

## Variant B — Freight gets there first

A Venn crew reaches Carrow before a return letter does, and Tavin is with it because the company needed him there.

Useful shape:

- Greg sees Tavin in work context before either has a private reunion.
- Current Tavin has visibly changed since whatever fragments Greg can access: body, hands, clothes, habits, authority, coworkers, maybe a small injury or work-specific irritation.
- Tavin makes the first intimacy move in a way that does not require Greg to remember the protocol. A hug is possible but not mandatory.
- Tavin expects one stupid ordinary thing to still be true and discovers it is not, or vice versa.
- Greg wants Tavin impressed by the sword, roads, Lower Rook, or business and becomes annoyed if Tavin notices something smaller first.
- Tavin has another person or obligation waiting. Greg does not get the whole evening by default.

What this variant tests:

- reference before invention without chronology copying
- whether current friendship can outrun memory recovery
- whether Greg can tolerate another old friend possessing an off-camera life

Do not recreate historical Chapter 97 wholesale. In particular, do not auto-summon Noll + Jorren + cards into one room merely because that later scene worked.

## Strong possible discovery

Tavin may be easier with current Greg than Greg expects precisely because Tavin does **not** need a perfect explanation immediately.

That does not mean Tavin is emotionally optimized.

He may also be:

- irritated by the late reply
- offended that Greg missed something important
- amused at the memory weirdness before understanding its weight
- jealous that Mara/Jorren/Noll know more about Greg's current life
- impatient because freight is waiting
- financially distracted
- wrong about what Greg needs

At least one of those imperfections should survive if/when this clock approaches A.

## Survival rule

C carries only this forward:

> Tavin's own freight/packet clock is now live, and contact can force Greg to let an old friendship become current before his memory catches up.

No chapter number, arrival date, group dinner, card game, or reunion wording is protected.
