# R2 Chapters 49–86 High-Fidelity Audit

Status: **EDITORIAL PASS COMPLETE / SOURCE WIN DEFAULT**

Branch: `editorial/r2-high-fidelity-049-086`

Purpose: challenge accepted Chapters 49–86 for experiential completeness and connected-listen quality without changing story truth, chapter numbering, or outcomes unless a clear source loss is proven.

Method:

1. read 6–8 connected chapters without editing
2. classify each `SOURCE WIN / LIGHT FINISH / REPERFORM`
3. edit only clear losers
4. reread the connected run
5. preserve source wherever the challenger does not clearly win

`LIGHT FINISH` means the chapter is already embodied and only loses on prose/listening execution. `REPERFORM` means the causal core survives but the chapter is still treatment-like and should be freshly staged from current authority. No word-count targets.

## Connected-listen findings

The dominant weakness across 49–78 was not missing story. It was repeated rhetorical machinery:

- isolated evaluator words (`Good`, `Useful`, `Correct`, `Different`)
- `Not X. Y.` and similar classification reversals used too often
- short paragraph stacks where a normal spoken paragraph carried better
- narration explaining dialogue/action after the scene had already proved the point
- tidy Greg-principle endings appearing too frequently across adjacent chapters

The repair deliberately **does not remove Greg's dry analytical voice**. It removes repetition of the same instrument so the surviving short lines and classifications regain force.

Across 79–86 the problem changes. Surface cleanup is insufficient because the chapters start skipping the human/professional action that should be the reading experience. Those units benefit from fresh re-performance rather than expansion of the old wording.

## Forward lesson

The audit supports the future-survivor doctrine strongly:

> **Causal survival and prose survival are different metrics.**

The future pipeline can preserve a large amount of useful territory, but farther survivors must earn higher fidelity as they approach publication.

Recommended forward behavior:

1. keep hard/near survivors aggressively when they win
2. use `LIGHT FINISH` for repeated listening-prose machinery rather than rewriting whole chapters
3. fresh-perform treatment-like survivors instead of padding them
4. use 6–8 chapter connected listening as a required longitudinal check
5. feed the 49–86 failure pattern forward so promoted C/D material is judged for embodiment before publication
