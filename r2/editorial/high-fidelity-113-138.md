## Connected-listen findings

The recurring prose weakness across this batch is not missing story or scene clay. It is the narrator repeatedly standing beside already-good material and naming what it proved.

Most common residue:

- isolated evaluator labels after competent action
- practical institutions described, then praised explicitly
- `Not X / Y` classification used after the scene already created the distinction
- compact chapters ending with a clean Greg principle instead of the last physical/social beat
- accumulation explained immediately after accumulation is shown

Edits therefore remain mostly subtractive. The pass preserves Greg's analytical humor and does not remove every classification line. The target is repetition of the instrument, not the instrument itself.

## No REPERFORM result

No chapter in 113–138 crossed the embodiment threshold that triggered fresh performance in earlier batches.

This matters because several chapters are very short. The audit confirms again that **length is not the criterion**. Chapters such as `The Drying House`, `The Marker`, `The Reedline`, and `The West Packet` would lose specific lived material if reduced to synopsis even though they occupy little space.

By contrast, earlier re-performance candidates such as 108–112 skipped the very work, waiting, attempts, social friction, or physical procedure that should have been the reading experience. That failure is absent here.
