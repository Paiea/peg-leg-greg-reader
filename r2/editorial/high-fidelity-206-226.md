## Embodiment finding

No chapter in 206–226 collapses into treatment-level prose. Even Chapter 226, which deliberately compresses six uneventful days, earns compression because the subject is ordinary competence and relief from predictive conflict. Replacing it with a competent synopsis would lose the texture of warehouse work, mill work, Noll, Jorren, Mara, and the closed WATCH notebook.

The accepted Round 06 prose therefore continues the recent pattern: future-generated material is arriving scene-complete enough that high-fidelity work is primarily listening-residue cleanup rather than fresh staging.
