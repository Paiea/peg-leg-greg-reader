# Connected Read — Candidate Chapters 131–134

Status: **EDITORIAL CHECK / NOT SELECTED AUTHORITY**

Read as one listening sequence after Candidate Chapter 130.

## Overall verdict

**KEEP THE FOUR-CHAPTER SHAPE.**

This sequence succeeds because it does not treat Mara's departure as an interruption between Greg jobs. The departure clock owns four chapters of actual lived time while the book still preserves one urgent fantasy-work beat and one deliberately unproductive day.

The rhythm is:

**packing / relationship friction -> urgent local action -> ordinary fantasy pleasure -> departure**

That is stronger than the temporal order that would have stacked more professional work before letting Mara leave.

## 131 `Six Days`

Strong survivor.

What works:

- Mara deliberately packing badly is character rather than incompetence. She knows how to move professionally and does not want to finish leaving too efficiently.
- Greg is asked to help without owning the packing system. This echoes his people-development pressure in an ordinary domestic context without explaining the theme.
- Tali and Mara have an independent relationship.
- Mara knows Veya through her own Vey work rather than because Greg introduces them socially.
- The first-visit conversation correctly leaves the date unresolved until Mara knows her actual Northbank work/housing life.
- Greg finding an old letter about specialization gives continuity residue without claiming destiny.

Watch:

- Do not repeatedly use `ask first` as a visible lesson in every social scene. Here it is earned by the trunk problem and should not become a slogan.
- The letter callback is strong enough. Do not keep mining old letters for perfect foreshadowing.

## 132 `Shape`

Strong action survivor.

What works:

- Red Quay is absent. Greg's specialization therefore proves portable rather than becoming chemistry unique to one preferred party.
- Captain Osa owns the fire site, Nerri/Pellin own heat, Dren's hook crew owns physical access, and Greg owns structural failure direction.
- The core solution is not `hold the burning warehouse`. Greg makes inward collapse easier than outward collapse and lets the doomed structure fail safely.
- This is a much better action expression of his lane than another static brace job.
- The fire creates danger and spectacle without requiring a villain or dungeon.
- Mara's post-job reaction reconnects the chapter to the departure clock immediately.

Watch:

- Greg currently has several chapters where the physical insight is `do not hold everything`. Do not state this again soon. Future scenes should make the specialization mutate rather than merely re-prove it.
- Nerri is good world texture. Do not retain her automatically because she had one good exchange.

## 133 `The Tourist`

Keep.

This chapter is structurally important specifically because no lesson is required to justify it.

What works:

- Tourist ferry and Halden from the river broaden setting.
- Glass chimes, glassberry food, the ring game, public gallery, and cheap charm prize make fantasy life pleasurable without becoming plot machinery.
- Greg loses repeatedly at a children's market game despite analyzing it.
- The little blue/green-wing bird is ordinary object residue that may or may not matter later.
- Greg's prosthetic remains present through stairs, socket heat, and bench/wall choices without dominating the day.
- Mara gets to enforce leisure without becoming Greg's therapist.

Watch:

- Do not turn the wooden bird into a symbolic artifact because it exists. It can remain a stupid prize.
- `Nothing happened. Excellent day.` is neat but earned here because the chapter's explicit function is ordinary life. Avoid repeating the construction elsewhere.

## 134 `The Traveler`

Strong survivor.

What works:

- Halden people come to Mara's departure because she built a life there. They are not Greg's crowd.
- Tali's second blue cup is excellent mundane emotional residue.
- Greg finally shows Mara the original comparison table only after her choice is complete.
- The original table already ranking Northbank highest professionally makes Greg's preference for Carrow more human and less disguised as analysis.
- The farewell remains physical, public, slightly inconvenient, and not overwritten.
- Greg does not run alongside the packet or convert departure into a route plan.
- The final fried-dough purchase is an ordinary appetite choice rather than a new mission.

Watch:

- Northbank must now develop offscreen and through Mara's own letters/work before Greg visits. Do not immediately teleport Greg there.
- The comparison-table motif is complete enough for now.
