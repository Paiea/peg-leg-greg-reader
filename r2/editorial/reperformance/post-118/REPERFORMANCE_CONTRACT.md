# R2 Post-118 Re-performance Contract

Status: **ACTIVE CANDIDATE BOOK RE-PERFORMANCE / NOT SELECTED AUTHORITY**

## Purpose

The temporal third-year pressure test through speculative Chapter 158 was used to discover the post-118 book. It is quarry, challenger evidence, and storyfinding material. This lane now fresh-writes the actual candidate book from selected Chapter 118 authority.

Do not mechanically expand the 1,000-word rehearsals. Re-perform each chapter from Chapter 118 authority plus surviving discoveries. Merge, cut, reorder, or replace speculative events whenever the fresh scene wants a better shape.

## Target chapter experience

- Default target: roughly 2,000 to 2,500 words.
- This is a listening target, not a quota. A chapter may run shorter or longer when the scene earns it.
- Audio/listening first. A listener should understand physical action, speaker identity, referents, stakes, and transitions without rereading.
- Mostly Greg POV and Greg attention. Outside characters retain their own agency, expertise, language, and decisions.
- Use clear dialogue attribution whenever a listener could lose speaker ownership. Do not rely on page layout alone to disambiguate conversation.
- Preserve processing time. Do not compress away the beat needed to build a room, understand a movement, absorb a joke, or register a social turn.
- Written finish should remain close enough to the shared listening surface that Read and Listen feel like the same chapter.
- No em dashes in manuscript prose.
- Avoid automatic Greg-cadence machinery: repeated `Good`, `Useful`, `Different`, repeated `Not X. Y.`, and neat thesis endings unless the exact moment earns them.

## Storyfinding residue that currently survives

These are pressures and discoveries, not a fixed outline:

- Greg's broad competence is a high floor. His forward ceiling should narrow.
- Strongest physical specialization: changing usable conditions through force relationships, selective movement, timing, load, position, redirection, anchoring, and controlled failure.
- Strongest people-development pressure: Greg can sometimes see one adjacent capability a specialist has not used, but capability does not equal aspiration. Ask, create bounded conditions, let the other person own meaning and adoption.
- Party formation should emerge through repeated choice among asymmetric specialists. No RPG-slot assembly and no protagonist-gravity requirement.
- Other people must improve Greg and one another. Greg is not the universal coach.
- Mara's career and geography move independently.
- Blackglass and Faultglass already make artifact escalation a repeat pattern, not a first temptation.
- The setter's cuff remains optional and should return only if a real limitation makes its tradeoff attractive.
- Mundane fantasy remains load-bearing: money, food, repairs, travel, letters, work, prosthetic maintenance, receipts, lodging, jokes, and ordinary pleasure.
- Conventional fantasy pleasure must remain frequent enough to prevent the book becoming professional-work procedural: ruins, monsters, fights, strange markets, fairs, treasure, dangerous people, travel, games, and adventure.
- Greg has no guaranteed survival and no assumed third life. Do not target death, but do not bend causality to prevent it.

## Re-performance method

For each candidate chapter:

1. Fresh-read selected Chapter 118 authority and the immediately prior re-performed candidate chapter.
2. Read only the relevant temporal survivor material as challenger evidence.
3. Ask what should actually happen next now, not what the rehearsal already said happened.
4. Write the full listening-first scene at lived fidelity.
5. Preserve clear speaker ownership and physical continuity.
6. Challenge repeated prose machinery and overly tidy thematic explanation.
7. Keep the candidate in this lane until explicitly selected/promoted.
