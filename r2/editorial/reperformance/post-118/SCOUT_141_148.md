- In a sustained barrier/hold event, he is merely respectable. A younger caster can simply make a better broad wall.

- Lysa Kerr and Tomel Rusk naturally re-enter through the exchange because they already share Field Support Three history with Greg.
- Team problem assignments create a temporary combination without declaring a party.
- Greg notices a possible adjacent capability in one specialist, but his first idea should not be perfectly correct.
- Another person should push Greg sideways in return.
- Jorren can be present through freight demonstration work without joining Greg's events.
- Pell can have an equipment-table reason to appear, again without becoming Greg's handler.

Strengths:

- recognizable tournament/exam/festival-adjacent fantasy pleasure;
- many sharply specialized people;
- low-cost social collisions;
- safe place for failure that does not require death or catastrophe;
- perfect environment to test the people-development half of Greg's emerging lane;
- naturally supports embodied-role chapter titles.

Risks:

- can become a syllabus of lessons if every event teaches Greg exactly one craft point;
- can become anime tournament sludge if every match needs detailed resolution;
- do not make everyone impressed by Greg.

Verdict: **STRONGEST NEXT ENGINE.**
