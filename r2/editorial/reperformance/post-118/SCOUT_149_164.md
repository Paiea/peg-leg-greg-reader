# R2 Book Scout — After Candidate Chapter 148

Status: **TEMPORAL STORY SEARCH / ZERO AUTHORITY**

Anchor: Candidate Chapter 148 `The Consultant`.

Search window: roughly 10–20 lived days.

Purpose: reopen overt fantasy/adventure after the field exchange and bounded Hearthline consult. Test Greg making his first intentional team-selection move without allowing him to assemble an ideal RPG party.

## Live state

Greg:

- back in Carrow by choice;
- has just proven case-by-case professional value to Hearthline under restrictions;
- no retainer restored;
- Daro remains hurt but may request Greg on one future install read;
- current strongest lane is small condition manipulation, not broad-force dominance;
- people-development pressure exists but must be demonstrated more than explained;
- has money enough to choose among jobs rather than accept everything;
- no immediate Mara visit; Northbank remains hers for at least another two weeks;
- no setter-cuff need currently earned.

People:

- Lysa has a self-chosen mobile-ward prototype but no reason to become Greg's permanent teammate.
- Tomel has real recovery work and does not want a new career assigned to him.
- Sera Nin wants pay, fights very well, and has shown no desire to orbit Greg.
- Red Quay is elsewhere and functioning without Greg.
- Pell/Kesta, Jorren, Noll, Mara, Hearthline all have independent work.

Book pressure:

- professional infrastructure scenes are near saturation;
- need overt fantasy pleasure: strange place, danger, loot/material, hostile creature/person, travel, camp/food, uncertainty;
- Greg should make at least one intentional people choice;
- at least one intended person should refuse or be unavailable;
- final working group should contain someone Greg did not select;
- do not automatically make the resulting group permanent;
- Greg's structural lane may help, but the adventure cannot exist solely to validate it.

## Route A — Standing-team reunion

Greg sees a mixed-field contract and asks Lysa + Tomel because the exchange proved they can work together.

Result possibilities:

- both say yes;
- course chemistry becomes job chemistry;
- first deliberate party begins.

Strengths:

- immediate emotional continuity;
- easy to understand;
- lets repeated vocabulary begin forming.

Weaknesses:

- far too clean after the exchange;
- turns one good course into destiny;
- makes Greg's first deliberate selection suspiciously correct;
- accelerates party formation before the world has tested alternatives.

Verdict: **DO NOT TAKE CLEANLY.**

May use as Greg's first instinct only if reality interrupts it.

## Route B — Flood-opened ruin contract

A spring flood or bank collapse exposes access to a pre-city ruin outside Carrow, but not another structural rescue site. The site contains a sealed archive/workshop/religious or civic complex with magical residue, creatures that moved in later, and a salvage-right component.

The Guild needs a small mixed team for a two- or three-day first survey before larger crews enter.

Contract requirements could include:

- one licensed ward/protection specialist;
- one recovery/rope person;
- one licensed fighter;
- one general field/structural support person;
- optional healer or local guide depending route.

Greg sees the contract and, for the first time, intentionally asks for specific people.

Important disruption:

- Tomel is unavailable on paid road recovery.
- Lysa may decline because she already has convoy work, or accept only if timing/rate actually fits.
- Greg does not get both.
- Sera may already be listed independently because she wants the pay, not because Greg recruits her.
- another specialist should be assigned by the Guild or contract holder and be someone Greg would not have chosen.

Possible new specialist shape:

**Nemi Voss**, resonance listener / magical surveyor.

Very narrow capability: hears or feels active magical repetition through stone/metal and can distinguish current from old residue, but is mediocre in combat and hates making interpretation claims beyond her signal.

Alternative if this feels too tailored to ruins:

A local creature handler, poison worker, map-runner, or devotional historian with a narrow practical reason to enter.

Do not lock Nemi unless the scene wants her.

Adventure texture:

- road out of Carrow;
- bad camp food;
- first view of flood-cut stone;
- creature sign that is not immediately identified;
- salvage tags and ownership arguments;
- one object Greg wants but does not automatically take;
- at least one fight where Sera or another fighter is clearly better than Greg;
- one magical problem whose answer is not `Greg understands structure`;
- one place Greg's condition-control becomes nasty in combination with another person's narrow tool;
- uncertain reward rather than fixed professional fee only.

Strengths:

- overt fantasy/adventure;
- supports intentional team selection plus refusal/unavailability;
- creates loot and artifact pressure without needing the setter cuff;
- allows a strange new person to arrive through job logic, not protagonist gravity;
- gives Greg a first chance to think about composition as a build while discovering that people have schedules.

Risk:

- another ruin too soon after Halden quarry if the visual/mechanical language resembles Bell Room.

Guardrail:

Make this ruin culturally/visually and mechanically different. No buried structural-training hall, no bell, no setter system, no collapse rescue as core objective.

Verdict: **STRONGEST ROUTE IF THE SETTING IS DISTINCT.**

## Route C — Monster convoy / live cargo

A merchant or Guild handler needs a small team to move a dangerous captured magical creature, eggs, or monster material between Carrow and another city.

Greg tries to choose people because the job has clear weaknesses.

Strengths:

- travel;
- monster proximity;
- money and weird cargo;
- team composition matters;
- can become chaotic without another ruin.

Weaknesses:

- risks becoming another freight/logistics procedural after Jorren and the trip home;
- Greg's load/position skill could dominate too conveniently.

Verdict: **GOOD BACKUP, NOT FIRST CHOICE.**

## Route D — Street/political danger in Carrow

A person from Greg's first-life knowledge resurfaces earlier/differently than expected. Greg attempts to redirect them, creating people-side danger.

Strengths:

- directly advances regression/hubris/person-management theme.

Weaknesses now:

- would follow several conceptual people-development scenes with another concept-heavy social test;
- less fantasy-motion pleasure;
- better after a more external adventure resets the book's texture.

Verdict: **KEEP WARM FOR LATER.**

## Selected pressure

Take **Route B**, but only the near edge.

Fresh-perform:

1. Greg finds or is offered a mixed-team first-survey contract into a flood-opened ruin distinct from Halden's Bell Room.
2. He deliberately thinks about who he would want.
3. At least one obvious choice is unavailable or refuses.
4. The actual contract roster includes at least one person Greg did not choose.
5. Stop before the group has proven chemistry.

Do not outline the entire ruin before the roster exists.

## Setting differentiation from Bell Room

Avoid:

- quarry blast;
- deep buried masonry corridors;
- structural-training rigs;
- old setter cuffs;
- bell motif;
- rescue of trapped workers;
- Gold team arriving to model professionalism.

Prefer something visually and culturally different, such as:

- a flood-cut riverbank exposing a tilted ceremonial complex open partly to sky;
- glazed tile, painted stone, water-worn courtyards, broken statuary, old garden channels;
- later creatures using open flooded spaces rather than tunnel crawlers;
- magical residue tied to reflection, memory, weather, sound, ritual, cultivation, or civic use rather than structural engineering.

The exact aesthetic should emerge in prose.

## Team-selection rule

Greg may think in composition terms, but people are not interchangeable components.

His first deliberate team-building action should teach him this through logistics before any moral lesson:

- people already have jobs;
- people prefer different rates;
- people dislike certain contract holders;
- someone cannot travel on the needed dates;
- someone simply says no;
- the person available may be less ideal on paper and better in reality.

## Stop condition

Stop after contract + actual roster + departure or first sight of the site.

Connected-read before solving the ruin or deciding anyone is recurring party material.
