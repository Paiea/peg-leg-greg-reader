## Route A — Open the seam

Morning air is clear enough for controlled entry.

Dessa strongly suspects the seam is a door to an intact room.

She is wrong.

Possible truth:

- the seam is one edge of a large maintenance shutter / seasonal sluice panel;
- opening it reveals a narrow covered service walk or caretaker gallery, not a ceremonial chamber;
- the hidden space gives practical access beneath/behind the garden network.

Benefits:

- expert error without making Dessa stupid;
- preserves forward momentum;
- changes spatial mode from open courtyard to a narrow built space while remaining garden/civic rather than quarry-like;
- allows small loose objects / tools / tags / strange horticultural equipment;
- can reveal a magical maintenance system or old caretaker construct without answering the whole site's purpose.

Potential fantasy toy:

An old **pruner** or **gardener** mechanism still functioning intermittently.

Not necessarily humanoid. Could be:

- jointed stone-and-brass armatures that move along wall rails;
- a basket-bodied caretaker construct with pruning hooks;
- a mobile trellis frame that identifies fast growth and cuts it back.

Danger should arise because it treats modern gear/ward growth/moving bodies as maintenance obstructions, not because it is an evil guardian.

Possible loot/pleasure:

- loose glazed maintenance tokens;
- old pruning blade/tool;
- colored ceramic seed markers;
- small civic coins caught in drains;
- dormant seed pods under city hold;
- one genuinely silly object that Greg is allowed to like.

Strengths:

- strongest momentum;
- gives visual novelty and loot;
- allows someone competent to be wrong;
- opens another layer without explaining everything.

Verdict: **STRONGEST ROUTE.**
