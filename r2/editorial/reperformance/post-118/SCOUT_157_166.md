## Route A — Limited lower-level descent on final survey day

The team returns at inactive phase, confirms air and route, then descends only far enough to identify the lower system and major hazards.

Possible lower-space identity:

- an old reservoir / rain-storage level beneath the garden;
- visually impossible water behavior distinct from ordinary cisterns;
- examples: water held in vertical skins along stone, suspended in ring frames, or collected in ceiling basins that feed downward only during cycles;
- service walkways and old maintenance marks keep the space civic/worked rather than mystical temple dungeon.

Strong discoveries:

- the central dry basin is not storage itself; it transfers water below;
- garden cycles may be load/distribution cycles rather than ritual;
- Nemi can distinguish repeating system rhythm but still not intent;
- Dessa can identify civic/work terminology on lower tags;
- Greg can read where old physical load and current magical load disagree, but not why the magic exists;
- Orra retains authority over air/ward safety;
- Fen keeps the descent bounded by contract.

Adventure pressure:

- lower walk is wet/slippery and partly damaged;
- one old mechanism activates unexpectedly;
- injured bank drake or another animal may use one outlet, but do not require a boss fight;
- salvage temptation increases because the lower level contains intact tools/components.

Strengths:

- pays off the stairs immediately;
- gives a strong fantasy image;
- completes the first-survey arc without solving the whole site;
- lets contract end with a useful map and unanswered deeper layers.

Verdict: **STRONGEST ROUTE.**
