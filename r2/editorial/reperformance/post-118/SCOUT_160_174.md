# R2 Book Scout — After Candidate Chapter 159

Status: **TEMPORAL STORY SEARCH / ZERO AUTHORITY**

Anchor: Candidate Chapter 159 `The Salvager`.

Purpose: change texture after the 11-chapter West Bramble expedition and bring the underused people-side regression pressure into the book without immediately creating another large adventure arc.

## Live state

- Greg is home in Carrow after a successful three-day first survey.
- West Bramble second survey is possible but not scheduled and should not win automatically.
- Hearthline has one future install-read possibility under the same restrictions.
- Mara remains in Northbank and has asked Greg not to visit yet.
- Greg has some choice money, legal salvage, and no giant external clock.
- Setter cuff still lacks a concrete current need.
- Greg has now experienced several different team shapes and deliberately tried to recruit one crew once.

## Search pressure

We want a texture change:

- home / food / letters / Guild ordinary life;
- less ruin mechanics;
- people-side uncertainty;
- first-life knowledge becoming socially dangerous rather than physically useful;
- one current person with independent wants, obligations, and reasons Greg did not know in Life One;
- Greg's belief that he can `reroute` a person should be understandable and partly rewarded, not immediately punished as arrogance.

## Route A — Future-known person appears through ordinary work

Greg encounters a younger version of someone he remembers from Life One as later dangerous, unreliable, brutal, or betraying.

Important rules:

- do not introduce them as a villain;
- current person must be doing something mundane and legible now;
- Greg's memory of their future must be incomplete and filtered through where/when old Greg knew them;
- current person has obligations Greg never knew;
- Greg should not immediately reveal regression knowledge;
- Greg's first intervention should be small enough to plausibly help.

Possible current situations:

- applying for a field contract and being rejected for reputation/poverty rather than ability;
- caught in a small supply theft or contract breach with a concrete present motive;
- excellent route reader / scout with poor paperwork and a history of quitting jobs;
- trying to cover family medical/debt obligations through bad short-term choices;
- working for an employer Greg remembers as one influence on their later trajectory.

Possible Greg action:

- recommend a trial job;
- redirect them toward a different contract;
- quietly structure pay/debt so one bad choice becomes unnecessary;
- introduce them to a person Greg thinks changes the path;
- keep them close because he believes he can monitor variables.

Core danger:

Greg may be correct about capability and still wrong about ownership/context.

Verdict: **STRONGEST NEXT PRESSURE.**

Strengths:

Verdict: **NOT YET.**

## Selected pressure

Use **Route A**, but begin with decompression.

Near-edge sequence:

1. One ordinary home chapter after West Bramble: unpack, snail, pay, food, perhaps a shorter Mara note or Northbank residue.
2. Greg returns to Guild/ordinary work rather than seeking the next major adventure.
3. He sees a current young worker/person he recognizes from Life One.
4. Recognition should be physically specific and unsettling, not a lore dump.
5. Greg remembers only enough future history to create a hypothesis about what went wrong.
6. Current situation immediately contradicts at least one assumption.
7. Stop before Greg has a full plan for the person.

## Person construction guardrails

Do not use the old temporal placeholder name/history automatically.

Fresh-perform the person.

Need:

- current age/role that fits Carrow;
- one narrow real competence;
- one present weakness/problem;
- one relationship/obligation Greg did not know in Life One;
- a future reputation old Greg remembers strongly enough to bias him;
- no chosen-one connection to Greg;
- no instant loyalty;
- no cartoon destiny language.

## Positive-reinforcement rule

If Greg later intervenes, some of it should work.

The story should not teach him `never use future knowledge on people` by immediate author punishment.

Better danger:

- Greg improves one current variable;
- the person genuinely benefits;
- Greg becomes more confident he understands the trajectory;
- another stakeholder later reveals he spent trust/choice that was not his.

## Stop condition

Write only through recognition + first current contradiction. Then connected-read before Greg begins actively rerouting the person.
