# R2 Team-Course Scout — After Candidate Chapter 143

Status: **TEMPORAL STORY SEARCH / ZERO AUTHORITY**

Anchor: Candidate Chapter 143 `The Candidates`.

The Guild has assigned Lysa Kerr / Tomel Rusk / Greg to the third-morning team course. They explicitly chose not to practice together beforehand.

## Course design requirement

The course must be understandable once by ear. Avoid a puzzle box with twelve mechanisms.

Use one visible objective:

**Move one loaded rescue sled and its fragile medicine case from the old freight shed to the marked safe bay across a damaged loading yard.**

The route contains three physically clear problems:

1. a sloped broken loading ramp;
2. an open drainage cut crossed by a narrow suspended plank/platform;
3. a final low gate where the sled must fit under a partially hanging beam.

The team is scored on:

- time;
- dummy/payload protection;
- medicine-case damage;
- unnecessary hazard exposure;
- whether they abandon recoverable equipment.

Judges can trigger only two controlled failures so audio remains legible:

- remove one marked anchor after the team commits to the drainage crossing;
- release the final hanging beam a few inches once the sled enters the gate.

No secret monster. No surprise fourth mechanism.

## Wrong stale assumptions

Greg's instinct before starting:

- Tomel rigs.
- Lysa wards.
- Greg handles movement/failure.

This is directionally true and still too crude.

Current people should complicate it:

- Tomel is now best positioned to own **route and sequence**, not merely rope. He should call the first plan because the objective is recovery/movement.
- Lysa's frame is not just a static shield. She has learned to shrink protected geometry under lost support.
- Greg is useful after movement becomes ugly, but if he starts pre-authoring every contingency he will slow the team.

## Course route

### Start / ramp

The rescue sled is heavier than expected and the medicine case must stay level.

Greg starts to propose lifting/force support.

Tomel rejects it. The fastest safe first move is physical: re-seat the sled runners, move one crate from sled to hand carry, then use rope only as a brake downhill rather than a pull uphill.

Lysa owns the medicine case initially because her ward can protect it from side strike, but she decides to strap the case into the center of the sled after seeing the ramp geometry. Greg's assumption that she should carry/protect it separately is wrong.

This establishes Tomel as route caller and Lysa making her own protection choice.

### Drainage cut

Narrow suspended plank carries the sled if load stays centered.

Tomel rigs one high guide line and one rear control. Greg uses small local planes only to prevent runner drift, not to carry weight. Lysa protects the dummy from a side swing with a narrow ward.

Judge removes the high anchor after the sled reaches midpoint.

Greg's instinct is to stop the sled in place.

Tomel calls **let it run six inches** because arresting it over the cut would overload the remaining line.

Greg obeys. He controls where that run ends instead of fighting movement.

Lysa independently folds her ward from broad side protection into a small angled catch around the medicine case when the sled yaws.

The team recovers.

This is the strongest action discovery.

### Final gate

Low hanging beam means Greg initially assumes Lysa should keep a roof ward and Tomel should drag.

Wrong again.

Tomel's rope angle is poor beneath the beam. Lysa uses one edge of her copper frame physically as a guide/skid for the line while keeping only a tiny ward over the medicine case. Greg gets underneath the moving relation and keeps beam load resolving into the intact gate post.

Judge releases beam a few inches.

Greg almost issues a full command string.

Instead Tomel owns sled movement and Lysa owns what she protects. Greg only calls the condition he needs: `Beam can come down. Keep the sled moving.`

They clear.

## Result

They should **not win**.

Recommended: fourth or fifth.

Reasons:

- they spend too long at the first ramp because old familiarity makes them assume too much;
- one medicine vial cracks during the drainage yaw, despite Lysa's recovery;
- their final gate work is excellent and earns strong adaptation marks;
- a standing three-person crew beats them cleanly on time and communication.

This preserves pleasure without making the reunion magically optimal.
