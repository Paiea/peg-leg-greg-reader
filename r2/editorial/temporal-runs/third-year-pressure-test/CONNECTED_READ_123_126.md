## 125 — Three Seconds

**Verdict: SOURCE WIN / strongest action discovery.**

What wins:
- Greg does not hold the whole room. He distributes jobs and controls where force goes.
- Renn, Veya, Korr, Mett, and Asha remain necessary. Remove any one and the solution changes materially.
- The clean win feels earned and is allowed to feel good.
- Veya's objection after the rescue creates a useful interpersonal boundary: Greg cannot treat another person's specialty as a component he owns merely because his plan works.

Important discovery:
- Greg's emerging specialty is less `force magic` than **relationship between forces**. He can be mediocre at producing raw force and still become exceptional at routing other people's force, terrain, load, and motion.
- This creates a future danger if Greg starts making technically correct calls about costs other people did not consent to.

Do not turn that danger into an immediate punishment. The scene works because Greg's call succeeds.

What wins:

- Setter cuffs are recontextualized without becoming harmless. Their cost remains real, but their original use was embedded in a deeply specialized team system.
- The room echoes the current party problem without requiring prophecy or chosen-one logic.

- The paragraph explaining why Greg wants `the arrangement` says slightly more than needed. Preserve the discovery and let later behavior prove whether it matters.

Survivor: old infrastructure demonstrates that extreme specialization can be made viable by a flexible system around it. Greg now has evidence, not a conclusion.

## Connected-run verdict

**KEEP THE ROUTE. DO NOT DECLARE THE PARTY. DO NOT DECLARE GREG'S CLASS.**

The useful new engine is:

1. Put Greg beside people who are dramatically better than him at narrow tasks.
2. Give those people limitations that remain real rather than secretly disappearing around Greg.
3. Let Greg deepen by controlling conditions, geometry, timing, transfer, position, and failure paths.
4. Make Greg increasingly dependent on trusting specialists instead of filling their jobs himself.
5. Let people object when Greg uses their capability as if agreeing to the task meant agreeing to every consequence.
6. Keep jobs, money, monsters, meals, gear, romance, travel, reputation, and setting movement alive around the progression.

The next run should not jump to `Greg recruits a party`. Red Quay is currently evidence and repeated-work possibility. Let the two-day follow-up test chemistry, then advance Mara's independent decision clock and ordinary-life residue before another large adventure beat.
