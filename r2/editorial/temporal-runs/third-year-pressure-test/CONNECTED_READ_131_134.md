## Verdict

The strongest discovery in this pass is not “Greg forms a party.” It is that repeated specialist work begins creating **selection between people** without anybody declaring a permanent group.

Candidate 131 works because Renn asks Greg back for a concrete operational reason, not affection or protagonist gravity: retraining a new structural specialist around Veya would cost time. The recurring-group pressure therefore emerges from accumulated coordination value.

Candidate 132 is the strongest scene of the pass. The canal failure makes Greg’s specialization concrete: he is not winning by generating more force. He is routing failure, selecting which loads are allowed to move, and using other people’s narrow capabilities. More importantly, Veya’s correction creates a needed ethical/social boundary: Greg’s tactical compression can turn people into commands. `Tell me what you need, not what I am` is useful challenger truth. Preserve the behavior even if the exact line changes in future re-performance.

Candidate 133 is useful because Mara’s Northbank choice stays independent of the adventure machine. The six-day packing clock gives Greg an ordinary-life commitment that competes with fantasy work without pretending one is more “real” than the other.

Candidate 134 is another strong survivor because Greg refuses the exact kind of specialist contract the experiment was designed to make attractive. This prevents the specialization/party nudge from becoming destiny. Veya says she will ask again; that is enough. The scene also identifies a promising technical direction: Greg may become less interested in holding more force and more interested in deciding **where force and failure are allowed to resolve**.

## What should survive

- Repeated contracts create coordination value before friendship or formal party membership.
- Veya’s fixed-line technique pairs unusually well with Greg’s ability to create temporary stable geometry.
- Greg should remain materially worse than specialists at their specialties. Mett hearing stone, Veya cutting, Korr’s burst, Asha’s medicine, Renn’s leadership all need to stay independently valuable.
- Greg’s command instincts are an asset and a character hazard. Under pressure he can compress people into positions/tasks. The answer is not to make him stop being decisive; it is to make consent, role ownership, and information quality matter.
- Mara choosing Northbank should remain a live independent clock if this future survives. Greg does not need to immediately relocate or immediately decide the relationship’s long-term geography.
- Greg refusing Veya’s two-day mill job because he wants Mara’s final ordinary days is a strong counterexample to the “preserve every option / take every valuable opportunity” pressure.
- The small crawler-tendon washer is good loot residue because it is partial, mundane, replaceable, and not a magical cure.

## What should not harden yet

- Do not label Greg’s final class/specialty. “Moving structure,” “fixed points,” “routing failure,” and “conditions/geometry” are discoveries, not final terminology.
- Do not lock Veya as a permanent party member yet. She is simply the strongest repeated-choice signal so far.
- Do not make Renn’s entire Gold team migrate into Greg’s future. They are currently excellent reference people and recurring work partners.
- Do not rush back to the setter’s cuff merely because Greg’s specialization is getting narrower. The story has now created enough organic narrowing that the cuff can remain optional pressure rather than required explanation.
- Do not make Greg’s social lesson too neat. In emergencies he will still sometimes issue blunt commands. The point is that people around him can push back and shape how he leads.

2. **Opportunity cost:** Mara/Northbank makes every accepted job carry calendar cost. This is useful mundane pressure.

5. **Setting expansion:** Halden now contains quarry, canal infrastructure, fire crews, prosthetic craft, salvage market, Vey work life, and Guild bureaucracy. Keep building cities through repeated practical use rather than exposition.
