## Verdict

This pass successfully widens the people-development concept beyond `Greg helps people grow` into a more reciprocal and less hierarchical network model.

Sera is the critical counterexample. Greg asks what she wants to improve and she says **nothing**. She wants the escort pay, she already values fencing/guard work, and she does not need a hidden second career or growth project for the chapter to justify her existence. This refusal is correct.

Despite refusing Greg's developmental framing, Sera independently identifies a Greg weakness and creates a tiny one-beat training constraint for him. That preserves reciprocity: Greg is not the only person capable of seeing unused range or bad habits in others.

The road ambush then pays off the drill without turning it into a named technique or magical breakthrough. Greg's benefit is attentional: answer the current beat before pre-authoring the next one. That improves his force intervention and sword work while remaining incomplete.

- People can develop Greg without joining his party, becoming his mentor, or caring about his larger arc.

- Greg's force specialization works best here when small: change a footfall, alter a bolt path, block a wrist line, shape one current beat.
- Glasswake Fair gives the run a needed fantasy-pleasure release after several work/problem chapters.
