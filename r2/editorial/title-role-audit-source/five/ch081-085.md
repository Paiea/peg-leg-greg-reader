## Chapter 081
PUBLIC TITLE: Five Days
WRITTEN HEADING: # Year Chapter 39: Five Days
TEASER: 

### Opening
By D360 I had forgotten what clean socks felt like.

Not literally.

Probably.

Seven days since Nine Teeth had reduced Field Support Three to a collection of repairs wearing people.

Kell carried an expedition spare spear with a head two inches shorter than his old one and hated it personally.

Bera's shield rim had been hammered straight enough that it no longer caught her coat, though one corner still folded inward where the rimewing hit.

Leth's ward frame wore a translucent patch cut from rimewing membrane over the cracked hinge. He had spent three nights testing whether the material actually resisted cold charge or merely looked expensive.

It resisted enough to keep.

Miri had sewn the torn finger of her glove with red thread because no black remained.

Senn had developed a cough he insisted was dry air and everyone else insisted was age.

Maren's tiny brass bell had lost its clapper somewhere on Low Venn road.

He kept the useless bell.

I approved.

My Blackglass Anchor survived Nine Teeth with one primary eye bent five degrees out of true and the dampener sleeve missing its forward screw.

We could not replace the eye safely in field.

Maren and Leth de-rated it instead.


### Closing

I listened.

Relief came so hard my knees felt weak.

I hated that too.

Maren found me after council.

"You okay?"

"Yes."

He stared.

"No. Happy."

"That's allowed."

Interesting.

"I told them switchback."

"I heard."

"Then Load Road wins anyway."

"Road doesn't care why you wanted it."

No lesson beyond fact.

We camped at Rook's Mouth.

Five days remained.

The expedition had chosen the route that might make my date and would abandon it the moment it stopped being the right route.

That was probably the only way the promise could stay healthy.

At dinner, Miri handed me something.

A small black piece of stone.

"What?"

"Overlook."

She had picked it from Load Road while I stared west.

"Souvenir?"

"Practice. You keep touching wrong black rocks."

I threw it at her.

She ducked.

Senn shouted because throwing rocks inside tent was apparently medical issue.

Five days.

I slept.

Not successfully.

"I left on this road."

"Then you've used it twice."

## Chapter 084

### Opening
I arrived at the river stairs early.

Not very early.

Seven minutes.

Mara had specifically forbidden this.

I had spent a year crossing half a continent toward a date I wrote myself. Apparently punctuality had become a disease.

The first Halden barge crew was already loading the forward deck.

Crates went down the ramp in pairs. One man called numbers. Another repeated them. A third appeared employed specifically to disagree with both.

River work still contained the correct amount of yelling.
