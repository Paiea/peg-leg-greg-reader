# Chapter 66: The Release

I used the brace ring badly on the third try.

Not third test.

Third actual job.

Meren had called me for a warehouse lintel ward replacement.

The ring made sense because the temporary support points were three ugly iron brackets somebody had installed in different decades.

First cycle clean.

Second clean.

Third, one bracket shifted.

I saw it.

Also saw the hanging ceramic channel.

Also saw Meren's left hand occupied with the replacement clamp.

Three variables.

There it was.

I tried to keep the channel aligned and compensate for the shifting bracket while preserving the original support plane.

My forehead tightened instantly.

Kesra's diagnosis arrived too late to prevent the mistake and early enough to prevent the second one.

"RELEASING," I called.

Meren looked up.

"Release."

I dropped the plane.

The ring rotated harmlessly.

The ceramic channel sagged into the padded sling Meren had installed before we began.

Because he was competent.

Nothing broke.

Nobody died.

I sat down for two minutes with my eyes closed.

Meren replaced the bad bracket before continuing.

"You saw too much?" he asked.

"Yes."

"Good problem."

"Kesra disagrees."

"Good problem if you stop. Bad problem if you prove something."

Different wording.

Same lesson.

We finished the job.

The ring worked on the next two cycles.

No redemption performance.

Just equipment used correctly after being used incorrectly.

Meren paid me full rate.

"You stopped before I told you," he said.

"Progress."

"Some."

I went home with a mild headache and no need to turn it into tragedy...
