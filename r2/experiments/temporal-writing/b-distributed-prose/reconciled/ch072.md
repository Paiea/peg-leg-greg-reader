# Chapter 72: The Quiet Day

I spent a day doing almost nothing and discovered I had become bad at it.

Not incapable.

Bad.

I woke without work.

No business note.

No packet.

No scheduled instruction.

No Jorren knock.

The Guild board could wait.

So I washed clothes.

Sharpened nothing because the sword did not need it.

Oiled the scabbard.

Checked the brace ring latch.

Moved the bed to sweep underneath and found one copper I had not known I owned.

Profit.

At midday Noll asked whether I wanted lunch.

"Yes."

We ate fried potatoes downstairs.

He told me a woman at the bakery had asked if he was available Fifthday evening.

I looked at him.

"For work?"

"No."

"Oh."

He stared.

"That took you too long."

"I was being careful."

"Don't."

Independent relationship outside me.

Good.

"You going?"

"Maybe."

"Who?"

"None of your business."

Excellent boundary.

After lunch I walked to East Market without needing anything.

Bought nothing.

Came home.

Practiced one short sword sequence.

Stopped before it became training by anxiety.

Read half of Mara's old packet.

Put it back.

The day ended.

No state transition announced itself.

My laundry dried anyway...
