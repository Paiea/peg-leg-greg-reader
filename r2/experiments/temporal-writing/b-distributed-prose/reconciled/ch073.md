# Chapter 73: The Survey

Harl Fen offered me eight days because a map had become expensive.

Not the map itself.

The disagreement around it.

Two road offices had different boundary markers for the same east-country maintenance district, which meant three bridges had spent a year being inspected by whichever office remembered first.

Harl considered this offensive.

"Bridges should know who neglects them," he said.

We stood outside East Annex while Pate tried to steal cabbage leaves from a produce crate.

"Eight days?"

"Expected. Ten if weather gets stupid."

"Escort?"

"One Bronze. Me. Pate. Lin Varo from district records."

I looked at the route.

Farther east than my current work usually went.

Not expedition country.

Just enough distance that later memory became less useful in detail.

Roads I remembered after widening.

Towns I remembered under different names.

Bridges replaced before I ever crossed them the first time.

Good.

"Why me?"

"You know when not to make survey work exciting."

Professional praise had remained aggressive.

Jorren had his own contract. Edda was west on orchard security. Nobody waited.

Rate covered field days and travel.

I could afford eight days away if nothing broke at home.

Something would break eventually.

Not necessarily this week.

"Yes," I said.

Harl handed me the route copy.

"First bell tomorrow."

Pate successfully stole one cabbage leaf.

The vendor shouted.

Harl paid one copper.

Pate had become a client expense...
