# Chapter 78: The Wrong Turn

I remembered the road correctly and still chose the wrong one.

That was worse.

We reached a fork near Mill Fen on the fifth day.

Later memory said left went toward the old quarry ridge and right toward South Vale.

Our next bridge lay on quarry ridge.

I pointed left.

Harl looked at the current marker.

"Right."

I stopped.

The marker clearly said quarry ridge right.

"Later it's left."

"Later can walk by itself."

Fair.

We went right.

Half a mile later the reason appeared.

The old left road had washed out years before current Greg's birth and been replaced by a loop around firmer ground.

Later still, someone would rebuild the direct line.

My memory had skipped an entire intermediate road.

No catastrophe.

No humiliation.

If I had been alone, I might have wasted an hour before current evidence won.

With Harl, I wasted one sentence.

Useful team.

"Write anything?" Lin asked.

"No," Harl said. "Map is already right."

Nothing in the official record needed my future error.

We kept walking...
