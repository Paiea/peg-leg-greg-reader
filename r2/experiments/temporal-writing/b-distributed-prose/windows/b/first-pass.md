# Chapter 61: The Raise

Dena had negotiated a raise without me.

This was excellent ownership practice because I owned part of the business and terrible emotional practice because I liked knowing things.

"Why?"

"Because she asked."

"That is generally how raises begin."

Arlo sat.

"She is doing more."

That mattered.

The venture had more customers than it had the last time I looked at a complete service sheet.

Not dramatically more.

Enough.

"No."

Good.

"I vote yes."

Arlo nodded.

"Yes."

"Well?"

Ivena said, "Yes."

Dena put the chambers down.

"Good."

Then Ivena turned the ledger toward me.

"Also distribution."

# Chapter 64: The Call

My professional reputation had become extremely specific.
