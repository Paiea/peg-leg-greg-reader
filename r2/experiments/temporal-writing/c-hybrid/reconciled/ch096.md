# Chapter 96: The Note

Tavin wrote that he would be in Carrow for one night.

Not to see me.

This was important enough that he underlined it.

**Venn transfer run. North Gate handoff. One night if road behaves. Less if foreman does.**

Below that:

**If you are busy, be busy. I know where Lower Rook is.**

Current Tavin had developed confidence in addresses.

I checked the date.

Twelve days.

No need to clear my life yet.

I put the note on the shelf beside Mara's last letter.

Then went to work.

The twelve days contained one warehouse escort, two filtration meetings I attended and one I missed, three short sword-practice sessions, one dinner at The Brass Spoon, and a day where rain made every plan smaller.

Tavin did not become more imminent because I thought about him.

On day ten I almost declined a two-day road job because his arrival window sat near the return.

Then I read his note again.

**If you are busy, be busy.**

I took the job.

Returned by midday on the twelfth day.

Tavin had not arrived.

Good.

I bathed anyway.

Self-respect did not need freight confirmation.

At dusk a runner from North Gate brought a scrap.

**Late. Horse threw shoe. Tomorrow maybe.**

No crisis.

No emotional preparation rewarded.

I ate with Noll after his shift.

Went to bed.

Tavin arrived the next afternoon three hours later than the revised maybe...
