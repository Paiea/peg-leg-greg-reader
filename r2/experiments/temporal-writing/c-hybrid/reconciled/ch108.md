# Chapter 108: The Distribution

The filtration venture paid me eighteen copper and immediately asked me not to become excited.

Ivena had learned management.

"Why eighteen?"

"Because reserve is healthy after wage increase, Pellin's first month, Bell Tile firing, and spring replacements."

Pellin had become usable enough to receive the higher apprentice wage.

Dena had become expensive enough that nobody questioned her route-day allowance anymore.

Arlo had become capable of letting Pellin shape simple bodies without standing directly behind him.

The business had changed while I was measuring mud.

Good.

I took the coins.

"Kiln?"

Arlo pointed at me.

"Do not."

Ivena said, "Still not buying one."

Bell Tile overflow remained cheaper than owning capacity we did not need every day.

The old temptation stayed unresolved because the economics stayed unresolved.

I put twelve copper into my personal reserve.

Spent three on new field socks.

One on brace-ring maintenance.

Two on dinner with Noll after his shift.

Money became several things before becoming power...
