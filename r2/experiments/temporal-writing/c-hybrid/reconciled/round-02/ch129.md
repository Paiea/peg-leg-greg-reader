# Chapter 129: The West Packet

Weeks later, Mara's next letter came from Tern.

I had to ask a packet clerk where Tern was.

This offended my geographical dignity.

West of Halden.

Smaller than Bracken.

Temporary route office.

Mara wrote three lines about a warehouse roof, four about a clerk named Sessi who refused to round figures "for the comfort of men who dislike arithmetic," and one about missing Carrow bread.

No invitation.

No warning she might appear.

Just an address that had not existed in my mental version of her life.

I read it twice.

Then I wrote back about Noll's move, the road wheel, and the existence of another Greg.

I did not ask when she was coming back.

She would tell me when there was an answer...
