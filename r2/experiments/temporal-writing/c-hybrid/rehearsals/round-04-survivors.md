## Strong survivors

### 1. Jorren can advance around the Guild ladder

Classification: **SOURCE PRESSURE / REPERFORM**

Accepted Chapter 161 already establishes that Jorren chose an eight-week seasonal road-office field-coordinator role while leaving his Silver application unsigned.

Future pressure:

- the city role should develop on its own terms
- it can pay better than ordinary Bronze road work before changing Guild grade
- Greg may eventually work under Jorren's road-office authority even while Greg is Guild Silver
- do not manufacture awkward rank-role drama; the interesting thing is that different institutions confer different kinds of authority
- Jorren's Silver application remains optional and personally timed

Do not replay the readiness note, offer, or departure.

### 2. Greg's remembered future should begin losing control-group status

Classification: **MAJOR DISCOVERY / SLOW REPERFORM**

Round 03 D found the strongest far-edge pressure yet:

- present-day repair, inspection, trade, and safety decisions may move events Greg once treated as fixed future facts
- West Cut drainage could happen earlier than memory
- post-Guild-fire inspection changes could plausibly prevent, move, or alter a later warehouse fire
- ferry infrastructure dates and road alignments can become uncertain

Do **not** immediately stage a famous remembered disaster and prove it prevented. That would cash the premise too cheaply.

Instead let Greg accumulate evidence that some future memories now belong in a new category:

> **WATCH**

not true, not false, no longer safe as a control.

The first unmistakable divergence should arrive because current causality earned it, not because the story needs a demonstration.

### 3. West / Halden-adjacent work can exist independently of Mara

Classification: **REPERFORM / LOCATION PRESSURE**

A real job may carry Greg west toward or near Halden.

Protect:

- work causes the travel first
- Mara may no longer be there
- current roads and drainage can contradict later memory
- local professionals can know the terrain better than Greg
- reaching Halden's edge does not obligate a reunion or pilgrimage to old addresses

Useful challenger from C: an older Bronze road hand can legitimately correct Silver Greg on ditch or route knowledge.

### 4. Good judgment can cost contract money

Classification: **REPERFORM**

The current run already established Pevi's lost bonus and Greg's missed dinner.

Future version should not simply repeat those mechanics, but preserve the pressure:

- current evidence may favor a safer / longer / more expensive route
- later-memory shortcuts may be unreliable
- a defensible choice can lose a bonus, incur tolls, delay freight, or upset a client
- do not reward every correct call with later proof that Greg was obviously right

A particularly useful C discovery: Greg may choose a longer current route because the remembered later route is not current evidence, then absorb the cost without needing vindication.
