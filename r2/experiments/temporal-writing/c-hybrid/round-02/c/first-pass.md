# Chapter 137: The Assessor

The Silver assessor was a woman named Orra Vale who did not care about my old life.

Promising.

She read my current work sheet instead.

"Mixed sword and support?"

"Apparently."

"Low reserve?"

"Also apparently."

She looked up.

"Do you dislike accurate records?"

"Only emotionally."

No duel followed.

Orra said she would observe ordinary work during the assessment window and leave if the contract became unsafe for evaluation.

"You're not there to save me?"

"I'm there to decide whether other people should pay Silver rates to trust you."

Better standard...

# Chapter 138: The Drain Job

The first assessment contract was a warehouse drain inspection.

I hated this immediately.

Two workers owned the drain tools.

A building manager owned access.

I owned one sword no rat respected and support magic nobody initially needed.

Orra walked behind us and wrote nothing I could see.

The blockage turned out to be collapsed packing cloth and grease.

No monster.

No spell.

I held a lantern, moved sacks, and stopped a worker from climbing onto a wet barrel because the floor beside it existed.

Orra wrote something then.

Rude...

Correct.
