# Chapter 179: The Rope Moths

Rell Ford's rope yard kept bowls of bitter oil beneath the hanging lines.

At dusk pale moths came out of the river grass and landed on tarred rope in clusters.

"Magic?" I asked.

The rope worker shrugged.

"Moths."

Classification remained somebody else's hobby...
