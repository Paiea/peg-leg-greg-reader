# Chapter 146: The Cord

"You passed," Kesra said again, because I had failed to become intelligent after the first attempt.

"Right."

"Try enthusiasm later. Registry first."

The assessment result was not immediate field authority.

Sella needed the examiner signature.

Guild registry needed the new grade entered.

My Bronze token needed replacement.

The clerk doing replacement punched the wrong month into the first Silver token.

She stared at it.

I stared at it.

"Can we agree this is not my fault?" I asked.

"Absolutely not."

She threw it into a scrap drawer and made another.

Institutional advancement consumed brass.

The cord itself was plain silver-gray braid.

No glow.

No stat increase.

No old strength returned because someone tied a different color near my shoulder.

Still, when Sella handed it over, my fingers tightened around it.

Nineteen had opinions.

Fifty-nine had too many memories.

Current me stood between them holding string.

Kesra signed the last line.

"Do not make me regret this."

"Personal standard or Guild standard?"

"Personal. The Guild has forms."

She left before I could thank her properly.

Maybe that was intentional.

Jorren was out on road work.

Noll would not finish until late.

Mara was somewhere west of places I knew well enough to imagine badly.

I went home alone.

Set the new cord on the table.

The old Bronze cord beside it looked smaller only because I knew which one I was staring at.

Then the window latch rattled in the wind and I had to get up to fix it...
