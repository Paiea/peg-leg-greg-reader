# Chapter 156: The Rell Ford Letter

Tavin wrote from Rell Ford because freight had moved him there before any of us discussed visiting.

**Venn has a temporary yard contract. Six weeks if the bridge people stop changing dates.**

Below that:

**Rell Ford still smells like wet rope and bad beer. You would hate the road office and like one bakery.**

No invitation.

Then, three lines later:

**If work ever brings you south, I know where the bakery is. This is not an invitation because then you will turn it into planning.**

He knew me too well in a different direction than Mara did.

I wrote back:

**I refuse to be manipulated by bread.**

Then asked for the bakery name...
