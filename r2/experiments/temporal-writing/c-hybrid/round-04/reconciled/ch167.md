# Chapter 167: The West Board

The first westbound Silver job I took was not actually in Halden.

This improved its chances.

City Works wanted a Guild support hand for a four-day drainage survey between Carrow and the west freight road.

One Silver.

Two Bronze.

One city surveyor.

No excavation beyond hand probes.

No independent authority over road closure.

The pay was ordinary for Silver and unusually specific about who could stop traffic.

I liked that.

Sella watched me read the contract twice.

"You're not supposed to marry it."

"I'm checking whether it wants me to sign anything exciting."

"It does not."

"Promising."

The city surveyor was Varin Cole, forty-something, narrow-faced, and already irritated because Guild staff had arrived seven minutes after he wanted to leave.

The older Bronze was Doss Fen.

Doss had been working west-road drainage longer than I had been alive this time.

He looked at my Silver cord.

Then at my boots.

Then back at the cord.

"You know ditch water?"

"Some kinds."

"Bad answer."

"Accurate answer."

He grunted.

The second Bronze, Mira Pell, was twenty and had brought enough waxed cord to restrain a horse.

Varin handed us route stakes.

"We are measuring three spill lines and checking two old culverts. Nobody repairs anything unless I say so. Nobody closes a lane unless I say so. If something magical begins behaving like it owns public property, the Silver gets to tell me whether it is my problem."

Doss looked at me.

"Congratulations."

We walked west...
