# Chapter 190: The Unsigned

Jorren took the city extension.

He did not sign the Silver application.

Not rejection.

Not fear.

Not a philosophical statement about institutions.

He wanted twelve more weeks of road work.

"I can apply later," he said.

"Yes."

"You sound disappointed."

"I'm trying not to decide what you should want because I got a cord."

Jorren studied me for a moment.

"Better."

He folded the Silver application smaller and tucked it into the back of his field book.

The next morning he reported to city yard three.

A clerk gave him two crew rosters and informed him his handwriting remained unacceptable.

Jorren signed the road-office attendance sheet anyway...
