# Chapter 194: The No

The cooper changed his mind.

His insurer did it for him.

The city access letter had included the phrase **historic service conduit**.

His insurer had discovered imagination.

"They want a structural guarantee before anyone opens the floor," the cooper said.

Issen read the letter twice.

"We are not opening the floor."

"They say inspection may reveal latent defect."

"Inspection may reveal rain."

"Tell them."

"I intend to."

Renn wanted to appeal directly to the owner.

Issen told him not to turn a legal delay into a personal one.

He disliked this.

I liked it because I was not the person being delayed.

Then Sella handed me the Guild support allocation for the East Mill investigation.

Three mornings reserved.

Two were now unusable because parcel access had stalled.

Reserved time paid less than active field work.

I disliked the delay more accurately.

"Can I take something else?" I asked.

"For tomorrow, yes. Third morning stays held until Issen knows."

"That's expensive uncertainty."

"Silver has verbs," Sella said.

I had already learned that.

Apparently some verbs were **remain available**...
