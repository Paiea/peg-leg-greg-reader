# Chapter 195: The Second Look

The cooper's insurer allowed non-invasive access nine days later.

They sent their own surveyor to make sure we obeyed the phrase.

Her name was Pell Ors.

She arrived with a brass measuring rule, polished boots, and the certainty that everyone else had already misunderstood the assignment.

Renn disliked her before she spoke.

This saved time.

The cooper's cellar floor had been rebuilt twice since the old conduit plan.

No exposed warm seam.

No useful cavity.

Pell measured wall thickness.

Issen checked parcel references.

Renn tapped three stones and got told to stop tapping stones.

I held no Barrier because there was nothing to support.

After forty minutes Pell found a six-inch discrepancy between the legal wall line and the actual foundation.

"There," Renn said.

"A discrepancy," Pell said.

"The conduit."

"A discrepancy."

"Do you charge extra per syllable?"

"Only when speaking to excavators."

Renn was not an excavator.

This did not help.

The six inches might be the service line.

Or an old foundation chase.

Or bad surveying seventy years ago.

We left with better measurements and no new opening.

Hara had been right about one thing.

Measuring did become more measuring...
