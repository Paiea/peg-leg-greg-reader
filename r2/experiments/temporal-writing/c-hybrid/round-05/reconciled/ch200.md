# Chapter 200: The River Roof

Mara wrote from a place neither version of me had visited.

South Vey Bend.

River town, two ferry slips, one packet office, and apparently a roof designed by an enemy of rain.

Her letter included a sketch of the office with water falling through three different parts of it.

**The clerk moves ledgers when it rains. I asked why they don't fix the roof. He asked why I don't fix freight. We have reached professional understanding.**

She was there for three weeks.

Maybe five.

A route-account dispute had pulled her south after the western work I had last heard about.

No warning.

No meaningful relationship to my own recent west jobs.

I marked South Vey Bend on the paper map.

Then discovered I had drawn it half an inch too far east.

Mara's sketch corrected me from several hundred miles away...
