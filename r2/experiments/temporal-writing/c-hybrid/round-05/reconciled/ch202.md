# Chapter 202: The Fresh Brick

Twenty-six days later I passed the timber yard on an unrelated escort.

Fresh brick divided the oil store from the scrap bay.

The owner was arguing with a mason about the width of the access door.

Good.

Not because the fire was gone.

I did not know that.

Good because the present had acquired another fact.

I stopped long enough to look stupid.

The yard owner recognized me.

"Guild man. You people owe me eight feet of useful floor."

"City inspection."

"Same species."

"Different paperwork."

"Worse species."

A wagon behind me honked a brass road horn.

My escort lead shouted my name.

I moved.

The brick wall stayed where it was...
