# Chapter 220: The Clear Weed

The third reservoir had glass growing in the ditch.

That was my first interpretation.

Thin clear sheets clung to the shaded stones below the overflow, catching afternoon light in hard little planes.

I stopped.

Ollo did not.

"Glaze moss," he said.

"Moss?"

"If you want to argue with the botanist, she comes Tuesdays."

He hooked one clear sheet with a wooden fork and peeled it from the stone.

It flexed before cracking.

Faint charge crawled along the broken edge and vanished into the wet fork.

"Don't touch bare."

"I had reached that conclusion."

"Good Silver."

The overflow had been running harder since a spring repair changed the upper spill angle.

Glaze moss liked shallow charged runoff.

Too much built a slick shell over drainage cuts and sent water where the ditch had not agreed to send it.

Ollo and two laborers scraped it every ten days during wet months.

No Guild contract.

No monster bounty.

Ditch maintenance.

"Useful?" I asked.

Ollo shrugged.

"Potter in town buys dry sheets sometimes. Says they grind it into glaze. I say he can buy all of it if he scrapes it himself."

He pointed at the fork in my hand.

I had apparently been promoted into municipal botany.

We scraped until the lower cut ran clean.

Then Sava arrived and asked why her Silver support hand was knee-deep in moss.

"Professional development."

Ollo said, "He volunteered."

I had not.

History remained vulnerable to witnesses...
