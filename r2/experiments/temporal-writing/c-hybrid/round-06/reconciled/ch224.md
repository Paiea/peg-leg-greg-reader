# Chapter 224: The Return Cart

The timber arrived the morning we were leaving.

Of course it did.

Sava extended the district carpenter crew.

She did not extend me.

"You have enough hands now."

"I could stay."

"You could also go home."

This felt suspicious.

Ollo loaded my field bag onto the south cart.

The five-day contract had produced no dramatic repair, one scraped ditch, two angry farmers, and a new dislike of horses beneath bedrooms.

At the lower reservoir Berr Olan was waiting beside the road.

He handed Ollo a sack of peaches.

Then, after consideration, one peach to me.

"You still don't own the gate?"

"Tragically."

"Waste of silver."

He walked away.

Ollo inspected my peach.

"Bruised."

"Authority has consequences."

He laughed hard enough to drop his own.

The cart rolled south.

At the ridge turn I looked east for the road I remembered.

Still brush.

I wrote **ROAD? / NO CURRENT CART ACCESS** in the margin of my current map.

Not WATCH.

A question mark was cheaper...
