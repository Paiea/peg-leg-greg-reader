# Chapter 229: The Dry Return

Pera Sol accepted the bathhouse chamber on the third try.

The first refusal had produced the draining stand.

The second had produced an argument over what counted as dry.

The third produced a cloth bundle with no damp mark beneath it.

Pera put two fingers under the wrapping.

Then under the base.

Then looked at Dena.

"Dry."

Dena crossed her arms.

"You sound disappointed."

"I have clean linen behind me. I prefer boring."

Senn held out the six-line condition sheet.

Pera signed **CARRIER RECEIVED**.

Then added **DRY** beside it even though there was already a dry line.

"That is redundant," Senn said.

"That is distrust."

"We have a field for that."

"No, you have six fields because someone stopped you at fourteen."

Dena laughed hard enough to lean against the cart.

Pera loaded the chamber beside three linen bundles.

She charged the agreed rate.

Dena watched her tie the load.

"Still a tyrant."

Pera looked at the old crossed-out route sheet pinned in Senn's folder.

"Still readable."

Business resumed without forgiveness...
