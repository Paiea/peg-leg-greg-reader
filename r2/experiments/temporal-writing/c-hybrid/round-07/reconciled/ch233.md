# Chapter 233: The Rain Mark

Bessa sent the water office a note during heavy rain.

It said:

**YOUR WALL IS DOING THE THING MORE.**

Olin arrived with gauges.

Issen arrived with permission forms.

Renn arrived without being invited and called that professional instinct.

Bessa called it trespassing with stationery.

The old plugged fitting had a fresh mineral track below one mortar seam.

Not a drip.

A dark line.

The warm strip measured warmer than it had on the dry morning.

I held a small plane across the upper joint while runoff pushed through the lane drains.

The sideways load was stronger too.

Still faint.

Still not enough to move anything.

Olin wrote:

**CORRELATION WITH HEAVY RUNOFF. MECHANISM UNKNOWN.**

Renn looked over his shoulder.

"That sentence has no ambition."

"Good measurements rarely do."

Bessa pointed at the clock.

"How long?"

"Ten more minutes."

"Seven. Sheets come out at forty."

The city got seven minutes.

Bessa got her wall back.

The rain continued without explaining itself...
