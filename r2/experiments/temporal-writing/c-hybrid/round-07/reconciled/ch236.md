# Chapter 236: The Second Sheet

My current map stopped folding properly.

This was not symbolic.

I had taped too many small corrections across one crease.

North Ridge maintenance route.

No east cart road yet.

South Reach spill channel.

Mara's current packet town.

West spur reopened.

Two carrier transfer yards.

The five-ton bridge plate.

Three question marks.

One WATCH circle I had not touched in days.

I added a second sheet instead of another patch.

Noll found me trimming the edge at the dining table.

"Bigger map."

"Worse folding."

"Can you still find the Guild?"

"Usually."

"Then you're fine."

He moved his soup away from the glue.

I taped the sheets together.

The join covered half an old road label, so I rewrote it in the margin.

Noll put his thumb on the wet ink.

"You missed one."

"You made one."

He looked at the smudge.

"Current condition."

The map acquired a black fingerprint near East Market...
