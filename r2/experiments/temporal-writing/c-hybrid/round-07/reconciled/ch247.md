# Chapter 247: The Upstairs Bed

The inn had one upstairs bed left.

Pel took it.

Not because she outranked anyone.

Because the lockboxes did not sleep under leaking roofs.

Kess took the stable loft and claimed hay was good for her back.

I got the remaining cot beside the far wall.

At two in the morning a goat climbed the stairs.

Nobody knew why.

It ate part of Venn's glove before he caught it.

My left knee had stiffened from two days of wagon work and one village survey spent standing beside a manure shed.

Nothing injured.

Enough to make the stairs irritating.

Pel appeared in the doorway wearing a blanket over her shoulders.

"Is the goat near the boxes?"

"No."

"Good."

"Driver's glove."

"Replaceable."

Venn heard her from below.

"It was a good glove!"

Pel closed the door.

At breakfast she added **GOAT ACCESS TO LODGING** to her travel notes.

The innkeeper said the goat was not included in the room rate.

Pel said that made the sentence worse...
