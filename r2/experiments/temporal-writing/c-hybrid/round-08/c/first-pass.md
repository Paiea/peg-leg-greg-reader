No packing unless something was actually on fire.

He wanted the service fee back.

Senn corrected the winter carrier hours.

Dena said the refund should come out of paperwork.

Senn asked how to charge paper.

"Burn it for heat."

"We need records."

"Then it has failed twice."

# Chapter 280: The Green Cover

That disappointed Renn until Olin pointed out the old screws had mineral staining identical to the bakery housing.

Same system?

Maybe.

Same old maintenance culture?

More likely.

Vara would not let us remove it.

The city had not asked.

Issen took measurements.

Olin copied paint layers.

I held no magic because there was nothing to hold.

Renn called this the least magical magical investigation in Carrow.

He was very bad at pretending...
