# Chapter 254: The Reserved Morning

The city reserved me for the bakery opening before the city had permission to open the bakery.

This was apparently normal.

My allocation sheet said:

**EAST MILL SERVICE REVIEW - HOLD 8 TO NOON.**

The pay for held time was less than field work and more than standing in my room complaining.

At ten the property office still wanted the building owner's lease rider.

At eleven the structural office still wanted a current floor sketch.

At eleven forty-five Issen sent a runner to the Guild Annex.

**NO ORDER TODAY. RELEASE SUPPORT.**

Sella handed me a warehouse brace replacement that started at one.

"Lucky."

"Is that what this is?"

"You wanted more money."

"I wanted the city to know whether it needed me before breakfast."

"Different department."

The warehouse job was boring.

A loading loft had one cracked temporary brace and two perfectly good permanent posts.

The carpenter replaced the brace.

I held a small plane under the load while she reset the shoe.

Two hours.

Five copper more than the reserved morning would have paid.

Nobody discovered history.

On the walk home I bought bread from Mila's bakery.

She recognized me.

"Floor still closed."

"I came for bread."

She narrowed her eyes.

"Better profession."

I bought two loaves...
