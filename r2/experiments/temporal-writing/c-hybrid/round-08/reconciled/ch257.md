# Chapter 257: The Opening Order

The bakery order arrived four days later.

Two boards.

No excavation.

No cutting beyond loose fasteners.

No work during morning production.

Mila read every line.

Then added one of her own.

"You pay for the flour-room shutdown."

Issen had already brought the compensation form.

Mila looked disappointed that the city had learned.

We started at two.

The first board came up cleanly.

The second did not.

Somebody had driven an iron nail through the tongue during a repair and then cut the head flush.

The carpenter Mila hired found it before Renn did.

This improved the afternoon.

Under the boards sat packed dust, one dead beetle, and the upper edge of an iron housing crusted white with mineral scale.

Not a valve I recognized.

Not intact enough for anyone sensible to name.

Renn inhaled.

Issen pointed at him.

"No nouns."

"Housing."

"Allowed."

Olin cleaned one corner.

A stamped maintenance symbol appeared beneath the scale.

Same three-line mark as the old service sheet.

Nobody said anything for several seconds.

Mila broke first.

"Is silence billable?"

"For them," I said. "Probably."

She approved of this answer...
