# Chapter 273: The Market Bend

The road curved around a village that later memory had reduced to nothing.

Current village objected by existing.

Thirty houses.

Two mills.

A market square.

A cracked blue fountain.

Smoked pears.

Soli stopped for feed.

Tamar wanted ten minutes.

The horse wanted longer.

A wheelwright noticed one loose cart band while they argued and tightened it before Soli finished paying.

"Road bends for the market?" I asked.

Soli shook his head.

"Market came to road. Then road got busy. Then everybody complained about bend."

Tamar said the bypass had been studied twice.

Neither study had money.

We lost forty minutes.

We also did not lose a wheel later.

Fenn bought pears and declared the delay profitable.

Tamar made him carry the feed sack...
