# Chapter 280: The Two Copper

Our first refund was for a chamber that worked perfectly.

This offended Arlo at a structural level.

The bathhouse return pickup had missed Thursday because North Exchange changed winter transfer hours.

Then it missed Friday because our route note still had the old hours.

The used chamber sat in the bathhouse service room through the weekend.

Dry.

Intact.

In the way.

The manager wanted two copper back from the service fee.

"Ceramic did its job," Arlo said.

Ivena said, "He bought service too."

That was the end of the useful argument.

Senn had already corrected the winter hours before the manager arrived.

This did not make Thursday happen again.

We refunded two copper.

The manager signed the corrected route sheet.

Then renewed for another month.

Arlo watched him leave.

"So we failed and kept the customer."

Dena said, "Try not to make a religion out of it."

Senn filed the refund under service adjustments.

Not disasters.

Good category...
