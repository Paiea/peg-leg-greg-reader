# Chapter 282: The Warm Thread

Vara sent the note herself.

**GREEN COVER WARM AFTER RAIN. TWO CARDS CURLED.**

The cards mattered more to her than the cover.

That was why the note existed.

Olin arrived before Renn.

A victory for municipal logistics.

The green iron was warmer than the adjacent wall by enough to measure easily and not enough to burn anything.

Vara showed us two thread cards bowed away from the surface.

"Never did that before."

"Never?" Olin asked.

"Never that I noticed. Better sentence?"

"Much."

The city requested permission to remove only the cover.

Vara read the form.

"No wall opening."

"Correct."

"No cutting line."

"Correct."

"You put it back today."

"Yes."

She signed.

Behind the cover sat a shallow cavity and one wrapped line passing through old mortar.

The wrapping had hardened dark brown around the lower half.

No housing.

No valve.

No useful noun for Renn.

I held a narrow plane near the line while Olin checked the cavity.

Faint load.

Not stronger than the bakery.

Vara looked at the old wrapping.

"Can that stain wool?"

Nobody knew.

That became the most urgent question in the room...
