# Chapter 285: The Early Release

Pell Arin asked after the warehouse job.

I remembered her because she had been the Bronze on the upper brace while I worked the moving load below.

Nothing had gone wrong.

That was apparently the question.

"Why did you release before Harl called clear?"

"Load moved off my plane."

"I couldn't feel it."

"You were holding from the other side."

"So?"

I thought about how to answer without giving her forty years of bad habits as a shortcut.

"My plane stopped being part of the useful path. Holding it longer would only cost reserve."

She frowned.

"How do I feel that?"

"Practice with somebody who gets paid to teach it."

"Do you?"

"No."

"Could you?"

Probably.

Again, different question.

Sella was three desks away pretending not to hear.

I pointed at her.

"Ask the institution."

Pell did.

Sella looked at me with the expression of a person receiving free inventory.

I regretted several choices...
