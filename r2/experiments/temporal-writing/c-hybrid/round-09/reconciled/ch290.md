# Chapter 290: The No-Problem Job

The grain loft passed inspection.

Every brace.

Every anchor.

Every charged tie plate.

Nothing needed me.

The client had hired one Silver support hand because the insurance form required one during the seasonal load check.

I walked behind the carpenter for three hours.

She found no cracked shoe.

The rigger found no bad rope.

The building inspector found no settlement worth marking.

I used zero reserve.

At the end the client asked whether that meant the Guild charge should be lower.

"No," the inspector said before I could.

"He did nothing."

"Correctly."

I liked her immediately.

I signed the support line.

Full pay.

Nothing happened.

Seasonal maintenance had achieved its most offensive outcome...
