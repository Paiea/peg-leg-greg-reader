# Chapter 292: The Cold Steps

Cold made the Lower Rook stairs louder.

Not magically.

The wood shrank.

Every third tread complained.

I learned which ones before I learned I had learned them.

Noll came down behind me carrying a pot and stepped on all the loud ones.

"You do that deliberately?"

"Do what?"

"Make the building announce you."

"I live here."

Fair.

My knee felt fine.

My new boot laces stayed tied.

The room upstairs held two maps, three letters, one cleaned sword, and less empty shelf than it used to.

I moved nothing.

Outside, somebody argued with a cart wheel in the lane.

Lower Rook continued its own work...
