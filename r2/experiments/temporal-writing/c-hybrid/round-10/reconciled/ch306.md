# Chapter 306: The Bad Hold

My first useful mistake at the clinic was assigned.

Omi told me not to release.

"How long?"

"Until it becomes stupid."

"Technical unit?"

"Widely recognized."

The second frame held a smaller sack between two support points.

Pell took the opposite side.

Omi shifted the load fully onto Pell's sling.

My plane no longer carried anything useful.

I kept it anyway.

Three seconds.

Five.

Seven.

Reserve pulled down for no result.

The plane stayed clean.

That made the waste worse.

Omi tapped the frame.

"There. Stop."

I released.

The young man in back frowned.

"But it didn't fail."

"Correct," Omi said.

"Then why is it bad?"

Pell answered before I did.

"Because his side stopped mattering."

Omi pointed at her.

"Again."

This time Pell held too long.

Her plane wandered before her reserve did.

She swore quietly.

Omi approved of the honesty more than the language.

On the third run Pell released when the useful load left her side.

Nothing dramatic happened.

The sack stayed up.

She looked at me.

"That feels early."

"It is early if you're waiting for failure."

Omi cut in.

"And late if the load already left. Do not turn him into scripture."

I liked her more every minute.

At thirty-eight minutes she stopped the block exactly because I had spent two minutes answering a question about offset planes.

Promises were apparently enforceable...
