# Chapter 307: The Demonstration Rate

The Guild paid me two copper for the clinic.

This was less than field work.

It was more than talking for free.

I found the distinction persuasive.

Sella added one line beneath my work note.

**SUPPORT CLINIC / DEMONSTRATION HAND / AS SCHEDULED.**

"That sounds almost official."

"It is official."

"Title?"

"No."

"Rank?"

"No."

"Then why write it?"

"So Omi doesn't have to explain why she wants you next time."

There was the institution again.

Making memory out of paperwork.

"Do I have to go next time?"

"No."

"Better institution."

Omi passed the desk carrying two cracked buckles and heard the last word.

"Next month. Same Saturday."

"Maybe."

"That means no."

Pell came down the stairs behind her.

"I told you."

I had apparently helped teach institutional language after all.

Sella put two copper in my hand and sent me away before the clinic invented another role for me...
