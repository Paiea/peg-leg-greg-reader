# Chapter 308: The Named Request

A warehouse requested me by name.

This felt flattering until Sella read the problem.

Resin-chewer marks in treated hoist rope.

Of course.

The client had heard about the dry-goods loft and the later rope work through Esa Porr.

"Can you take it?" Sella asked.

I was already booked for a mill brace inspection that afternoon.

"No."

She moved one card on the board.

**NERIS HAL.**

Silver.

Wet-country rigging before I was born this time.

"Tell him bitterleaf oil works if the wax is already compromised."

Sella looked at me.

"He knows."

Right.

I went to the mill.

Neris went to the warehouse.

The city continued despite a scheduling catastrophe in which Greg could only be in one place.

Two days later I saw Neris carrying a sealed jar of bitterleaf through the Guild hall.

"How bad?"

"One coil. Four adjacent treated. Client tried to stand under the hoist while we unloaded."

"Learn anything?"

"He learned where I can shout from."

That sounded complete.

The warehouse had requested me.

The warehouse got its rope fixed anyway...
