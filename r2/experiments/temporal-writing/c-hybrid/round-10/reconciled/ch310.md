# Chapter 310: The Wrong Expert

My reputation produced the wrong request next.

A property manager wanted me for a retaining-wall assessment.

The note said:

**SILVER GREG / INFRASTRUCTURE EXPERIENCE.**

Sella read it.

Then crossed out my name.

"Structural Gold," she said.

"Yes."

"Then why did they ask for you?"

"They saw me at East Mill."

"East Mill is not a retaining wall."

"I remain aware."

She assigned Eda Vell, Gold structural assessor.

The client objected to the rate.

Sella explained that asking for a cheaper wrong person did not turn the wall into cheaper work.

The client asked whether I could at least come first and decide if Eda was necessary.

Sella said no.

I agreed with her so completely that it became irritating.

Eda passed us on the way out carrying a plumb line, a leather folder, and no interest in my feelings.

"Wall?" she asked Sella.

"South retaining."

"Old fill?"

"Probably."

"Good."

She left.

I took a warehouse support call instead.

Professional recognition had developed the ability to send me somewhere else.

Useful, if not heroic.
