# Chapter 312: The Tuesday No

Miko asked Tessa to do the oil count on Tuesday.

Tessa said no.

This surprised him enough that I heard about it at dinner.

"She said no?" Noll asked.

"Her mother needs her at the market stall."

"Did you ask before writing her name?"

Miko looked offended by the question because the answer was visible.

"She's done oil twice."

"That is not a schedule."

"I know that now."

Tessa had offered Thursday instead.

Thursday was also the day Miko wanted her on dry goods.

Delegation had apparently reached the advanced stage where other people possessed calendars.

Noll rubbed both hands over his face.

"What did you do?"

"Moved Jalen to dry goods Thursday. Tessa does oil. I do Tuesday count."

"Fine."

"You sound angry."

"I wanted Tuesday off."

Miko stared at him.

"You didn't tell me."

Noll stared back.

There was a long, beautiful silence in which management became hereditary.

I ate soup.
