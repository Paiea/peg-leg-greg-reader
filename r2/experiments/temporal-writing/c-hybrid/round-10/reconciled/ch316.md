# Chapter 316: The Second Pair

Two pairs of work gloves became necessary only because one pair was wet.

I learned this after the river job.

The old pair had dried stiff enough that the fingers felt personally offended.

I bought a second pair for three copper.

Noll found both on the shelf below my map.

"Two."

"Correct."

"Of the same thing."

"One can be wet."

He looked at me carefully.

"You're becoming rich."

"Prepared."

"Worse."

Old Greg had owned enough gear that gloves had not qualified as memory.

Current Greg rotated two pairs near the stove and considered the system excellent.

The second pair cost less than one sword lesson.

I began calculating that relationship.

Then stopped because some knowledge was actively harmful...
