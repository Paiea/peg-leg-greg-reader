# Chapter 318: The Empty Afternoon

I had nothing scheduled on Thursday afternoon.

This remained true after I checked twice.

No Guild call.

No East Mill access order.

No filtration meeting.

No sword lesson.

No letter requiring an answer before post.

Noll was working.

Jorren was somewhere on city roads.

Mara was on the river.

Arlo was firing chambers.

The world had distributed itself offensively well.

I walked to the market and bought apples.

One seller tried to charge me winter storage price for bruised fruit.

I told him the bruise was not a storage feature.

He told me to buy prettier apples somewhere else.

I bought the bruised ones for one copper less.

At home I cut away the bad spots.

Three apples became two and a half.

I read half a book.

Fell asleep in the chair.

Woke after sunset with the room cold and one sock missing because it had fallen behind the bed.

Nothing had advanced while I slept.

I found the sock anyway.
