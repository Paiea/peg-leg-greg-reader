# Chapter 319: The Stone Sample

By late winter, a piece of blue-gray stone appeared on the Guild desk.

Not rubble.

Sample.

A contract card sat beneath it.

**BLACKCUT QUARRY / WINTER LIFT SUPPORT / OLD CHARGED WEDGE INSPECTION / SIX DAYS.**

Sella moved the stone because it covered the rate.

"Quarry sends paperweights?"

"Quarry sends stone. Paper happened to be under it."

The rate was good.

Travel and lodging separate.

Silver support required because Blackcut still used several old charged load wedges in the upper cut.

"Engineer?" I asked.

"On site."

"Quarry foreman?"

"On site."

"Who owns load decisions?"

"They do. You own whatever plane they ask you to hold."

Promising.

I turned the sample over.

Fine grain.

Small silver flecks.

No charge.

Old Greg remembered Blackcut as a much deeper quarry with powered winches and three lower terraces.

Current contract said **UPPER CUT**.

That was all I needed to know.

I signed for six days.

The stone stayed on Sella's desk because she refused to let me steal the client's stationery...
