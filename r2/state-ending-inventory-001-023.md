## ch011.md

## ch012.md

## ch013.md

That mattered more tonight.

## ch014.md

## ch015.md

I ate one of the apples Mara's mother had forced into my pocket instead.

## ch016.md

## ch017.md

I set the letter on top of the old cards so I would have to move one to forget the other.

Then I took off my boot and discovered the porter had left a purple footprint across three of my toes.

That, at least, required no interpretation.

## ch018.md

The receipt in my coat was six weeks from becoming useful.

For the first time since waking nineteen, the correct next action might actually be waiting.

I hated it immediately.

## ch019.md

The smell followed.

History had remembered the clean water.

Somebody still had to carry the dirty part home.

## ch020.md

For the first time in either life that I could remember, somebody had paid me specifically because I could hold a barrier five inches wide.

Later history would have called that ridiculous.

Later history had not been there.

## ch021.md

The support receipt touched my knuckles on one side.

Mara's Halden envelope was folded in the other.

I left them there and went to breakfast.

## ch022.md

That memory, at least, had been accurate.

I lay awake for a while listening to him and smelling smoke in my hair.

Then I slept too.
