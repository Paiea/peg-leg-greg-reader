# PEG-LEG GREG — ADVENTURE ARC SCALE

Development guidance, not canon.

## Core rule

Let adventure length follow earned movement rather than a preset chapter count.

A major departure should be long enough to create:
- travel texture;
- new social nodes;
- a meaningful change in Greg's understanding or relationships;
- at least one genuinely larger-world encounter;
- consequences that justify leaving Carrow.

But do not prolong an adventure merely because it is the first large one.

## Useful arc scales

### Short excursion
Rough shape: a handful of chapters.

Best for:
- one outside job;
- one nearby regional problem;
- one trope used cleanly;
- testing travel texture without changing the book's center of gravity.

### Major adventure
Rough shape: enough chapters to establish a temporary life outside Carrow.

Best for:
- retrieval / escort arcs;
- multiple settlements or route stages;
- another character's emotional story developing over time;
- one primary trope plus a transformed mythic mechanism;
- real contrast between home and away.

### Long-range expedition
Use sparingly.

Requires:
- a strong reason Greg cannot casually return;
- multiple independently worthwhile phases;
- continued ordinary-life texture on the road;
- enough social density to prevent the trip from becoming scenery plus encounters;
- meaningful offscreen movement back in Carrow.

## Rhythm inside travel

Do not make every chapter advance danger.

Allow:
- meals;
- repairs;
- lodging;
- arguments;
- boredom;
- route decisions;
- shopping;
- local gossip;
- weather;
- injuries becoming inconvenient rather than dramatic;
- people becoming familiar.

Adventure should feel like another form of lived life, not a permanent climax state.

## Exit condition

End or turn the arc when its governing question changes.

Examples:
- retrieval target found;
- contract becomes morally or practically different;
- journey home becomes the real problem;
- outside conflict becomes Carrow-relevant;
- Greg's role in somebody else's story is no longer useful.

Do not keep extending because more cool encounters are available.
