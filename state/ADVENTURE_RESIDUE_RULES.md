# PEG-LEG GREG — ADVENTURE RESIDUE RULES

Development guidance, not canon.

## Core principle

Adventure should leave residue.

A major trip outside Carrow should not behave like an isolated episode that ends when Greg crosses the city gate.

Possible durable residue includes:
- changed relationships;
- new obligations;
- injuries or altered physical habits;
- money gained, spent, lost, or tied up;
- objects with provenance;
- favors owed;
- new contacts;
- distorted stories about Greg;
- local knowledge Greg now carries;
- changed assumptions about the world's scale;
- unresolved romantic / political / family consequences belonging to other people;
- routes that can be reused later;
- regional businesses, inns, ferries, farms, specialists, or institutions that become recurring nodes;
- somebody from the adventure eventually visiting Carrow;
- ordinary work complicated or improved by what Greg learned.

## No hard reset

On return, Carrow should also have changed.

Other people continue to:
- work;
- argue;
- date;
- earn;
- lose;
- rehearse;
- make decisions;
- solve problems without Greg;
- create new problems while Greg is gone.

Greg should occasionally return to discover that something he expected to handle has already been handled, badly handled, changed shape, or become irrelevant.

## Scale of residue

Not every adventure needs permanent trauma or geopolitical change.

Residue can be tiny and still matter:
- a food he now misses;
- a joke with a traveler;
- a supplier contact;
- a new scar;
- a tool purchased on the road;
- somebody writing him later;
- a local phrase he accidentally keeps using;
- a place he now knows well enough to recommend or avoid.

The serial's strength is accumulation. Let adventures contribute to that accumulation rather than temporarily suspending it.

## Production test

After planning a major adventure, identify at least three plausible categories of residue without locking exact outcomes in advance.

If nothing from the trip matters once Greg returns, the arc is probably too disposable.
