## Moderate-strength governing rule

Compress repeated dramatic functions, not quietness.

The target is not to make the first tour feel summarized. The target is to reduce how many examples are required to prove the same fact once the reader already believes it.

At MODERATE strength, preserve:

- first touring-life logistics;
- disability-specific access and travel consequences;
- Greg becoming a real paid theatre worker;
- distinct performer relationships and methods;
- Serra's independent private-life boundary;
- Marek/Teren/Coln old-company history;
- Greg's architecture/roof excursion and local reputation consequence;
- the first public rehearsal and both paid shows as separately legible events;
- money continuity;
- later callbacks and reputation evidence;
- all current illustration beats as protected anchors pending a later art-aware execution map.

Primary reduction targets:

- repeated prop/costume/backstage disorder after the pattern is established;
- repeated arrival, packing, eating, and room-assignment procedure that does not change state;
- repeated explanation that theatre adapts around mistakes;
- repeated examples of Greg chasing or resisting laughs after the lesson has landed;
- repeated body-status checks when no variable changes;
- repeated local-worker arguments that perform the same “messy but competent workplace” function;
- repeated transcription of performance material after the governing difference is already clear.
