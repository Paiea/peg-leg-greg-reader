# PEG-LEG GREG — CURRENT EDGE 352 ADVENTURE NOTES

Non-canon development note anchored to current `main` authority at Chapter 352 — **THE COUNT**.

## Why the adventure push is more ready now

The story has already done more outward work than the older Chapter-300 notes assumed.

Recent authority establishes:
- Bell Ford travel and return already proved Greg can leave Carrow briefly without breaking the serial;
- Greg has encountered outside routes, road work, transport, and regional infrastructure;
- artifact/provenance pressure is active without becoming a loot treadmill;
- Greg has 8 private copper after Ch352 and remains undercapitalized rather than desperate;
- South Quay gives Greg a new bounded access node through temporary counting work;
- Greg is increasingly legible as useful because he is careful, procedural, and does not invent answers;
- Carrow is no longer the only physical range the reader has experienced.

Therefore the next development question is not `how do we prove the world exists outside Carrow?`

It is:

**What independent external situation becomes attractive enough, strange enough, or convergent enough that Greg chooses to leave for longer than a brief road excursion?**

## 352-specific pressure

Do not turn South Quay temp-list access into a career ladder.

Instead, treat it as one more believable channel through which Greg may encounter:
- shipping origins;
- damaged or disputed cargo;
- regional makers;
- return destinations;
- sealed family/property disputes;
- escort needs;
- unusual provenance;
- someone who needs a reliable third party outside Carrow.

Greg's current value proposition is not `strongest man available`.

It is increasingly:

**competent, observant, physically formidable, procedurally reliable, willing to ask the ugly question, and hard to bullshit completely.**

That is an excellent profile for a job whose initial explanation is mostly ordinary but incomplete.

## Near-future bias

Favor pressure that makes a longer trip feel like the next expansion of an already-broadening life.

Avoid resetting to a generic guild board or pretending Greg has never traveled before.

The first major outward arc should feel larger than Bell Ford, not like Bell Ford never happened.
