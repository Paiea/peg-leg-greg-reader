# PEG-LEG GREG — DIALOGUE VARIANCE INVISIBILITY RULE

Durable craft guardrail. Not canon.

## Core rule

Dialogue variance must be audible in the dialogue. The narration should not explain that the dialogue variance system is working.

Do not write lines equivalent to:

- `Tavi did not have Greg-style dry answers.`
- `She spoke more formally because she was nervous.`
- `He was not keeping up with Greg's timing.`
- `This was her version of being terse.`
- `Different speech register. Good.`

unless Greg has a real in-story reason to consciously notice that exact social difference.

## What to do instead

Let the person talk.

If Tavi sprawls through causal logic, let the sentence sprawl.

If Saret is terse because he is work-focused, let him be terse.

If Jessa answers with a different kind of sarcasm than Greg, let the contrast stand without annotation.

If someone misses a joke, show the miss.

If someone overexplains, let Greg react to the content rather than mentally classifying the speech pattern.

## Greg can notice people

Greg is observant and may notice:

- someone is unusually quiet today;
- a person who usually talks around things gives a direct answer;
- a normally formal person swears;
- a joke lands late;
- somebody keeps speaking after he thought the exchange was over;
- a person is distracted enough to answer the wrong question.

That is social observation.

The failure is when narration reads like an editor labeling the designed voice distinction.
