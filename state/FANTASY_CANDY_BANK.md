# PEG-LEG GREG — FANTASY CANDY BANK

This is a non-canon reservoir of recognizable fantasy pleasures that PLG is allowed to use when they create momentum, contrast, fun, or useful structure.

Do not treat this as a queue. Do not consume multiple entries merely because they exist.

Possible candy:

- tournament / public contest;
- ruin or dungeon expedition;
- caravan escort;
- dangerous mountain pass;
- magical forest;
- monster hunt;
- bounty target;
- rival party;
- famous retired adventurer;
- cursed object;
- coveted relic;
- treasure map;
- hidden vault;
- noble masquerade;
- old family feud;
- missing heir;
- haunted estate;
- forbidden road;
- outlaw camp;
- mercenary company;
- border dispute;
- old battlefield;
- traveling fair with real magic mixed into performance;
- artifact auction;
- public duel;
- magical animal migration;
- dangerous river crossing;
- ancient bridge / gate / tower with a practical present-day use;
- remote inn where unrelated travelers are forced together;
- expedition with a specialist Greg dislikes but needs;
- monster whose reputation is worse than reality;
- monster whose reputation is not bad enough;
- map everyone thinks is fake;
- low-status prize Greg values more than the grand prize;
- ordinary contract that becomes extraordinary only after departure.

## Candy rule

Familiar pleasure is allowed to remain familiar.

Do not automatically subvert, invert, parody, deconstruct, or morally complicate every item.

The PLG identity should come from:
- who participates;
- why they care;
- what it costs;
- how people talk and work;
- what Greg knows;
- what persists afterward.

## Useful combinations

Prefer one clear familiar scaffold plus one unusual pressure rather than five tropes stacked together.

Examples:

- caravan escort + rare territorial entity;
- tournament + wrong-prize objective;
- ruin expedition + old love conflict among the expedition sponsors;
- monster hunt + Greg correctly knows the creature but the town's politics are wrong;
- masquerade + artifact provenance problem;
- missing-person retrieval + emotional triangle Greg is not part of;
- forbidden road + local rule whose original reason has been misunderstood.

The candy should help the story move. It should not become a checklist of fantasy credentials.
