# PEG-LEG GREG — FIRST ADVENTURE B/C CHEMISTRY

Non-canon development guidance for the first sustained outward adventure.

## Core rule

B and C should not fall into attraction because the plot says so. Their bond should grow through travel-shaped competence, inconvenience, shared observation, and small acts of protection.

Preferred progression:

**USEFUL STRANGERS -> FRICTION / DIFFERENT METHODS -> RESPECT -> PRIVATE EASE -> MUTUAL PROTECTION -> EMOTIONAL RISK -> RECOGNITION**

## Good chemistry material

- one notices how the other handles fatigue without making a performance of it;
- one quietly solves a practical problem the other was too proud to name;
- they disagree about route, money, obligation, or risk and later discover both were partly right;
- one defends the other's dignity in front of locals without turning it into rescue;
- private jokes emerge from repeated travel inconvenience;
- they learn each other's food, sleep, packing, weather, and silence habits;
- one becomes the person the other looks for first after a sudden problem;
- attraction appears first as changed attention, not confession.

## Greg's position

Greg may notice the pattern before they do, but he should not become narrator-therapist or romance coach.

His best role is practical awareness:
- he notices they choose each other in small ways;
- he realizes route decisions are becoming emotional decisions;
- he sees danger before either admits why it matters;
- he occasionally says less because the obvious conclusion is not his to announce.

## Avoid

- instant soulmate recognition;
- constant banter as the sole chemistry signal;
- B becoming competent only so C can admire them;
- C becoming vulnerable only so B can rescue them;
- Greg repeatedly commenting on the attraction;
- romance beats replacing road life.

The relationship should feel like something travel accidentally made possible rather than the declared purpose of the journey.
