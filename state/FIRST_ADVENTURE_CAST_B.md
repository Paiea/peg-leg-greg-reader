# PEG-LEG GREG — FIRST ADVENTURE CAST B

Non-canon candidate geometry for the first sustained outward adventure.

## Role

B is the person who travels toward C and gradually becomes the emotional center of the retrieval story.

B may be A's trusted representative, relative, employee, old friend, junior partner, messenger, specialist, or somebody with a separate reason to reach C.

Greg travels with B long enough for ordinary familiarity to accumulate before romance becomes obvious.

## Strong candidate shape

B should be competent in a lane Greg is not.

Possible lanes:
- regional etiquette / family politics;
- medicine / ritual / specialist care;
- maps / records / language;
- trade / contracts;
- magic specialty;
- animal handling;
- navigation through a specific region;
- social access Greg could not buy himself.

This prevents B from becoming cargo Greg protects.

## Why Greg may like B

Greg should have specific reasons to enjoy or respect B before he notices the romantic problem:
- B is useful without needing applause;
- B can disagree with him intelligently;
- B has messy speech or a distinct verbal rhythm rather than Greg-style clipped sarcasm;
- B treats travel like work instead of a heroic performance;
- B can be irritating in a way that becomes familiar;
- B notices practical things Greg misses because their domain differs.

## Relationship to A

B should owe A something real, but not necessarily obedience without limit.

Possible ties:
- sibling / cousin / household relation;
- long-term employee with genuine loyalty;
- former student;
- protected dependent who is now fully adult;
- friend who believes A is usually right;
- someone personally invested in preventing A from escalating the situation.

B initially believes the mission is solvable.

## Relationship to C

B may know C only by reputation, old correspondence, childhood memory, or A's account.

Travel / retrieval allows B to meet the actual person rather than A's version.

Attraction should emerge through repeated lived contact rather than destiny signals.

Useful progression:
**professional curiosity -> irritation / respect -> private ease -> protecting each other's dignity -> emotional problem.**

## Greg's position

Greg should notice the shift because he is older-minded, observant, and trapped near them for days.

He does not need to become their relationship counselor.

The stronger comedy / pressure comes from Greg repeatedly deciding that this is not his business while logistics keep making it his business.

## Failure mode to avoid

Do not make B instantly fascinated with C because the plot says romance.

Let B have an independent reason to be on the road and an independent future that existed before C.
