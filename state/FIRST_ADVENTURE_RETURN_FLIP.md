# PEG-LEG GREG — FIRST ADVENTURE RETURN FLIP

Non-canon development guidance.

## Core rule

The first adventure should pivot when the outward objective is substantially achieved but the meaning of completion changes.

Finding C is not the climax. The return becomes the real problem.

## Strong flip conditions

Use one or two, not all:
- C agrees to return, but only under terms A did not authorize;
- C refuses, and Greg must decide whether the contract ever entitled him to force the issue;
- B/C attachment becomes explicit enough that travel configuration itself becomes consequential;
- A sends new instructions that are reasonable on old information but dangerous on current information;
- a route closes or becomes unsafe, forcing a detour through the myth-scale local problem;
- C reveals a fact that changes what A's urgency meant;
- an outside party moves while Greg is away, making delay materially costly;
- the local entity imposes or exposes a condition that makes the original return plan impossible.

## Escalation ladder

**TARGET FOUND -> CONTRACT QUESTION CHANGES -> PEOPLE CHOOSE SIDES IN SMALL WAYS -> RETURN ROUTE TIGHTENS -> A'S PRESSURE BECOMES IMMEDIATE -> MYTHIC / REGIONAL PRESSURE INTERSECTS -> GREG MUST DEFINE HIS ACTUAL OBLIGATION**

## Greg's decision

The central decision should not be `who deserves the romance?`

Better questions:
- What did Greg actually sell when he took the contract?
- Does retrieval mean location, safe contact, voluntary escort, or physical delivery?
- What does he owe B after relying on B's competence?
- What does he owe C as an adult with agency?
- What does he owe A after accepting money and incomplete information?
- What risk may he impose on unrelated people to satisfy private obligations?

## Action permission

The return may contain straightforward danger. Bandits can attack. A creature can be hostile. A bridge can fail. Retainers can attempt to enforce instructions.

Do not make every action beat symbolic.

## End condition

The arc is ready to turn homeward when Greg has made one consequential choice that cannot be undone by simply refunding money.

The eventual return to Carrow should carry practical, relational, reputational, and world-scale residue rather than resolving all consequences before the gate.
