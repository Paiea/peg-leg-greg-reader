# PEG-LEG GREG — FIRST ADVENTURE ROUTE STAGE 1: DEPARTURE CORRIDOR

Non-canon route development for the first sustained outward adventure.

## Purpose

The first stage should make **leaving Carrow itself** feel consequential.

Do not rush to wilderness, danger, or mythic weirdness. The early route should still feel economically and socially connected to Carrow while making distance increasingly real.

Useful texture:
- freight roads and passenger arrangements;
- outer workshops, yards, market sheds, tolls, coach changes, roadside food, ordinary magical infrastructure;
- Greg noticing which Carrow habits stop applying first;
- B revealing practical travel competence Greg did not need to see in the city;
- Greg's body logistics changing as road surface, seating, weather, and bag weight matter over hours rather than blocks;
- ordinary people traveling for work, family, trade, illness, court business, seasonal labor, or nothing dramatic.

## Relationship function

This stage belongs primarily to **Greg + B**.

Before C appears:
- let B become legible as a person;
- establish one thing B does better than Greg in this region/domain;
- let Greg learn one annoying habit and one useful habit;
- allow practical shorthand to begin;
- do not seed romantic destiny toward C yet because C is not present.

## Fantasy rule

It is acceptable for this stretch to contain familiar fantasy-world scenery with no twist.

A road ward can simply be a road ward.
A coach inn can simply be a coach inn.
A pair of adventurers heading the other direction can simply be adventurers.

The expanded world does not need to announce significance constantly.

## Exit condition

Stage 1 ends when Carrow stops feeling immediately recoverable.

The reader should feel that turning around is still physically possible but would now be a real decision rather than walking home before supper.
