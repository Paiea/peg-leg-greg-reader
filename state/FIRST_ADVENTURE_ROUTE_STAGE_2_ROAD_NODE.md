# PEG-LEG GREG — FIRST ADVENTURE ROUTE STAGE 2: ROAD NODE

Non-canon route development.

## Purpose

Give the first outward adventure one early place where Greg and B must stay long enough for the world to become social rather than scenic.

Possible forms:
- road town;
- relay settlement;
- inn cluster around a crossing;
- market village;
- small regional center;
- estate-adjacent service town.

Do not lock a name yet.

## What this stage should accomplish

- Greg and B eat, sleep, wait, bargain, ask directions, hear local gossip, and deal with ordinary inconvenience.
- Introduce 2–4 local people with functions unrelated to Greg's destiny.
- Let regional differences appear through prices, speech, food, road assumptions, local magical practice, adventurer attitudes, and what locals consider normal.
- Give B at least one relationship or competence that exists independently of Greg.
- Let Greg be socially unknown, incorrectly known, or only partially recognized.

## Trope permission

This is a good place for a recognizable fantasy pleasure:
- crowded travelers' inn;
- rival party at another table;
- caravan gossip;
- bounty notice Greg does not take;
- local adventurer telling a story badly;
- a minor magical animal problem;
- someone selling a map that may actually be decent.

Do not make every visible trope a future plot thread.

## Emotional function

Stage 2 should make Greg and B feel like they have begun having a trip together, not merely progressing between plot markers.

One ordinary memory from here should plausibly survive the entire arc: a bad meal, a shared joke, a room arrangement, somebody snoring, a local drink, a petty argument, or a person they both remember later.

## Exit condition

Leave once the node has social weight but before it turns into a second Carrow.
