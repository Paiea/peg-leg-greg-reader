# PEG-LEG GREG — FIRST ADVENTURE START TIMING

Development guidance, not canon.

## Core rule

Do not launch the first major outward adventure because development files now exist.

Launch when the current manuscript has accumulated enough concrete outward pressure that another ordinary Carrow chapter would begin to feel like postponement rather than texture.

## Current-era judgment

By the Chapter 352 era, the world outside Carrow is already established through Bell Ford, road travel, South Quay, artifact provenance, transport, referral networks, and outside work.

The story no longer needs horizon proof.

The next useful threshold is **specific convergence**:
- one person wants something outside Carrow;
- Greg has a rational reason to care;
- a route or destination is specific;
- the offer contains one visible inconvenience or omission;
- leaving would cost Greg something at home;
- the manuscript has shown enough of the offer that departure would feel chosen rather than abrupt.

## Timing pressure

A handful of chapters of buildup is plenty if each chapter exists for its own reason.

Do not stretch setup for ten or twenty chapters merely because the first big adventure feels important.

Likewise, do not force the contract into Chapter 353 if current prose produces a better immediate claim.

Useful principle:

**SEED UNTIL THE NEXT SEED WOULD FEEL LIKE STALLING. THEN LET SOMEBODY MAKE AN OFFER.**

## Structural note

The first major departure may justify a new Act only if the manuscript's movement supports it.

Do not create an Act break because `adventure starts now` sounds neat. Let the actual shift in governing story question determine structure.
