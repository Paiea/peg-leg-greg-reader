# PEG-LEG GREG — FIRST ADVENTURE TRIANGLE GEOMETRY

Non-canon relationship-pressure guide for the first sustained outward adventure.

## Core requirement

The triangle should not be reducible to:
- A is jealous;
- B and C are true love;
- Greg knows the answer.

All three people should have legitimate wants that cannot all be satisfied cleanly.

## Preferred pressure shape

### A wants
- C back / available / accountable / safe;
- the original obligation completed;
- private resolution before outsiders make the situation worse;
- B to succeed without becoming part of the problem.

### B wants
- to fulfill the mission and preserve loyalty to A;
- to understand what actually happened;
- eventually, to treat C as a person rather than a task;
- to keep their own future rather than becoming absorbed into A/C history.

### C wants
- autonomy over return;
- recognition that circumstances changed;
- relief from an old obligation or a new bargain;
- eventually, B's trust / affection without using B merely as an escape route.

## Why Greg gets trapped

Greg's contract, sympathies, competence, and access pull in different directions.

Possible conflicts:
- contract says return C, but Greg did not agree to force an unwilling adult;
- A paid honestly for the job as she understood it;
- B is both coworker / traveling companion and emotionally compromised;
- C has information that changes the risk but withholds part of it too;
- Greg's practical choices determine who can travel, where they sleep, when messages move, or whether A gets warned.

Neutrality becomes consequential because logistics are choices.

## Adult ambiguity

Nobody needs to behave perfectly.

Allow:
- jealousy without villainy;
- attraction while obligations still exist;
- guilt;
- contradictory loyalties;
- people wanting incompatible things while still loving one another;
- avoidance;
- delayed honesty;
- anger that contains a valid point;
- a person making the emotionally wrong choice for materially sensible reasons.

Avoid modern therapy-language exposition in the manuscript. Let behavior, timing, work, silence, travel, and decisions carry the conflict.

## Greg's observational role

Greg may recognize the broad shape early, but he should not know the correct ending.

Useful internal posture:
- `That was attraction. Wonderful.`
- `Not my business.`
- later: `Unfortunately, transport arrangements were my business.`

His age / experience gives pattern recognition, not omniscience.

## Success condition

By the point the triangle becomes explicit, the reader should be able to answer:

**Why does each person think they are being reasonable?**

If only one side has a defensible answer, the geometry is too simple.
