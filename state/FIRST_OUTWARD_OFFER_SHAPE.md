# PEG-LEG GREG — FIRST OUTWARD OFFER SHAPE

Development guidance, not canon.

## Goal

The first serious outward offer should feel like work Greg could rationally accept before the story reveals why it matters.

Do not begin with prophecy, guild spectacle, a famous client announcing destiny, or a contract whose weirdness is already screaming `major arc`.

## Good initial job properties

- clear bounded task;
- destination outside Carrow for a real logistical reason;
- compensation materially meaningful to current Greg;
- enough uncertainty to create friction but not enough that accepting is obviously stupid;
- one or more independent stakeholders besides Greg;
- some practical reason the client wants Greg specifically or accepts the referral;
- job language that remains ordinary even if the deeper situation is not.

Possible surfaces:
- escort one person to meet / retrieve another;
- accompany a claimant, courier, family representative, clerk, or specialist;
- verify a person or object at destination;
- travel with a small party because they need one more competent adult;
- protect a route segment rather than an entire expedition;
- deliver something that cannot be entrusted to ordinary freight because custody / identity matters.

## Best concealment style

The client should omit or simplify something human before anything supernatural.

Examples:
- `family disagreement` hides a former lover;
- `return home` hides that the person left voluntarily;
- `private matter` hides inheritance / reputation / shame;
- `escort` hides that one traveler is emotionally central to the person being retrieved;
- `dangerous road` is true, but not the most important danger.

Greg notices the omission and prices it as ordinary social bullshit.

That is often the right call at first.

## Why this works for PLG

The adventure can grow from work logic the story has already earned.

Greg says yes because the visible job makes sense.

The larger story begins after departure when the visible job stops being the full job.
