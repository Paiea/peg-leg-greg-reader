# PEG-LEG GREG — FIRST OUTWARD REFERRAL CHAIN

Non-canon route sketch for how Greg plausibly gets considered for the first sustained outside contract after Chapter 352.

## Core idea

The first serious outward opportunity should preferably reach Greg through accumulated mundane competence rather than fame.

A strong chain is:

**current work node -> person who needs a narrow kind of reliability -> private recommendation -> Greg gets considered -> Greg asks questions -> only then does the larger story begin**

## Plausible referral sources

### South Quay / Merren-adjacent
Greg has just demonstrated:
- accurate reading;
- procedural restraint;
- mismatch escalation rather than invention;
- ability to sit through boring work correctly;
- enough physical self-management to function in a busy work environment.

A future outside contact might ask Merren or another quay worker for someone who can accompany papers, inventory, custody, or a representative without turning every uncertainty into a theory.

Do not make Merren a permanent patron. One sentence of recommendation can be enough.

### East gate / Saret-adjacent
Saret already supplied Greg's name once for a bounded task. A later referral can exist if a genuinely fitting request appears, but do not repeatedly use Saret as Greg's career engine.

### Vale-adjacent clerical reputation
Greg has a history of doing bounded comparison / records work. A private client may value someone who distinguishes observation from explanation.

Do not automatically reactivate Vale debt or make Vale mastermind the adventure.

### Theatre / road contacts
Someone may know Greg as reliable under travel, staging, custody, timing, or social ambiguity rather than as a combat legend.

Use only if current prose naturally reactivates the contact.

## Preferred shape

The referrer should describe Greg in narrow terms, not heroic ones.

Examples of reputation content:
- `He reads what is actually there.`
- `If he does not know, he says he does not know.`
- `He has traveled.`
- `He does not panic easily.`
- `He is annoying about terms.`
- `He can keep up with a bad road better than he looks like he should.`

The client may initially underestimate Greg's deeper history.

## Payoff

Later, when the route becomes genuinely dangerous, the client or companion can discover that the boringly reliable nineteen-year-old they hired has a much stranger competence ceiling than the referral implied.

This should feel like accumulated life creating leverage, not secret-protagonist destiny.
