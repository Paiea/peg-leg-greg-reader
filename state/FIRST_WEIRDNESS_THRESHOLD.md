# PEG-LEG GREG — FIRST WEIRDNESS THRESHOLD

Development guidance, not canon.

## Goal

The first major outward arc should widen PLG's fantasy ceiling without turning the setting into sudden myth soup.

The reader should feel the world becoming larger in category, not merely louder in spectacle.

## Escalation shape

Prefer:

1. ordinary contract;
2. ordinary travel;
3. one human/social inconsistency;
4. one local rule, rumor, ecological sign, or impossible material fact that Greg cannot dismiss cleanly;
5. delayed confirmation that something genuinely outside normal adventurer assumptions is present.

Do not begin by showing the rare entity at full scale.

## Enough weirdness

One major category violation can carry a large arc.

Examples:
- a human-presenting person whose physical nature is radically different;
- a territorial intelligence tied to one place;
- an old prohibition whose practical explanation is only half the truth;
- a person whose condition makes `retrieve them alive` an obsolete contract definition;
- a landscape consequence that is materially real but socially remembered as a love story;
- an old construction whose labor / magical history cannot be reconciled cleanly.

One of these is enough.

Secondary fantasy can remain familiar: road magic, ordinary creatures, ruins, wards, artifacts, weather, hostile monsters, or practical spellwork.

## Greg's calibration

Greg's first-life experience should help him eliminate ordinary explanations before he accepts an extraordinary one.

Good sequence:

- he recognizes what it is not;
- he proposes two or three plausible categories;
- local evidence refuses to fit them;
- he becomes cautious before becoming certain;
- certainty may arrive through behavior rather than exposition.

The story should never require Greg to forget decades of adventuring so a surprise can work.

## Reveal restraint

A rare being does not need:
- a species lecture;
- a rank;
- a complete origin;
- a fight;
- a divine label;
- a permanent place in every later arc.

The strongest result may simply be that Greg now knows this category of reality exists.

## Threshold test

Before escalating further, ask:

**Has the current weird thing already changed Greg's risk model, route choices, relationships, or contract interpretation?**

If yes, let it breathe before adding another revelation.
