## Bottom line

**Jori / Davin is a real recurring work relationship, but not an established friendship / rivalry / formal technical partnership.**

The pair is strongest when understood as:

**OVERLAPPING THEATRE LABOR + DIFFERENT DEFAULT DOMAINS + REPEATED HANDOFF / CORRECTION + SHARED OBJECT HISTORY**

Story Control's earlier `repair / hauling / credit / blame / practical geometry` shorthand is directionally useful but slightly too compressed.

The strongest durable correction is:
- **Davin** predates Jori in the recovered span as a major road / wagon / mechanical / repair authority.
- **Jori** enters later as an established caller / cue worker and gradually appears across lamps, set movement, transport, physical geometry, and repair/build work.
- They are not interchangeable.
- Their pair relationship becomes much more legible from the cart-pin sequence onward.
- Their dry comic friction is real but newer / narrower than the much longer work history.

## CH210 — CART PIN: STRONGEST RECOVERED PAIR SEQUENCE

This is where the relationship becomes clearly pair-shaped.

The cart wheel is off because the retaining pin is gone.

Established division:
- Jori physically supports / manipulates the cart and reports what he checked previously.
- Davin inspects the axle / hole, distinguishes missing from broken, and reaches the narrow conclusion that the pin was pulled rather than simply snapped in place.
- Jori is tired of holding the axle while Davin keeps inspecting: `Can we put this down?` / `No.`
- When Greg independently reaches `Pulled`, Jori says `Thank you`; Davin objects that Greg has merely confirmed what Davin has been saying.
- Davin fabricates the replacement pin and adds a second wedge because he dislikes the first fit.
- Jori rolls the cart at Davin's instruction while Davin crouches and watches the wheel: `Again.` / Jori rolls it back / `Good.`
- Jori checks the opposite pin as part of the same work.
- Jori later marks the replacement pin / axle with chalk so movement can be seen.

This supports several durable conclusions:

### ESTABLISHED — mutual operational trust
Jori is willing to move / test the cart according to Davin's repair judgment. Davin is willing to rely on Jori's manipulation / checking of the cart.

### ESTABLISHED — domain-specific authority is movable
Davin directs the mechanical test here. Jori does not need to be `the boss` because he is the person physically moving the cart.

### ESTABLISHED — dry task friction
They can irritate each other while continuing the job. The irritation is local to the work rather than evidence of rivalry.

### ESTABLISHED — shared object memory begins
The cart pin becomes an object both have specific knowledge about.

## HIGHEST LIFE-PER-WORD BEHAVIORS ALREADY EARNED

### 1. DOMAIN-SHAPED CORRECTION WITHOUT FIXED HIERARCHY
One can tell the other what matters when the problem is inside that person's stronger domain.

Cheap form:

### 2. REPAIR / OPERATION HANDOFF
One person's completed repair becomes another person's boring operating knowledge.

Cheap form:

Recent prose supports a small comic language around contribution, method, and material fact.

- Jori narrows it;
- neither stops working.
