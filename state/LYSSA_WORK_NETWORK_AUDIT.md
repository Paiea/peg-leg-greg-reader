# PEG-LEG GREG — LYSSA WORK-NETWORK AUDIT

Manuscript-first evidence map for Lyssa's working world. This file is **canon support, not canon authority**: exact manuscript prose outranks it.

Audit basis:
- exact recovered Chapters 156–219: `state/manuscript/Peg_Leg_Greg_Recovered_Ch156-219_EXACT.md`
- permanent running manuscript from Chapter 220 onward
- current manuscript state when this audit was completed, through Chapter 228

No external research was used for this audit.

## Core finding

Lyssa already has a substantially richer independent work world than compact state previously remembered.

The correct developmental move is **not** to invent a customer network from scratch. Preserve and selectively deepen what the manuscript already built:
- recurring work people;
- customer recognition;
- material/supply routes;
- messages and favors that move without Greg;
- offscreen deliveries and work decisions;
- multiple simultaneous garments / customers;
- work-specific shorthand Greg does not fully understand;
- economic exchange in cash, material, and occasional in-kind form.

Do not force all of this to recur. The value is that it already exists when a future scene needs it.

**Classification:** ESTABLISHED recurring work relationship and work location.

**Do not invent:** formal partnership, employment title, ownership structure, exact hierarchy, or modern business category.

## False-repeat / anti-conflation guardrails

Preserve these explicitly:

1. **Marra is not Maren.** Similar names, separate nodes.
2. Similar cloth colors do not establish customer identity.
3. Similar cuffs do not establish customer identity; Chapter 211 explicitly disproves this once.
4. Similar shoulder work does not establish one dress/customer; Chapter 219 explicitly says `Different dress. Different shoulder.`
5. The Chapter-217 referral is canon, but the referred shoulder-seam customer is not automatically every later altered dress / second fitting.
6. The Chapter-227 pale-coat customer is explicitly new; her referral source remains unknown.
7. Do not retroactively claim brown-cuff rework delayed the green garment unless prose states that causal link.
8. Pins/chalk/open seams are established physical work states, but not every such state means `waiting for customer confirmation` unless a scene establishes it.
