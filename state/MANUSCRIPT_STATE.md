### New market-information layer
- Ch483 separates at least five questions:
  1. what an object physically is;
  2. what it actually does;
  3. who owns it / whether official title was released;
  4. what its story/provenance says;
  5. what the market will pay.
- Official service/calibration marks are not automatically current certification.
- Legal private possession does not automatically prove district title release.
- Clean release records can expand the buyer pool and reduce resale uncertainty without changing the object's mechanical or magical capability.
