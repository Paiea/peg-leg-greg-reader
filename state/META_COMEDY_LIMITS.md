# META COMEDY LIMITS

Purpose: preserve Greg's self-aware humor without letting production language, editorial rules, or chapter mechanics become recurring jokes.

## Greg may be self-aware

Greg can think in terms of stories, bad timing, suspicious convenience, dramatic irony, terrible planning, or the general absurdity of his life.

He should not sound like he can see the manuscript engine.

## Avoid recurring jokes built from backend vocabulary

Flag or limit lines about:
- plot relevance;
- chapters or chapter endings;
- narrative significance;
- progression as a genre mechanic;
- character development described as `growth`;
- tropes being obeyed or subverted;
- the story refusing a conspiracy;
- side characters proving they have lives;
- dialogue voices being distinct;
- scene grammar;
- roles existing because of title architecture.

A rare fourth-wall-adjacent joke can be funny. Repetition turns it into author leakage.

## Better Greg comedy

Keep humor attached to:
- immediate inconvenience;
- money;
- objects behaving badly;
- social awkwardness;
- bodily logistics;
- other people's phrasing;
- his own bad decisions;
- professional habits crossing into ordinary life;
- first-life associations;
- literal interpretation;
- irritation at systems.

## Test

Ask whether the joke would still make sense to a reader who had never seen the backend rules.

If the joke's real target is an editorial instruction, cut it.
