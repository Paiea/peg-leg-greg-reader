# PEG-LEG GREG — MOʻOLELO SEED BANK

Non-canon development quarry. Source labels exist only so future workers can recover the inspiration. PLG prose should normally contain no Hawaiian names, deity names, cultural vocabulary, or recognizable place references from these sources.

Use `MYTH_TRANSLATION_ENGINE.md` before turning any seed into story material.

## Seed 01 — Retrieval journey that becomes the real relationship

**Source geology:** Hiʻiaka is sent on a difficult journey to retrieve Lohiʻau for Pele. The journey accumulates danger, loyalty tests, revival, old lovers, attraction, jealousy, and catastrophic consequences when the person who sent the mission interprets delay and intimacy as betrayal.

**Deep mechanism:** A powerful person sends a trusted second person to retrieve someone they desire. Travel creates intimacy between courier and target. The original claimant becomes increasingly dangerous as distance, delay, uncertainty, and jealousy compound.

**PLG mutation directions:**
- Greg is neither claimant nor love interest.
- He is escort, hired muscle, route expert, witness, mediator, or accidental confidant.
- He recognizes the attraction before the two people inside it admit it.
- He may like all three people, making neutrality impossible.
- The original client need not be divine. She may be a dangerous mage, regional power, wealthy patron, old adventurer, rare shifter, noble-adjacent figure, or something whose category remains unclear.
- The return journey can become more dangerous than the retrieval.
- The client’s emotional reaction may affect roads, commerce, estates, retainers, weather-like magic, or local safety without requiring world-ending scale.

**Best PLG question:** What does Greg owe when fulfilling the contract exactly may create the worst human outcome?

## Seed 02 — Powerful stranger, ordinary hospitality

**Source geology:** Pele traditions include appearances in human form, including stories where people do not know the significance of the stranger in front of them. Hospitality, insult, generosity, recognition, and later consequence can matter enormously.

**Deep mechanism:** An apparently ordinary traveler is socially mispriced. Small acts of kindness, disrespect, greed, or refusal create consequences far larger than the participants understood.

**PLG mutation directions:**
- Avoid a simple morality test where good people get treasure and bad people explode.
- The stranger may not be testing anyone at all.
- Greg may help because it is practical, cheap, or habitual rather than saintly.
- Someone else may treat the stranger badly for understandable reasons.
- The later consequence may be social, economic, ecological, or magical.
- Greg can realize afterward that he had been speaking casually to something extremely dangerous.
- The entity may remain unnamed and unclassified.

**Expansion layer:** If stories of such encounters exist locally, merchants may exploit them, frauds may impersonate strangers, households may develop customs around travelers, and locals may disagree over whether any of it is real.

## Seed 03 — Human face, impossible second body

**Source geology:** Kamapuaʻa traditions include movement between attractive human and enormous hog forms, conflict with Pele, elemental opposition, insult, desire, and transformation.

**Deep mechanism:** A socially legible person also possesses a radically different physical nature. Both forms are real. Transformation changes the scale of a conflict instantly.

**PLG mutation directions:**
- True transformations of this magnitude are rare.
- Do not create a routine race of giant animal-people.
- The human-form person may have a job, reputation, neighbors, debts, lovers, and ordinary habits.
- Greg may know that large transformations exist without having personally seen this category.
- Reveal through inconsistency first, transformation much later.
- The being does not need to attack Greg.
- A terrifying power can be neutral, embarrassed, romantic, petty, protective, territorial, or simply busy.

**Best PLG beat:** Someone asks Greg what rank it is. Greg answers that the question does not apply.

## Seed 04 — Water guardian / hidden predator / local woman who is more than local

**Source geology:** Moʻo traditions include powerful lizard-associated beings, often female, tied to water and specific places; Hiʻiaka traditions include battles with moʻo during travel.

**Deep mechanism:** A place has a human-social layer and a second older ecology. A person who appears embedded in ordinary local life may also be tied to the dangerous geography itself.

**PLG mutation directions:**
- No generic “lizard woman race.”
- One individual may be enough.
- Keep personhood primary if the being is intelligent.
- Locals may know more than outsiders but use euphemism rather than exposition.
- Greg may suspect the truth from field signs without being certain.
- Water, crossings, irrigation, fishing, mills, ferries, or property boundaries can make the being economically relevant.
- The conflict may be caused by humans trying to alter the environment rather than by a monster hunting people.

**Best PLG feeling:** Greg realizes the woman arguing over fish prices could probably kill everyone on the street and has chosen not to for decades.

## Seed 05 — Hidden builders and impossible labor

**Source geology:** Menehune traditions include extraordinary construction and a long history of interpretation around who performed old works and how later communities remembered them.

**Deep mechanism:** Major infrastructure exists whose labor history is disputed, mythologized, or impossible by ordinary assumptions.

**PLG mutation directions:**
- Do not create tiny Hawaiian-coded builders.
- Turn the seed into disputed magical labor, vanished workers, hidden contracts, nocturnal construction, or a class of builders whose methods were later misunderstood.
- Greg encounters a bridge, wall, road, terrace, canal, or structure whose economics make no sense.
- Scholars, workers, locals, and owners disagree about the explanation.
- Current people may falsely romanticize old exploitation or erase ordinary workers by attributing everything to magic.
- A modern contractor may profit from the legend.
- The mystery can remain partly historical rather than becoming a creature encounter.

**Expansion layer:** labor law, guild resentment, pilgrimage, tourism, counterfeit “old work,” property disputes, maintenance problems, and magical salvage.

## Seed 06 — Jealous transformation creates landscape memory

**Source geology:** Stories associated with Pele include lovers transformed and landscapes or plants carrying relationship memory.

**Deep mechanism:** Intimate conflict leaves durable physical evidence in the world. Landscape becomes social memory rather than neutral backdrop.

**PLG mutation directions:**
- Avoid literal lovers-turned-tree unless heavily transformed.
- A magical conflict generations ago may have permanently altered a road, grove, quarry, spring, ruin, weather pocket, or mineral deposit.
- Modern people disagree about whether the romance story is history, metaphor, propaganda, or nonsense.
- Greg may care because the altered place affects trade or contains a useful material.
- The current adventure may deal with consequences rather than reenacting the original event.

**Best PLG use:** Greg discovers that a local “love legend” is materially relevant to a contract he accepted for completely practical reasons.

## Seed 07 — Prohibition whose explanation has decayed

**Source geology:** Hawaiian narrative traditions frequently carry restrictions, obligations, protected spaces, and consequences whose force is social, political, spiritual, environmental, or some combination.

**Deep mechanism:** A community obeys a rule that outsiders find irrational. Nobody agrees anymore on why the rule exists.

**PLG mutation directions:**
- Never lift a specific sacred restriction one-to-one.
- Build a new PLG-native local rule around a new phenomenon.
- Some explanations can be superstition, some practical, some historically distorted.
- Children may dare each other to violate it.
- Merchants may route around it.
- Greg may initially think he has identified the practical reason and be only partly right.
- Breaking the rule need not trigger instant magical punishment.

**Best PLG use:** The rule becomes inconvenient to the job, forcing Greg to choose whether saving time is worth violating a custom he does not fully understand.

## Seed 08 — Rival powers whose private conflict becomes public geography

**Source geology:** Pele and Kamapuaʻa conflict combines desire, insult, transformation, fire, water, terrain, and powers that neither side easily overwhelms.

**Deep mechanism:** Two dangerous people have a deeply personal conflict, but their methods operate at a scale that affects everyone nearby.

**PLG mutation directions:**
- Neither side must be evil.
- Greg does not need to defeat either.
- Their feud can interrupt roads, markets, farmland, river crossings, estates, or migration.
- Their public reputations may radically simplify a more complicated personal history.
- Greg may be hired to solve a collateral problem while everyone important is focused on the feud itself.

**Best PLG question:** How do ordinary people continue living when two beings capable of wrecking a region are emotionally incapable of leaving each other alone?

## Seed 09 — The dead person who is not simply dead

**Source geology:** In Hiʻiaka’s retrieval story, Lohiʻau is found dead and is restored before the return journey continues.

**Deep mechanism:** The person being retrieved has crossed a boundary that makes the original mission definition obsolete.

**PLG mutation directions:**
- Avoid easy resurrection as a routine spell.
- “Dead” may mean suspended, displaced, trapped, transformed, magically absent, preserved, or legally dead while physically recoverable.
- Greg’s job contract may become ambiguous: retrieve the person, retrieve the body, retrieve proof, retrieve an object carrying identity, or decide whether recovery is humane.
- The recovered person may not want to return.

**Best PLG use:** The supposedly straightforward retrieval job becomes ethically different before the romance complication even begins.

## Seed 10 — Old lover / social contest during the return

**Source geology:** On Hiʻiaka’s return with Lohiʻau, an earlier lover reenters the journey and romantic/social competition complicates an already dangerous mission.

**Deep mechanism:** A journey that already contains forbidden or unstable intimacy encounters somebody with legitimate prior claim, history, or affection.

**PLG mutation directions:**
- Make the interruption socially rich rather than melodramatic.
- The old lover may be likable.
- Greg can accidentally become adjudicator because he is the only person perceived as neutral.
- A game, wager, performance, contract, local custom, or public event can become the mechanism through which private tension surfaces.
- The interruption can delay the party long enough to worsen pressure from whoever sent them.

**Best PLG use:** Greg keeps trying to finish a paid job while everyone around him converts travel time into romantic catastrophe.

## Source provenance / research caution

Seed geology above was synthesized from public descriptions and historical/source projects including National Park Service material on Pele, Hiʻiaka, and Kamapuaʻa; University of Hawaiʻi Kahoʻiwai resources on Hiʻiaka; the UH Institute for Hawaiian Language Research and Translation historical newspaper database; and Manoa Heritage Center material discussing Menehune traditions and later interpretations.

Do not treat any compact seed summary as a complete or authoritative account of a living cultural tradition. If a future storyline needs closer fidelity to a particular source, research that source separately before development.

## Usage priority

For the next outward expansion, prioritize seeds that generate movement and relationship pressure rather than cosmology.

Strong first-wave candidates:

1. retrieval journey / unstable love triangle;
2. suspiciously lucrative escort or retrieval contract;
3. rare water-associated shifter encountered along the route;
4. disputed local prohibition;
5. one impossible-history / hidden-labor location as world depth.

Do not use all five in one arc unless the manuscript naturally earns them. One primary skeleton plus one or two secondary textures is usually stronger.
