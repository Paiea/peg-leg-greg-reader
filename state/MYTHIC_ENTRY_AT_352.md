# PEG-LEG GREG — MYTHIC ENTRY AT CH352 ERA

Non-canon guidance for introducing the stranger moʻolelo-derived layer without jumping straight to gods or cosmology.

## Current scale rule

At this stage, prefer **mythic consequences before mythic taxonomy**.

The story does not need to establish a pantheon, divine hierarchy, or theological system merely because source inspirations contain gods walking among people.

PLG can instead use:
- rare beings with unclear categories;
- people whose power is socially embedded rather than publicly ranked;
- old local rules whose original explanation is disputed;
- shape-changing entities that are exceptional enough to remain shocking;
- relationships whose private conflict produces public material consequences;
- landscapes or infrastructure carrying residue from older extraordinary events.

## Best first-entry behavior

A strong first major mythic-scale encounter should not begin with a lore reveal.

Prefer:
1. Greg has a normal reason to be there.
2. locals know one practical rule.
3. Greg thinks he understands most of the risk.
4. something small does not fit his model.
5. another person reacts as if the mismatch is ordinary or socially dangerous.
6. Greg realizes the category is larger than expected.
7. no immediate exposition fully resolves what the being / event is.

## Rare shifters

Keep dramatic full-body shapeshifting rare.

Weak glamours, bounded changes, disguises, or minor inherited traits may be more common if current worldbuilding supports them.

But a person capable of becoming something enormous should feel like encountering a once-in-a-career fact, not a routine fantasy species.

## Power without rank

Do not force adventurer letters onto ancient beings, environmental entities, rare shifters, or unknown categories.

Greg can still make useful practical judgments:
- `I can survive being near this.`
- `I do not know if I can beat this.`
- `It is not hunting us.`
- `Do not make it decide we are a problem.`

That preserves S-class credibility while leaving room above and beside the adventurer system.

## Desired reader effect

The first larger encounter should create:

**Oh. The world is much stranger than Carrow made it look.**

Not:

**Here is the new cosmology chapter.**
