# PEG-LEG GREG — OUTWARD REFERRAL PATHS

Development guidance, not canon.

## Current edge context

At Chapter 352 Greg has demonstrated a useful reputation pattern: he reads what is actually in front of him, follows bounded procedure, does not invent explanations, and can be referred into temporary work without being absorbed into a career lane.

This creates a strong outward-adventure doorway that does not require anyone to seek out `Peg-Leg Greg the legendary adventurer`.

## Plausible referral logic

Future outside work may reach Greg through people who value one or more of these traits:

- quiet reliability;
- physical competence without swagger;
- discretion;
- willingness to travel;
- ability to notice inconsistencies;
- ability to work inside somebody else's process;
- experience with roads / field conditions;
- practical judgment around magical objects or ecology;
- reputation for not making up causes when evidence is incomplete.

The referrer does not need to know Greg's first-life identity.

## Strong referral shapes

- South Quay contact mentions somebody who needs a temporary traveling third hand.
- Saret or another route worker knows a job that requires somebody who can read tags / road conditions and not freelance the paperwork.
- Vale or a Vale-adjacent contact has work outside Carrow where Greg's existing trust is relevant but does not guarantee the hidden layers.
- theatre or garment-work network indirectly knows a household / patron / customer with an outside problem.
- artifact dealer / repair worker / transporter knows Greg as someone who asks useful questions about objects and provenance.
- a person Greg met on the road remembers him later and routes a request back to Carrow.

## Important restraint

Do not make all Greg's networks collapse into one referral super-network.

The value is that different people know different Gregs.

A person may refer him because he is `the guy who reads tags correctly` while having no idea he once reached S-class.

Another may know he can survive rough travel but not trust him with bookkeeping.

Another may know him socially and only vaguely know he is capable.

## Best adventure-door effect

A strong first major outward offer may arrive through an ordinary referral chain where the client's actual criteria are practical and narrow.

The reader knows Greg's deeper capability. The client may not.

This preserves scale, avoids protagonist gravity, and gives later reveals more room.
