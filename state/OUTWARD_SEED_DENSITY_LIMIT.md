# PEG-LEG GREG — OUTWARD SEED DENSITY LIMIT

Development guidance, not canon.

## Problem this file prevents

Once an outward-adventure direction exists, generation may begin stuffing every chapter with foreshadowing.

Do not.

PLG should not suddenly sound like the universe is tapping Greg on the shoulder every six pages.

## Density rule

Prefer one meaningful outward-facing fact inside a chapter that already has another reason to exist.

Several consecutive chapters may contain no adventure seed at all.

Avoid stacking:
- suspicious stranger;
- distant map;
- strange artifact;
- missing person;
- road closure;
- ominous rumor;
- secret letter;
- huge offer;

into the same short span merely to prove momentum.

## Signal strength

A seed should usually do one job:
- widen geography;
- establish referral plausibility;
- clarify incentive;
- create relationship ambiguity;
- establish route cost;
- show home opportunity cost.

If one beat is already doing two or three of these naturally, let it carry the load.

## Reader experience target

The reader should notice in retrospect that several ordinary things began pointing in the same direction.

They should not feel the author assembling glowing quest arrows.

Useful phrase:

**CONVERGENCE, NOT FORESHADOWING SPAM.**
