# PEG-LEG GREG — POST-352 OUTWARD ROUTE

Non-canon route pressure for Writers' Room / Story Control.

## Current preferred movement

The first major departure should now be treated as a **near-future convergence target**, not a distant someday idea.

That does not mean Chapter 353 must launch it.

A useful progression from current authority is:

1. **outside-facing fact** enters through normal work / social life;
2. **person with independent stakes** becomes legible;
3. Greg has a reason to care that is not solely altruism;
4. he notices the offer / story is incomplete;
5. another incentive points down the same route;
6. Greg makes an actual decision to go;
7. preparation and household consequences become visible;
8. departure lasts long enough to create a temporary life outside Carrow.

## Strongest current candidate

The retrieval / somebody-else's-love-story structure remains the strongest first major arc candidate because it can enter through ordinary paid work while opening into larger emotional and mythic-scale consequences.

Current preferred mutation:
- Greg is not the romantic center;
- one participant already has reason to travel before Greg becomes relevant;
- the original client is powerful but not automatically divine;
- Greg accepts because the economics / access / route / curiosity make sense;
- he realizes partway through that the real conflict is relational and much older than the contract;
- fulfilling the literal job may become the dangerous choice.

## Secondary pressure

Pair the primary retrieval structure with at most one strong secondary layer:
- dangerous magical ecology;
- rare shifter / guardian;
- old local prohibition;
- disputed historical infrastructure;
- artifact/provenance reason Greg independently cares about.

Do not stack all of them.

## Why now

By Chapter 352, PLG has already established enough normality, money pressure, procedural competence, local relationships, artifact interest, and brief travel that a longer departure would feel like expansion rather than genre replacement.
