The blue hour and Hanging Road demonstrate the preferred scale expansion:

- strange fantasy behavior;
- local procedures or expertise already exist;
- Greg is competent but not central authority;
- ordinary consequences remain;
- the world feels older than his involvement.

Repeat the principle, not the scene shape.
