# PEG-LEG GREG — RESEARCH LEDGER

Durable research memory for story development.

This file stores reusable external knowledge that can improve character, world, theatre, craft, disability, economy, magic-methodology, travel, institutions, and plot texture.

Research is **not canon**. Manuscript prose and explicit author decisions outrank it.

## Entry format

### TOPIC — short descriptive title

**Question:**
What story/craft question was being investigated?

**Source / authority:**
- title / organization / expert
- URL
- publication/date when useful

**Supported finding:**
A compact paraphrase of what the source actually supports.

**Confidence / limits:**
Primary / expert / secondary / anecdotal, plus important uncertainty.

**Story translation:**
How this might improve behavior, work, social hierarchy, objects, routes, mistakes, dialogue, staging, physicality, or plot pressure in Peg-Leg Greg.

**Canon status:**
POSSIBILITY unless separately established.

---

## Research principles

- Research real practice when it can replace generic invented texture.
- Prefer primary, institutional, expert, or strong craft sources where possible.
- Community anecdotes are useful for lived texture but must be labeled anecdotal.
- Do not paste long source text. Distill the useful principle and preserve the source link.
- Do not turn historical practice into mandatory fantasy-world history.
- Translate knowledge into behavior and constraints rather than exposition dumps.
- Keep contradictory evidence when it matters.
- If a fact changes with time, record the date checked.

## High-value research queues

These are standing categories, not a checklist for every session.

### Theatre / acting / stagecraft
- rehearsal note culture
- blocking and traffic
- repertory / ensemble dynamics
- understudy / replacement practices
- prop tracking and handoffs
- costume quick changes and repair
- backstage authority / stage management analogues
- practical illusion and readable stage effects
- sightlines and audience readability
- actor timing, receiving scene partners, recovery from mistakes

### Social / workplace texture
- informal roles in small companies
- shared housing / touring-company friction
- jokes, reputations, rituals, favors, borrowing, covering shifts
- how long-running teams develop shorthand and unofficial authority

### Greg / mobility
- two-crutch gait and load
- shoulder / wrist / hand fatigue
- wet surfaces, stairs, carrying, doors, crowds
- below-knee residual-limb and phantom-sensation realities where relevant
- adaptation by coworkers and household members over time

### Lyssa / garment work
- alteration workflow
- fitting and refitting
- customer change requests
- pricing changes / rework
- sewing labor, materials, delivery, repair, customer relationships

### Magic / experimental method
- controls
- predeclared trial order
- repeated-measures thinking
- contamination / confounds
- stopping rules
- interpreting weak or ambiguous effects without overstating claims

### City / economy / logistics
- small-business cash handling
- suppliers and informal warning networks
- cart repair and maintenance
- market / lodging / freight / road practices
- debt pressure and ordinary household economics
