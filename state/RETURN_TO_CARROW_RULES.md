# PEG-LEG GREG — RETURN TO CARROW RULES

Development guidance, not canon.

## Core principle

Returning home is not an epilogue. It is part of the adventure payoff.

Carrow is the accumulated baseline that makes leaving meaningful. The return should therefore reveal both:
- how Greg changed while away;
- how Carrow changed without him.

## Avoid the hard reset

Do not resume the exact pre-departure status quo as though no time passed.

Useful return beats include:
- people having solved things without Greg;
- Greg discovering he was missed by some people and barely noticed by others;
- work piled up unevenly rather than theatrically;
- relationships needing re-entry rather than instant continuity;
- somebody being annoyed he left;
- somebody being glad he left because they gained room to act;
- a joke or routine feeling slightly different after absence;
- new outside contacts reaching into Carrow later;
- Greg realizing a familiar place now feels more like home because he left it.

## Changed-normal rhythm

Preferred broad movement:

**RETURN -> REENTRY FRICTION -> ORDINARY TASKS -> RESIDUE SURFACES -> NEW NORMAL**

Do not immediately launch the next major adventure unless the previous one directly causes it.

Give the serial enough ordinary chapters for:
- sleep;
- food;
- sex / intimacy where natural;
- theatre;
- money;
- errands;
- repairs;
- retelling the trip badly;
- people asking different questions about what happened;
- Greg withholding some details because explaining them is exhausting;
- outside-world consequences arriving later rather than all at once.

## Reputation after travel

Stories about the adventure may spread independently of Greg.

Prefer:
- partial retellings;
- wrong emphasis;
- heroic exaggeration;
- embarrassing omissions;
- someone else receiving credit;
- Greg becoming known for the least important part;
- accurate accounts that Greg wishes were less accurate.

This preserves networked reputation rather than converting one adventure into universal celebrity.

## Home as contrast, not confinement

Returning to Carrow should not imply the world outside was a temporary gimmick.

The trip should enlarge the map permanently. Future travel becomes easier to imagine because:
- routes are known;
- contacts exist;
- Greg has evidence that distant pressures can reach home;
- people elsewhere now know him;
- ordinary Carrow life has new comparison points.

The goal is for PLG to become a story with a home, not a story trapped in one city and not a serial that abandons home once adventure begins.
