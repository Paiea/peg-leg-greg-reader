# ROLE TITLE RETROFIT — CHAPTERS 351-360

Approved editorial correction: chapter titles in this stretch drifted from the series' identity-card convention into object/event/topic labels. The intended convention is **THE + ROLE / IDENTITY GREG EMBODIES IN THE CHAPTER**, not a synopsis noun.

This file is the durable title authority for the retrofit until exact checkpoint headings and downstream reader/index surfaces are synchronized. **Prose is unchanged. Chapter numbers are unchanged.**

## Forward title rule

From Chapter 361 onward, default chapter titles to **THE + ROLE / IDENTITY / SOCIAL FUNCTION GREG EMBODIES**.

Good title question: **Who is Greg being here?**

Bad title question: **What object/event/problem is this chapter about?**

Titles can be ironic, temporary, aspirational, humiliating, professional, social, relational, or situational. They do not need to be permanent occupations. They should feel like another identity card Greg wears for that chapter.

Examples already aligned with the intended grammar include **THE WARRIOR**, **THE STAGEHAND**, and **THE APPRENTICE**.

Avoid defaulting to object labels (`THE LANTERN`), event labels (`THE BLUE HOUR`), location labels (`THE HANGING ROAD`), or abstract plot labels (`THE REFUSAL`) when a clean embodied role exists.
