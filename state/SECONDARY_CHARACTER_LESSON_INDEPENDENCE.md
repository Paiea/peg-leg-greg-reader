# SECONDARY CHARACTER LESSON INDEPENDENCE

Durable scene guardrail. Not canon.

## Problem
A strong PLG scene often lets another character illuminate something Greg has missed. That can become too clean when every secondary character is recruited into teaching, validating, or completing Greg's chapter-level lesson.

## Rule
Other people are not thematic delivery systems.

They may:
- misunderstand Greg;
- care about a different problem entirely;
- interrupt before the neat conclusion;
- make a choice that complicates Greg's interpretation;
- be right for the wrong reason;
- be wrong and remain wrong;
- have no opinion about what Greg just learned;
- talk about food, money, gossip, work, family, weather, or something else instead;
- leave before Greg gets closure.

## Especially with Lyssa, Senna, Cala, Tavi, Hessa, Jori, Saret and other recurring people
Do not use their final line merely to restate or sharpen the chapter's thematic point.

Let them continue their own lives past the moment where Greg thinks the scene is finished.

## Thematic asymmetry
Greg may learn something from a scene that nobody else believes is important.
Another character may take away a completely different thing.
The reader may understand a third.

This asymmetry is desirable.

## Test
If the last secondary-character beat sounds like the perfect line needed to complete Greg's internal argument, ask what that person would actually be thinking about instead.
