## Place-writing rule

A place becomes memorable through repeated use, routes, work, sensory identity, social relationships, and changed meaning over time. When a future setting pass inspects the manuscript, add compact established details such as what Greg notices first, how movement works there, who controls/accesses the space, recurring sounds/smells/materials, economic/social function, relationship history, and continuity traps.

Do not invent precise geography merely to make a map look complete.
