# PEG-LEG GREG — SETTING BIBLE

Durable setting/world knowledge for writing. Manuscript evidence outranks this file.

The purpose is to make places feel lived-in and consistent, not to complete an encyclopedia.

## Carrow

Greg's current city/home base. Treat it as a functioning place of work, trade, roads, neighborhoods, institutions, theatre, suppliers, repairers, food, lodging, errands, and social memory.

When deepening Carrow, prefer manuscript-established details about how people actually move through and use it.

## Theatre / hall

A real workplace and performance space, not an abstract backdrop.

Track durable facts about:
- stage geometry
- entrances/exits
- backstage traffic
- seating/audience behavior
- storage
- props/costumes
- lighting/oil
- money handling only where established
- nearby suppliers/repairers

Current commercial pressure has made the theatre's relationship with neighboring businesses story-relevant.

## Supplier / neighborhood network

Olin and other actual businesses used by the theatre are becoming a small practical information network. Rinna has instructed suppliers to verify claims with her and avoid giving strangers useful information about purchases, payment, carts, cargo, or timing.

This is ordinary commercial/social infrastructure, not a spy network.

## Mason's Cut

Current evidence only supports that the brown-coat man was seen walking toward Mason's Cut. He was not seen entering it. Do not promote the location into his base, residence, territory, or organization headquarters without new manuscript evidence.

## Economy

Currency: copper / silver / gold.

No conversion rate has been established. Do not invent one.

Money should remain materially meaningful through wages, purchases, debt, supplies, repairs, food, transport, and trade without forcing every scene into accounting.

## Antonius Vale debt

Debt is an economic/story pressure, not a setting fact to re-explain constantly. Exact current balance is not stated in authoritative narration. Original hard terms include 35% interest, 8 days, labor through Antonius on default; original principal unknown.

No Vale/Bren connection is established.

## Institutions / guild / magic infrastructure

Record exact institution names, procedures, permissions, social status, and physical spaces only as manuscript evidence supports them. Hessa's controlled testing implies meaningful institutional/evidentiary practice; do not invent bureaucracy merely to fill taxonomy.

## Ordinary material culture

Prefer concrete recurring materials and constraints from scenes: lamp oil, wicks, repairs, carts, chairs, clothing, paper/receipts, food, tools, stage materials, weather, roads, and bodily labor.

Magic should coexist with ordinary material systems rather than erase them.

## Place-writing rule

A place becomes memorable through repeated use, routes, work, sensory identity, social relationships, and changed meaning over time.

When a future setting pass inspects the manuscript, add compact established details such as:
- what Greg notices first
- how movement works there
- who controls/accesses the space
- recurring sounds/smells/materials
- economic/social function
- relationship history
- continuity traps

Do not invent precise geography merely to make a map look complete.
