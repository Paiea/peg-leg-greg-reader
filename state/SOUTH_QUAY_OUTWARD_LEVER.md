# PEG-LEG GREG — SOUTH QUAY OUTWARD LEVER

Non-canon development guidance anchored to Chapter 352.

South Quay should not become Greg's new permanent workplace just because it generated a successful paid chapter.

Its better long-range use may be as an **information and opportunity intersection**.

## What South Quay can expose naturally

Without making every held return mysterious, the quay can plausibly surface:
- names of distant makers and workshops;
- regions Greg has not visited in this life;
- damaged cargo whose destination matters more than the damage;
- people seeking temporary reliable labor for one leg of a journey;
- merchants, insurers, clerks, guards, teamsters, and specialists with outside obligations;
- returns that need to go back out rather than deeper into Carrow;
- someone whose family/property problem is disguised as a shipping problem;
- a person asking for discretion because ordinary business records reveal too much.

## Preferred use

A future outward hook may touch South Quay only briefly.

Example shape:
1. Greg is called for ordinary temp work or encounters a quay contact elsewhere.
2. He notices one outside-facing fact.
3. The fact is not automatically a mystery.
4. A separate person later makes that fact useful.
5. Two reasons converge on the same route.

This supports the preferred departure pattern:

**one mildly attractive job + one independent reason to care about the same road = rational yes.**

## Avoid

- South Quay secretly running every adventure;
- held crates becoming mystery boxes by default;
- Greg promoted into clerk hierarchy because the story needs access;
- fake procedural expertise replacing actual travel;
- every unusual item at the quay becoming a magical artifact;
- using Merren as a permanent quest giver.

South Quay is valuable because it connects Carrow to elsewhere. Preserve that network function without turning it into headquarters.
