# PEG-LEG GREG — VISUAL BIBLE

Durable visual language for illustrations and reader graphics.

Manuscript canon outranks image reference. Continuity is a target, not an excuse to stop production.

## North star

**SKETCH + INK + PAINT**

Illustrated-novel energy rather than glossy concept-art sameness.

Use mixed fidelity intentionally:
- many loose sketch beats
- stronger scene illustrations
- occasional higher-resolution feature art

The reader should feel illustrated throughout without requiring every image to be a poster.

## Movement rule

Default toward movement and visual flow rather than static posed compositions.

Ask:

1. WHERE DOES THE EYE ENTER?
2. WHERE DOES THE MOTION CARRY IT?
3. WHERE DOES IT LAND?

Movement can come from:
- posture
- gesture
- eyelines
- crutches/body angle
- clothing/hair
- foreground objects
- perspective
- people crossing background
- doors/entrances/exits
- weather
- smoke
- fabric
- stage traffic
- carts
- magic force/direction

Quiet scenes should still have directional energy unless deliberate stillness is the point.

## Camera rotation

Avoid repeated centered eye-level two-person compositions.

Rotate:
- wide / medium / close
- high / low / eye-level
- foreground obstruction
- over-shoulder
- entering/leaving frame
- diagonal travel
- backstage/front-of-house
- audience/stage reversal
- environment-first composition
- detail/hand/prop beat where continuity permits

## Greg continuity

Greg is nineteen. Permanent LEFT BKA, knee preserved; right leg intact; two crutches.

Reject major disability/anatomy contradictions. Do not demand frame-perfect facial consistency if identity remains recognizable.

## Lyssa continuity

Black woman; tall relative to Greg; thin/lithe; natural Afro-textured hair. Do not invent unsupported precise features as fixed canon.

## KEEP / RETRY standard

KEEP normal illustration variation.

RETRY major failures such as:
- wrong ethnicity/skin tone
- Greg's disability materially wrong
- severe anatomy failure
- wrong age category
- major established facial-hair/identity contradiction where relevant
- character collision
- absurd equipment/location substitution
- manuscript contradiction that changes the scene

Do not retry merely because a face is not identical to the previous panel.

## Magic

Magic should show force and direction rather than merely glowing. Preserve the narrowness of what the manuscript establishes.

## Theatre

Use stage geometry, audience, wings, props, furniture, costumes, sightlines, rehearsal traffic, and backstage labor. Theatre images should not all look like posed publicity stills.

## Website graphics

Graphics should support reading. Favor book-like restraint, whitespace, texture through art, and clear hierarchy over decorative UI chrome.

Do not force low-resolution images to full bleed. Let image role and intrinsic resolution determine display weight.

## Prompt construction ingredients

For each panel, build from:

**SUBJECT + ACTION + CAMERA + FOREGROUND + ENVIRONMENTAL MOVEMENT + EYE PATH + MANUSCRIPT DETAILS + CONTINUITY + STYLE**

Do not use one sacred prompt for every scene. Preserve shared style language while varying composition aggressively.

## Development rule

New visual ideas can be explored in `READER_DESIGN_LAB.md` or batch notes. Promote only durable successful principles into this bible.
