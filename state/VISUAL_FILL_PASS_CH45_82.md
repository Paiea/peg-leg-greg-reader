## Prioritized future illustration queue — Chapters 45–82

### 1. Chapter 63 — West River Yard load test / failure
**Scene:** The professionally prepared loaded wagon crosses the timber-plate test system, followed by the failure sequence that pins Greg.
**Why:** This is the strongest spatial and causal visual gap in the current reader. Seeing the tested system before the accident makes the consequence legible rather than arbitrary.
**Continuity:** Greg is nineteen, lean, messy dark hair, uneven young beard/stubble, practical Bronze-level clothing. Preserve established yard equipment and professional workers. The Barrier works; it is small, planar, precise, directional, and revealed through physical consequence rather than glow.
**Composition / movement:** Wide industrial setup with wagon motion carrying the eye across the timber plates toward Greg and the failure point. A second candidate can use a lower, closer angle at the instant the slat is redirected and the system fails.

### 2. Chapter 64 — The blanket after surgery
**Scene:** Immediate post-amputation physical reality, with the blanket defining the changed silhouette before Greg fully incorporates it.
**Why:** Quiet, definitive transition image. It gives visual weight to the novel's title-level bodily change without turning the surgery into spectacle.
**Continuity:** Do not invent prosthetic details. Medical environment and dressing must follow the chapter. Greg remains physically nineteen.
**Composition / movement:** Still, restrained bedside composition. Eye enters through Greg's face/hand, follows the blanket line, and lands on the absence implied beneath it.

### 3. Chapter 60 — Coin Barrier clearing the culvert
**Scene:** Greg uses a tiny angled Barrier to move water enough to clear a leaf mat from a culvert outlet during road inspection.
**Why:** One of the clearest demonstrations of Greg's practical magic becoming useful ordinary work.
**Continuity:** Barrier is coin-sized/small, planar, precise, directional, nearly invisible. Show redirected water, leaves, pressure, and Greg's hand/body positioning instead of a luminous shield.
**Composition / movement:** Low angle near the culvert. Eye enters at blocked water/leaves, follows the redirected flow, lands on Greg's controlled hand and analytical attention.

### 4. Chapter 68 — First crutch work between the rails
**Scene:** Greg confronts the adjustable wooden crutches beside the parallel rails and begins learning the new mechanics.
**Why:** Strong body-mechanics image and the first clear visual grammar of adaptation after the injury.
**Continuity:** Do not invent later prosthetic technology. Crutches are wood with padded tops, hand grips, peg adjustment, and rough dark traction wrapping on the feet.
**Composition / movement:** Medium-wide training-space view. Rails frame a path through the image. Greg's weight shift and the crutch geometry carry the eye toward Sera/Nerin's professional observation.

### 5. Chapter 56 — Sparring because Greg wants to
**Scene:** Greg arrives for voluntary sparring with Alden, physically healthy enough to enjoy movement rather than treating training as obligation.
**Why:** Important pre-injury contrast: confidence, play, friendship, and a body he is beginning to inhabit comfortably.
**Continuity:** Greg's healing hand is nearly closed/usable; ordinary Guild training environment; established Alden appearance if available.
**Composition / movement:** Dynamic diagonal exchange, feet and hips doing as much storytelling as swords. Avoid posed duel symmetry.

### 6. Chapter 75 — The finished staircase rail
**Scene:** Greg puts one hand on the newly installed ash rail while his room remains fourteen steps above him.
**Why:** A small practical object carries independence, money, frustration, adaptation, and desire for his own room.
**Continuity:** Ash rail, three posts, solid return at top, old dark stair wood. No invented accessibility technology.
**Composition / movement:** Greg low in frame at stair foot or lower landing. Rail creates a strong diagonal upward toward the unseen/partly seen room. Eye lands on his hand gripping the new ash.

### 7. Chapter 79 — Watching Pessa hit Alden with the practice staff
**Scene:** Greg sits/stands at the south training yard as Pessa tags Alden in the face with a padded practice staff and Greg laughs.
**Why:** Excellent post-injury social image because Greg is present in a physical world he previously occupied differently, yet the scene is funny rather than mournful.
**Continuity:** Respect Greg's current crutches/residual-limb state and wound restrictions. Pessa/Alden appearances only from established references/manuscript.
**Composition / movement:** Action enters with Pessa's staff, lands on Alden's reaction, then carries to Greg laughing at the edge of the training space.

### 8. Chapter 45 — Cracked egg at the west gate escort
**Scene:** First-light-but-not-really morning at the already-busy west gate; Greg has sat on one of the boiled eggs in his coat while Alden actually checks a cart wheel, Pessa eats an apple, and Dorn sharpens his broad knife.
**Why:** Strong ordinary-life ensemble image. It establishes Greg embedded in a working team and preserves the book's physical comedy.
**Continuity:** Pessa has cropped brown hair, green coat, one boot unlaced, short spear. Dorn has crooked nose, heavily repaired padded coat, broad knife. Greg is pre-injury.
**Composition / movement:** Busy gate environment with multiple simultaneous actions. Greg's cracked-egg realization is foreground comic beat while the cart inspection and morning traffic keep the world moving behind him.

### 9. Chapter 61 — Sevren pushes the plate toward drifting Greg
**Scene:** During ordinary social time after road work, Sevren notices Greg mentally drifting and physically pushes the plate toward him.
**Why:** Small relationship action that shows friendship without turning it into a staged conversation portrait.
**Continuity:** Use established Sevren appearance only; if no stable visual reference exists, generate after continuity reference is confirmed.
**Composition / movement:** Table-level composition. Plate movement creates the visual vector from Sevren to Greg; surrounding social activity prevents the pair from feeling isolated on a stage.

### 10. Chapter 82 — Invoice reconciliation at Vale's
**Scene:** Greg at the ground-floor side room with three invoice sheets and three receiving tallies, working through Antonius's deliberately prepared reconciliation problem.
**Why:** Strong endpoint image for Chapter 82: Greg's analytical identity, economic systems, changed mobility, and continued usefulness all coexist in one quiet working scene.
**Continuity:** Greg is post-amputation and using crutches as established; wrapped hands if present at this moment; three invoice sheets and three receiving tallies. Do not over-emphasize Antonius if he is not physically present in the selected instant.
**Composition / movement:** Slight overhead/three-quarter table view. Eye enters on mismatched paper stacks, follows Greg's hand and marks across the table, and lands on his concentrated face/crutches positioned within reach.
