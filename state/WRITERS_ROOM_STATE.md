- Theatre must remain socially and professionally alive without Greg.

## SOCIAL / CRAFT WATCHES
- Greg should increasingly participate before fully understanding: accept constraint → receive another person → act → understand afterward, maybe.
- Greg may enjoy ambient company without needing a specific person or task.
- Free time can reveal desire through leakage: staying after usefulness ends, seeking people, buying comfort, playing meaningless games, wandering from curiosity.
- Greg may initiate easily when the lane is clear, but informal social permission can remain harder.
- Keep distinct: NEEDED, INVITED, WANTED, DESIRED, EXPECTED, MISSED. Do not make them a progression ladder.
- Greg may trust repeated behavior more than declarations.
