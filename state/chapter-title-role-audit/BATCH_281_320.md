## Pattern note

This block shows the naming rule slipping from **who Greg is** to **what the chapter is about**. Common current-title subjects include infrastructure (`The High Line`, `The Dry Bench`), props/objects (`The Blue Horse`, `The Red Pot`), test geometry (`The Far Mark`, `The Marked Third`), events/opportunities (`The Open Seat`, `The Missed Coin`), and places (`The Landing`).

The prose itself usually already contains a much cleaner Greg-role. The audit is recovering that role rather than changing what the chapter is about.
