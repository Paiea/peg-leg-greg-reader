# EDITORIAL WORKTREE RULES

These rules apply to editorial work under `state/editorial/` on the voice/compression branch.

Before structural compression or redundancy decisions, read:
- `../EDITOR_STATE.md`
- `BOOK_LEVEL_COMPRESSION_ENGINE.md` when running the authorized book-level compression mode
- `REVISION_ORDER_AND_REDUNDANCY.md`
- `REDUNDANCY_CANDIDATES.md`
- the exact manuscript prose and relevant voice pages

## Revision order

Default order is:

**DIALOGUE / ATTRIBUTION FIRST -> REDUNDANCY REVIEW SECOND -> APPROVED STRUCTURAL SURGERY THIRD**

Do not derail an active voice pass into a manuscript-wide tightening pass unless the author has explicitly activated the dedicated book-level compression mode.

### Authorized book-level compression exception

The author has explicitly activated `BOOK_LEVEL_COMPRESSION_ENGINE.md` for a stronger structural audit beginning at Chapter 430 and continuing through current manuscript authority.

For this authorized run:

**BOOK-LEVEL AUDIT MAY PROCEED FROM CHAPTER 430 WITHOUT WAITING FOR THE DIALOGUE-VARIANCE EDGE TO REACH THE CURRENT ENDPOINT.**

Existing dialogue/attribution findings remain binding where already reviewed. The book-level pass must not reintroduce settled attribution, POV, referent, canonical-name, or voice regressions.

First traversal freezes structural judgments before destructive manuscript surgery. Use `state/editorial/book-level-compression/` for bounded audit artifacts and live-edge state.

During ordinary dialogue/attribution work outside this authorized mode, flag suspected repeated narrative functions in `REDUNDANCY_CANDIDATES.md` and keep moving unless an existing frozen structural map already authorizes the edit.

## Core rule

**Mundane is not the problem. Repetition without new value is.**

Protect quiet, ordinary, procedural, domestic, work, social, travel, rehearsal, maintenance, and low-stakes chapters when they provide something meaningfully new.

Judge repeated narrative function, not repeated subject matter.

**Repetition creates a debt that later payoff must repay.**

A repeated routine can earn its space through accumulation, changed meaning, escalation, a changed variable, relationship movement, expectation, or a later payoff. If a sequence keeps asking the reader to buy substantially the same setup/process/conclusion without enough new value or payoff, it gains compression pressure.

Do not cut or merge merely because a scene is mundane or undramatic. Do not manufacture conventional confrontation to solve a payoff problem when clearer systemic or quiet resolution better fits the novel.

Use `REVISION_ORDER_AND_REDUNDANCY.md` for the general decision test and `BOOK_LEVEL_COMPRESSION_ENGINE.md` for the stronger current Ch430+ sequence-level pass.
