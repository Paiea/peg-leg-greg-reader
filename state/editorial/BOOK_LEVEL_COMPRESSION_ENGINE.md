# PEG-LEG GREG — BOOK-LEVEL COMPRESSION ENGINE

Branch: `editor/voice-compression-pass`

Status: **ACTIVE SPECIALIZED EDITOR MODE**

Parent authority: `state/EDITOR_STATE.md` plus exact manuscript authority and active correction overlays.

This engine exists because a chapter can be individually competent and still be redundant inside a long book.

## Core question

Do not ask only:

**Does this chapter work?**

Ask:

**Does this chapter earn its space relative to the chapters around it and the manuscript as a whole?**

A good chapter may still need compression, merger, relocation, or deletion if a neighboring chapter performs the same narrative function more strongly.

## North star

**PROTECT DISTINCTIVE MUNDANE TEXTURE. COMPRESS REPEATED DELIVERY OF MUNDANE TEXTURE.**

PLG should not be converted into a conventional plot-only fantasy novel. Quiet life, work, travel, food, money, tools, mobility, maintenance, bureaucracy, friendships, errands, recovery, and low-stakes choices are structural parts of the story.

The target is repeated narrative function, not ordinary subject matter.

## Scope

This mode is intentionally stronger than dialogue-only, attribution-only, or line-polish passes.

It may recommend:

- KEEP
- COMPRESS
- MERGE
- MOVE
- CUT

It may identify chapter-boundary changes and whole-scene deletions.

It does not automatically apply destructive edits during first traversal.

First traversal = audit and freeze structural decisions.
Second traversal = approved manuscript surgery against verified exact prose and downstream dependencies.

## Primary failure mode to detect

The manuscript engine can become too good at producing PLG-shaped chapters.

Watch for repeated skeletons such as:

Greg enters a new place -> notices infrastructure -> meets competent local authority -> bounded operational problem emerges -> multiple legitimate interests conflict -> Greg avoids overclaiming -> distributed expertise resolves or narrows the problem -> Greg extracts a lesson -> body/money/tool state updates -> next route appears.

That grammar is allowed.

It is not allowed to become invisible default architecture merely because each execution is competent.

## Compression targets

Aggressively inspect:

- repeated arrival / observation / specialist / bounded-problem sequences
- repeated institutional conflicts where everyone has legitimate pressure
- repeated demonstrations that one instrument, witness, expert, or observation is insufficient
- repeated road, market, survey, harbor, transport, auction, repair, or procedure scenes
- repeated Greg restraint beats proving the same competence
- repeated `not my job`, `not authority`, `not enough evidence`, or equivalent boundary lessons once behavior already proves them
- repeated body / skin / prosthetic checks without a new capability, constraint, decision, emotional meaning, or social consequence
- repeated money accounting that does not materially change behavior
- repeated artifact curiosity without acquisition, loss, changed strategy, relationship consequence, or useful information
- repeated worldbuilding whose primary value is another clever local mechanism
- repeated explanatory paragraphs after the scene has already demonstrated the point
- repeated chapter-closing lessons
- repeated comic machinery, especially when branded shorthand spreads across characters
- procedural detail after the reader already understands the process
- chapter boundaries that exist because generation produced a chapter, not because the book needs one

## Strong protection targets

Protect aggressively when material creates genuinely new value:

- relationship movement or changed relationship language
- supporting characters making independent decisions
- Greg failing in a way that changes future behavior
- mobility/body progression that changes ordinary life or available action
- money/leverage progression that changes actual choices
- magic evidence that materially changes the evidence map
- consequential artifact acquisition, loss, maintenance, or use
- new social configurations
- ordinary-life scenes where a capability acquires emotional or practical meaning
- distinctive local culture that changes behavior rather than decorating setting
- emotional residue that survives into later chapters
- unusually strong comedy that is not merely another version of a nearby joke
- moments where Greg chooses not to optimize
- consequences that alter later decisions
- scenes where another character owns the problem, joke, expertise, failure, recovery, or departure

## Recent-sequence comparison rule

Never judge a chapter only in isolation.

For each target chapter, compare against at least:

- 2-3 immediately preceding chapters
- 2-3 immediately following chapters when available
- recent chapters with the same narrative function

Ask:

1. What does this chapter do that its neighbors do not?
2. What would be lost if the chapter vanished entirely?
3. Can its strongest material survive inside an adjacent chapter?
4. Is the chapter adding a new variable or merely replaying a proven system?
5. Is the changed location disguising repeated architecture?
6. Is procedural specificity still producing texture, or has it become logging?
7. Is the chapter carrying later dependencies that make apparently minor material structurally important?

## Chapter verdicts

### KEEP
The chapter has a distinct narrative, relationship, emotional, world, capability, or structural function that deserves its current chapter identity.

KEEP does not mean no line compression later.

### COMPRESS
The chapter earns existence but contains repeated procedure, explanation, accounting, reaction, or worldbuilding that can shrink substantially.

Record what must survive.

### MERGE
The chapter's best material is valuable, but the chapter boundary is not. Identify the strongest neighboring host and what moves.

### MOVE
A beat belongs elsewhere for sequence rhythm, causality, or concentration of value.

### CUT
The chapter or scene contributes little unique value after comparison, and downstream dependencies can be preserved without it.

A competent scene may be CUT.

## Structural burden of repetition

**Repetition creates debt.**

A repeated routine can earn space if repetition itself creates accumulation, changed meaning, expectation, escalation, relationship residue, or payoff.

If repetition asks the reader to buy substantially the same setup/process/conclusion again without enough new value, compression pressure rises sharply.

## Body / prosthetic logging rule

Preserve checks when they establish:

- a new wear duration
- new terrain
- new task
- new pressure point
- changed gait or fatigue threshold
- new hand freedom
- new pain or injury state
- a decision based on fit/body evidence
- relationship/craft consequences

Compress routine `pink cleared / no pain / skin normal` reporting when it only confirms an already stable pattern.

Ordinary functional milestones may be more important than larger numerical milestones. Laundry, packing, carrying, standing work, travel, or free-hand use can deserve space when they change what the limb means.

## Money / leverage logging rule

Preserve exact economic facts needed for canon and decisions.

Do not require every transaction to receive full narrative ceremony.

Compress arithmetic when the amount does not change behavior, risk tolerance, available route, debt position, working capital, or emotional scarcity response.

## Worldbuilding rule

Different places need different grammar, not merely different gadgets.

Protect local systems when they change:

- behavior
- law
- logistics
- social hierarchy
- risk
- economics
- travel
- work
- language
- choices

Compress another interesting device if its main function is proving again that the world is interesting.

## Institutional-conflict rule

PLG benefits from conflicts where nobody is stupid or evil.

But this pattern can repeat too cleanly.

If recent chapters repeatedly contain:

competent professionals + incompatible obligations + procedural uncertainty + Greg correctly staying bounded,

then later examples need a new variable, stronger consequence, different social texture, or compression.

## Dialogue / voice relationship

Existing dialogue-variance and attribution authority remains active.

This engine does not replace those passes.

When structural compression removes scenes, preserve settled speaker clarity, voice distinctions, canonical names, POV, referents, and relationship-earned shorthand in surviving prose.

Greg remains the gravitational center of short dry compressed comic reduction.

Do not preserve a repeated joke merely because it is funny in isolation.

## Authority and safety

Exact manuscript prose outranks summaries.

Active correction overlays outrank superseded checkpoint drift.

Do not change:

- canon outcomes
- relationship status
- magic results/counts
- money facts needed for continuity
- body continuity
- established threat evidence
- protected uncertainty
- ownership or contractual facts
- causal dependencies

unless stronger current authority explicitly supports the correction.

Hard manuscript prose rule: **NO EM DASHES.**

Illustration state, role cards, chapter numbers, and existing art are advisory during structural judgment. Do not preserve weak structure because art points at it.

Do not renumber incrementally. Structural decisions freeze first, then renumber through an audited old->new migration.

## Bounded workflow

Default audit unit: **10 sequential chapters**.

For each batch:

1. Verify current manuscript endpoint and active overlays.
2. Read exact prose for the full batch.
3. Read enough neighboring prose to compare sequence function.
4. Classify every chapter KEEP / COMPRESS / MERGE / MOVE / CUT.
5. Record the chapter's unique surviving value in one concise statement.
6. Record repeated narrative functions against neighboring chapters.
7. Identify exact scenes or sections under compression pressure.
8. Flag downstream dependencies and canon risks.
9. Propose the smallest structural intervention that removes repetition.
10. Save the audit artifact durably under `state/editorial/book-level-compression/`.
11. Fetch the saved artifact back from GitHub.
12. Advance the live edge only after persistence is verified.

## First-pass restraint

The audit is allowed to be aggressive in judgment and conservative in destruction.

Do not rewrite chapters during the initial map simply because a verdict is obvious.

Freeze the map first.

This prevents a locally sensible cut from breaking later money, travel, relationship, body, artifact, or progression dependencies.
