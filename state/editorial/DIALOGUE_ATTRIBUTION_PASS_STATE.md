## Voice philosophy

People from the same town may sound like people from the same town. Do not manufacture maximal contrast or bespoke dialects. Important recurring characters should instead accumulate a slight persistent fingerprint across scenes.

Education/register may influence vocabulary and explanation style, but **education is not intelligence**. Trade expertise, household expertise, institutional education, theatrical experience, and street/local knowledge may produce different speech without ranking competence.
