## Dialogue variance rule

Use:

**SPOKEN MOMENT = BASE VOICE + RELATIONSHIP + CURRENT STATE + SCENE PRESSURE + SMALL HUMAN VARIANCE**

Greg is the gravitational center of the short dry compressed counterpunch register.

Other people may be witty, dry, sarcastic, fast, or funny. Do not make Greg the only funny person in Carrow. But do not distribute his exact reduction architecture evenly across competent adults.

Review for **comic register convergence**:
- several speakers landing similarly shaped short counters;
- every competent adult able to keep up with Greg's timing;
- professional specialists sounding like Greg/Hessa with different job nouns;
- stress producing better jokes instead of sometimes suppressing cleverness;
- new characters immediately entering the same sarcasm academy.

Do not fix this mechanically by making everyone verbose. Preserve character-specific wit and let relationship, mood, cognitive load, physical pressure, formality, misunderstanding, hesitation, overexplaining, bad metaphors, literalism, and refusal create variable performance.

## High-value findings to carry forward

- **Short is not the same as Greg-shaped.** Chapter 321 proves compact speech can remain highly differentiated when the cognitive source is different.
- Speaker clarity can be excellent while conversational ecology is too uniform.
- Preserve character-specific jokes. Remove density, not humor.
- In dangerous or cognitively loaded work, let stress sometimes suppress wit.
- Let specialists become longer when explaining their own domain if that is natural; do not convert their expertise into Hessa-style methodological language.
- A supporting character can fail to understand Greg, ignore the joke, answer the wrong question, overexplain, become formal, or simply be less fast.
- Relationship/state modulation outranks permanent gimmicks.
- Hazardous multi-person work with short commands needs heavier attribution when ownership of the physical system is unclear.
- Stable two-person scenes can stay light once rhythm is established; re-tag after phase changes.
- Professional jurisdiction, object handling, and scene task often separate voices better than decorative diction.
- **Incidental-stranger optimization is now a high-value watch target.** One-scene clerks, sellers, couriers, and passersby should not automatically know how to volley with Greg unless their job, state, or personality independently supports it.
- Shared dryness among recurring characters is acceptable when the underlying engines differ. Nessa stops downstream physical mistakes; Marek preserves social momentum; Greg analyzes the rule.
- Specialist qualification can resemble Hessa on the surface without being contamination when the professional reference system is different, as with the Chapter 330 linewright's seat/load/bypass reasoning.
