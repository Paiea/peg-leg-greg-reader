## High-value watch targets

- Coverage follows current exact manuscript authority. A prior `scope complete` status automatically reopens when `main` advances.
- Greg owns the highest density of compressed dry counterpunches. Supporting characters may be funny or terse without inheriting his reduction architecture.
- Repeated `No / I didn't say / you were about to`, `you were thinking`, generic `you looked`, and generic `your face` ladders have a high burden after manuscript-wide overdistribution.
- Background and first-contact characters especially should not inherit branded prediction/argument architecture merely because the exchange is funny.
- Wrong predictions can be healthy when the miss is visible and corrected.
- **Let assumptions be correctable instead of telepathically correct.** A seller may guess Greg's intention, but Greg should be allowed to say what he actually asked.
- Greg's own overprediction is useful when another person explicitly corrects him.
- Visible sustained attention, stopping, reaching, leaning, handling, affect, transport geometry, object arrangement, or visible orientation can replace generic personality commentary.
- **Visible orientation beats exact face-reading.** If Greg actually turns toward the locked chest, household members can object to the chest without inferring the noun from his expression.
- Visible setup can replace face prediction before anyone speaks. Harness plus bundles, an active theatre incident, an open artifact case, or current work material may already create the question.
- Immediate safety situations lower the inference burden when the relevant behavior is physically visible and movement matters now.
- Stable cognition with changed wording is positive variance evidence. Relationship knowledge should remain stable while surface performance varies.
- Relationship familiarity should alter workflow and sharpen questions, not grant transcript access or undisclosed transaction vocabulary.
- Broad relationship categories can remain when they name a recurring class instead of an exact hidden sentence. `If this is yours, no` or `object face` has a lower burden than `your face already asked this exact thing`.
- Explicit learned borrowing is not accidental leakage when the manuscript marks where the borrowed boundary came from.
- Household jurisdiction can replace thought policing. Cala can say what happens in her house without deciding what Greg secretly thought.
- Equipment identity is stronger than generic face-reading. Nessa/Jori should name the prop, light, route, rail, or equipment lane they own.
- Let Greg actually say the system, schedule, money, transaction, experiment, work, or travel question when another character's authority is strongest in the answer.
- Scheduling authority should use scheduling language. Scope authority should use scope language. Experiment authority should use protocol language. Market authority should use price and condition. Household authority should use storage, safety, privacy, and shared-space consequences.
- Phrase/catchphrase ownership matters. Relationship phrases may recur, but they should not migrate automatically to unrelated speakers.
- Child speech is a major anti-compression lane. Preserve associative overexplaining, literal category logic, repeated follow-ups, incomplete social calibration, legalistic self-defense, and inability to accept an adult stopping point.
- Long speech is strongest when caused by state and role. Strong current models include Mevi's household emergency, Pera's social sprawl, Mett's nervous overexplaining, Jessa's shop gossip, Lyssa's tired work muttering, Deren's plumbing narration, Olin's load geometry, Kett's procurement/drawing frustration, Rell's local ecology, Dema's permit history, and Vessa's field requirements.
- Temporary roles can shift speech without replacing base voice. Greg as instructor becomes more explicit, Naro as guide temporarily more precise, Lyssa as paid finisher more analytical, and Greg as visitor/delegate more formal about scope.
- Institutional and temporary-work reputation can propagate through named recommendations without turning every contact into permanent employment or friendship.
- Institutional access is a voice-generating lane: permission to enter, inspect, buy, transfer, return, pass, count, read, teach, use an object, cross a closure, or access a worksite can shape dialogue without exposition.
- Prefer current evidence, object, role, rule, schedule, contract, damaged equipment, route, body position, records, work history, price, count, storage, transport geometry, permit, complaint record, or assigned tool over personality commentary.
- Predeclared procedure is stronger than predicted intention.
- Shared caution is not automatically Hessa voice. Keep motive distinct: Hessa protects evidence; Orsen transfer protocol; Marra work terms; Sella drainage operations/paperwork; auction staff sale condition; Jori live theatre safety; Veya passenger-use inspection; Dema festival licensing; Vessa field requirements; Mara handling classification.
- Paid consultation can justify longer specialist speech when explanation itself is purchased or required work.
- Distributed expertise protects Greg's competence. Greg may possess old-life category knowledge while current-city routing, law, custody, pricing, maintenance, appraisal, infrastructure, species detail, procedure, local records, permit history, complaint systems, and field routes belong to other people.
- Independent supporting-character life is structural variance. Their work, customers, routes, money, family, tools, errands, mistakes, festivals, schedules, and grudges should continue whether Greg is present or not.
- Ensemble disagreement is major voice variance. Grief, work, market, public-memory, dinner, travel, and institutional scenes can stay alive through correction, contradiction, silence, misrouting, and uneven knowledge rather than one polished group register.
