## High-value watch targets

- Coverage follows current exact manuscript authority. A prior `scope complete` status automatically reopens when `main` advances.
- Greg owns the highest density of compressed dry counterpunches. Supporting characters may be funny or terse without inheriting his reduction architecture.
- Repeated `No / I didn't say / you were about to`, `you were thinking`, generic `you looked`, and generic `your face` ladders have a high burden after manuscript-wide overdistribution.
- Background and first-contact characters especially should not inherit branded prediction/argument architecture merely because the exchange is funny.
- Wrong predictions can be healthy when the miss is visible and corrected.
- Greg's own overprediction is useful when another person explicitly corrects him.
- Visible sustained attention, stopping, reaching, leaning, handling, affect, transport geometry, or object arrangement can replace generic personality commentary.
- Visible setup can replace face prediction before anyone speaks. If harness plus bundles already creates the carrying question, or a theatre incident already creates the instruction problem, use that.
- Immediate safety situations lower the inference burden when the relevant behavior is physically visible and movement matters now.
- Stable cognition with changed wording is positive variance evidence. Relationship knowledge should remain stable while surface performance varies.
- Relationship familiarity should alter workflow and sharpen questions, not grant transcript access or undisclosed transaction vocabulary.
- Broad relationship shorthand can remain when it names a category rather than an exact hidden sentence. `Object face` has a lower burden than predicting the exact request.
- Explicit learned borrowing is not accidental leakage when the manuscript marks where the borrowed boundary came from.
- Household jurisdiction can replace thought policing. Cala can say what happens in her house without deciding what Greg secretly thought.
- Equipment identity is stronger than generic face-reading. Nessa/Jori should name the prop, light, route, or equipment lane they own.
- Let Greg actually say the system, schedule, money, transaction, experiment, or work question when another character's authority is strongest in the answer.
- Scheduling authority should use scheduling language. Scope authority should use scope language. Experiment authority should use protocol language. Market authority should use price and condition. Household authority should use storage, safety, privacy, and shared-space consequences.
- Phrase/catchphrase ownership matters. Relationship phrases may recur, but they should not migrate automatically to unrelated speakers.
- Child speech is a major anti-compression lane. Preserve associative overexplaining, literal category logic, repeated follow-ups, incomplete social calibration, and inability to accept an adult stopping point.
- Long speech is strongest when caused by state and role. Mevi's household emergency, Pera's social sprawl, Mett's nervous overexplaining, Jessa's shop gossip, and Lyssa's tired work muttering are positive models.
- Temporary roles can shift speech without replacing base voice. Greg as instructor becomes more explicit, Naro as guide temporarily becomes more precise, and Lyssa as paid finisher becomes more analytical about pace and terms.
- Institutional and temporary-work reputation can propagate through named recommendations without turning every contact into permanent employment or friendship.
- Institutional access is a voice-generating lane: permission to enter, inspect, buy, transfer, return, pass, count, read, teach, or use an object can shape dialogue without exposition.
- Prefer current evidence, object, role, rule, schedule, contract, damaged equipment, route, body position, records, work history, price, count, storage, or transport geometry over personality commentary.
- Predeclared procedure is stronger than predicted intention.
- Shared caution is not automatically Hessa voice. Keep the motive distinct: Hessa protects evidence, Orsen protects transfer protocol, Marra protects work terms, Sella protects drainage operations/paperwork, auction staff protect sale condition, and Jori protects live theatre route safety.
- Paid consultation can justify longer specialist speech when explanation itself is the purchased service.
- Distributed expertise protects Greg's competence. Greg may possess old-life category knowledge while current-city routing, law, custody, pricing, maintenance, appraisal, infrastructure, species detail, procedure, and local records belong to other people.
- Independent supporting-character life is structural variance. Their work, customers, routes, money, family, tools, errands, and mistakes should continue whether Greg is present or not.
- Ensemble disagreement is major voice variance. Grief, work, market, and public-memory scenes can stay alive through correction, contradiction, silence, and uneven knowledge rather than one polished group register.
- Craft-specific face observation is legitimate when the craft actually requires expression, pose, shoulder angle, load path, movement, or unconscious control.
- Shared game, performance, auction, emergency, gate-window, crowd-routing, count-floor, or live-work pressure can legitimately compress speech when responsibility remains distinct.
- Operational compression remains healthy when the stopping point is owned.
- Work continuing can replace curiosity policing. Other people's jobs may simply continue without explaining themselves to Greg.
- Messy systems protect voice. Exceptions may arrive only when encountered; real work need not front-load every rule.
- Repeated work words such as `Wait`, `Good`, `No`, `Again`, `Hold`, `Clear`, and `Less` remain healthy when current object and jurisdiction keep changing what they mean.
- Do not optimize away bad metaphors, incomplete explanations, wrong guesses, silence, awkwardness, underperformance, long speech, practical underexplanation, tired muttering, or local disagreement when they belong to the speaker.
- Higher stakes do not grant universal mind-reading.
- Rejecting a spoken speculative claim is not predictive dialogue.
- Do not upgrade evidence into reliability, range, generalized object moving, casting, control, artifact function, safe load, market value, road cause, species mechanism, or discrepancy cause without exact support.
