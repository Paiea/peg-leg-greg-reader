## High-value watch targets

- Greg owns the highest density of compressed dry counterpunches. Supporting characters may be funny, dry, clever, sarcastic, or terse without inheriting his reduction architecture.
- Repeated `No / I didn't say / you were about to`, `you were thinking`, generic `you looked`, and generic `your face` ladders have a high burden after manuscript-wide overdistribution.
- Background and first-contact characters especially should not inherit branded prediction/argument architecture merely because the exchange is funny.
- Consecutive prediction beats deserve a higher burden.
- Wrong predictions can be healthy when the miss is visible and demonstrates fallibility. Greg's own overprediction is especially useful when another person explicitly corrects him.
- Visible sustained attention, stopping, reaching, leaning, handling, affect, or transport geometry can replace generic personality/face shorthand when that physical fact is what another person actually observes.
- Stable cognition with changed wording is positive variance evidence. Relationship knowledge should remain stable while surface performance varies.
- Relationship familiarity should alter workflow and sharpen questions, not grant transcript access. When possible, name the local boundary, object, rule, or shared history rather than the hidden thought.
- Relationship familiarity does not automatically reveal transaction vocabulary. A partner may know Greg wants the object without somehow knowing a deposit, fee, hold term, or other undisclosed mechanism exists.
- Broad relationship shorthand can remain when it names a category rather than an exact hidden sentence. General `artifact face` is lower risk than correctly predicting the seller's specific deposit term.
- Explicit learned borrowing is not accidental leakage when the manuscript marks where the borrowed boundary came from. Antonius's `Depends on the task / I learned` is a model.
- Object-specific preemption can be ordinary care or custody rather than mind-reading. A visible object can itself be the question when history makes the interaction obvious.
- Visible transport geometry should beat predicted request. If the basket will swing from a crutch, let Lyssa name the swing.
- Let Greg actually say the system, schedule, money, transaction, or experiment question when another character's authority is strongest in the answer.
- Scheduling authority should use scheduling language. Scope authority should use scope language. Experiment authority should use protocol language. Market authority should use price, demonstration, hold terms, demand, and condition.
- Jurisdiction transfer is stronger than cognition policing. A gate supervisor should say an incident belongs to road office now rather than announce that Greg is thinking about it.
- Experiment design should make Greg verbalize the desired next test. Hessa is strongest when she denies or permits an explicit proposal from protocol rather than forecasting the proposal.
- Phrase/catchphrase ownership matters. Relationship phrases may recur, but they should not migrate automatically to unrelated speakers.
- Incidental merchants, couriers, gate workers, auction staff, counter staff, and municipal workers should joke from transaction state, route permission, merchandise, queue, price, documentation, transfer, returns, or safety procedure rather than generic psychology.
- Market sellers should defend price from demonstrated condition, actual demand, available alternatives, and sale terms rather than `you were about to bargain` telepathy.
- Institutional access is a voice-generating lane. Permission to enter, inspect, buy, transfer, return, pass, or use an object can shape dialogue without becoming exposition.
- Customer-memory and neighborhood-recognition expertise are real social lanes when provenance is explicit.
- Prefer current evidence, object, role, rule, schedule, contract, damaged equipment, route, body position, records, work history, price, or transport geometry over personality commentary.
- Material timing, direct handling rules, object custody, and visible safety conditions are often stronger than predicted questions.
- Predeclared procedure is stronger than predicted intention.
- Information-discipline voices defend thresholds for different reasons: Antonius ledger/contract/task scope, Rinna known/unknown and schedule, Hessa apparatus/evidence/session design, Mara inspection scope, Senn testimony, Deni procedure, Toma physical comparison, Jori fit/room behavior, Saret gate jurisdiction, and Vale account state.
- Shared caution is not automatically Hessa voice. Mara, Hessa, Antonius, auction participants, municipal workers, Jori, Vale staff, road staff, and market sellers can all be cautious for different professional reasons.
- Paid consultation justifies longer specialist speech when explanation itself is the purchased service. Mara's housing and bench summaries are positive evidence.
- Inherited local lore can be longer and messier than institutional procedure. Nera's road memory does not need shrine-book precision to be useful.
- Distributed expertise protects Greg's competence. Greg may possess old-life expertise while current-city routing, law, custody, pricing, maintenance, appraisal, infrastructure, procedure, market access, or local records belong to other people.
- Independent supporting-character life is structural variance. Lyssa's customers and work furniture, market sellers' other buyers, shrine records, road-office procedure, and theatre operations should continue whether Greg is present or not.
- Higher stakes do not require broader dialogue authority.
- First-contact professional scope is stronger than instant Greg calibration.
- Craft anticipation remains legitimate when character knowledge, gaze, cue timing, scene continuity, fit, acoustic behavior, or rehearsal state is the actual variable.
- Craft-specific face observation is legitimate when the craft actually requires looking at expression, pose, shoulder angle, weight, movement, or unconscious facial control.
- Countable visible behavior can support social inference when the evidence is observable and stated.
- Shared game, performance, song-improvisation, lie-game, auction, live-recovery, gate-window, and crowd-routing pressure can legitimately compress speech. Check whether rule ownership, valuation, strategy, temperament, and off-task identities remain distinct.
- Specific technical face-reading can remain when the exact inference is narrow, immediately evidenced, and domain-bound.
- Work-family phrases can spread through direct imitation without becoming generic voice if the manuscript shows where the phrase came from.
- Road friendship can stay dry without becoming Greg-shaped when its nouns remain road nouns.
- Market compression is healthy when valuation lanes differ. Buyers can be terse around the same object while caring about different prices, repair access, documentation, transport, condition, or use.
- Operational compression remains healthy when the stopping point is owned.
- Work continuing can replace curiosity policing. Other people's jobs may simply continue without explaining themselves to Greg.
- Messy systems protect voice. Exceptions may arrive only when encountered; real work need not front-load every rule.
- Repeated work words such as `Wait`, `Good`, `No`, `Again`, `Hold`, `Clear`, and `Less` remain healthy when current object and jurisdiction keep changing what they mean.
- Do not optimize away bad metaphors, incomplete explanations, wrong guesses, local disagreement, silence, awkwardness, underperformance, or practical underexplanation when they belong to the speaker.
- Threat, civic, commercial, domestic, magical, animal, infrastructure, travel, or market pressure does not justify universal mind-reading.
- Hessa asks before explaining, narrows nouns, records before interpreting, and stops when the current question is answered or apparatus/body/session design no longer justifies more attempts.
- Rejecting a spoken speculative claim is not predictive dialogue.
