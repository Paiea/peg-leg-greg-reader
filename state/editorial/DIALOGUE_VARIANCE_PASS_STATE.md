## High-value watch targets

- Greg owns the highest density of compressed dry counterpunches. Supporting characters may be funny, dry, clever, sarcastic, or terse without inheriting his reduction architecture.
- Repeated `No / I didn't say / you were about to`, `you were thinking`, generic `you looked`, and generic `your face` ladders have a high burden after manuscript-wide overdistribution.
- Background and first-contact characters especially should not inherit branded prediction/argument architecture merely because the exchange is funny.
- Consecutive prediction beats deserve a higher burden.
- Wrong predictions can be healthy when the miss is visible and demonstrates fallibility. Greg's own overprediction is especially useful when another person explicitly corrects him.
- Visible sustained attention, stopping, reaching, leaning, handling, affect, or transport geometry can replace generic personality/face shorthand when that physical fact is what another person actually observes.
- Stable cognition with changed wording is positive variance evidence. Relationship knowledge should remain stable while surface performance varies.
- Relationship familiarity should alter workflow and sharpen questions, not grant transcript access. When possible, name the local boundary, object, rule, or shared history rather than the hidden thought.
- Relationship familiarity does not automatically reveal transaction vocabulary. A partner may know Greg wants the object without somehow knowing a deposit, fee, hold term, or other undisclosed mechanism exists.
- Broad relationship shorthand can remain when it names a category rather than an exact hidden sentence. General `artifact face` is lower risk than correctly predicting the seller's specific deposit term.
- Explicit learned borrowing is not accidental leakage when the manuscript marks where the borrowed boundary came from. Antonius's `Depends on the task / I learned` is a model.
- Object-specific preemption can be ordinary care or custody rather than mind-reading. A visible object can itself be the question when history makes the interaction obvious.
- Visible transport geometry should beat predicted request. If the basket will swing from a crutch, let Lyssa name the swing.
- Let Greg actually say the system, schedule, money, transaction, or experiment question when another character's authority is strongest in the answer.
- Scheduling authority should use scheduling language. Scope authority should use scope language. Experiment authority should use protocol language. Market authority should use price, demonstration, hold terms, demand, and condition.
- Jurisdiction transfer is stronger than cognition policing. A gate supervisor should say an incident belongs to road office now rather than announce that Greg is thinking about it.
- Experiment design should make Greg verbalize the desired next test. Hessa is strongest when she denies or permits an explicit proposal from protocol rather than forecasting the proposal.
- Phrase/catchphrase ownership matters. Relationship phrases may recur, but they should not migrate automatically to unrelated speakers.
- Incidental merchants, couriers, gate workers, auction staff, counter staff, municipal workers, temporary counters, and quay staff should joke from transaction state, route permission, merchandise, queue, price, documentation, transfer, returns, mismatch, or safety procedure rather than generic psychology.
- Market sellers should defend price from demonstrated condition, actual demand, available alternatives, and sale terms rather than `you were about to bargain` telepathy.
- Institutional access is a voice-generating lane. Permission to enter, inspect, buy, transfer, return, pass, count, or use an object can shape dialogue without becoming exposition.
- Customer-memory and neighborhood-recognition expertise are real social lanes when provenance is explicit.
- Prefer current evidence, object, role, rule, schedule, contract, damaged equipment, route, body position, records, work history, price, count, or transport geometry over personality commentary.
- Material timing, direct handling rules, object custody, and visible safety conditions are often stronger than predicted questions.
- Predeclared procedure is stronger than predicted intention.
- Information-discipline voices defend thresholds for different reasons: Antonius ledger/contract/task scope, Rinna known/unknown and schedule, Hessa apparatus/evidence/session design, Mara inspection scope, Senn testimony, Deni procedure, Toma physical comparison, Jori fit/room behavior, Saret gate jurisdiction, Vale account state, Merren return-count procedure, and Cala household infrastructure use.

- Paid consultation justifies longer specialist speech when explanation itself is the purchased service. Mara's housing and bench summaries are positive evidence.
- Inherited local lore can be longer and messier than institutional procedure. Nera's road memory does not need shrine-book precision to be useful.
- Distributed expertise protects Greg's competence. Greg may possess old-life expertise while current-city routing, law, custody, pricing, maintenance, appraisal, infrastructure, procedure, market access, local records, household feed conventions, or return-yard rules belong to other people.
- Independent supporting-character life is structural variance. Lyssa's customers and work furniture, market sellers' other buyers, shrine records, road-office procedure, theatre operations, neighborhood exchange, and quay returns should continue whether Greg is present or not.
- Domestic infrastructure can generate voice without exposition. Household feed literacy, backup lamp use, neighbor reserve, and manual work fallback are local knowledge lanes.
