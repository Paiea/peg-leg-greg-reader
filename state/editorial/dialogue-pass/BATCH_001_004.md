## Chapter 3 — THE INVESTOR

**Verdict: 2 ATTRIBUTION REPAIRS, ONE OF THEM MULTI-LINE**

### Fix 3.1 — Antonius office sequence after Arlo's written statement

Stale reader rendering begins:

`"What?" I asked.`

`"Nothing," Pell said.`

Canonical name-mapped form would use `Arlo`, but Arlo is not present in this scene.

Replace the sequence with:

`"What?" Antonius asked.`

`"Nothing," I said.`

`"You keep looking at me," Antonius said.`

`"I have a memorable-face problem," I said.`

`"Your face?" Antonius asked.`

`"Other people's." Antonius held out his hand.`

Reason: the reader rendering contains a stale Arlo/Pell tag even though the scene has moved to Antonius's office, and the first two speakers are reversed. The corrected exchange also adds invisible tags where the scene has crossed a location/interiority boundary, which is exactly where this pass should re-establish speakers.

### Fix 3.2 — predatory financing exchange

Current:

`"That's predatory."`

`"Yes," I said.`

`"Good. I was worried you were losing your touch."`

Replace with:

`"That's predatory," I said.`

`"Yes," Antonius said.`

`"Good. I was worried you were losing your touch," I said.`

Reason: Greg calls the clause predatory; Antonius agrees. The current `I said` assigns Antonius's agreement to Greg and breaks the conversational logic. Tags are useful here because the exchange is fast and both men use dry, compact replies.

### Voice / tagging note

Arlo's dialogue is already somewhat distinct from Greg's through technical/workshop focus, but later editorial work should keep an eye on how often Arlo uses Greg/Hessa-style epistemic language. No additional rewrite is authorized in this chapter during the attribution-only pass.
