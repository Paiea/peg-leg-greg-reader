`"You keep looking at me."`

### Fix 3.2 — predatory financing exchange

Current:

`"That's predatory."`

`"Yes," I said.`

`"Good. I was worried you were losing your touch."`

Replace with:

`"That's predatory," I said.`

`"Yes," Antonius said.`

`"Good. I was worried you were losing your touch," I said.`

Reason: Greg calls the clause predatory; Antonius agrees. The current `I said` assigns Antonius's agreement to Greg and breaks the conversational logic. Tags are useful here because the exchange is fast and both men use dry, compact replies.
