## Chapter 14 - THE ESCORT

**Verdict: REVIEWED - NO ATTRIBUTION CHANGE REQUIRED**

This chapter contains a larger cast than Chapters 11-13, but it handles that cast well because the conversation repeatedly collapses into small local exchanges:
- Greg / Jorren in the yard and on the road;
- Greg / clerk with Jorren explicitly interrupting;
- Greg / Lara while Jorren questions Tavin separately;
- Greg / Jorren / Tavin around the disturbed road patch;
- Mara / Tavin / Greg / Jorren at the yard, with names/actions re-establishing the speaker;
- Greg / Jorren / clerk in the final report, already heavily tagged.

The result remains clear in audio without adding extra tags.

### Voice note

Lara is a good example of why not every competent person needs an analytical voice. She recruits Greg with an object and a task: hold the clamp, move to the other side, drive slowly, get the wagon home. Her expertise is embedded in work rather than explanation.

Jorren also stays distinct from Greg by seeing the road and fight without visibly performing constant analysis. `You keep checking things you've already checked` is a strong peer correction because it comes from field habit, not a theory of evidence.

### Voice note - Alden

Alden already has a strong early fingerprint without manufactured quirks:
- confidence is assumed rather than loudly advertised;
- he accepts useful correction faster than expected;
- he treats initiative as naturally his when no one else has clearly taken it;

Do not make him sound grand merely because Greg remembers a future song.

### Voice note - Jorren versus Alden

Both men are combat-capable and direct, but they should not converge:
- Jorren's competence is practiced, patient, local, and correction-friendly. He often sees the physical habit before Greg builds a theory around it.
- Alden's competence is fast, ambitious, and initiative-seeking. He learns aggressively and then tries to spend the new advantage too soon.

Jorren can be dry without becoming Rusk. Rusk protects the job boundary. Jorren protects the physical truth of the fight and the training lane.
