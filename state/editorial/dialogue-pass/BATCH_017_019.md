### Voice note

This chapter is strong Hessa evidence and should be protected from contamination in both directions.

Hessa's method is specific to teaching and controlled magical experimentation:
- she uses beans because the exercise needs cheap repeated targets;
- she counts casts because Greg does not reliably notice his own volume once interested;
- she narrows `brief structures cost less` to what happened today, at this size, with this shape, against a bean;
- after accepting the Chronoclast disclosure, she does not ask for future trivia. She sets teaching conditions around the body she can actually observe.

Greg can learn this evidence discipline. Other characters should not automatically begin speaking in the same instructional architecture.
