## Chapter 23 - THE AIDE

**Verdict: TARGETED ATTRIBUTION PASS REQUIRED**

The chapter is generally strong because Captain Ressa repeatedly controls the conversational floor. The places worth repairing are transitions where Greg, Alden, and Ressa all use short lines around the same intake table.

### Fix 23.1 - arriving at the blue table

Current:

`"Guild?"`

`Alden showed his Bronze pin. She pointed at me.`

`"Him too?"`

`"I'm visibly here."`

`"That wasn't the question."`

Replace with:

`"Guild?" the woman asked.`

`Alden showed his Bronze pin. She pointed at me.`

`"Him too?" she asked.`

`"I'm visibly here," I said.`

`"That wasn't the question," she said.`

Reason: this is Ressa's entrance, before her name is known. A few plain tags establish her as the intake authority and prevent the clipped exchange from depending on alternating-line arithmetic.

### Fix 23.2 - Ressa's intake priorities

Current:

`Alden said quietly, "You hate this."`

`"What?"`

`"Waiting."`

`"I am excellent at waiting."`

Replace with:

`Alden said quietly, "You hate this."`

`"What?" I asked.`

`"Waiting," Alden said.`

`"I am excellent at waiting," I said.`

Reason: Ressa has just interrupted Greg and remains physically present. Re-establish the private Greg/Alden exchange instead of making the listener infer that Ressa has dropped out.

### Voice note - Captain Ressa

Ressa's competence is **intake triage**, not generic epistemology:
- need first;
- firsthand report second;
- move people before improving their story;
- silence is useful when a witness is actually trying to remember;
- she will accept Greg's useful question without surrendering control of the interview.

Her `Do not interrogate people who need water` is the chapter's cleanest voice rule. Do not turn her into Hessa in a watch coat.

This chapter contains several overlapping dry speakers: Greg, Alden, Antonius's Rusk, Pellor, and a toll clerk. Their voices are already distinct. The pass should make speaker identity easier so those distinctions can carry character rather than bookkeeping.

Combat already uses action to anchor most speech. The risky spots are when Greg, Alden, Cal, and the driver exchange short tactical lines while bodies are moving.

### Fix 25.3 - repair under pressure

Current:

`"Driver?"`

`"Wheel's fine."`

`"Horses?"`

`"Three here. One gone."`

`"Can we move?"`

`"Not with traces like this."`

Replace with:

### Voice note

The driver's voice is a good one-scene example of trade authority: he does not theorize the creatures. He needs traces, wheel, horses, cargo weight, and time. Preserve that narrowness.
