## Book II result through Chapter 114

This batch adds three useful editorial rules.

First, a three-person social scene often only needs a tag at the moment one speaker opens a temporary two-person subexchange. In Chapter 110, `I asked` at the first Jorren question is enough to let Greg/Jorren alternate while Alden remains present.

Second, emergency scenes can remain fast without blanket attribution if each transient worker is physically introduced just before speaking. The tag belongs where **authority** would otherwise be ambiguous, such as the soot-shirt woman answering whether the fire is contained and whether the actor should return to stage.

Third, referent continuity remains part of attribution clarity even when the recurring person has no personal name. The lodging keeper's established female referent must survive comic callbacks and scene transitions just as carefully as a named character's identity.

Voice differentiation also continues by decision type. Pessa asks what physical choice Greg makes when the first plan breaks. Hessa asks which magical control operation the evidence supports and what permission still does not follow. Lyssa asks what happened to the person she cares about and acts on immediate social/material reality without adopting either expert's framework.
