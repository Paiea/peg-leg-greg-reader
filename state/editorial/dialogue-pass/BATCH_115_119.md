### Voice update - Teren

Chapter 116 materially corrects and sharpens Teren's durable theatre authority.

Teren is not best understood as a generic acting-note voice. His demonstrated lane here is **prompting and scene continuity at actor scale**.

He knows the script, but the job is not simply supplying words when silence occurs. He watches whether a particular actor is:
- intentionally pausing;
- still inside the scene;
- breathing or thinking;
- actually unable to continue;
- dealing with a page or rehearsal problem rather than forgetting a line.

The key sequence is person-specific:
- Greg sees a long silence and expects a prompt;
- Teren waits;
- the actor continues;
- later the actor genuinely forgets;
- Teren prompts;
- Greg asks how he knew;
- Teren says `Watch his hand.`
- Greg asks whether that is universal;
- Teren says `No.`
- asked why he mentioned it: `For him.`

That should guide future Teren prose. One useful cue does not become a universal acting rule merely because Greg notices it.

Teren can miss too. He prompts early once and misreads a wife actor's silence as breathing. The work remains human rather than mystical expertise.
