The fruit-seller interruption is the weaker spot. A one-scene stranger immediately enters the same clean counterpunch rhythm as Greg:

`She pointed at a stack of old wrapping cloth.`

`"Take one."`

`"Free?"`

`"You bought nothing."`

Reason: Greg may still produce the compressed joke. The incidental seller does not need to match him beat-for-beat. Her useful function is generosity plus impatience, not comic parity.

## Durable variance finding

The pass should distinguish three kinds of dry dialogue:

1. **Greg-owned analytical compression** — he spots the structural absurdity and returns the shortest useful/funny version.
2. **Earned recurring-character dryness** — Nessa, Marek, Lyssa, and others may be funny because relationship history, professional jurisdiction, or established social rhythm produces it.
3. **Incidental stranger optimization** — the dangerous category. A clerk, seller, courier, or passerby should not automatically arrive with perfect Greg-compatible counterpunch timing unless the scene gives that speaker an independent reason.

The third category is the current high-value watch target.
