## Chapter 6 — THE LABORER

**Verdict: LIGHT DIALOGUE-VARIANCE PATCH REQUIRED**

Rusk is mostly healthy. His shortness is strongly work-shaped: `Cart.`, `Floor.`, `Money.`, pointing instead of explaining, and refusing Greg's attempts to turn one assignment into six. Do not lengthen him merely because he overlaps Greg in line length.

The issue is density. Several consecutive Rusk exchanges turn his operational refusal into a polished comic counterpunch engine. One early beat should be pulled back toward task ownership.

### Patch 6.V1 — opening flour handoff

Current:

`"What is this?"`

`"Flour."`

Replace with:

`"What is this?"`

`"Flour."`

`"I know what flour is."`

`Rusk pointed at the cart.`

`"Then carry it."`

Reason: Rusk stays terse and funny through refusal to dignify Greg's framing, but the humor comes from the job rather than a crafted comeback.

### Patch 6.V2 — Hessa after the warehouse

`"You chose the debt," she said.`

`"I chose several things."`

`"That appears to be the problem."`

Replace with:

`"You chose the debt," she said.`

`"I chose several things."`

`Hessa tapped my wrist.`

`"Then leave them outside this room. Again."`

Reason: the current reply is a clean Greg-compatible aphoristic punchline. Hessa's stronger distinction here is procedural state control under fatigue: Greg is carrying warehouse irritation into mana work, so she returns him to the task.

### Keep

Keep Rusk's later `You bought chalk.` / `That's evidence of frustration.` exchange. It is relationship-earned observation after sustained work together and specifically exposes Greg's behavioral contradiction.

Keep Antonius's `Flour pays.` line. That is a durable scope-discipline fingerprint, not generic wit.

## Durable craft findings

### Shared dryness versus shared machinery

The pass should not ask only whether two characters are both dry. It should ask whether they repeatedly use the same **conversational mechanism**.

Healthy overlap:
- Rusk says little because the assignment is narrow.
- Antonius says little because he controls scope and information.
- Arlo says little when the physical object gives a narrow answer.
- Greg says little because he compresses an absurdity into a joke or analytical reduction.

Unhealthy convergence:
- everybody predicts Greg's question before he asks;
- everybody answers with the same polished `No` rhythm;
- everybody converts uncertainty into a neat one-liner;
- everybody wins a counterpunch before returning to work.

### Silence is variance

A character who is comfortable with silence does not need a verbal comeback. Chapter 9 especially benefits from letting Antonius simply refuse to fill the space.

### Specialists may earn longer lines

A slightly longer specialist answer is not dialogue bloat when the line explains the exact variable that matters. Arlo saying why he cannot price an active matched set is more character-specific than `Unknown.` followed by another joke.
