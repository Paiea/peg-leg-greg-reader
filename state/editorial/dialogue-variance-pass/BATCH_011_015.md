## Durable variance findings

### 1. Safety `No`, job `No`, and comedy `No` are not interchangeable

A short refusal survives when refusal itself is the work:
- Hessa stops an unsafe recast;
- Rusk refuses scope outside the assignment;
- Antonius refuses access to private information.

The same word becomes a problem when several scenes repeatedly use it as the setup for identical punchline ladders.

### 2. Let actions replace rebuttals

For operational characters, moving the crate, shelving the test frame, covering the ledger, taking out rope, or returning to the bench can differentiate better than another clever line.

### 3. Specialists should expose the variable they care about

Lara can name the cracked axle. Arlo can name test condition, winding tension, or live stones. Hessa can name channel cost. That is usually stronger than giving each of them a different flavor of sarcasm.

### 4. Intentional convergence is allowed when the manuscript knows it is convergence

Greg and Alden saying the same thing at the same moment can be valuable because their dangerous compatibility is part of the relationship. The test is whether the echo is narratively noticed and character-relevant, not whether two speakers ever share a line shape.

### 5. Early Book I confirms the main disease is density, not possession

Arlo, Antonius, Rusk, Jorren, Hessa, and Alden are all allowed to be dry. The problem appears when too many consecutive turns require every person to be dry **at Greg's speed**.
