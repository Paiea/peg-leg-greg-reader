## Chapter 16 — THE TEACHER

**Verdict: MODERATE DIALOGUE-VARIANCE PATCH REQUIRED**

The chapter contains intentional overlap among Greg, Jorren, and Alden. That overlap is not automatically a defect. Alden is specifically valuable because he can absorb Greg's correction quickly, answer with confidence, and push the exchange forward. Jorren is the stabilizer: he notices the body, proves the point in the spar, and should be less interested in extending every verbal bit.

The variance problem appears when Jorren repeatedly joins the same face-reading / comeback machinery as Greg and Alden instead of returning to the physical exchange.

### Patch 16.V1 — Greg turns Alden into a model

Current:

`"That face. Stop."`

`"Stop."`

`"What?"`

`"You stopped watching his feet."`

`I looked at him.`

`"You do that when you start building the whole person in your head."`

Reason: Jorren still recognizes Greg's habit, but from the physical thing he can actually see: Greg stopped observing the fight and began theorizing the fighter.

## Chapter 18 — THE EXPERIMENTER

**Verdict: VARIANCE REVIEWED — NO CHANGE REQUIRED**

This chapter survives the new rules strongly.

Hessa is clipped, but the clipping is doing actual experimental work:
- she counts casts Greg stops counting;
- she distinguishes what happened from what Greg thinks caused it;
- she narrows `brief structures cost less` to today, this shape, this size, this target;
- she changes the exercise from `solve Barrier` to `move the bean one finger`;
- she stops the session when Greg's interest masks fatigue.

Her occasional dry lines do not dominate the scene because they are embedded inside a method and frequently followed by silence, observation, or another bounded instruction.

### Important distinction

`That is not the same sentence` is Hessa-shaped here because the distinction is the work: Greg knows the hook moved, but does not yet know exactly what magical action produced the result.

`Excellent. You have discovered size.` is sharper and more comic, but it is isolated rather than the chapter's governing rhythm. Do not purge every Hessa joke merely because it lands cleanly.

The bean exercise also contains productive imperfection on Greg's side: excitement, lost count, overgeneralization, memory gaps, and the urge to solve the whole spell instead of the assigned movement.

No new attribution repair identified.

## New craft findings

### Relationship-owned convergence can increase while cast-wide convergence decreases

Greg and Alden are allowed to become more compatible as their relationship develops. That compatibility becomes meaningful precisely because Jorren, Hessa, Edrin, clerks, specialists, and incidental workers are not all matching them at the same frequency.

### Role authority should sound like responsibility, not universal cleverness

A useful discriminator for specialists and institutional workers:
- clerk: assignment, custody, schedule, contract;
- Edrin: seal state, access, test condition, ward responsibility;
- quartermaster: people out, sacks moved, roof danger;
- Jorren: feet, distance, fatigue, usable ground.

If all four instead say `No` before Greg asks and explain it with a neat joke about his face, the role has disappeared beneath house voice.

### Repeated face-reading is now a specific watch target

One person learning Greg's tells is relationship evidence. Everyone learning `the face` is project-wide shorthand. Preserve it where earned, but stop using it as the default mechanism by which new competent adults prove they understand Greg.
