## Durable craft findings

### 1. Familiarity does not excuse repeated machinery

A recurring professional may genuinely learn Greg's habits, but the same exact predictive-refusal joke should not recur every time Greg walks back into the room.

Relationship continuity is stronger when familiarity changes the workflow:
- healer starts with the wrist tag;
- clerk already knows which contract changed;
- Jorren notices the physical tell relevant to training;
- Alden notices room behavior because he was actually nearby.

### 2. Cross-domain disagreement is better than unified cleverness

Kellan and the butcher are a positive reference. Both are competent, both are terse, and both can be funny, but they do not think from the same hierarchy of evidence.

Let specialists disagree because their jobs privilege different truths.

### 3. Emergency procedure can create natural shortness

Iris does not need extra verbal quirks. Rescue work already gives her clipped language through role assignment, threat timing, route control, and usable information.

Do not lengthen procedural speech merely to force variance when responsibility already differentiates it.

### 4. Relationship-earned perceptiveness is distinct from universal mind-reading

Alden noticing Greg's chair placement, windowsill check, combat mismatch, or self-deprecating misdirection is earned by proximity and attention.

A stranger diagnosing the same things in their first thirty seconds remains high-risk convergence.

### 5. Ordinary family argument is valuable voice texture

Marden's mule, Fera/Noll's roof fight, Sava/Osric's sister problem, and Leni's wizard questions all resist the project's analytical house voice without requiring stylized dialect or gimmicks.

Protect ordinary irrational attachment, old grievances, literal child logic, and family shorthand.
