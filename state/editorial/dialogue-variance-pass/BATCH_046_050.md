## Durable craft findings

### 1. Secondhand reputation should stay lower resolution than lived relationship

It is fine for friends, siblings, coworkers, and customers to talk about Greg when he is absent. That makes the city social.

But a new character receiving secondhand information should usually get a rough trait, not a perfect compressed diagnosis.

Healthy:
- `Octavia said you stare.`
- `Sevren said you ask a lot of questions.`

Riskier:
- a stranger already knowing exactly what Greg's stare means, what question comes next, and how to beat his framing.

### 2. A recurring relationship can keep dry chemistry without keeping the same shorthand

Jorren and Greg are allowed fast, dry exchanges. Removing `face` does not mean slowing their relationship down. Jorren can know Greg through money-counting, stopped attention, stance, soreness, appetite, and concrete habits.

### 3. Workshop authority is strongest as sequencing and capacity

Vessa is most distinct when she says what the shop can safely do now: glaze firing, burner boundary, cooling rack, support, production changes, tomorrow's hot-line slot.

Arlo is most distinct when he decides what mechanism question is worth the next fixture change.

Neither needs repeated predictive banter to prove familiarity with Greg.

### 4. No-change chapters are evidence, not missed opportunities

Chapter 47 demonstrates the engine working without intervention. Sevren is dry and efficient, but his unit of thought is route, arrival, custody, current ground, and whether the form actually requires an answer.

Do not patch a chapter merely to maintain a quota.

### 5. Productive disagreement can remain mild

Greg and Sevren disagree about planning the stove path versus solving obstructions as they appear. Neither needs to become the designated correct philosophy. The door comes off, the stove moves, and both methods contributed.
