## Durable craft findings

### 1. Relationship similarity is safe when desire differs

Sevren and Alden can both be dry peers because they want different things from Greg. Sevren wants company, food, routes, and an ordinary evening. Alden wants challenge, training, physical proof, and another attempt.

Distinct motive can carry voice even when sentence length overlaps.

### 2. Professional scope can be a stronger fingerprint than diction

Mara Fen, Lerris, Edrin, Sera, and the keeper all say short practical things, but each protects a different object: count, structure, evidence, recovery, household function.

Do not force decorative speech differences when responsibility already separates them.

### 3. Teasing should attach to remembered behavior

Nerin teasing Greg for counting stair repetitions is stronger than `Face`. Tam teasing Greg about salvaging the boot is stronger when Tam names what leather is actually usable.

Specific remembered behavior makes familiarity feel accumulated.

### 4. Let mundane competence stay mundane

The cinnamon discrepancy can be a tare problem. A carpenter can install a rail. A friend can play cards for an hour. A clerk can let another person take a job. The manuscript does not need to convert every ordinary solution into a revelation or joke exchange.

### 5. Significant emotional progress is stronger when bystanders keep their own priorities

The keeper still needs the stairs. Jorren still has work. Nerin has patients. Sera will not leave the infirmary for a ceremonial homecoming. The city continuing normally makes Greg's private milestones feel more real.
