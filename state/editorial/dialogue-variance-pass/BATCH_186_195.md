## Durable findings added by this batch

1. **Self-aware narration can be a convergence alarm.** `Everyone keeps accusing me of looking` and `the entire city had agreed on one sentence` are reasons to inspect the surrounding mechanism, not automatic permission to keep repeating it.
2. **The protagonist can verbalize the impulse that a close relationship then challenges.** This preserves intimacy without requiring the partner to narrate an exact unsaid plan.
3. **Possessive misunderstanding is enough.** A friend can say `Mine` / `Still mine` without proving Greg's gaze meant he intended to steal the food.
4. **Craft authority can answer the object instead of the abstract question.** `Not this one` / `It answers the cloth` lets Jessa remain materially competent without turning into a color-theory lecturer or Greg mind-reader.
5. **Let Greg own the final reduction when retiring a shared catchphrase.** Replacing a city-wide `That wasn't praise` with a stranger-specific answer and Greg's `shelves were not aspirational` restores comic gravity without flattening the scene.
6. **Wrong predictions remain useful.** Greg mispredicting Lyssa's parcel question in Chapter 187 and Pell making low-stakes guesses about food preserve fallibility and should not be cleaned away.
7. **Observation remains legitimate when attention is the craft variable.** Chapter 191's purse problem is performance evidence, not the face-reading disease.
8. **Name casing is continuity, not variance.** Once `Evin` is source-settled, later reviewed `EvIn` drift should be normalized separately rather than treated as a voice edit.
