## Durable findings added by this batch

1. **A self-aware convergence alarm gets more weight after the pass has already identified the mechanism.** Once the manuscript itself says everyone keeps accusing Greg of looking, another identical ladder should have a high burden to justify itself.
2. **Schedule authority can answer behavior without diagnosing intent.** `Third bell` followed by `Then eat` is enough when the relationship already shares the schedule.
3. **Craft disagreement improves when both people state what they actually know.** Greg can say `It looks the same`; Lyssa can answer `It isn't` and explain the stitch consequence without either narrating the other's hidden sentence.
4. **Front-house authority cares about timely usefulness.** `Then say it before I do` / `Thinking sells nothing` is more Rinna-specific than denying whether Greg privately had the answer.
5. **Wrong prediction is positive evidence when the miss remains visible.** Rinna assuming Greg's raised hand is about smoke when he asks about food is healthier than making her perfectly calibrated.
6. **Contract provenance can replace predictive prohibition.** `Havel. Nobody uses it.` expresses ownership, customer risk, and job boundary in one local rule.
7. **Damaged-object custody can replace gaze interpretation.** Davin holding up the bent tin and saying `Leave this one` keeps repair authority physical.
8. **Marked contamination can stay.** Greg borrowing Hessa language is useful characterization when the narration notices it as contamination and Greg self-corrects instead of the whole cast silently adopting the register.
9. **Face/body observation remains legitimate when the body is part of the apparatus.** Hessa can regulate jaw, posture, shoulder, breath, and visible effort during tiny-motion testing without this becoming generic mind-reading.
10. **No-change work pressure remains positive evidence.** Scheduling, private-contract constraints, current-version changes, repair custody, and cramped venue traffic naturally separate voices without adding quirks.
