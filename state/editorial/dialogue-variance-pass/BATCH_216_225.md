## Durable findings added by this batch

1. **Catchphrase ownership matters.** A relationship-specific label can be excellent when it belongs to the relationship that created it and weak when it migrates to unrelated speakers.
2. **Stable cognition should still vary in surface performance.** Lyssa can recognize Greg's money-brain repeatedly without saying `money face` every time.
3. **Self-aware `predictive management` is a convergence alarm, not automatic permission.** If the narration jokes about the repeated mechanism, inspect whether the exchange can simply state the topic.
4. **Visible operational changes usually need less dialogue.** A moved cash box or hidden ledger can be noticed and confirmed without a prediction duel.
5. **Volunteering a denial can be healthy protagonist inference.** Greg can infer what another person may reasonably fear he will do and say `I'm not following him`; that is different from the other character asserting his exact unsaid plan.
6. **Craft anticipation remains legitimate when character knowledge is the variable.** Teren correcting Greg for reaching toward a future receipt or moving early for another actor is specific acting work, not generic mind-reading.
