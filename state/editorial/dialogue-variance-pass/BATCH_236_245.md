## Durable findings added by this batch

1. **Wrong quantity prediction can be social shorthand without becoming mind-reading.** A seller can know Greg's buying habit and still be contradicted by what he says he intended today.
2. **Repeated work words are safest when the current object keeps changing their meaning.** `Wait`, `Good`, `No`, and `Less` remain healthy when they route wax, customers, shoes, props, paint, or evidence rather than personalities.
3. **Actor foreknowledge is a legitimate face/attention problem.** Teren may correct where Greg looks when the actor knows something the character does not.
4. **Procedural exceptions can arrive only when encountered.** A queue can be `order unless called`; a house mark can have a route exception; a regular customer can know her own terms. Real systems need not front-load every rule.
5. **Self-aware convergence alarms still deserve surgery even late in the pass.** `Apparently everyone in Carrow had learned this technique` is not enough to justify replaying the same prediction ladder once a stronger character-specific boundary is available.
