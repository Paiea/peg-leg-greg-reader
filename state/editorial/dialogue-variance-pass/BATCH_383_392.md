## Durable findings added by this batch

1. **Post-production first-person drift is a separate regression class.** New chapters can preserve speaker clarity while slipping into editorial third-person references such as `Greg watched`, `Greg gave`, or `Greg and Orin carried`. The variance sweep must keep checking narrator referents after the older attribution-pass edge.
2. **Crew familiarity should be earned asymmetrically.** Sori and Dask may predict each other's recurring work habits after accumulated history. A new seller, camp keeper, or licensing worker should not instantly inherit the same exact prediction ladder.
3. **Visible professional explanation beats facial transcript.** Pella can explain why a shoulder yoke is a poor match with crutches; Dema can tell Greg not to stop at his own harness inspection; Maro can explain residue because Greg is visibly watching it.
4. **Stress should change speech quality.** The trapped porter repeats himself. Sori swears at a bad closure template. Orin's beautiful handwriting degrades after nine bells. Maro becomes directive under live river-light pressure. These are strong human-variance models.
5. **Long speech continues to separate cognition.** Sori's work likes/dislikes, Orin's embarrassed money/job confession, Baret's harness explanations, Nessa's route detail, and Vessa's field requirements are all long for different reasons.
6. **Greg's old S-class competence can compress under state without becoming default narration.** His cart-load coordination in Chapter 389 is a legitimate lock-in mode because the problem requires it. He explicitly does not run that mode all day because attention and social cost matter.
7. **Following is a competence state.** Chapters 390-392 repeatedly show Greg benefiting from local route readers, road readers, camp keepers, Maro, Vessa, Nessa, and Toren rather than proving himself by replacing them.
8. **Same-name authority remains separate.** The Alder Basin field reader Nessa is not automatically the same identity as existing Carrow Nessa. Toren Ash is not Kel Toren. Repeated Pell/Vale/Rell/Toren/Nessa names are not destiny evidence.
9. **No-change chapters remain positive evidence.** The field crew is diversifying naturally through responsibility, fatigue, travel, rivalry, local knowledge, and accumulated familiarity.
