2. **Greg may still own the branded face inversion.** When Dask openly lights up over survey benchmarks and immediately declares he is coming, Greg's `you made face` joke remains Greg-shaped rather than leaked supporting-cast architecture.
3. **Short acquaintance should not instantly create named relationship categories.** Enna can see Greg stare at the Merehook sign, but `road face` is better reserved for a relationship that has actually accumulated that shorthand.
4. **Authority language is stronger than thought prediction.** Enna can simply tell Halen not to hand Greg the staff rather than announce what Halen was thinking.
5. **Object-directed gaze can carry silent intent.** Jorren looking from a repaired shoulder to the test wagon is enough for Enna to reject an immediate second pass.
6. **Terse ensemble speech remains healthy when jurisdiction differs.** Survey, carrier, village, crib, field-command, and recordkeeping voices all compress differently under weather and road pressure.
7. **Procedural learning should stay imperfect.** Jorren can catch himself before overclaiming without becoming converted into a calm evidence technician.
8. **Craft voices can be funny without becoming Greg.** Toll's dry lines come from timber, access, rain, and being interrupted by people asking impossible timing questions.
9. **Productive restraint is part of voice.** Greg choosing not to use the trial limb on bad surfaces is not passivity; it reflects the newer baseline-not-verdict mindset.
10. **First-person drift is now sparse but still present.** Chapter 430 contains three plain external self-references that should return to `I/me` narration.
