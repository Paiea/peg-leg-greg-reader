# PLG EXCHANGE RHYTHM GATE

Status: ACTIVE SECOND-PASS CHECK

Use after normal line-level dialogue variance review.

A scene can contain individually valid character lines and still fail at the exchange level because several speakers perform the same short, dry, optimized rhythm.

## Gate

For each substantial multi-turn exchange, answer:

1. Who initiates the rhythm?
2. Who extends it?
3. Who gets the cleanest final button?
4. Who exits through work, action, silence, uncertainty, misunderstanding, or emotional reaction?
5. Could several supporting lines be swapped without changing the social texture?

## Collision flags

Flag for review when:

- three or more consecutive turns are similarly shaped compact counters;
- Greg starts a dry bit and more than one supporting character improves or finishes it;
- two unrelated professionals manage Greg with the same predictive joke machinery;
- the specialist's domain offers a natural exit but the prose keeps choosing another comeback;
- the same response architecture repeats in neighboring scenes with different characters.

A flag is not an automatic rewrite.

## Preferred repair order

1. Preserve scene intent and canon.
2. Preserve the strongest character-owned line.
3. Change one supporting response mode rather than changing everybody.
4. Prefer domain action, silence, partial answer, direct boundary, physical consequence, or a longer necessary explanation.
5. Keep Greg's strongest compressed comic move when the scene supports it.
6. Keep Alden capable of matching Greg when initiative and competitive chemistry earn it.

## Pass condition

The exchange passes when different people can remain terse, competent, and funny without all behaving like equally optimized versions of Greg.

Do not force maximum contrast. Seek asymmetric participation.
