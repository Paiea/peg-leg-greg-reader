# PLG READER-EAR DIALOGUE AUDIT

Status: ACTIVE AUDIT TEMPLATE

Use after a completed dialogue-variance span, normally every 10 to 20 chapters.

This is not another line-by-line voice pass. Read continuously and listen for cumulative rhythm.

## Audit questions

### Greg gravity

- Does Greg still feel like the main owner of compressed dry comic reduction?
- Are his jokes stronger because other people sometimes refuse, miss, delay, literalize, or redirect them?
- Is Greg himself varying under fatigue, embarrassment, fear, affection, anger, or genuine stakes?

### Cast convergence

- Which recurring characters begin to feel interchangeable in cadence?
- Which pairs repeatedly use the same correction or refusal architecture?
- Are several specialists funny in exactly the same way?
- Are incidental strangers too quickly calibrated to Greg?

### Repeated machinery

Count patterns by feel, not by mechanical quota:

- predictive `No`;
- face or hidden-intention reading;
- `you problem` style transferables;
- short correction followed by sharper counter followed by final button;
- generic epistemic language that could belong to Greg or Hessa;
- competent authority figures who all become excellent straight-faced comedians.

### Character-owned exits

Check whether important speakers still know how to leave a bit in their own way:

- Antonius returns to decision, borrower, ledger, inventory, or work consequence.
- Arlo returns to the object, test, tool, time cost, or authorship.
- Hessa returns to procedure, evidence, body state, permission, or silence.
- Jorren lets the body, spar, road, fatigue, or ordinary action answer.
- Alden converts information into initiative, testing, or the next opening.
- Greg often keeps the wording alive longer than the others.

## Output

For each audit span record:

- **Overall cadence:** DISTINCT / MILD CONVERGENCE / MATERIAL CONVERGENCE
- **Most distinct voices:** names and why
- **Collision pairs:** names and repeated architecture
- **High-value repair candidates:** only exchanges noticeable at normal reading speed
- **No-change evidence:** scenes where shared terseness remains healthy
- **Overcorrection watch:** anyone becoming gimmicky, unnaturally verbose, or artificially incapable of wit

## Patch rule

Do not patch to meet a quota.

If the reader-ear problem is mild, change only the smallest number of high-leverage exchanges needed to restore asymmetry.

If the audit passes, save the pass as positive evidence and move on.
