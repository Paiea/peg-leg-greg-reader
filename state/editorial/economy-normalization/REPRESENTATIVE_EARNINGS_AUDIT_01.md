# REPRESENTATIVE EARNINGS AUDIT 01

Status: **AUDITED / PATCHES NOT YET APPLIED**

Purpose: sample later earning scenes against the approved value/risk pay engine and identify where the manuscript should preserve copper, move into silver, move into tens of silver, or expose gold-scale value.

This is not a conversion table. Recommended bands are editorial calibration targets. Exact replacements must be reconciled with neighboring purse arithmetic and any later references before prose is patched.

## Executive finding

The later manuscript has a real compensation compression problem.

The strongest issue is not that every copper job is wrong. It is that several economically different products are being paid in the same tiny band:

- ordinary clerical labor;
- independent commercial witness work;
- brokerage;
- gray freight participation;
- heavy-yard coordination;
- four-day multi-crew organizing;
- urgent one-day receiving-house coordination.

Those should not all live between roughly 1c and 14c.

The correct repair is a widening ladder, not blanket inflation.

Three correction types appear repeatedly:

1. **KEEP / REFRAME** — the work really is cheap or token; preserve amount and reduce milestone language.
2. **RAISE GREG'S FEE** — underlying commercial scale can survive, but Greg's scarce/risk-bearing contribution is underpaid.
3. **RAISE WHOLE TRANSACTION** — the client-side value is itself too small to support the risk, commission, or specialist contribution. Raise underlying deal value and Greg's compensation together.

## Calibration baseline

Early authority already contains a decisive contrast:

- generic Bronze/manual work can pay copper;
- Antonius pays Greg **6 silver** for the gauntlet appraisal because his judgment affects a valuable lending decision;
- Arlo testing costs **6 silver**;
- Greg gambles and spends at silver scale;
- Vale already handles gold-scale commercial decisions.

Therefore later skilled/rare/high-risk work paying less than ordinary early specialist judgment requires an explicit reason.

## Detailed gray / criminal freight finding

### Ch473 is under-scaled at the transaction level

Current scene logic:
- legal ride to Cinder Cross costs 5c;
- seller expects roughly 1s for whole mixed load;
- two route-hardware lots lack export release;
- seizure/hold/fine/possible charge are explicit risks;
- seller knowingly uses an incomplete category at departure;
- Greg knowingly participates;
- Greg also provides Three Rays screening and helps restructure the transaction when buyer reprices the paper risk;
- driver initially receives normal day rate and Greg himself objects that the risk premium is irrational.

The scene already understands the economics conceptually.

The numbers betray it.

If the entire load is worth only 1s, then meaningful risk premium for seller, driver, and Greg quickly consumes the transaction. Therefore the cleaner repair is to raise the **underlying freight value**, not merely Greg's five-copper fee.

### Recommended Ch473 posture

Preserve the scene as **small-to-medium gray freight**, not major organized contraband.

That means:
- legally owned surplus remains morally gray rather than sinister;
- missing export release remains real legal exposure;
- Greg's role remains broker/screener/rider, not smuggling mastermind;
- no hidden compartments, violence, or organized-crime escalation is required.

Recommended scale:
- whole load: tens of silver rather than 1s;
- Greg: several silver to low double-digit silver;
- driver: real several-silver risk premium;
- seller retains enough margin that violating the rule is economically legible.

Only move Greg toward **30s+ on Ch473 itself** if the underlying cargo is also upgraded toward low-gold value and the legal consequences are made correspondingly more serious. Otherwise 30s+ would make Greg's cut disproportionate to his role.

### Where 30s+ should live

Use **30s+ Greg compensation** for later or stronger cases where several of these stack:
- valuable contraband or high-value controlled artifacts;
- deliberate concealment rather than incomplete categorization;
- meaningful seizure/arrest/blacklist risk;
- criminal counterparties or retaliation risk;
- Greg is difficult to replace because of route, appraisal, trust, or coordination knowledge;
- underlying transaction is comfortably gold-scale or otherwise much larger than his fee;
- Greg's legal alternatives make low pay irrational.

### Gold-scale Greg pay

No sampled current scene should be blindly changed to gold pay.

Gold compensation should appear when Greg's own cut is supported by a genuinely large operation, for example:
- rare artifact movement/resale;
- major contraband shipment;
- organized high-risk logistics;
- specialist judgment unlocking or protecting a large gold-scale position;
- ownership/capital exposure where Greg takes real downside.

The current sampled scenes mostly need a move from copper into silver and from silver into tens of silver. **Gold should remain occasional and earned.**

## Current ladder implied by the sample

### Copper should remain
Use for:
- manual labor;
- token theatre pay;
- cheap errands;
- ordinary clerical comparison;
- incidental short work;
- small social payments.

### Low silver should appear much earlier and more often
Use for:
- independent witness work;
- bounded specialist review;
- useful sourcing commissions;
- half-day coordination where failure costs real money;
- commercial record work requiring earned trust.

### Mid / high silver should become normal for leverage
Use for:
- meaningful brokerage;
- urgent commercial coordination;
- specialist artifact/provenance work;
- valuable one-day contracting;
- gray freight where legal risk is real but operation is not major.

### Tens of silver should now be visible
Use for:
- multi-day multi-crew organizer work;
- serious specialist brokerage;
- meaningful illicit logistics;
- high-value commissions;
- larger management/coordination responsibilities;
- dangerous or institutionally exposed jobs where Greg has legal alternatives.

### Gold should be occasional
Use for:
- major artifact / contraband / capital operations;
- genuinely large underlying transactions;
- ownership stakes or specialist decisions protecting gold-scale positions.

Do not manufacture gold pay merely to show progression.

## Bottom line

The manuscript should not be normalized into a world where every later job pays more.
