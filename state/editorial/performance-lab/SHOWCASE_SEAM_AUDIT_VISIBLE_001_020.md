## Purpose

The curated reader can hide whole canon chapters while preserving them as canon. That makes visible-to-visible continuity a separate editorial concern from canon-to-canon continuity.

This audit checks all 19 handoffs in the displayed first 20 chapters for:

- missing setup or antecedent
- dangling setup that resolves only in hidden canon
- duplicated recap
- unexplained time, location, cast, object, or goal changes
- knowledge that appears to have been acquired offscreen
- the reader sensation of having accidentally skipped a page or chapter

A seam patch is justified only when the visible sequence needs one. Hidden canon is not restored merely to make a transition literal.

## Patch specification: canon 004 / displayed Ch 3

Current visible problem:

- displayed Ch 2 establishes the shale as a planned test/investment
- hidden canon 003 performs the first experiments
- displayed Ch 3 then says `sixth disk` and `twenty-percent improvement` as if the visible reader watched those tests

The patch should preserve the current financial reasoning while making the hidden work legible in one sentence. It must not summarize canon 003 wholesale.

Target paragraph concept:

```text
The room did not object. The shale project had become the most dangerous kind of thing: promising. The sixth disk had barely beaten the control; by the end of the night, Arlo's best result was closer to twenty percent. Failure would have been cleaner. If that result had collapsed on replication, the project could die with dignity. Instead we had a path toward better tests, and no idea whether the final product would take two weeks or two years.
```

This preserves the actual hidden-canon evidence: the sixth sample first produced a measurable result, Arlo replicated it, and later tests improved the result to closer to twenty percent. It also removes the feeling that `sixth disk` and `twenty percent` arrived from nowhere.
