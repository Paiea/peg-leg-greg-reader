## Aggregate findings

### What PERFORMANCE improved

- It was most useful where current prose contained individually good lines but too much shared optimized exchange architecture.
- It gave operational characters permission to answer with their actual work: Antonius with inventory/ownership, Arlo with the fixture/notebook/object, Hessa with procedure and access control, Jorren with physical consequence.
- It preserved Greg as the primary extender and interpreter rather than solving convergence by making him less Greg-like.
- It made state/self-presentation useful when state changed observable behavior, without forcing every known feeling onto the page.
- It exposed clean scene shifts, especially Antonius moving from cleanup to valuation after Greg's value claim.

### What PERFORMANCE did not improve automatically

- The Ch 2 loan scene already has strong curiosity -> transaction -> suspicion -> persona-shift behavior. The performed script helps asymmetry but does not clearly replace the source's handling.
- The Ch 12 training scene already has strong human topology: Alden learns, succeeds, overapplies, gets punished, and keeps learning. PERFORMANCE mostly clarifies role ownership rather than discovering a better route.
- This is positive falsification evidence: the layer did not require every source scene to be rewritten or made messier.

### Failure checks

- Dramatic-truth failures: 0
- Scenes clearly stronger: 3
- Scenes worse / flattened: 0
- Systematic loss of Greg interiority: not observed at performed-script level
- Systematic loss of mundane/domain texture: not observed
- Quota-driven misunderstanding or mess: not used

## Decision

**PROMOTE TO NOVELIZATION DESIGN**

The lab meets the design's promotion threshold:

- zero dramatic-truth failures;
- at least three `CLEARLY STRONGER` scenes;
- no `WORSE / FLATTENED` scenes;
- no systematic loss of Greg interiority, mundane texture, or domain-specific behavior at the script level.

This decision recommends only a separately designed five-scene novelization comparison.

## Hard stop

This lab does **not** authorize:

- novelizing these scenes;
- changing canonical chapter prose;
- merging PERFORMANCE into the forward Manuscript Engine;
- promoting temporary frames into character authority;
- treating the performed scripts as canon.

The experiment stops at performed-script evaluation.
