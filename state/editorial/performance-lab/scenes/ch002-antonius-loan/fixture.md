## SOURCE EXCERPT

"Have we met?" Antonius asked.

"No," I said.

"You are staring."

"I'm adjusting."

"To what?"

"Your age," I said.

Antonius smiled. "How old did you expect me to be?"

"Richer."

That got me inside.

"What do you want?" he asked.

"A loan," I said.

"How much?" Antonius asked.

I named the offensive number.

The man with the scar laughed.

Antonius looked at my Bronze plate. "Collateral?"

"No," I said.

"Property?" he asked.

"No," I said.

"Guarantor?" Antonius continued.

"No," I said.

"Family?" he asked.

"Dead," I said.

"Useful friends?" Antonius asked.

"Not yet," I said.

"What do you have?" Antonius asked.

"Information," I said. I gave him the Kestrin argument. Present facts only. Guild salvage price. Material properties. Arwick's willingness to test it. What the test would cost.

"You're asking for twenty times that," Antonius said.

"Yes," I said.

"Why?" Antonius asked.

"Because if it works, I don't want to come back tomorrow."

His man laughed again.

Antonius did not. "How does a nineteen-year-old Bronze know this?"

"If I had an answer you'd believe, I wouldn't need your money."

He denied the large loan. Of course he did. Old Greg's reputation did not exist. Charisma was not collateral. So I changed roles.

"Smaller loan," I said. "Enough for the shale, the test, food, and guild fees. I repay principal plus twenty percent in ten days."

"Forty."

"Twenty-five."

"Forty."

"Thirty, and if I fail, I work contracts through you until the balance is cleared."

His expression changed.

"Thirty-five," Antonius said. "Eight days."

"Nine."

"Eight."

"Fine," I said.

Antonius counted silver onto the desk.

"If you run," Antonius said, "I find you."

"I know," I said.

His hand stopped. Too fast.

"How?"

Shit. I let some uncertainty into my face. Nineteen-year-old uncertainty. A boy pretending he had more control than he did.

"I asked around."

I had shifted before deciding to. Confident expert to nervous boy because nervous boy was safer. Voice slightly smaller. Shoulders less certain. Let Antonius feel older, sharper, more in control.

Antonius pushed the coins toward me. "Eight days, Greg."

I picked them up.

"You're going to remember me."

"I remember everyone who owes me money."

"No. I mean later."

His eyes narrowed. I should have stopped. Apparently being nineteen again had restored my need for an audience too. I grinned. "Eight days."

## DRAMATIC SCRIPT

### LOCKED
- Antonius makes the loan decision.
- Greg voluntarily offers future contract labor if he fails.
- Antonius settles the deal at thirty-five percent and eight days.
- Greg reveals suspiciously immediate knowledge that Antonius can find him if he runs.
- Greg deliberately performs a younger, less certain version of himself after being caught.

### REQUIRED
- Antonius learns Greg has no conventional collateral and is instead offering information plus future labor.
- Greg understands that present reputation does not support the large loan.
- Antonius becomes more suspicious after Greg says he already knows Antonius can find him.

### MUST NOT DRIFT
- Antonius does not know Greg's time-displacement secret.
- Greg receives the silver.
- No new debt terms or collateral are invented.
- Antonius owns the final decision; Greg owns his persona shift and interior interpretation.

### STATE IN
- Greg is excited, financially pressured, overconfident, and fascinated by young Antonius.
- Antonius is evaluating an unusual unknown Bronze from a position of leverage.

### STATE OUT
- Greg has the loan and eight-day deadline.
- Antonius is watching Greg more closely.

### ENVIRONMENT
- Antonius's cheap current office/storage room.
- Antonius, Greg, and Antonius's scarred man are present.
- Money, Bronze status, and credibility are immediate pressures.
