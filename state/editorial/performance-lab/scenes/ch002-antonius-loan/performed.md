# Ch 2 Antonius Loan Performed Script

## PERFORMANCE FRAMES

### GREG
STATE: Financially pressured, excited by leverage, and entertained by seeing young Antonius.
PERFORMED STANCE: Confident oddball who expects his plan to be interesting enough to finance.
ATTENTION: Get enough capital to avoid returning tomorrow while hiding why he knows Antonius.
BASELINE BEND: material embarrassment makes Greg deliberately shrink his social presentation when his hidden knowledge leaks.
REALIZED BY: B039, B040

### ANTONIUS
STATE: Curious about an implausibly composed Bronze, then increasingly alert to unexplained competence.
PERFORMED STANCE: Amused appraisal that becomes transactional and then suspicious.
ATTENTION: Price the actual risk and find what Greg can truly pledge.
BASELINE BEND: material curiosity gives Greg more runway than a normal unsecured borrower, but suspicion abruptly narrows that runway once Greg reveals knowledge he should not have.
REALIZED BY: B003, B008, B037, B041

## PERFORMED SCRIPT

[B001] ANTONIUS -> GREG [DIALOGUE]
"Have we met?"

[B002] GREG -> ANTONIUS [DIALOGUE]
"No."

[B003] ANTONIUS [ACTION]
Lets the answer sit, still looking at Greg instead of moving immediately to business.

[B004] ANTONIUS -> GREG [DIALOGUE]
"Then why are you staring?"

[B005] GREG -> ANTONIUS [DIALOGUE]
"I'm adjusting to your age."

[B006] ANTONIUS -> GREG [DIALOGUE]
"How old did you expect?"

[B007] GREG -> ANTONIUS [DIALOGUE]
"Richer."

[B008] ANTONIUS [ACTION]
Smiles once and motions Greg farther into the storage-room office.

[B009] ANTONIUS -> GREG [DIALOGUE]
"What do you want?"

[B010] GREG -> ANTONIUS [DIALOGUE]
"A loan."

[B011] ANTONIUS [ACTION]
Looks from Greg's face to the Bronze plate before asking anything else.

[B012] ANTONIUS -> GREG [DIALOGUE]
"How much?"

[B013] GREG [ACTION]
Names the large amount.

[B014] VALE MAN [ACTION]
Laughs.

[B015] ANTONIUS -> GREG [DIALOGUE]
"Collateral?"

[B016] GREG -> ANTONIUS [DIALOGUE]
"No."

[B017] ANTONIUS -> GREG [DIALOGUE]
"Property? Guarantor?"

[B018] GREG -> ANTONIUS [DIALOGUE]
"No."

[B019] ANTONIUS -> GREG [DIALOGUE]
"Family?"

[B020] GREG -> ANTONIUS [DIALOGUE]
"Dead."

[B021] ANTONIUS [ACTION]
Does not apologize or fill the silence.

[B022] ANTONIUS -> GREG [DIALOGUE]
"What do you actually have?"

[B023] GREG -> ANTONIUS [DIALOGUE]
"Information."

[B024] GREG [ACTION]
Lays out the present-day Kestrin facts, Arlo's willingness to test them, and the test cost.

[B025] ANTONIUS -> GREG [DIALOGUE]
"That explains the test. Not the rest of the money."

[B026] GREG -> ANTONIUS [DIALOGUE]
"If it works, I don't want to come back tomorrow."

[B027] ANTONIUS [ACTION]
Taps one finger against the desk, then refuses the large loan without debating Greg's theory.

[B028] GREG [THOUGHT]
Right. Present me had no reputation to borrow against. Different role.

[B029] GREG -> ANTONIUS [DIALOGUE]
"Smaller loan. Shale, test, food, guild fees. Thirty percent. If I fail, I work contracts through you until the balance is clear."

[B030] ANTONIUS [ACTION]
Looks at Greg again only after the labor pledge enters the deal.

[B031] ANTONIUS -> GREG [DIALOGUE]
"Thirty-five. Eight days."

[B032] GREG -> ANTONIUS [DIALOGUE]
"Nine."

[B033] ANTONIUS [ACTION]
Begins counting silver instead of answering the counteroffer.

[B034] GREG [THOUGHT]
Eight, then. Apparently silence could negotiate too.

[B035] ANTONIUS -> GREG [DIALOGUE]
"If you run, I find you."

[B036] GREG -> ANTONIUS [DIALOGUE]
"I know."

[B037] ANTONIUS [ACTION]
Stops with a coin between his fingers.

[B038] ANTONIUS -> GREG [DIALOGUE]
"How?"

[B039] GREG [ACTION]
Lets his shoulders lose certainty and makes himself look more nineteen than he feels.

[B040] GREG -> ANTONIUS [DIALOGUE]
"I asked around."

[B041] ANTONIUS [ACTION]
Studies the smaller version of Greg without saying whether he believes it, then pushes the coins across.

[B042] ANTONIUS -> GREG [DIALOGUE]
"Eight days, Greg."

[B043] GREG [ACTION]
Takes the silver.

[B044] GREG -> ANTONIUS [DIALOGUE]
"You're going to remember me."

[B045] ANTONIUS -> GREG [DIALOGUE]
"I remember everyone who owes me money."

[B046] GREG -> ANTONIUS [DIALOGUE]
"No. I mean later."

[B047] ANTONIUS [ACTION]
Narrows his eyes instead of rewarding the line with another comeback.

[B048] GREG [THOUGHT]
I should have stopped two sentences ago.
