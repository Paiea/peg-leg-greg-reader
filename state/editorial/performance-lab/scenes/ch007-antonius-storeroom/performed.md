# Ch 5 Antonius Storeroom Performed Script

## PERFORMANCE FRAMES

### GREG
STATE: Tired from sorting but highly activated by a possible future-memory connection.
PERFORMED STANCE: Tries to sound cautious after already revealing excitement.
ATTENTION: Identify the object, then recover negotiating leverage he just destroyed.
BASELINE BEND: material excitement overfires Greg's normal strategic intelligence, making him disclose value before he has secured terms.
REALIZED BY: B009, B010, B023

### ANTONIUS
STATE: In work mode, mildly entertained by Greg's categorizing, not initially invested in the gray box.
PERFORMED STANCE: Practical indifference until the object becomes a pricing problem.
ATTENTION: Clear the room first; after the shift, determine what Greg knows and what that knowledge is worth.
BASELINE BEND: material attention shift from cleanup to valuation makes Antonius speak less playfully and ask narrower buyer/use questions.
REALIZED BY: B011, B014, B025, B028
SHIFT TRIGGER: Greg calls the object extremely valuable.

## PERFORMED SCRIPT

[B001] GREG [ACTION]
Pulls the little wooden box from under the broken chair and opens it.

[B002] GREG [THOUGHT]
Three teeth. I knew that mark. I just did not know from where.

[B003] ANTONIUS [ACTION]
Passes close enough to glance into the box without stopping his cleanup.

[B004] ANTONIUS -> GREG [DIALOGUE]
"Trash."

[B005] GREG -> ANTONIUS [DIALOGUE]
"No."

[B006] ANTONIUS [ACTION]
Nudges the box with two fingers, then keeps moving toward the discard pile.

[B007] ANTONIUS -> GREG [DIALOGUE]
"Failed artificer. Nobody bought it. Toss it."

[B008] GREG [THOUGHT]
The correct move was to say nothing and ask for the trash later.

[B009] GREG -> ANTONIUS [DIALOGUE]
"That might be extremely valuable."

[B010] GREG [THOUGHT]
Excellent. I had survived almost twelve seconds as a competent negotiator.

PERFORMANCE SHIFT:
Greg's value claim changes the object from cleanup debris into a transaction.

[B011] ANTONIUS [ACTION]
Stops walking, comes back, and picks up the gray frame himself.

[B012] ANTONIUS -> GREG [DIALOGUE]
"How valuable?"

[B013] GREG [ACTION]
Turns one stepped plate toward the light while trying to reconstruct the mark.

[B014] ANTONIUS [ACTION]
Waits. He does not help Greg fill the silence.

[B015] GREG -> ANTONIUS [DIALOGUE]
"Forty gold. Maybe more. To the right buyer."

[B016] ANTONIUS -> GREG [DIALOGUE]
"And you are the right buyer?"

[B017] GREG -> ANTONIUS [DIALOGUE]
"No."

[B018] ANTONIUS [ACTION]
Looks back at the frame instead of at Greg.

[B019] ANTONIUS -> GREG [DIALOGUE]
"Then tell me why it is worth anything."

[B020] GREG -> ANTONIUS [DIALOGUE]
"I think it is a master calibration gauge. It does not do the useful work. It tells precision tools where correct is. To almost everyone here, it is scrap. To the few artificers trying to reproduce the standard, rebuilding the reference could cost more than buying this."

[B021] ANTONIUS -> GREG [DIALOGUE]
"Think?"

[B022] GREG -> ANTONIUS [DIALOGUE]
"Arlo could authenticate it. I recognize the standard, not the condition."

[B023] GREG [THOUGHT]
That was the answer I should have led with. After buying it.

[B024] ANTONIUS -> GREG [DIALOGUE]
"Who buys it?"

[B025] GREG [ACTION]
Hesitates.

[B026] GREG -> ANTONIUS [DIALOGUE]
"I cannot name one yet."

[B027] ANTONIUS [ACTION]
Sets the frame on the keep shelf instead of handing it back.

[B028] ANTONIUS -> GREG [DIALOGUE]
"Five silver. End of week."

[B029] GREG -> ANTONIUS [DIALOGUE]
"You said three."

[B030] ANTONIUS [ACTION]
Writes Greg's name on a scrap and tucks it beneath the box.

[B031] ANTONIUS -> GREG [DIALOGUE]
"Before forty gold."

[B032] GREG -> ANTONIUS [DIALOGUE]
"Four."

[B033] ANTONIUS [ACTION]
Picks the broom back up.

[B034] ANTONIUS -> GREG [DIALOGUE]
"Five."

[B035] GREG [THOUGHT]
The negotiation was over because Antonius had physically returned to cleaning. Offensive.

[B036] GREG -> ANTONIUS [DIALOGUE]
"Fine. Five."
