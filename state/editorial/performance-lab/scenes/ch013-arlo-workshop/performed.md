# Ch 9 Arlo Workshop Performed Script

## PERFORMANCE FRAMES

### GREG
STATE: Excited by the improved regulators and hungry for every variable at once.
PERFORMED STANCE: Curious expert who keeps trying to turn one result into the whole system.
ATTENTION: Find the mechanism behind the improved spread and connect it to future significance.
BASELINE BEND: material excitement overextends Greg's usual systems thinking into too many follow-up tests and abstractions at once.
REALIZED BY: B003, B004, B009, B027

### ARLO
STATE: Tired after two days of work, proud of the result, protective of authorship, and faintly embarrassed by how crude the fixtures look.
PERFORMED STANCE: Workshop ownership. He will show what the work proved but not surrender the whole process because Greg is excited.
ATTENTION: Keep the current test bounded and get back to the object.
BASELINE BEND: material defensiveness makes Arlo use the fixture, notebook, failed regulator, and tools to answer Greg instead of sustaining a polished verbal argument.
REALIZED BY: B005, B010, B014, B020, B030

## PERFORMED SCRIPT

[B001] GREG -> ARLO [DIALOGUE]
"Again."

[B002] ARLO -> GREG [DIALOGUE]
"Six runs answered this test."

[B003] GREG -> ARLO [DIALOGUE]
"Then change the order. Warm them first. Cold after. Change the input."

[B004] GREG [THOUGHT]
Every answer had opened three more doors and apparently I intended to kick all of them at once.

[B005] ARLO [ACTION]
Lifts the testing frame off the bench and puts it on the shelf.

[B006] ARLO -> GREG [DIALOGUE]
"Those are other tests."

[B007] GREG -> ARLO [DIALOGUE]
"Which part changed?"

[B008] ARLO [ACTION]
Slides the notebook closer to himself.

[B009] GREG [ACTION]
Looks from the notebook to the six regulators, visibly trying to inventory every variable.

[B010] ARLO -> GREG [DIALOGUE]
"You can ask about the result. The process is mine until I decide otherwise."

[B011] GREG [ACTION]
Closes his mouth.

[B012] ARLO [ACTION]
Takes a cracked regulator from beneath the bench.

[B013] ARLO -> GREG [DIALOGUE]
"First I changed the clay. Then the winding. Then the firing. Then the etch."

[B014] ARLO [ACTION]
Sets the ruined regulator between them.

[B015] ARLO -> GREG [DIALOGUE]
"Then I had six worse regulators and no idea what mattered."

[B016] GREG -> ARLO [DIALOGUE]
"So you stopped changing the object."

[B017] ARLO -> GREG [DIALOGUE]
"Eventually."

[B018] ARLO [ACTION]
Points to the ugly wooden fixture on the shelf.

[B019] ARLO -> GREG [DIALOGUE]
"Same regulator. Twenty measurements."

[B020] ARLO [ACTION]
Opens the notebook to the page of readings and turns it toward Greg without giving him the rest of the notes.

[B021] GREG [ACTION]
Reads the spread twice.

[B022] GREG -> ARLO [DIALOGUE]
"That dropped by more than half."

[B023] ARLO -> GREG [DIALOGUE]
"I stopped holding the input lead by hand."

[B024] GREG -> ARLO [DIALOGUE]
"You separated measurement error from object variation."

[B025] ARLO [ACTION]
Taps one finger against the wooden fixture.

[B026] ARLO -> GREG [DIALOGUE]
"I stopped holding the wire."

[B027] GREG [THOUGHT]
Arlo's version was cheaper and, annoyingly, harder to turn into a lecture.

[B028] GREG -> ARLO [DIALOGUE]
"Then the regulators still spread?"

[B029] ARLO -> GREG [DIALOGUE]
"Yes. So I changed one thing. Winding tension."

[B030] ARLO [ACTION]
Points to a stick, spool, string, and hanging weight beside the mandrel.

[B031] GREG [ACTION]
Follows the copper wire from the spool, over the peg, beneath the weight, and toward the winding mandrel.

[B032] GREG -> ARLO [DIALOGUE]
"You built a tensioner."

[B033] ARLO -> GREG [DIALOGUE]
"I hung a weight on a string."

[B034] GREG -> ARLO [DIALOGUE]
"That is what a tensioner is if you are poor."

[B035] ARLO [ACTION]
Laughs once despite himself, then reaches for the next regulator instead of continuing the joke.

[B036] GREG [THOUGHT]
A man stopped holding a wire, hung a weight from a string, and history began being insulting again.
