# Ch 12 Jorren + Alden + Greg Performed Script

## PERFORMANCE FRAMES

### GREG
STATE: Excited by the pattern he sees and increasingly pleased by the possibility of making Alden better.
PERFORMED STANCE: Analytical teacher trying to keep the lesson smaller than the theory in his head.
ATTENTION: Name one transferable correction without turning Alden into a future-history project.
BASELINE BEND: material teaching pleasure makes Greg's diagnosis briefly expand beyond the sword exchange before he deliberately reduces it.
REALIZED BY: B009, B010, B011

### ALDEN
STATE: Interested, competitive, and highly responsive to anything that produces immediate usable advantage.
PERFORMED STANCE: Treats correction as something to test rather than something to admire.
ATTENTION: Find the move that makes him harder to beat.
BASELINE BEND: material appetite for usable advantage makes Alden overapply a successful correction before he has actually integrated it.
REALIZED BY: B026, B028, B029, B030

### JORREN
STATE: Calm, physically engaged, unsurprised by both men's habits.
PERFORMED STANCE: Lets the spar answer claims whenever possible.
ATTENTION: Keep the training honest and punish what the body actually does.
BASELINE BEND: none material

## PERFORMED SCRIPT

[B001] GREG -> ALDEN [DIALOGUE]
"Stop."

[B002] JORREN [ACTION]
Lowers his practice sword.

[B003] ALDEN [ACTION]
Keeps his sword up and looks at Greg.

[B004] ALDEN -> GREG [DIALOGUE]
"Why?"

[B005] GREG -> ALDEN [DIALOGUE]
"You keep spending the first advantage before you know what it bought you."

[B006] ALDEN -> GREG [DIALOGUE]
"I lost."

[B007] GREG -> ALDEN [DIALOGUE]
"Eventually."

[B008] ALDEN [ACTION]
Looks to Jorren for confirmation.

[B009] GREG -> ALDEN [DIALOGUE]
"You're better when the fight tells you you're in danger than when it tells you you're right."

[B010] GREG [ACTION]
Hears how large that sounded, points at the practice sword, and narrows the lesson back down.

[B011] GREG -> ALDEN [DIALOGUE]
"Take the first advantage. Keep it. Make him solve the new problem before you spend anything else."

[B012] ALDEN -> GREG [DIALOGUE]
"Show me."

[B013] JORREN [ACTION]
Tosses a spare practice sword toward Greg without commentary.

[B014] GREG [ACTION]
Catches it badly enough to answer part of Alden's question.

[B015] GREG -> ALDEN [DIALOGUE]
"Not reliably."

[B016] ALDEN [ACTION]
Looks at Greg's grip, then turns back to Jorren instead of arguing the distinction.

[B017] ALDEN -> JORREN [DIALOGUE]
"Again."

[B018] JORREN [ACTION]
Raises his sword and settles into range.

[B019] ALDEN [ACTION]
Does not attack first.

[B020] JORREN [ACTION]
Tests the outside line and begins the same recovery that punished Alden before.

[B021] ALDEN [ACTION]
Catches the blade, steps inside, feels the opening, then takes one small step back instead of chasing it.

[B022] JORREN [ACTION]
Lets his prepared recovery cut empty air and rebuilds from the worse angle Alden has left him.

[B023] ALDEN [ACTION]
Waits until Jorren commits again, then touches his shoulder cleanly.

[B024] GREG [THOUGHT]
There it was. One correct observation in the correct mind and the person in front of me became harder to beat. I had forgotten how good that felt.

[B025] ALDEN [ACTION]
Looks at Greg with appetite rather than gratitude.

[B026] ALDEN -> JORREN [DIALOGUE]
"Again."

[B027] JORREN [ACTION]
Resets without answering.

[B028] ALDEN [ACTION]
Tries to manufacture the same opening as a repeatable trick instead of responding to the new exchange.

[B029] JORREN [ACTION]
Changes the rhythm before Alden finishes setting the trick and strikes him across the mouth.

[B030] ALDEN [ACTION]
Sits in the sand, checks his lip, then laughs hard enough to make it hurt.

[B031] GREG [THOUGHT]
Good correction. Bad religion.

[B032] JORREN [ACTION]
Offers Alden a hand without explaining what just happened.
