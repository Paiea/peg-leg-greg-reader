## Major findings

### 1. Repetition is often recursive rather than redundant

PLG repeatedly returns to roads, contracts, tools, auctions, measurements, evidence, work, money, and partial authority. In the later book especially, these are often the same *surface form* testing whether a prior lesson survives at a higher scale.

A compressor that keys only on recurring nouns or recurring scene shapes would over-cut the manuscript.

The stronger test is:

> Is the chapter repeating the same result, or applying a familiar decision process under a materially different constraint?

The completion run overwhelmingly found the latter.

### 2. Hidden canon and Showcase are doing different jobs

Showcase hiding is functioning as publication compression.

Hidden canon is functioning as episodic depth.

Many hidden chapters are safely skippable for the public reading path because a neighboring visible chapter carries the stronger beat. But the hidden chapter may still preserve useful state such as:

- recovery and changed-body adaptation
- work habits becoming reliable
- theatre repertoire/version competence
- relationship behavior becoming ordinary
- magic evidence discipline
- economic repricing after disability
- customer/scope boundaries
- household and local-system knowledge

That material is valuable to the project brain even when it is not required in the Showcase sequence.

**Publication redundancy is not the same thing as prose malfunction.**

### 3. Greg's progression moves from evidence discipline into decision discipline

Earlier Greg repeatedly learns not to promote observations into conclusions.

Later chapters increasingly give him a harder problem: the facts can be reasonably clear and somebody still has to decide who carries the cost.

Road closures, transport priority, recovery custody, household work, employment, debt, travel, and capital allocation all turn this into a mature recurring pressure.

The manuscript generally preserves that distinction well.

### 4. New capability changes the option set instead of erasing the old tool

This is especially strong in the work-limb arc.

The limb does not replace crutches, repair Greg, or transform bad ground into good ground. It creates new functional options while introducing fitting, skin, gait, fatigue, maintenance, terrain, and training constraints.

The same pattern increasingly applies to:

- money
- magic
- reputation
- professional references
- intermediary skill
- relationships
- travel knowledge

**New capability does not erase the old tool. It changes the option set.**

### 5. Short prose remains highly context-sensitive

The completion pass found many clipped sequences that should not be recomposed:

- physical threat and injury
- unstable terrain
- controlled experiments
- route/traffic calls
- live theatre cues
- body inventory under pressure
- interrupted inference

The prose-rhythm lever should continue to collapse analytical lists when they are one cognition, but should not collapse thought turns, physical sequence, experimental steps, or performed discovery.

A useful retained rule is:

> Collapse lists. Do not collapse thought turns.

### 6. The late economic arc changes relationships, not only numbers

Canon 491 is the cleanest example. Paying Vale to zero removes creditor authority from Antonius. Greg can then voluntarily ask him to drink, and the conversation changes because neither man is standing inside the old debt relationship.

The financial milestone therefore produces a relationship-state change rather than a payoff speech.

## Editorial implication

This run does **not** support a new global compression rule.

It supports continuing to make edits prove local malfunction before canon changes.

The book's current risk is not that every recurring form is redundant. The larger risk would be teaching an automated editor that familiarity itself is sufficient evidence to compress.

For future broad passes:

1. classify the dramatic result and state change first
2. identify whether a repeated surface form is carrying new constraint/evidence/relationship state
3. preserve performed physical and experimental sequence where timing is information
4. only then adjudicate rhythm, compression, speaker ownership, or scene rewrite

## Speaker attribution follow-up

This completion pass did not manufacture speaker-ownership repairs where the prose remained legible and correct.

A separate deliberately more-generous **light attribution / more-tags pass** can still be useful. That pass should optimize reader orientation rather than use the full PERFORMANCE pass as an excuse to rewrite otherwise functioning scenes.
