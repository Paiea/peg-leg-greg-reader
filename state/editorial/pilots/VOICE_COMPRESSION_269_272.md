4. Greg keeps his systems brain while redundant self-explanation is cut.
5. Hessa keeps rigorous experimental thinking while familiar protocol is compressed.
6. Craft/local specialists may be cautious without becoming Hessa clones.
7. Quiet civic life survives compression. We are removing repetition, not ordinary life.
