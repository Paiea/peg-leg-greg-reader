Status: **COMPLETE / CONVERGED**

Canon prose remains story authority. REHEARSAL evidence is derived editorial memory. Nothing in this synthesis can silently override exact manuscript prose, current `MANUSCRIPT_STATE.md`, or explicit author decisions.

## What the campaign established

The experiment began by asking whether named synthetic actors, scene pressure, private performed interpretation, take variance, and high-authority FREE rehearsal could improve existing prose without taking story authority away from canon.

The answer is **yes, but selectively**.

Early calibration found real local repairs and useful behavioral discoveries. Hotter waves also established the failure boundary: when actors were allowed too much local authorship they tended to overact the novel, inflate quips, recurse on signature props, crowd Greg's POV, manufacture movement, compress useful repetition, or turn characters into cleaner caricatures.

As the manuscript progressed, SOURCE WIN became the dominant result. That was not a failed experiment. It showed that later PLG prose was already performing much of the useful work REHEARSAL was designed to discover: characters solved scenes through task ownership, physical behavior, silence, attention, imperfect knowledge, local authority, and pressure instead of exposition or protagonist privilege.

The durable production rule is:

**WILD REHEARSAL / CONSERVATIVE PUBLICATION.**

A hot take is allowed to be wrong. A source win is a successful result. Publication requires the alternative to beat exact current prose without violating story truth, authority boundaries, reader legibility, or source-match gates.

## Greg — promoted behavioral memory

Do not flatten these into adjectives. They are behaviors under conditions.

### Observation versus attribution

When Greg encounters something interesting, his mature pattern is increasingly:

**OBSERVE → FORM A NARROW HYPOTHESIS → CHECK WHO OWNS THE NEXT QUESTION → TEST OR REPORT → REVISE.**

He may still overreach mentally. Growth is visible when he stops before turning an observation into a causal claim, identity claim, safety claim, or profession he does not own.

An observation can be real while its attribution remains unsettled.

### Authority discipline

Under multi-person work pressure, Greg is most competent when he knows exactly what he owns and what he does not.

He can own current state, sequence, records, handoffs, timing, notices, questions, and coordination while another person owns safety, acceptance, craft method, diagnosis, appraisal, repair, driving, fieldcraft, hiring, or final judgment.

His authority becomes more trustworthy when other people can predict what he will **not** decide.

### Support identity

Greg's strongest long-range professional shape is not universal expertise. It is support at increasing scale.

He notices mismatches between people, records, objects, timing, routes, and current reality. He can make specialists more effective without pretending to become them. This grows naturally from theatre support into field organization, commercial coordination, contracting, brokerage, references, and eventual capital deployment/ownership.

The value is often preventing expensive coordination failure.

### Restraint as competence

Skipping a bad attempt, leaving a tool packed, refusing to chase a clue, not filling a silence, not correcting another actor's timing, not forcing a magical result, or allowing another person to solve the problem can all be active competent behavior.

Do not confuse "Greg does less" with "nothing happened."

### Body / mobility

Greg's body is not a symbolic obstacle or a progression meter.

With fitting, work-limb use, crutches, roads, stages, shops, crowds, and fatigue, the strongest pattern is honest reporting and terrain/task-specific tool choice. Existing adaptation skill can sometimes hide useful fitting information because Greg compensates around error.

The work-limb priority remains **usable hands / useful work**, not visual normalization or a triumphant walking milestone.

### Money / work

Greg's economic growth is increasingly about preserving options, understanding capacity, accumulating references, coordinating expensive work, connecting expertise, and distinguishing asset value from liquidity.

Wanting or recognizing value does not create capacity.

Small work can remain socially or practically valuable after his earning ceiling rises. Do not convert every future chapter into a larger paycheck, but do not reset his primary economic scale back to copper desperation.

### Social growth

Greg is increasingly able to be beside other people's lives without annexing them into his own arc.

He can let Lyssa work, let Jonah return to his job, let Dask choose a different curiosity, leave a town without owning its future, and maintain acquaintance-level relationships without forcing them into family, mentorship, romance, or permanent cast.

## World / scene-level convergence

### Scenes are tests

Useful scene pressure asks what a person does under conditions rather than reciting traits.

Prefer:

- goal
- concealment
- belief
- pressure
- resources
- observable access

Then let behavior produce evidence.

### Distributed authority makes the world feel real

Roads, theatres, shops, clinics, markets, ferries, survey offices, houses, archives, and crews should have people who already own their jobs.

A new place should broaden systems before it broadens Greg's importance.

### Mundane causality is progression

Food, routes, paperwork, fitting, references, queue order, storage, weather, money, bench geometry, tool custody, and ordinary mistakes are not automatically filler. When they change Greg's available options or relationships, they are the progression system.

Do not compress away the causal chain merely because the final result can be summarized cleanly.

## Private performed interpretation

Non-Greg private inner performance remains rehearsal evidence, not canon thought.

It may improve observable timing, silence, gaze, gesture, blocking, object handling, withholding, interruption, or spoken language. It does not become character fact without independent support.

No actor hears another actor's private cognition. Greg does not hear it. No telepathy by tool leakage.
