# Canon 001-020 Character-Discovery REHEARSAL

Status: derived editorial campaign

Authority: current `editor/rehearsal-simulation-engine` branch only

Main touched: no

## What changed in the method

This campaign uses the approved high-authority REHEARSAL model with one additional operating rule:

> Treat scenes as tests. Store behavior under conditions rather than broad character traits.

Each meaningful actor can now receive compact scene pressure covering goal, concealment, current belief/interpretation, pressure, resources, and observable access.

Actors solve the pressure in character through BODY, VOICE, and private inner performance.

Greg/Nico uses BODY / VOICE / INNER VOICE. Other meaningful actors use BODY / VOICE / PRIVATE INNER VOICE.

Private thought remains actor-generated performed interpretation, not literal character truth. Only accepted Greg prose can turn Nico's performed cognition into actual first-person canon thought.

## Campaign calibration

Rehearsal freedom remained HIGH.

Actors were allowed to overperform internally and explore strong private interpretations.

Prose retention remained a separate, stricter knob. The campaign did not force rewrites simply because rehearsal generated additional behavior or thought.

Result: 20 chapters covered, 15 deep FREE/variance reads, five lighter FAITHFUL controls, one non-canon character probe, one variance group, six durable behavioral discoveries, and one local prose return.

## Rejected overperformance

1. Adding extra polished Greg internal sentences to chapter 001 made live cognition feel more essayistic.
2. Adding more Antonius ledger gestures repeated the known Wave 4 prop-recursion failure.
3. Making Hessa's hidden safety/suspicion interpretation explicit in Greg-facing prose solved ambiguity the scene benefits from preserving.

The resulting production principle remains:

> Let the actors overperform. Let the prose keep only what earns survival.
