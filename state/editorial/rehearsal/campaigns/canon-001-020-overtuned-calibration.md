## What ran

The named REHEARSAL company was intentionally given more soft-prose authority than the proposed production setting.

The calibration applied 35 actor-preferred exact-source writes across nine canon chapters:

The writeable surface was intentionally broad inside the dramatic lock:

- dialogue wording and amount

Hard story surfaces remained locked:

- plot
- facts

Each applied write required actor preference, preserved dramatic lock, reader-check pass, exact source match, a non-main target branch, and soft-surface-only change classification.

Full provenance is recorded in `canon-001-020-overtuned-report.json`.

## What improved clearly

### Speaker ownership

The canon 005 debt exchange now assigns `"Same thing,"` to Greg rather than Antonius. Antonius then answers by turning the ledger toward Greg instead of verbally contradicting his own distinction.

This is a clear repair, not merely calibration flavor.

### Arlo / Rowan

The strongest overtuned win is the replacement of the repeated `I know / I know / I know` volley in canon 013.

Arlo now turns the page, points to the next column, and finally answers that the result is why he is still working. The exchange keeps Greg's excitement but gives Arlo process-owned rhythm rather than a second copy of Greg's clipped cadence.

This direction should survive production calibration.

### Alden / Noa

Alden becomes more distinct when he answers with the job in front of him:

- tapping the water-support warning instead of replying with a polished dry quip
- lifting the contract copy instead of saying `Unfortunately` to identify himself as guild
- staying crouched at the damaged boards while making the inside-route observation
- continuing to work a widened nail hole while Greg thinks rather than passively waiting

These changes reduce junior-Greg voice collision without inventing a catchphrase.

### Hessa / Mara

Hessa improves when procedure carries social pressure:

- waiting beside the bean table until Greg looks up
- sliding the bowl between them instead of merely not smiling
- withholding the next bean until Greg answers what changed
- using silence to let Greg's joke die rather than supplying a perfect comeback

This direction is strong because it preserves Hessa's evidence/procedure ownership.

### Jorren / Kellan

Jorren improves when teaching and concern stay physical:

- keeping a hand out until Greg proves he can stand
- lowering the practice sword and checking Greg's breathing and legs
- demonstrating road attention while continuing to walk rather than explaining attention abstractly

This makes his teaching authority local and embodied.

## Where overtuning becomes visible

### Antonius prop overfitting

The experiment pushes the correct principle too literally.

Antonius is stronger when judgment remains attached to transaction, work, and information boundaries. But the overtuned cut begins reusing the ledger as an emotional subtitle too often:

- tapping the ledger
- keeping a finger on the ledger
- rubbing a thumb along the closed ledger
- resting a hand on the ledger
- stilling a hand beside the ledger
- stopping a thumb at the ledger edge

Canon 005 even creates an immediate local repetition where a new ledger tap is followed by the preexisting sentence `Antonius tapped the ledger.`

Production guidance should therefore be:

> Prefer owned task behavior over generic cognition, but vary the behavior naturally and do not force the character's signature object into every reaction.

The underlying character tendency is useful. The repeated prop is not the tendency.

### Stage-direction risk

The overtuned setting demonstrates that physicalizing every reaction can make prose feel externally choreographed.

A first-person novel still needs compression. Some thoughts should remain thoughts. Some reactions can remain unperformed. Movement earns its place when it changes timing, ownership, pressure, legibility, or character specificity.

Production should not replace every cognition verb with a gesture merely because a gesture can be generated.

### Actor motif recursion

A successful actor discovery can become self-reinforcing if later takes receive it as identity guidance. The anti-self-confirmation rule remains necessary.

For production, repeated synthetic use of a behavior should not make that behavior more authoritative. Independent canon support must still do that work.

## Calibration recommendation

Keep the overtuned branch as the upper-bound reference.

Do not simply adopt `35 writes / 20 chapters` as a production quota. Edit density should follow scene opportunity.

The production dial should retain most of the freedom demonstrated here while raising the preference threshold for literal gestures and repeated motifs.

Recommended production bias:

- aggressive on broken speaker ownership
- aggressive on dialogue-shape collisions between recurring characters
- aggressive when task/object behavior makes character ownership clearer
- permissive on scene-scale prose rebuilding when a performed take is clearly stronger
- conservative about repeating a character's signature prop or gesture
- conservative about converting every internal reaction into blocking
- preserve Greg's first-person compression even when the performed screenplay contains more physical detail

In short:

> Let movement, dialogue, and tone generate prose. Do not make prose prove that a rehearsal happened.

The overtuned run successfully exposes both the upside and the failure mode needed to calibrate the next campaign.
