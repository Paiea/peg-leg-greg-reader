## Book-level discoveries from 1-20

### 1. Generic cognition is a real early-prose tic

`Considered me` is not inherently forbidden, but this range uses abstract cognition in places where the scene already contains a better behavioral carrier. The repair rule should be:

> When a reaction can be expressed through a character's active task, object, boundary, or body state, prefer the owned behavior over generic cognition narration.

Do **not** manufacture random gestures merely to remove words such as `considered`, `looked`, or `watched`.

### 2. Desmond / Antonius is becoming legible through work

The strongest Antonius pattern is not a catchphrase. He keeps doing the transaction, cleaning, walking, reading, pricing, or withholding information until something Greg says materially changes the decision.

This remains a rehearsal hypothesis until supported/promoted through independent canon evidence.

### 3. Kellan / Jorren works

Jorren becomes immediately different from Hessa or Antonius when his teaching occurs through contact, timing, fatigue, road attention, and the next exchange. He does not need to become a philosophical mentor for the lesson to land.

### 4. Noa / Alden needs pressure ownership, not Greg-lite jokes

Alden is strongest when he commits to the immediate job, notices a practical problem Greg has not yet named, accepts useful correction without deference, and moves. Dry humor can remain, but Greg should retain gravitational ownership of compressed interpretive comedy.
