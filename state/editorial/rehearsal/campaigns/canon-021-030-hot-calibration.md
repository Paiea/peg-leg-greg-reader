# REHEARSAL 021–030 hot calibration

This pass keeps the approved REHEARSAL architecture unchanged and tunes prose-retention behavior after the first scaled wave produced 10/10 source wins.

## Calibration intent

Keep actor authority high and make FREE rehearsal more willing to survive when it adds character-specific embodiment or dialogue shape.

Prefer a candidate when it materially improves one or more of:

- movement ownership
- task/object behavior
- interruption or response timing
- silence or hesitation
- character-specific phrasing
- subtext carried by action rather than explanation
- relationship-specific rhythm

Do not impose an edit quota. Source may still win.

## Repetition rule

Repeated words, objects, or gestures are not automatically synthetic tics. Keep repetition when it escalates irritation, pressure, rhythm, obsession, or pays off as an outburst. Penalize repetition only when it reads like model recursion without new scene-local work.

## Movement rule

Movement should not exist merely to prove a rehearsal happened. Prefer movement that tells us who owns the task, who is withholding, who is impatient, who is measuring, who is controlling access, or who is changing strategy.

## Dialogue rule

Raise the prior for character-specific dialogue. A line may survive even when the source is already clear if the new phrasing is more specifically this character under this pressure and does not turn them into a catchphrase machine.

## Current 021–030 target

This calibration reruns current canon 021–030 as fresh source and allows surgical FREE returns. The first return set intentionally favors small embodiment and dialogue-shape changes over broad rewrites.
