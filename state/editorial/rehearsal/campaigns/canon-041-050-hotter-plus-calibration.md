# REHEARSAL 041–050 slightly hotter calibration

This wave turns production one notch hotter than canon 031–040 while preserving the same dramatic locks, private-thought boundaries, and exact-source return gates.

## Intent

FREE rehearsal may now reshape a short exchange, not only a single beat, when the performed version is materially more character-specific and preserves all locked story truth.

Prefer survival when a candidate materially improves:

- character-owned movement instead of generic reaction summary
- object or task behavior that carries subtext
- refusal, interruption, delay, silence, or compliance specific to this relationship
- dialogue wording that sounds more like this actor under this pressure
- exchange-level rhythm where source repetition or redundant response weakens the performance
- physical behavior that makes a decision legible without explaining the decision

Source still wins when the actor cannot beat it. There is no edit quota.

## Exchange-level permission

A short exchange may be recomposed when rehearsal discovers that the existing exchange repeats itself, over-explains, or loses the actor's rhythm. Recomposition may alter wording, paragraphing, action placement, and timing, but may not alter facts, knowledge, causality, relationship state, chronology, competence, injury state, economics, or plot outcome.

## Repetition rule

Do not confuse performed repetition with accidental repetition. Repeated words, props, or gestures remain legal when repetition escalates pressure, irritation, obsession, rhythm, or an eventual outburst. Recompose only when the repetition is redundant rather than performed.

## Movement heat

Movement remains functional rather than decorative. Prefer movement that reveals task ownership, resistance, physical limitation, familiarity, access control, transaction state, or change of strategy.

## Dialogue heat

Dialogue may now win against already-readable source when the performed wording or exchange shape is more specifically this character. Do not make everyone quippier. Plain speech remains valid when plain speech is the strongest character choice.
