# REHEARSAL 051–060 boundary hunt

This wave intentionally pushes beyond the comfortable production calibration used through canon 50.

The purpose is not to maximize accepted edits. It is to discover where actor authority starts imposing itself on the novel.

## Deliberately over-hot permissions

FREE rehearsal may:

- substantially recompose a short exchange
- interrupt or cut off another actor
- withhold an answer and let silence force the other actor to act or infer
- change local response order and who closes an exchange
- collapse repetitive dialogue or expand a compressed exchange when performance earns it
- move task objects and blocking across a local scene
- let an actor answer through work before speech
- propose plausible entrances and exits when dramatic locks permit them
- give Nico much richer private cognition than final prose is expected to print

## Locks remain unchanged

No actor may alter plot outcome, established facts, knowledge state, chronology, economics, earned competence, injury/physical state, or major relationship milestones.

Exact-source matching, reader check, hard-surface refusal, no-em-dash policy, PERFORMANCE reference validation, Showcase validation, and repository tests remain unchanged.

## Failure signals we are actively hunting

Do not hide these if they appear:

- everyone becoming quippy
- actors interrupting because they are allowed to rather than because pressure produces it
- task objects becoming signature props
- movement on every line
- dialogue becoming theatrically compressed and losing natural mess
- useful repetition being mistaken for redundancy
- private actor interpretation leaking into canon truth
- character differentiation becoming caricature
- Greg losing first-person cognitive texture because actors seize too much scene space
- scenes becoming visibly staged instead of lived

The critic should identify the first point where a more performed take is technically stronger beat-by-beat but makes the chapter worse as a novel. That point is useful calibration evidence.
