# CH496 PERFORMANCE EVIDENCE

Derived editorial evidence only. Canon prose remains authority.

## Locked scene
Greg attends the scheduled Hark & Venn second fitting in Duskport. Masta owns fit/safety. Greg owns honest body report and desired task. No final acceptance is required. Loaded tray is attempted only if Masta says safe.

## Take A: rails first, tray exposes rotation
- Revised shell feels less globally alien than first fitting but still not natural.
- Masta deliberately makes Greg repeat several rail lengths without correcting early, watching what his body chooses.
- Greg's old compensation returns as a subtle rightward trunk shift and shortened right step when left socket load increases.
- With one hand released, he can correct consciously.
- With both hands on a lightly loaded tray, the correction disappears because hands cannot borrow rail support and attention moves to keeping cups level.
- The left test foot rotates slightly outward late in stance. Greg counters by turning pelvis and shortening right step. Tray stays mostly level, making the compensation look successful until Masta stops him.
- Observable useful result: he crosses more than once with both hands occupied. Observable bad result: successful tray carriage is partly purchased by a gait compensation that changes socket pressure behind knee/upper lateral edge.
- Masta stops before pain or skin damage, removes shell, checks pressure marks, and treats the exposed compensation as valuable fit data rather than failure.

## Take B: object task changes authority
- Masta does not begin with tray. She has Greg move an empty wooden board, then one cup, then two cups, increasing task only after body report stays clean.
- Greg wants the harder task sooner. Masta refuses because difficulty is not the goal.
- Venn or shop assistant places destination table slightly off straight line, requiring a small turn rather than a heroic corridor walk.
- Greg can stand hands-free longer than first fitting, but turning with hands occupied exposes hesitation and an instinct to reach for support.
- He chooses to set tray down rather than save a wobble. Masta treats that as correct behavior because the task is carrying safely, not proving independence.
- Observable useful result: Greg independently aborts one attempt before a spill/fall and then completes a shorter turn after alignment adjustment.
- This version better preserves the durable rule that restraint is competence and lets Masta's safety authority remain decisive.

## Convergence
Both takes support:
- real improvement without finality;
- hands-occupied movement reveals information rail walking can hide;
- Greg's compensation skill remains double-edged;
- Masta should stop/adjust based on evidence, not Greg's desire for more repetitions;
- practical success is carrying an ordinary object safely, including choosing to set it down when needed;
- no triumphant walking milestone;
- no unsupervised take-home use merely because one task succeeds.

## Selected embodied discoveries for prose
Use Take B's graduated object loading and voluntary set-down, plus Take A's specific late-stance outward rotation / pelvis compensation if it emerges cleanly in prose. Keep exact biomechanics modest and observable. Masta may decide the shell is close enough for another bounded supervised session after adjustment, but final acceptance remains unearned.
