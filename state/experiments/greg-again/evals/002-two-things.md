## Outside-agency lock

### Sella

**WANT**

Keep the Guild desk moving, enforce process, avoid wasting time, and do her job correctly.

**ACTION**

She handles the Bronze fighter's paperwork, assesses Greg's obvious lack of money, gives him current access/rate information, provides the date, later processes his token, and continues her required staff drill without caring that Greg has turned her competence into a private revelation.

**SCENE DELTA**

She confirms the date, exposes Greg's immediate money constraint, reveals the gap between his future knowledge and present competence, and accidentally becomes a comparison target through her clean side step.

**GREG MISREAD**

Greg is tempted to read Future Sella into Present Sella and to convert ordinary competence into a story about himself.

**CONTINUATION**

If Greg leaves, Sella keeps processing forms, enforcing access rules, completing staff drills, and becoming competent through boring repetition.

### Silver instructor

**WANT**

Keep the yard functional and correct beginners efficiently without donating private instruction.

**ACTION**

She notices Greg's overcomplicated movement, gives minimal correction, refuses to provide detailed coaching for free, and answers one unusually well-framed training question.

**SCENE DELTA**

She collapses Greg's twenty-problem training model into two useful movements: overhead cut and side step.

**GREG MISREAD**

Greg initially treats simplicity as beneath his knowledge instead of as the corrective his knowledge requires.

**CONTINUATION**

If Greg disappears, she keeps correcting dozens of beginners with the smallest useful instruction she can give.

### Jorren

**WANT**

Practice, test Greg, have fun, and exploit openings available in front of him.

**ACTION**

He initiates contact, points out the flaw in practicing evasion without an attacker, changes the promised line when Greg moves early, hits Greg repeatedly, and punishes an ambitious unpracticed counter.

**SCENE DELTA**

He converts Greg's abstract insight into painful evidence: seeing an attack is not the same as physically answering it.

**GREG MISREAD**

Greg initially treats Jorren's grin as a challenge to ego rather than useful information about his own inability to stay narrow.

**CONTINUATION**

If Greg leaves, Jorren keeps training, sparring, getting better, and finding people willing to hit or be hit.

## Lens candidates

### LIGHT

Existing R2 control is the current LIGHT-ish baseline. Direct exchanges establish Sella, the instructor, and Jorren quickly and give them obvious verbal identity.

Primary strength: outside characters arrive with almost no mediation cost.

Primary risk: the chapter can fall into familiar dialogue ping-pong and Greg becomes less continuous as the book's linguistic surface.

### GREG-DOMINANT

Candidate target for the next pass.

Keep the Extreme cut's continuous Greg experience, but restore only voice breaches that beat Greg's mediation.

Likely voice-breach candidates:

- the instructor's `fight or assemble furniture` line, because the wording itself is the joke and characterizes her economy
- a compact Jorren line near the `same line` deception if hearing his verbal playfulness is more alive than Greg paraphrasing it
- possibly one Sella line if it gives Present Sella a sharper verbal edge than behavioral rendering alone

Do not restore all three by default. Test whether each line materially improves the scene.

### EXTREME

`subjective-cut-001.md` is the current extreme stress test.

Primary strength: Greg's consciousness becomes unusually continuous and the whole chapter feels metabolized through one mind.

Primary risk: some excellent outside-language moments lose character-specific bite when reported rather than heard.

## QC findings to watch

### Flattening risk

Low-to-moderate in the current Extreme cut because Sella, the instructor, and Jorren all continue to act independently.

Highest-risk zones:

- administrative information at Sella's desk
- instructor corrections
- the Jorren name/introduction exchange

These can become factual relay if Greg's interpretation stops adding lived behavior.

### Greg tic saturation

Watch the training sequence for repeated `Again`, `Right`, fragments, and dry reaction beats. Much of this is structurally earned by repetition, but the cut should not turn practice into a metronome of Greg mannerisms.

### Voice-breach hypothesis

The furniture line is the strongest current example of a line that the Extreme experiment likely made worse by paraphrasing.

That is useful failure evidence, not a reason to reject the lens.

## Current comparison question
