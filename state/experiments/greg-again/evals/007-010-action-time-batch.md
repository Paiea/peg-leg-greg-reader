## Batch result

The batch succeeds as a useful direction test because it materially changes both **story motion** and **time behavior** rather than simply applying the Greg-dominant renderer to inherited scenes.

The useful unit is no longer `one chapter = one adjacent day / one progression update`.

The batch moves through:

- immediate departure pressure
- several days of road acclimation
- a road attack
- additional travel and weather
- an environmental rescue
- Jorren continuing north
- a southbound return
- roughly two weeks of off-camera change in Carrow

Time is compressed where repetition adds no new decision and expanded when changed state meets pressure.

## Chapter 7 — The Extra Guard

Dominant action: runaway loaded wagon on a descent.

Why it works:

- the first road action is logistical rather than combat, widening the meaning of action
- Greg's old instinct is initially wrong at current power scale
- Meral falsifies him in motion instead of delivering a lesson first
- the tiny brace matters because it changes one failure path
- Carrow leaves the page early enough that the book physically widens

Surviving learning:

> **SUPPORT CONTROLS WHICH FAILURE REACHES PEOPLE.**

A large remembered solution may be worse than a tiny current solution placed correctly.

## Chapter 8 — Road Work

Dominant action: prepared road robbery after a false blockage compresses the caravan.

Why it works:

- travel residue proves several days without showing them
- professional caravan defenses attract a cheap prepared counter
- Greg cannot solve the fight alone
- Jorren remains the better current fighter in several exchanges
- Meral remains the better support mage
- Greg changes the field through warning, placement, one deflection, and one useful sword exchange
- recovery remains an active physical weakness and gets Greg punched

Surviving learning:

> **A SUPPORT MIND SHOULD CHANGE THE FIELD EVEN WHEN ITS INDIVIDUAL ACTIONS ARE SMALL.**

And:

> **PREPARED SYSTEMS CREATE PREPARED OPPOSITION.**

Do not treat a newly acquired capability as permanent safety.

## Chapter 9 — The Bridge

Dominant action: rain-damaged approach collapses beneath a loaded wagon.

Why it works:

- future knowledge creates false familiarity rather than a cheat answer
- the problem has no villain
- Jorren, Meral, draft animals, ropes, drivers, and Greg all solve different parts
- Greg's tiny plane becomes useful through angle and placement rather than magnitude
- Greg intentionally rotates the plane under load, creating a qualitative control gain

Surviving learning:

> **SMALL CAPABILITY BECOMES LARGE AT THE CONSTRAINT.**

And:

> **FUTURE KNOWLEDGE IS A MAP OF LATER REALITY, NOT PRESENT TERRAIN.**

The future can mislead through confidence even when the remembered fact is technically true later.

## Chapter 10 — What Moved

Dominant action: return under changed state plus a live full-load filter test.

Why it works:

- Jorren's departure is allowed to remain his rather than becoming Greg's emotional scene
- six return days compress aggressively because little new choice occurs
- road residue remains on Greg's body and habits
- Arlo and Ivena have made meaningful technical and commercial progress without Greg
- the live test creates tension without requiring another disaster
- the revised system handles pressure safely, allowing competence to be dramatic
- Greg has to resist correcting a design merely because it does not resemble the future version he remembers

Surviving learning:

> **TIME IS ALLOWED TO MAKE GREG LESS CENTRAL.**

The world moving without Greg is not lost protagonist agency. It is evidence that the world has agency.

## Action-rate finding

`More action` works best here when action means **state under pressure**, not `more fights`.

The batch uses:

- mechanical emergency
- human violence
- environmental rescue
- live industrial test

Only one chapter is primarily combat.

This keeps the action promise high without flattening the story into encounter cadence.

## Thought-rate finding

Greg's thought works best when it is short enough to lose arguments with events.

Strong pattern:

```text
STIMULUS
→ GREG MODEL / EXPECTATION
→ ACTION INTERRUPTS OR FALSIFIES IT
→ MOTOR RESPONSE / DECISION
→ THOUGHT UPDATES AFTER CONSEQUENCE
```

Weak pattern to resist:

```text
STIMULUS
→ COMPLETE GREG ESSAY
→ ACTION WAITS
```

This is especially important now that Greg's thinking itself is becoming one of the novel's main pleasures.

## Progression finding

The batch avoids four identical reward beats.

Progression instead behaves as:

- Chapter 7: accidental useful shape becomes deliberate drill
- Chapter 8: deliberate shape works in danger but resource limit becomes visible
- Chapter 9: same tiny shape gains directional control under load
- Chapter 10: practice survives without Meral and becomes part of Greg's available decision set

No numeric power jump is required.

The strongest general rule is:

> **PROGRESSION SHOULD CHANGE THE PROBLEM SPACE BEFORE IT CHANGES THE POWER FANTASY.**

## Time finding

The written run should feel comfortable skipping:

- uneventful travel days
- repeated practice after the pattern is established
- ordinary meals / sleep / chores
- off-camera work by supporting characters

Then re-enter when residue changes a live choice.

Useful residue classes:

- body
- equipment
- money
- habits
- relationships
- competence
- institutional familiarity
- absence
- independent supporting-character progress

## Risks surfaced

### Constant competence payoff

Greg cannot always be the person who notices the decisive constraint.

Future chapters should include failures where another person sees the field better, Greg follows instead of leads, or his model produces the wrong action before correction.

### Meral as convenient teacher

Do not convert Meral into a standing mentor who exists to deliver Greg's magic curriculum.

Her current value came from shared work and bounded contact.

### Action inflation

Do not assume every chapter after this needs an emergency.

Action density should remain higher than prior PLG, but contrast matters. A quiet scene can still move hard if a relationship, ownership structure, opportunity, or choice changes irreversibly.

### Greg linguistic saturation

The more action we add, the easier Greg's fragments and dry resets can become a repetitive action voice.

Keep page-native paragraph rhythm. Use fragment bursts when perception actually compresses.

## Provisional written priorities after Chapter 10

1. **Causal motion over procedural completeness.**
2. **Time skips over repetition, then returns through residue.**
3. **Action is state change under pressure, not combat quota.**
4. **Greg's thought must compete with moving reality.**
5. **Supporting characters advance while off-camera.**
6. **Progression exposes new constraints instead of closing old ones permanently.**
7. **Future knowledge can mislead through missing history and present conditions.**
8. **Tiny support acts can matter enormously when placed at the constraint.**
9. **Greg does not need to be the decisive actor in every successful scene.**
10. **End chapters when the causal movement lands.**

## Frontier after Chapter 10

Live pressures now include:

- Greg has road pay and no Vale debt.
- he can intentionally produce and angle a tiny support plane for several breaths under calm conditions.
- he has evidence that support capability has high leverage even at tiny scale.
- recovery remains an active sword weakness.
- Jorren is north and no longer available as Greg's convenient local training partner.
- Arlo and Ivena have a safer two-stage filter design and a four-unit real order.
- Greg's future image of the mature filter no longer cleanly matches the present development path.
- Greg can afford one or two meaningful choices but not all available progression lanes.
- Meral is no longer present to supervise practice.
- Greg has now seen that leaving a system can make it healthier rather than weaker.

Do not choose Chapter 11 by asking what old PLG did next.

Choose which pressure creates the most interesting irreversible movement from this state.
