## Why formal PERFORMANCE was worth using here

The plot state was simple: Mara leaves Carrow.

The uncertainty was behavioral:

- does Greg showing up buy reconciliation?
- what does Mara want from the changed Greg?
- can Greg be honest without turning honesty into another form of control?
- how much direct outside voice does the relationship need?

This was a good use of the existing high-heat character-pressure / PERFORMANCE machinery.

It would not have been a good use for routine packing logistics or another ordinary contract.

## Result

Selected relationship movement:

> **PRESENCE IS EVIDENCE, NOT PAYMENT.**

Greg gives up useful income and spends the remaining departure window with Mara.

That choice matters, but it does not purchase:

- forgiveness
- restored memory
- a renewed romance
- a promise that Mara will wait
- a request that Greg follow her to Halden

Mara instead chooses a smaller future channel: packet-boat letters, with the condition that Greg not let her plan around false certainty.

## Writing signals

### More outside voice survives naturally

Mara's exact wording carries:

- relational history
- falsification
- boundary setting
- fear
- future state change

The Greg-dominant lens remains intact without absorbing her into paraphrase.

### Emotional action can remain physically active

Packing, carrying crates, closing the old window, moving cargo, and boarding the barge prevent the relationship scenes from becoming static conversation rooms.

### Ordinary memory has weight

The back room, sticky window, chipped cup, old cards, and remembered habits matter more than another famous-future data point.

### Do not solve memory on demand

Greg receives fragments and procedural familiarity, not convenient complete scenes.

The missing past remains a real constraint.

### Reduced explanatory sealing works

Chapter 15 ends with the cord, brass key, and old cards available as obvious symbolic material.

Greg does not explain what the three objects mean.

He eats an apple.

Keep using this restraint when the scene already carries the inference.

## Story state out

- Mara has left Carrow for Halden on schedule.
- Mara and Greg remain emotionally unresolved.
- a packet-boat letter route exists every seventh day.
- Mara explicitly wants truthful contact, not confident invention.
- Greg has old cards originally belonging to Tavin, producing one small ordinary-memory fragment.
- Mara's mother remains alive and independently connected to Greg's lost younger-life history.
- Greg sacrificed a useful two-day contract to preserve the departure window.
- Greg still needs income.
- support training remains deferred.
- North Gate long-shadow remains open and untouched by this relationship arc.

## Tool-use lesson

Do not increase ceremony globally because this PERFORMANCE helped.

Use the strongest available tool only when it resolves a consequential uncertainty better than direct story judgment.

For the next frontier, cheap story search is sufficient unless a new high-heat behavioral or long-range causal uncertainty appears.
