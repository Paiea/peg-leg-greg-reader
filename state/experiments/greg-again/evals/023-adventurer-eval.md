Greg does not become an adventurer because the Guild promotes him or because he recovers a memory. He takes field work, carries his share, crosses bad water, maintains his weapon, and takes watch for other people.

The identity is behavioral before it is institutional.

> **USE THE FUTURE AS A LEAD. MAKE THE PRESENT PROVE IT.**

Sometimes the present should prove it true.
