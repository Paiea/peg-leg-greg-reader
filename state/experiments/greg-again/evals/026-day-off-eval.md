## Surviving discovery

> **Paid adventure work can buy maintenance, recovery, and a little unsold time.**

Chapter 25 established that adventure can accumulate as completed paid work.

Chapter 26 establishes what completion is for.

The money does not need to become an upgrade. The body does not need to become stronger overnight. A successful job may simply pay for food, feet, equipment, future obligations, and the ability to wake up without immediately selling the next day.

That is meaningful professional progression because Greg's adventurer life now has a sustainable middle state between `on contract` and `desperate for the next contract`.
