# Greg, Again — Chapters 7–10 Story Batch

Status: **SELECTED FOR WRITTEN EXPERIMENT / NON-CANON**

Source frontier: `state/experiments/greg-again/prose/006-the-first-customer.md`

Purpose: advance R2 through Chapter 10 with materially more action, stronger causal chaining, and larger honest movements of time.

R1/canon and prior R2 remain quarry and controls, not route authority.

## Batch pressure

The batch should feel as though the book has moved by the time Chapter 10 ends.

Do not fill four chapters with four adjacent days merely because the prior run did.

Use time when repetition is producing state. Show the residue when the state matters.

Use action when the story can reveal character, capability, failure, relationship, or world state through pressure instead of explanation.

Action does not mean combat quota. For this batch it includes:

- a moving caravan emergency
- road violence
- a physical rescue under environmental pressure
- travel that changes bodies, habits, relationships, money, and competence

## State entering Chapter 7

- Greg has just repaid Vale and is nearly cash-poor again.
- Greg and Arlo are bounded partners.
- Ivena's first filter worked, then failed under real heat / flow / duration / residue conditions.
- Greg produced one palm-sized barrier for less than a heartbeat under emergency pressure.
- Greg now wants support magic back badly.
- Jorren leaves north soon on a caravan escort.
- Greg's useful sword package remains overhead cut + side step, with recovery now exposed as the next gap.
- Greg's old strength is increasingly visible as prioritization, systems thinking, and support instinct rather than restored power.
- Arlo, Ivena, Sella, Jorren, and Vale continue acting independently.

## Alternatives considered

### A. Stay in Carrow and buy support lessons

Useful for magic progression, weak for motion. Risks another chapter of prices, negotiation, workshop tests, and training explanation after Chapters 5–6 already worked those muscles.

### B. Vale pulls Greg into collection / enforcement work

Good money pressure and character leverage. Risks making Vale too central too early and repeating negotiation/social machinery before the world physically opens.

### C. Greg joins the first leg of Jorren's north-road caravan

Selected.

Why it wins:

- Jorren's departure becomes action rather than a note in state.
- Greg can earn money instead of immediately borrowing again.
- the road physically widens the book after six Carrow chapters.
- a caravan naturally exposes Greg to a working support specialist without giving him a bespoke teacher.
- travel allows honest multi-day time movement.
- moving systems create action that is not only monster combat.
- Greg's old support instincts can be tested where failure affects multiple people.
- Arlo/Ivena can progress off-camera, making return-time meaningful later.

## New supporting actor — Meral

Role: caravan warder / practical support mage.

Do not make Meral Greg's personal master.

Meral is paid to keep a caravan moving, not to repair Greg's second life.

Behavioral signature:

- measures usefulness by whether people and cargo remain functional
- dislikes dramatic spellcasting that solves the wrong problem
- gives short corrections when Greg's mistake creates work for everyone else
- treats barriers as geometry, timing, and load direction before raw power
- has no reason to be impressed by Greg's future knowledge

Meral may let Greg watch, copy a basic shaping drill, or answer a narrow question because an extra useful set of hands benefits the caravan.

## Chapter 7 — The Extra Guard

### Story function

Greg chooses motion over another week of trying to optimize Carrow.

The caravan needs a last-minute Bronze body for the first road leg after an escort turns an ankle badly enough to miss departure.

Greg notices the contract because Jorren is already attached to it. The pay is useful. More importantly, the roster includes a working warder.

Greg takes the first leg, not the entire north route. This creates a bounded absence from Carrow while allowing Jorren's trajectory to continue farther north afterward.

### Time

Departure occurs quickly after Chapter 6. Do not spend a chapter packing.

By the midpoint, Carrow should already be physically behind them.

### Action event

On a long downhill grade, a rear harness pin shears and a loaded wagon begins overtaking the team in front of it.

This is not a random set piece. It tests support thinking.

Greg's old instinct reaches for force he does not have. Meral instead forces the problem into geometry and load transfer.

Greg uses rope, wheel chocks, people placement, and one tiny mana brace at the exact moment a rope would otherwise slip.

Nobody needs to defeat an enemy for this to count as action.

### Discovery

> **Support is not the spell. Support is controlling which failure reaches people.**

### Exit state

- Greg earns his place on the road socially, not by being secretly overpowered.
- Meral notices he understands failure shapes but lacks basic mana control.
- Greg receives one practical shaping drill, not a training curriculum.
- Carrow is now behind him.

## Chapter 8 — Road Work

### Time

Jump several days.

Prove elapsed travel through residue:

- new blisters becoming harder skin
- Greg sleeping through noises that kept him awake on night one
- who takes which watch without discussion
- dust in clothes and food becoming repetitive
- Jorren and Greg no longer narrating every small task to each other
- Greg's shaping drill improving from random pulse to repeatable tiny plane

Do not summarize every travel day.

### Action event

A small road gang attacks at dusk after first creating a false blockage ahead.

Keep the attackers competent enough to have a plan and ordinary enough not to become a war arc.

Greg's future knowledge is not useful here. Current observation is.

The attackers use one cheap ward-break charm against Meral's first broad defense, proving that professional systems attract counters.

Greg cannot win a duel cleanly. He can:

- identify the real pressure point
- move a noncombatant before the attack reaches them
- use side step / overhead cut only where the room of the fight permits
- throw a tiny barrier into one projectile path
- call a warning before Jorren sees the second attacker

Jorren and Meral both solve problems Greg cannot.

### Discovery

> **A support mind should change the whole field even when its individual actions are small.**

And:

> **Prepared systems create prepared opposition.**

### Exit state

Greg is useful but depleted. His forearm tremor and headache make the cost real.

The caravan keeps moving because stopping after surviving an attack can create a second vulnerability.

## Chapter 9 — The Bridge

### Time

Jump another two or three days.

Weather has changed. Greg has improved slightly. The road has become routine enough that the next disruption matters.

### Action event

A rain-swollen crossing has damaged an old bridge approach. The structure is technically open but one loaded wagon breaks through softened edgework while crossing.

Do not make Greg omniscient about civil engineering.

His remembered future version of this road had a rebuilt stone bridge. The current structure is older and worse. Future knowledge actively misleads him for one beat.

A wagon lists toward the water with people and cargo still attached.

The rescue requires:

- Jorren physically getting to a trapped driver
- Meral holding a real ward against debris / line snap
- Greg identifying where a small brace changes the load
- ropes and draft animals
- people following clear commands under noise

Greg's tiny plane cannot hold the wagon. It can stop one rope from sawing sideways across a damaged post for a few critical seconds.

That is enough.

### Discovery

> **Small capability becomes large when placed at the constraint.**

And:

> **Future knowledge is a map of later reality, not present terrain.**

### Exit state

- Greg has a repeatable support act now, still tiny but intentional.
- the act is understood as brace / plane / load redirection rather than generic barrier.
- Meral gives Greg one correction that materially improves it.
- the caravan reaches the first-leg destination late and damaged rather than triumphantly untouched.

## Chapter 10 — What Moved

### Time

Do not make this another immediate roadside crisis.

Let Greg return to Carrow on a southbound caravan after the first leg while Jorren continues north.

Advance enough time that the absence has consequences. Target roughly two weeks away total, but render the exact duration through evidence rather than a date card.

### Primary action

The chapter's action is **return under changed state**, not another required fight.

Greg comes back physically altered by the road, carrying pay, a tiny repeatable support technique, and fewer illusions about what future knowledge buys him.

Meanwhile:

- Jorren is now genuinely absent and headed north.
- Arlo and Ivena have run warm-flow tests without Greg.
- at least one design choice Greg would have argued about has already been made.
- the filter project has either earned repeat interest or acquired a new real-world constraint.
- Sella's treatment of Greg reflects accumulated institutional familiarity.
- Vale has not been waiting motionless for Greg.

### Active scene

Do not make the whole chapter reunion recap.

Greg arrives while Arlo and Ivena are in the middle of a live test / customer decision that has already advanced beyond his last instructions.

He must decide whether to seize control because he recognizes the future technology or accept that the present project now belongs partly to people who learned while he was gone.

The scene should contain physical work and a real decision with consequences, not merely conversation.

A stronger version may let the revised filter survive a demanding run while Greg is useful mainly as an observer / risk-check rather than inventor.

### Discovery

> **Time is allowed to make Greg less central.**

His absence should create competence elsewhere, not freeze side plots until the protagonist returns.

### Batch-ending state

By Chapter 10:

- meaningful calendar time has passed without showing every day
- the setting has physically widened beyond Carrow
- Greg has experienced multiple kinds of action
- support magic has progressed from accidental flicker to one tiny intentional technique
- Greg remains far from old power
- Jorren has left on his own trajectory
- Arlo/Ivena have advanced without Greg
- Greg has earned money through action rather than debt
- future knowledge has both helped and misled him
- the story has at least two live forward pressures that do not require returning to the old plot

## Written-renderer pressure for the batch

Keep the existing prior:

> **Greg owns continuity. Other voices own moments only they can make happen.**

But action adds stricter timing.

In danger, favor:

**STIMULUS → DECISION / MOTOR RESPONSE → THOUGHT CATCHES UP**

over

**STIMULUS → EXPLANATION → DECISION → ACTION**

Greg's thought remains central, but the world does not wait for complete sentences.

## Stop rule

Do not add a fifth event to a chapter because the batch wants more action.

Each chapter gets one dominant causal movement. End once that movement creates the next live pressure.
