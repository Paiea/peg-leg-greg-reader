# Greg, Again — Chapter 14 Mara PERFORMANCE

Status: **DERIVED HIGH-HEAT REHEARSAL / NON-CANON**

Source state: selected written frontier after Chapter 13

Purpose: test the behavioral crossing around Mara's departure without prescribing reconciliation.

## State in

- Mara leaves Carrow in two days for a ledger position with Vey Shipping in Halden.
- Greg told her he wanted to see her before she left.
- Mara explicitly refused to wait for him to become organized around that desire.
- Greg now knows he lived in Mara's mother's back room for eight months after his parents died.
- Greg remembers Mara unevenly and does not remember two kisses she does.
- Greg has spent most of his road pay on a better sword and needs income again.
- Greg's strongest recurring failure is converting everything into a solvable system.

## Character pressure

### Mara

**WANT** — leave Carrow on her own terms, preserve dignity around Greg, and decide what relationship with this changed version of him is actually possible.

**INDEPENDENT MOTION** — accepted the Halden job, prepared her move, arranged departure, maintained family ties, and stopped organizing her future around Greg's promises.

**LEVERAGE** — she knows young Greg's ordinary life better than he currently does. She can falsify his self-story without trying.

**LIMIT** — she still cares enough that Greg's memory failure hurts. Anger is partly protective because hope would cost more.

**SCENE AGENCY** — she does not reward Greg merely for appearing. She decides what contact, if any, continues after departure.

### Greg

**WANT** — avoid repeating the mistake of letting urgent systems erase an ordinary person, recover some truthful continuity with his younger life, and not make promises based on memory he does not possess.

**PRESSURE** — useful paid work is available now; choosing Mara costs money. Once with Mara, there is no technical solution to missing history.

**LIMIT** — he wants to transform uncertainty into a plan. That instinct is not automatically useful here.

## High-heat uncertainty

> What do Greg and Mara actually do when he chooses to show up, but showing up cannot repair the missing relationship?

## Cheap rejected versions

### A. Clean reconciliation

Greg chooses Mara over work, apologizes well, remembers something important, and they restore romantic momentum.

Reject. Too reward-shaped. It makes the correct priority choice purchase intimacy.

### B. Emotional rupture

Mara decides Greg is too changed and cuts him off before leaving.

Viable but premature. It converts uncertainty into a hard answer before the relationship has accumulated enough new evidence.

### C. Practical contact with unresolved intimacy

Greg turns down useful work, goes to Mara's mother's house, helps with the move, encounters physical evidence of his forgotten ordinary life, and refuses to invent memories he does not have. Mara does not forgive the loss or ask him to come. She chooses a smaller state transition: continued contact after Halden, on terms she can control.

**Selected.** This preserves hurt, agency, forward possibility, and cost without making the chapter a romantic reward.

## PERFORMANCE beats

1. Sella offers Greg a useful two-day contract. It conflicts with Mara's departure window. He declines before constructing a clever workaround.
2. Greg uses the brass key at Mara's mother's house and discovers the lock still works.
3. Mara's mother recognizes him immediately and behaves from old familiarity, creating emotional pressure before explanation.
4. The back room contains mundane evidence of Greg's younger life, not a magical clue or destiny artifact.
5. Greg and Mara pack together. Work gives their hands something to do while the conversation remains difficult.
6. Greg recovers fragments, but no complete lost romance. Do not solve memory through effort.
7. Mara asks a future-facing question whose answer changes relationship state.
8. Greg refuses a grand promise. He can offer something smaller and verifiable.
9. Mara chooses whether that smaller offer is acceptable.
10. End on an ordinary object or action, not a stated lesson.

## Voice / renderer notes

- Mara's exact language is high-value. Do not over-mediate her.
- Greg tics should be sparse. Avoid repeated `Good`, `Fair`, `Right`, `There`, `Progress` markers.
- Do not explain the emotional lesson after the scene demonstrates it.
- Let packing, carrying, folding, and the old room carry memory residue.
- No support-magic progression in this chapter.
- No North Gate exposition unless it naturally intrudes and changes behavior. It likely does not belong here.

## Desired state out

- Greg has materially chosen time with Mara over available income.
- The choice does not buy forgiveness or romance.
- Greg has more evidence of young Greg's ordinary life.
- Mara leaves on schedule.
- A continuing relationship channel exists only if Mara actively chooses it.
- Greg has less cash than he would have had, preserving priority cost.
