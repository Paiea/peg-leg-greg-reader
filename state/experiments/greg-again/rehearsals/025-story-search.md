## Candidate A — The Tracker / follow the herd corridor

Greg and the others spend most of the next day following glasshorn sign to establish where the herd enters and leaves the Cut.

**Strength:** direct consequence of Chapter 24, keeps magical ecology practical, gives Harl present evidence.

**Weakness:** making the herd itself own a second full chapter would turn one earned ecological fact into a creature arc. The route needs to become larger than the glasshorns again.

**Use only as part of the remaining survey, not the dominant movement.**

## Candidate B — The Hired Sword / finish the route

The remaining survey days compress around the actual question the road office hired this party to answer.

Working movement:

1. Harl follows the fresh glasshorn tracks far enough to locate where the herd leaves the main Cut near the old quarry spur. The seasonal constraint is real but geographically bounded rather than fatal to the whole route.
2. The west section produces a different kind of evidence: actual present-day traffic already using the Cut because the formal roads / North Gate are slow. A lone courier or very small light pack movement comes east through the route.
3. Edda owns the encounter and safe passing geometry. Greg behaves as the second paid escort rather than taking over.
4. Harl questions the current user briefly because present use is survey evidence. The traveler confirms the Cut is being used opportunistically, not reliably: dry weather, light loads, one animal at a time in the narrow west section, and avoidance when the slope is soaked.
5. Harl verifies the west slope himself rather than accepting either the traveler or Greg as authority.
6. The rest of the route is completed without manufacturing another emergency. Repetition becomes residue: walking, measuring, wet gear, Pate's habits, Edda / Greg watch shorthand, Harl's increasingly compressed instructions.
7. Harl's final result is neither `the road works` nor `the road is dead`.

Working verdict:

> **USEFUL FOR FOOT COURIERS AND LIGHT EXPERIENCED PACK TRAFFIC UNDER CONDITIONS. NOT RELIABLE FOR CARTS OR GENERAL FREIGHT IN ITS CURRENT STATE.**

The broken stone crossing, usable ford, seasonal glasshorn corridor, and wet west slope all matter to the conditions.

This can plausibly become one small ancestor of the later West Cut Greg remembers as mostly foot traffic, goats, and people avoiding tolls, but the chapter does not prove that this survey caused the later road history.

The party returns to Carrow and completes the contract. Greg receives the agreed field pay. Exact coin value need not be invented if the existing contract text did not establish it clearly.

A tiny return-home residue may show that Lower Rook continued without Greg, but no second plot should start after the route verdict lands.

**Strength:**

- completes the active paid transaction instead of serializing a four-to-six-day job indefinitely;
- turns the road from an empty object into infrastructure people are already adapting around;
- lets another current person exist independently without making them a future-famous figure or new cast obligation;
- preserves Edda's escort authority and Harl's survey authority;
- allows Greg to be a real adventurer whose job is often paid competence, patience, walking, restraint, and being the second sword rather than the hero;
- produces a durable material answer for the North Gate logistics pressure without returning to the North Gate itself;
- gives the West Cut a nuanced current identity instead of a binary success/failure state;
- creates natural repeated-work shorthand among Greg, Edda, Harl, and Pate without declaring a party.

## Candidate C — The Brace / west-slope failure and support magic

Post-rain ground gives way under Pate or a loaded pannier. Greg uses a tiny Barrier plane at high leverage while Edda and Harl manage the animal and load.

**Strength:** magic would be causally available and could give Greg another clean win.

**Weakness:** recent R2 already contains bridge failure, gate failure, building fire, and multiple small support-rescue beats. Selecting another wet structural / mechanical near-failure because `Greg may do magic` would look like curriculum and repeated action grammar.

**Preserve.** Magic does not need to be used merely because two field chapters passed without it.

## Candidate D — The Returner / go back to Carrow immediately

Skip the remaining survey and open on Greg returning home to whatever changed while he was gone.

**Strength:** preserves serial breadth and lets Carrow move without him.

**Weakness:** Chapter 24 explicitly leaves the route verdict unresolved. Skipping the job's answer would make the multi-day contract feel like scenery rather than a durable transaction.

**Reject for 25.** Return-home residue can follow the completed field result briefly, or own a later chapter if Carrow has actually changed enough.

## Selection

**B — The Hired Sword / finish the route.**

The chapter identity is:

> **THE HIRED SWORD**

Not because Greg wins a duel.

He is the second Bronze escort on a paid field job. He walks where Edda tells him, watches where Harl needs time to measure, helps manage encounters, carries what needs carrying, and remains dangerous enough that other people can do their own work safely.

The useful irony is that a hired sword's good day may involve almost no sword use.

The chapter should complete the contract and let that completion count as progression in profession, money, field relationships, and geographic range without promoting Greg or declaring a permanent party.

## PERFORMANCE decision

Do **not** run full PERFORMANCE.

Outside agency is bounded enough:

- Edda wants safe passage and owns escort decisions;
- Harl wants an evidence-supported route verdict and owns certification;
- a current road user wants to get through, not join Greg's story;
- Greg owns his paid escort work, old field judgment where relevant, and the strange satisfaction of doing a familiar profession at a lower rank in a younger body.
