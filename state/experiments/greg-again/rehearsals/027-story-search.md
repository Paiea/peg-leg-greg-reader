## WHAT SHOULD ACTUALLY HAPPEN NEXT?

Chapter 26 gives Greg something new: margin.

He has one maintained body, maintained road kit, some cash still available, no immediate crisis, and a field pack beside the door because another adventure now feels plausible.

The strongest current decision is what he does with a day that is no longer forced by immediate scarcity.

Greg keeps saying, through action rather than speech, that he wants to be an adventurer and a warrior in this life. In Chapter 2, lack of money reduced sword development to a free yard block, one borrowed question, and self-directed repetition. He has since accumulated real field work, a better sword, repeated Bronze combat experience, body conditioning, and enough cash to buy one proper instructional block.

That makes the live question:

> **WHAT HAPPENS WHEN GREG FINALLY PAYS TO BE A STUDENT AGAIN?**

## Candidate A — The Student / paid sword instruction

Greg returns to the Guild yard and pays for a real block with the faded-Silver instructor from Chapter 2.

She remembers him as the man who was trying to `assemble furniture` with his sword and immediately refuses to teach the imaginary expert in his head. She evaluates the body and movement he actually has now.

The chapter should prove that Greg has improved materially since Chapter 2 without pretending he is secretly elite:

- his overhead cut is now serviceable Bronze work rather than a committee meeting;
- his side step is smaller and more reliable;
- road work has improved balance, endurance, and movement under load;
- he reads shoulder, weight, breath, and commitment very well;
- the good sword fits him better than the old cheap blade did;
- he still loses mechanical continuity after commitment.

The instructor identifies the present bottleneck as **recovery**.

Not stamina recovery.

Weapon/body recovery after a committed action.

Greg still has a tendency to treat `I saw the opening` as permission to leave his current movement before it has finished safely. That is the same family of problem Chapter 2 exposed when he stepped correctly, saw an opening, improvised an unpracticed counter, and got tapped in the forehead.

The paid lesson gives that old residue a real next layer rather than inventing a new training doctrine.

Possible practical grammar:

> **CUT. ARRIVE. RECOVER.**

or another equally plain instructor phrase discovered in prose.

The lesson should include one clean Greg advantage. When the instructor feeds him repeated attacks or changes rhythm, Greg reads one change unusually early and responds correctly because decades of watching bodies are real experience. She notices. It does not promote him.

### Gold ceiling opportunity

The Guild yard is one of the few places where a Gold party can appear without narrative contrivance.

If the prose earns it, a working Gold party may use or cross an adjacent reserved lane during Greg's paid block.

Rules:

- they are **not** created for Greg to embarrass;
- Greg should be able to understand why they are excellent even when he cannot execute what they do;
- competence should appear through recovery, spacing, quiet role handoff, integrated magic, equipment, and timing rather than giant spell spectacle;
- the instructor remains Greg's authority and can tell him to stop watching them if necessary;
- they do not recruit Greg, know his destiny, or become a new quest;
- they may remain unnamed or only partially identified if names do not matter yet.

Best use: the Gold party makes Greg's recovery lesson legible at a higher scale. Their actions do not end when a strike lands. Every action already contains the next safe position, another person's movement, or the team's next option.

This lets the reader see that **Gold is genuinely excellent** while preserving enormous distance to Old Greg's eventual S-class status.

**Strength:**

- directly spends the economic margin Chapter 26 earned;
- pays Chapter 2 training residue after twenty-five chapters instead of resetting it;
- advances warrior identity without rank-up or montage;
- gives Greg a clean competence win and a real present limitation in the same domain without mandatory humiliation;
- naturally permits a first Gold-party glimpse without making Gold the plot;
- keeps support assessment distant and does not turn every progression beat into magic;
- makes one silver instruction cost meaningful again while showing Greg can now choose to pay it.
