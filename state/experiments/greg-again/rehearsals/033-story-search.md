## Selection — first distribution

Greg is called to the workshop because Arlo / Ivena need all three owners to agree on what stays in the venture versus what can come out.

Useful pressures:

- cash in the box is not profit until current obligations / reserve are accounted for;
- Arlo may want more retained for tools / firing risk;
- Ivena may want enough reserve to protect service promises and customer trust;
- Greg's future memory may make him want aggressive growth, but present governance should remain evidence-driven;
- Greg should recognize that taking some money out is not betraying growth and reinvesting everything is not automatically wisdom.

Result:

- a modest distribution is approved;
- Greg receives a concrete small amount greater than zero and meaningful against ordinary wages, but nowhere near wealth;
- this is the first selected moment where Greg is paid partly because an owned system operated while he was absent;
- business retains enough reserve to remain healthy;
- Ivena / Arlo continue to own operations rather than handing the enterprise back to Greg.

No new product breakthrough required.
