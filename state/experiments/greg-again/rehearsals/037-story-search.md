## WHAT SHOULD ACTUALLY HAPPEN NEXT?

The relay job is complete. Greg has accumulated enough professional and economic improvement that the ordinary shape of his life should be allowed to change too.

Do not send him immediately into another contract.

## Live pressure

Greg still lives in the same narrow Lower Rook room where Chapter 1 began:

- cramped enough that early sword work threatened furniture
- little separation between sleep, desk, sword, road gear, and washing
- useful cheap location
- current neighbor relationship with Noll
- building now carries post-fire repair history

Since then Greg has:

- completed multiple paid road / field / ward / creature jobs
- maintained a real field kit
- bought a better sword
- paid one silver voluntarily for instruction
- accumulated some cash reserve
- received a first 9-copper venture distribution
- begun expecting the field pack to be used again

A recurring-cost decision can now matter more than another one-time purchase.

## Selected movement

A slightly larger room in the **same Lower Rook building** becomes available because another tenant moves out.

The building's rent collector / manager offers it to Greg before advertising it because:

- he has paid reliably enough
- he is already in the building
- moving an existing tenant is easier than finding a new one

The room should not be luxurious.

Useful differences:

- enough floor space for a real practice line if furniture is moved
- better drying space for road gear
- a small second window / better light
- maybe a narrow alcove or shelf where the field pack does not compete with the bed

Cost should be meaningfully higher every week / month.

Do not invent an exact grand economic conversion system. Use relative numbers already meaningful to Greg.

## Decision

Greg should **take the room** if the prose makes the recurring cost rational.

This is stronger than having him admire improvement and remain frozen in Chapter 1 housing forever.

The move should remain inside Lower Rook so ordinary social accumulation survives:

- Noll remains nearby
- same cookroom / stairs / neighborhood
- Greg changes capability without deleting home history

Young / Old memory pressure may arise because Chapter 1 room itself is evidence, but do not make the move melodramatic.

Greg is allowed to improve his life.

## New bounded actor

Create a bounded building manager / rent collector only as needed. Do not give them plot gravity merely because they are named.
