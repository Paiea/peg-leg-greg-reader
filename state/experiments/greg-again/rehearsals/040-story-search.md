## WHAT SHOULD ACTUALLY HAPPEN NEXT?

The North Gate restriction has been affecting Carrow for weeks through rerouted freight, support staffing, lower-quay pressure, West Cut demand, and ordinary prices.

Enough time has passed for the city's repair program to produce a current result.

Selected movement: **North Gate reopens one controlled lane to light carts and ordinary foot traffic after current engineers complete a bounded repair phase.**

Greg does not cause the reopening and does not receive privileged warning.

## Why now

- repair crews and support rotations have been working throughout the intervening weeks;
- First Deepwinter is a credible pressure point for restoring some logistics before harsher weather;
- current Carrow has economic reason to reopen usable capacity as soon as it passes present load testing.

## Greg pressure

Greg remembers the North Gate eventually collapses.

He does **not** remember enough chronology or causation to know whether:

- today's repair later fails;
- a different section fails;
- later modifications cause the collapse;
- today's work delays the disaster;
- Greg's remembered event is farther away than he thinks.

Let current engineers be right **today**.

The reopening should have practical residue immediately:

- carts move differently;
- lower-quay / alternate-route premiums soften;
- some Guild work disappears while other work returns;
- ordinary goods may become slightly easier to move.

Do not solve the long shadow.
