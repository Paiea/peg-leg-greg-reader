## Artifact/tool quarry

Use an old **brace ring**:

- palm-to-fist sized metal ring with a ceramic inner seat and movable attachment tabs;
- originally used to create temporary known connection points on rope, timber, carts, or field structures for low-load support / ward work;
- obsolete compared with later standardized hardware;
- current unit is missing one ceramic seat segment and is unsafe for rated heavy load;
- current seller/appraiser knows it is damaged and obsolete;
- Greg knows from later field work that the older ring remains useful at modest load once repaired because it accepts improvised anchor geometry later models became less tolerant of.

Do not make it secretly priceless.

## Decision

Greg must still price:

- purchase cost;
- repair cost;
- whether he has any current use;
- whether buying it competes with support instruction / rent / ordinary cash.

Let him get a real bargain if the numbers work.

Do not repair or use it in the same chapter. Unspent option value may remain on the shelf.
