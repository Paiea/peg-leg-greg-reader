## Current written grammar under pressure

### GREG OWNS CONTINUITY

Greg's perception, wrong models, body, memory, money sense, tactical attention, embarrassment, and associative thought may carry the reader from beat to beat when that is the most interesting surface.

Greg owns POV continuity, not reality, causality, expertise, or everybody else's language.

### OUTSIDE VOICE EARNS DIRECT SPACE FOR

- **FALSIFICATION** — another mind challenges Greg in a way he cannot honestly generate for himself
- **STATE TRANSITION** — offers, refusals, prices, promises, threats, wagers, consent, deadlines, ownership terms, and other language that changes obligations
- **MOTOR INTERRUPT** — the useful sequence is HEAR → MOVE → UNDERSTAND
- **IRREPLACEABLE CHARACTER LANGUAGE** — exact wording carries humor, intimacy, threat, leverage, identity, or surprise that Greg's mediation weakens
- **DOMAIN AUTHORITY** — another actor owns information Greg needs faster or more accurately than he can infer it
- **RELATIONSHIP STATE** — another person's wording changes what the relationship is allowed to be afterward

### TIME AS RESIDUE

> **Elapsed time should arrive as changed evidence before summary when possible.**

Skip repetition once its pattern is established. Return when residue changes a live choice.

### PROGRESSION OPENS THE NEXT CONSTRAINT

Success should often reveal the next deficiency instead of functioning as a static reward screen.

Do not turn that into curriculum. Progress may disappear from the foreground, regress, develop bad compensation, improve off-page, or become irrelevant for several chapters.

### THOUGHT MUST EARN CAUSAL SPACE

Thought is action when it changes the next bet, cut, step, purchase, refusal, question, instruction, or choice under pressure.

Strong pattern:

```text
STIMULUS
→ GREG MODEL / EXPECTATION
→ ACTION INTERRUPTS OR FALSIFIES IT
→ MOTOR RESPONSE / DECISION
→ THOUGHT UPDATES AFTER CONSEQUENCE
```

Do not explain the lesson after the scene already proved it unless the interpretation itself changes the next action.

### DO NOT MANUFACTURE GREG

Recurring Greg language is allowed only when it carries a real thought turn.

Do not stack surface markers to simulate voice.

The audio listen test removes any remaining excuse to add those markers merely for synthetic performance.

### GREG'S STRENGTHS CREATE SPECIFIC FAILURE MODES

Greg is genuinely good at models, systems, future leads, people-reading, support prioritization, and leverage.

Those strengths should create temptations specific to the strength.

### VALUE TYPES STAY DISTINCT

Potential value, cash, capability, reputation, ownership, access, information, and leverage are related but not automatically interchangeable.

### SCALE CHANGES THE PROBLEM

Small successful evidence does not automatically survive more time, flow, heat, people, money, danger, or institutional load.

### OLD EXPERTISE MAY ARRIVE BEFORE OLD POWER

Greg may recognize the shape of a failure, danger, negotiation, or support problem long before his nineteen-year-old body or mana can execute the remembered solution.

### TINY CAPABILITY CAN MATTER AT HIGH LEVERAGE

The useful variable may be angle, timing, load direction, or placement rather than magnitude.

### ACTION IS STATE CHANGE UNDER PRESSURE

Combat is one kind of action.

Technical failure, gambling, negotiation, industrial hazard, mechanical emergency, road violence, rescue, travel, intimacy, magical failure, institutional pressure, relationship confrontation, and binding ownership decisions can all count when decisions immediately change state.

### FUTURE KNOWLEDGE IS LATER REALITY, NOT PRESENT TERRAIN

Future knowledge may be correct and still be insufficient because Greg lacks chronology, causation, intermediate history, current ownership, present infrastructure, and the people who actually produced the remembered result.

### TIME MAY MAKE GREG LESS CENTRAL

Supporting characters are allowed to become more competent, leave, gain opportunities, build relationships, and change systems while Greg is absent.

### LONG SHADOW

The local chapter engine is strong enough.

The novel now also needs pressures whose meaningful payoff lives much farther ahead.

Do not immediately validate or solve them.

### STOP WHEN THE CAUSAL CHAIN LANDS

Do not append extra rewards merely because good material is available.

### 19 — Who Cleans It

`state/experiments/greg-again/prose/019-who-cleans-it.md`

Story search:

`state/experiments/greg-again/rehearsals/019-story-search.md`

Five ordinary days pass after Greg books the support assessment. He trains lightly, earns a little ordinary warehouse-count money, and deliberately does not keep checking Arlo's workshop.

Arlo and Ivena move the filtration venture without him. A tannery trial proves the unit works under real process load, then exposes a less glamorous failure: by the third day the removable chamber is packed with tannery solids and the customer does not want to buy a device that creates an hour of recurring cleaning labor.

Greg reaches for the mature-world solution of interchangeable replacement chambers. Current fabrication falsifies the shortcut. Firing shrinkage, hand-finished collars, and small dimensional variation mean one clean chamber does not simply fit another housing even when both were built to the same nominal design.

Ivena solves the present problem instead of the forty-year problem. Each tannery unit can have its own matched spare chamber. The clean matched chamber is swapped in quickly; the dirty one returns to Arlo's workshop for cleaning and later return.

A tannery wash worker also improves the pressure tongue by asking for visible warning colors that survive dirty working conditions better than black marks.

The tannery accepts one unit for a two-week service trial, not a six-unit order. The venture receives a small weekly service payment only while the unit remains usable. If a needed swap is missed, the tannery bypasses the unit and does not pay for that interval.

The result opens another scale problem: recurring revenue creates recurring labor, route time, spare-stock needs, cleaning capacity, and failure responsibility. North Gate rerouting appears only as ordinary traffic friction on the walk home.

Surviving discovery:

> **A product that works once is not yet a system that keeps working.**

And:

> **Recurring value and recurring obligation are the same structure seen from opposite sides.**

Do not convert that second line into business-theory dialogue. The chapter proves it by making Greg carry the dirty chamber home.

### 20 — Ward Hand

`state/experiments/greg-again/prose/020-ward-hand.md`

Story search:

`state/experiments/greg-again/rehearsals/020-story-search.md`

Sella uses Greg's new `ward-support intake / low reserve` classification in the least glamorous useful way available: a bounded paid assistant shift under commercial warder Meren Voss while ordinary support capacity is backlogged.

The work is maintenance rather than curriculum. Greg carries tools, scrapes commercial ward seams, records site details, and holds a five-inch temporary plane exactly where Meren directs while she opens or replaces small ward sections.

His existing plane is enough to be useful for several breaths without increasing in magnitude. A routine lamp-oil warehouse replacement establishes that the difference between several breaths and several useful breaths is placement, timing, and following the working warder's procedure.

At an apothecary storehouse, Greg's later support experience earns a real advantage before failure. He notices that a newly installed adjacent ward line is coupling through a shared wall / metal shelf brace into an older threshold ward, creating intermittent heat at one anchor. Meren verifies the coupled load and shuts down the adjacent charge cabinet before opening the old seam.

Greg then imports a later-world instinct and angles his temporary plane toward a floor drain as a preferred discharge path. Meren immediately corrects him because this current building has iron under the stone grate. Later practice assumed infrastructure that does not exist here.

The repair remains current-world, procedural, and supervised. Meren restores temporary isolation and records that permanent spacing work requires a later authorized call.

Greg finishes four sites before evening bell and earns six copper directly for support-adjacent work. He receives no rank change, no assessment credit, no lesson, no exemption from the First Deepwinter appointment, and no standing job.

Surviving discovery:

> **Support identity is not waiting behind the assessment. It already exists at the scale Greg can actually perform.**

And:

> **Several breaths become capability when somebody can depend on where they happen.**

Do not convert that into a power-up. The chapter's strongest evidence is that boring commercial maintenance can pay Greg for five inches of correctly placed mana.
