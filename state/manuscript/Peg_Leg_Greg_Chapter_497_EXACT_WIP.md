# CHAPTER 497

## THE CORRESPONDENT

Mail found me at breakfast.

This was suspicious.

Pell held envelope between two fingers.

"Greg?"

"Unfortunately."

"Carrow."

Lyssa's handwriting on front.

Not Lakeward.

Pell noticed.

Of course.

Growth everywhere.

Two pages.

First line:

**Tavi says if you can carry soup now, you owe him soup.**

I laughed.

There.

News traveled faster than dignity.

Then:

**I told him you carried water and one drop escaped. He says water is easier because soup has consequences. I don't know what this means. Neither does he.**

Good.

Next paragraph was not about me.

Better.

Lyssa had picked up two extra evening shifts because a woman named Sera had gone home to help sick mother.

That sounded correct.

Lyssa might go if shifts allowed.

No promise.

No Greg-shaped hole in every sentence.

I read slower.

Then request.

**Can you check whether Duskport has the narrow blue binding cord you bought last winter? Carrow seller hasn't had it for two weeks. Don't buy a crate. I need maybe six arm lengths. If it's expensive, leave it.**

There.

Task.

Not rescue.

Not confession.

Cord.

I knew exactly shop.

Maybe.

Dangerous phrase.

Last line:

**Tell Masta I support soup science only if she does.**

Pressure.

Two days.

No.

Then went looking for cord.

Not immediately.

After tea.

Important distinction.

Not really quarter.

Two streets and alley pretending to be district.

Blue binding cord existed in first shop.

Wrong blue.

Too bright.

Second shop had right color but thick.

Third had narrow cord in five colors.

One looked correct.

I did not buy crate.

Heroism.

"Six arm lengths," I said.

Shop woman looked at my arms.

"Whose?"

Fair.

"Twelve feet."

"That's not six of yours."

"I revised standard."

She measured.

Price three bits.

I had no idea if expensive relative to Carrow cord.

Lyssa had said if expensive leave it.

Three bits for twelve feet felt not expensive.

Dangerous intuition.

"Usual?" I asked.

"For dyed narrow cotton? Yes."

"Any cheaper undyed?"

"Half."

"Need blue."

"Then you need blue price."

Excellent economy.

I bought it.

Not crate.

Cord fit pocket.

I considered sending immediately.

Post office could.

But I would likely return Carrow after next fitting unless Masta changed plan.

Two days.

Could carry myself.

No urgency stated.

I wrote reply at Anchor's End.

**Lyssa,**

Then stopped.

Too formal.

Crossed out.

New sheet.

**Found cord. Narrow blue, twelve feet, three bits. If wrong blue I will claim Duskport lighting fraud.**

Good.

**Fitting went better. Masta still has leg. I carried two cups with water across room after adjustment. One earlier attempt ended on wrong table because I started reaching for support and pressure changed. Masta said putting it down was correct. Next fitting in two days.**

Enough.

I wanted to write:

**It felt good.**

I wrote it.

Then stared.

Why was that harder than posterior pressure report?

Annoying.

I left it.

**Tell Tavi soup remains advanced certification.**

Then ordinary things.

Anchor's End food decent.

Pell insults clients equally.

Duskport still vertical.

That sentence looked bad.

**I did not buy the broken compass. This is important context.**

Better.

No Lakeward paragraph.

Nothing had happened.

Silence was not update.

No need to send anxiety as information.

Could send cord with letter.

Would cost postage.

Could carry in two days.

Her request was maybe six arm lengths because Carrow seller out two weeks.

If I waited two days, still useful.

Unknown.

Three bits cord.

Small packet postage maybe worth option.

Asked.

One bit to Carrow with letter at this weight.

Fine.

Sent.

There.

Task complete.

Not because chapter needed event.

Because afternoon existed.

I watched from outside work area.

Normal.

Normal.

Then stopped noticing.

Important maybe.

Not every two-handed carry was prophecy.

Both crutches made carrying paper tray annoying.

Same as yesterday.

No lesson.

Just logistics.

Could have asked vendor carry it.

Did not need.

Could have waited for table.

Did not want.

Body remained body.

At second bell, rain started.

Duskport rain came sideways because city had opinions.

One hand.

Crutch under other.

Nothing transformative.

Not to me specifically.

Good.

Entire crisis.

Of course.

Brain still checked.

Stupid.

Crutches against bed.

**NO LAKEWARD WORD.**

Duskport continued around me.

Strange.
