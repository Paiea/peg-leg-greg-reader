# CALA — VOICE AUTHORITY

Exact manuscript prose outranks this file.

## Core lens

Cala sees the household as a stream of immediate practical needs: food, cleaning, children, repairs, who is where, what can be carried, what has to happen before noon, and whether Greg is making a simple problem more complicated by speaking.

She often knows local people and routes because she lives inside the neighborhood rather than because she is collecting a network.

## Spoken rhythm

Compact, ordinary, frequently missing words Greg would include because she assumes the rest is obvious.

Her brevity should feel domestic/local, not methodological.

She may answer a complicated Greg question with the one fact that matters to the household.

## Humor

Understated. Sometimes teasing Greg by refusing the grandeur of his framing.

She does not need to understand his joke to beat it. Literal household reality often does the work.

## Disagreement / refusal

Cala can simply say no, move an object, take the knife, hand him the thing she wants carried, or continue working.

Her authority is often behavioral rather than argued.

## Information behavior

She knows a great deal of neighborhood context casually:
- where somebody is working today;
- whose basket it is;
- what a person usually carries;
- whether a local repair exists.

She does not conceptualize this as a system and should not sound like she does.

## With Greg

Familiar enough to order him around in small household ways and ignore most of his mock bureaucracy.

She can also make his disability logistics ordinary by noticing what works without turning it into a speech.

## Canon lines / fingerprints

- **CANON LINE:** `Useful.`
- **CANON LINE:** `No.`
- **CANON LINE:** `Bag.`
- **CANON LINE:** `Do not break other handle.`
- **CANON LINE:** `Mera's.`

These lines are evidence of assumed context, not a mandate that Cala speak only in fragments.

## Voice tests — NON-CANON

- `Eat first.`
- `You can talk after you carry it.`
- `Olan is south today.`

Calibration only.

## Overlap allowed

Cala and Lyssa can both puncture Greg's abstraction with practical reality. Cala's version is more household/neighborhood and less intimate-partner specific.

## Watch list

Avoid:
- mystical neighborhood oracle;
- Hessa-style uncertainty rhetoric;
- making every line comic minimalism;
- turning local familiarity into formal information infrastructure.
