# HARA — VOICE AUTHORITY

Exact manuscript prose outranks this file.

## Core lens

Hara reads scenes from inside performance. She notices whether a partner is giving her something playable, whether a beat lands, whether an audience can follow, and whether a recovery preserves the scene rather than merely fixing mechanics.

She is a strong performer first, not Greg's acting teacher.

## Spoken rhythm

Usually direct and peer-level.

Her notes tend to come from what she just experienced in the scene rather than abstract theory. She can say more when explaining a live recovery or why a partner's choice stole/returned agency.

## Humor

Dry, social, occasionally teasing. She does not need to compete with Greg for the cleverest line.

She can let him embarrass himself and then name the exact stage problem.

## Disagreement / refusal

Hara can correct Greg without softening the note into mentorship.

She may also simply recover around him in performance and address it later.

## Information behavior

She knows performance timing and partner behavior that Greg may not notice from inside his own head.

She does not automatically care how his analytical model works if the stage result is already obvious to her.

## With Greg

Peer energy should increase as he becomes more competent. She can tease, correct, recover, and occasionally trust him without turning every interaction into instruction.

## Canon lines / fingerprints

- **CANON LINE:** `Early.`
- **CANON LINE:** `Don't.`
- **CANON LINE:** `You're already interesting because the face changed. You don't have to tell us you noticed.`
- **CANON PATTERN:** In live performance she can bridge around Greg's mistake and restore the scene without stopping to teach him in the moment.

## Voice tests — NON-CANON

- `You gave me the answer before I asked for it.`
- `Keep the look. Lose the announcement.`
- `I can fix that onstage. I would rather not.`

Calibration only.

## Overlap allowed

Hara and Teren may identify the same performance flaw. Teren tends to frame it as a playable correction from authority; Hara can frame it as what her scene partner just did to her side of the scene.

## Watch list

Avoid:
- acting mentor promotion;
- making her universally insightful;
- having her always explain Greg to himself;
- stealing her strongest performance beats by immediately translating them through Greg's analysis.
