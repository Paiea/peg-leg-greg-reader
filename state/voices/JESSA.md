# JESSA — VOICE AUTHORITY

Exact manuscript prose outranks this file.

## Core lens

Jessa tends to read the work directly: marks, pieces, material, what has already been done, what still needs doing, and whether Greg is currently useful or in the way.

Her work relationship with Lyssa/Marra carries assumed context Greg often does not share.

## Spoken rhythm

Direct, work-first, often economical because she is handling something while talking.

She can use compressed shorthand with people who already know the job. Greg may hear only the surface words.

## Humor

Dry and sometimes dismissive. She can tease Greg without turning the exchange into a comedy routine.

## Disagreement / refusal

Jessa does not need to entertain irrelevant questions. She can tell Greg he is done and return to the work.

When another worker already knows what she means, she may not stop to translate for him.

## Information behavior

She is often more interested in marks/material than Greg's story about how the object reached her.

She may know where Lyssa is or what Lyssa meant because that work relationship predates Greg's involvement.

## With Greg

Greg increasingly learns where to stand, what not to touch, and when his part is over. Jessa's voice toward him should reflect decreasing need for onboarding.

## Canon lines / fingerprints

- **CANON LINE:** `For me.`
- **CANON LINE:** `I can read.`
- **CANON LINE:** `Now you know six means six.`
- **CANON LINE:** `Go home, Road one.`
- **CANON PATTERN:** She can read Lyssa's work marks immediately where Greg cannot.

## Voice tests — NON-CANON

- `Put it there.`
- `I need the cloth, not the story.`
- `She marked it. That's enough.`

Calibration only.

## Overlap allowed

Jessa can be concise like Nessa because both work with physical continuity. Jessa's domain is garment/material workflow; Nessa's is theatre object/reset/route continuity.

## Watch list

Avoid:
- Hessa-style methodological speeches;
- generic stern worker;
- explaining garment work for reader benefit when Greg does not need the explanation;
- making her relationship to Marra/Lyssa formally cleaner than the manuscript establishes.
