# MAREK — VOICE AUTHORITY

Exact manuscript prose outranks this file.

## Core lens

Marek is a performer with social momentum. He notices what is funny, what can be played, what object is available to touch, what embarrassment can be survived through confidence, and sometimes what practical route/problem everyone else missed because he interacted with the thing first.

He is not Greg with louder jokes.

## Spoken rhythm

More willing than Greg to commit before fully analyzing.

He can:
- answer with confidence;
- improvise;
- exaggerate;
- defend a bad idea because it is already happening;
- pivot quickly when a concrete correction lands.

His rhythm can be more socially forward and less internally qualified than Greg's.

## Humor

Performance energy. Teasing, shamelessness, self-preservation, turning embarrassment into material.

The joke should sometimes cost him something or expose him, not always prove he is clever.

## Disagreement / refusal

Marek can argue reflexively, but he responds well to concrete domain-shaped correction when the person has earned authority there.

He does not need a long debate every time Nessa says stop or a performer corrects a cue.

## Information behavior

He may know current theatre changes better than paper because he was in the rehearsal where they changed.

He can also create information by messing with an object, route, or scene. That result may be useful or annoying.

## With Greg

More peer/rival/comic energy than methodological debate. They can needle each other, compete lightly, and share performance context.

Marek should sometimes care more about whether something plays than whether Greg has correctly categorized it.

## Canon lines / fingerprints

- **CANON LINE:** `Again?` after Greg claims to have seized the theatre.
- **CANON LINE:** `Pavin makes it one.`
- **CANON LINE:** `List is wrong. We changed it yesterday.`
- **CANON PATTERN:** Concrete correction such as Nessa's `Royal stillness` can make him immediately become still.
- **CANON PATTERN:** His object meddling sometimes creates downstream work and sometimes discovers useful route information.

## Voice tests — NON-CANON

- `It worked until you looked at it.`
- `I knew that. I was testing whether you did.`
- `The chair survived. This feels judgmental.`

Calibration only.

## Overlap allowed

Marek and Greg can both joke constantly in the right scene. Difference: Greg often uses humor to analyze/control discomfort. Marek more often uses humor to keep social momentum and convert embarrassment into performance.

## Watch list

Avoid:
- permanent idiot;
- chaos gremlin with no competence;
- Greg syntax plus extra exclamation;
- every object touch causing trouble;
- every Nessa exchange becoming scold/comic punishment;
- making him secretly profound whenever the manuscript needs a lesson.
