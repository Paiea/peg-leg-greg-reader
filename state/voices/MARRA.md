# MARRA — VOICE AUTHORITY

Exact manuscript prose outranks this file.

## Core lens

Marra lives inside garment-work traffic, customer memory, material movement, favors, and people arriving with partial context. She often knows who Greg is through Lyssa before Greg understands why.

Her voice should feel socially embedded rather than analytically systematized.

## Spoken rhythm

Conversational and work-interrupted. She can answer while doing something else, call across a room, hand off a message, or give Greg one phrase that only makes sense because the work network already has history.

She does not need to translate all shorthand for him.

## Humor

Social teasing and familiarity. She may enjoy Greg's confusion more than solving it.

## Disagreement / refusal

Marra can redirect Greg toward the person who actually needs the information, ignore his attempt to formalize the situation, or tell him the one message worth carrying.

## Information behavior

She often has more social context than Greg and less interest in turning that context into a theory.

She can know that:
- Lyssa asked something;
- Jessa already handled something;
- a customer is expected;
- a count changed or did not change.

She does not need to explain how everybody became connected.

## With Greg

Greg is partly an amusing peripheral person inside a work world that existed before him. His belonging appears through less explanation and more assumed history.

The label `the road one` is relationship/social residue, not a universal nickname.

## Canon lines / fingerprints

- **CANON PATTERN:** Recognizes Greg through Lyssa's prior social history before he fully enters the work relationship.
- **CANON PATTERN:** Can pass him a narrow message without making him manager or courier.
- **CANON PATTERN:** Work continues around him and does not pause to orient him.

## Voice tests — NON-CANON

- `Lyssa knows. Just tell her six stayed six.`
- `You don't need the rest.`
- `Ask Jessa. She has the marks.`

Calibration only.

## Overlap allowed

Marra and Jessa share garment-work shorthand because they share work. The distinction need not be theatrical. Marra can lean more social/traffic-facing; Jessa can lean more immediately material/task-focused where manuscript evidence supports it.

## Watch list

Avoid:
- formal business-manager voice;
- exposition hub;
- making every appearance a Greg referral opportunity;
- turning ordinary relationships into explicit network analysis.
