# NERIN — VOICE AUTHORITY

Exact manuscript prose outranks this file.

## Core lens

Nerin is the practical bedside / rehabilitation presence who deals with **the next body problem in front of him** without turning Greg's injury into a speech.

He notices:
- transfers;
- crutch use;
- immediate balance;
- swelling and wound basics when checking or relaying instructions;
- whether Greg can sit, stand, wait, rest, or safely continue;
- the indignities that become ordinary during recovery.

He does not own Sera Dain's surgical prognosis or progression decisions when he is relaying them.

## Spoken rhythm

Compact, conversational, and willing to tease.

He can answer Greg's escalating hypotheticals with the practical exit Greg keeps overlooking:
- sit;
- wait;
- get help;
- you are not being chased.

His explanations usually stop once the immediate physical action is clear.

## With Greg

Nerin treats Greg like a capable person who is temporarily bad at being a novice in his own body.

He does not protect Greg from embarrassment. That is part of why Greg tolerates him.

He can joke about the injury without denying its seriousness because he is also paying attention to the physical basics.

## With Sera Dain

Sera owns surgical and rehabilitation progression.

Nerin may:
- carry instructions;
- inspect routine wound signs;
- demonstrate movement;
- spot or teach spotting;
- tell Greg what to do if the attempt fails.

When the distinction matters, dialogue should make clear whether Nerin is giving his own immediate coaching or relaying Sera's clearance.

## Humor

Bedside practicality, mild mockery, and refusal to dignify overcomplicated questions.

He does not become a comedy sidekick. The humor works because he remains useful.

## Canon lines / fingerprints

- **CANON LINE:** `I'll decide.`
- **CANON LINE:** `Enough for inspection.`
- **CANON LINE:** `No one hates Alden. We understand Alden.`
- **CANON LINE:** `Wait. Get help. You are not being chased.`
- **CANON LINE:** `What if phantom foot goes first?` / response pattern: practical sequence rather than philosophy.

Exact wording should be checked against current manuscript authority before reuse; these lines are evidence, not catchphrases.

## Voice tests — NON-CANON

- `Sit before your leg makes the decision for you.`
- `That's Sera's restriction. Mine is don't fall while arguing about it.`
- `You can be annoyed from the chair.`

Calibration only.

## Overlap allowed

Nerin can be dry like Jorren, Hessa, or Sera Dain.

The difference:
- Jorren's help is friendship and ordinary-life care;
- Hessa's is procedural/magical safety;
- Sera Dain's is clinical progression;
- Nerin's is bedside execution and immediate body management.

## Watch list

Avoid:
- giving him Sera Dain's prognosis authority;
- therapist language;
- making every line a joke;
- treating disability logistics as inspirational;
- making him an all-purpose healer beyond manuscript evidence.
