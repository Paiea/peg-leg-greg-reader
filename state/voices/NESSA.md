# NESSA — VOICE AUTHORITY

Exact manuscript prose outranks this file.

## Core lens

Nessa notices objects in motion: where a prop belongs, what must reset, what somebody is about to touch, which route is blocked, what a costume is doing, and what downstream work a small mistake will create.

She is concrete before abstract.

## Spoken rhythm

Operational, immediate, often short because the work is physically happening while she speaks.

Her lines commonly function as:
- stop;
- move this;
- put it back;
- hold still;
- use this route;
- don't touch that;
- here's the corrected shorthand.

Do not make her explain the whole prop system when one usable sentence does the job.

## Humor

Dry and situational. Often comes from accumulated history with objects and people rather than crafted jokes.

Her funniest lines can be corrections that are funny because everyone already knows the history.

## Disagreement / refusal

Nessa responds to material consequence.

If Marek creates a problem, she handles the object/problem. She does not need to prosecute his personality every time.

If somebody else discovers useful information, she can absorb it immediately without praise ceremony.

## Information behavior

She knows practical continuity inside her domain. She may assume context that newcomers lack because the theatre has already been living with the object for days.

She is not omniscient and can be surprised.

## With Greg

As Greg becomes familiar, she should need fewer directions. Her dialogue with him can become route/object shorthand rather than repeated onboarding.

She may trust him with a specific object because of history without promoting him into her domain generally.

## Canon lines / fingerprints

- **CANON LINE:** `Royal stillness.`
- **CANON LINE:** `Marek.`
- **CANON LINE:** `That is painted.`
- **CANON LINE:** `Sideways through the door, upright after.`
- **CANON LINE:** `Marek learned through his face.`

## Voice tests — NON-CANON

- `Not there. Back table.`
- `You can explain after you put it down.`
- `Wet side out. Then carry it.`

Calibration only.

## Overlap allowed

Nessa and Rinna can both be concise theatre voices. Rinna routes business and assignment. Nessa routes physical object continuity and execution.

Nessa and Teren can both correct quickly. Teren corrects performance behavior. Nessa corrects the material path around it.

## Watch list

Avoid:
- prop cop caricature;
- Marek babysitter;
- constant scolding;
- universal backstage authority;
- Hessa-style explanation of why every observation matters;
- treating every Marek interaction as the same joke.
