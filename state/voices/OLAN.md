# OLAN — VOICE AUTHORITY

Exact manuscript prose outranks this file.

## Core lens

Olan sees the repair in front of him: attachment, wear, binding, latch, fit, whether the existing material still holds, what can be fixed quickly, and what hidden damage might appear once he opens the thing.

He is a practical repair person, not a universal mechanic or magical theorist.

## Spoken rhythm

Plain, work-shaped, often shorter than Greg wants.

He may name:
- the visible failure;
- the likely next action;
- the price condition;
- whether he needs permission;
- what `quick` means for this repair.

He does not need to isolate every variable verbally before touching a basket or latch.

## Humor

Understated, often through refusing Greg's framing or continuing the work while Greg talks.

## Disagreement / refusal

Olan can say no before Greg finishes because he recognizes the class of nuisance coming toward him.

He also respects ownership and authorization: he does not begin somebody else's paid repair merely because the object is already in his hands.

## Information behavior

He knows local construction details Greg may only recognize broadly.

He can distinguish visible failure from magical failure without converting the distinction into a lecture.

He may use practical tests because that is how the trade works, not because he shares Hessa's epistemology.

## With Greg

Enough familiarity for teasing and shorthand. Greg may understand the broad magical/material family while Olan knows this specific repair better.

## Canon lines / fingerprints

- **CANON LINE:** `Basket.`
- **CANON LINE:** `Attachment first.`
- **CANON LINE:** `Tie may still be fine.`
- **CANON LINE:** `One bit if simple wrap and set. More if rim opens when I pull old binding.`
- **CANON LINE:** `Quick means quick, not pretty.`

## Voice tests — NON-CANON

- `Hold it still.`
- `If that opens too, price changes.`
- `It works. I didn't say it was new.`

Calibration only.

## Overlap allowed

Olan can make careful distinctions. Difference from Hessa: his distinctions are usually trade decisions attached directly to whether he repairs, charges, or stops.

## Watch list

Avoid:
- repair philosopher;
- Hessa with reeds and screws;
- Greg apprenticeship lane by default;
- universal handyman;
- making every repair magically interesting.
