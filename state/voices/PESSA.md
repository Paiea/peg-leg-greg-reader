# PESSA — VOICE AUTHORITY

Exact manuscript prose outranks this file.

## Core lens

Pessa notices **what the body, road, route, or drill is actually doing** and what decision follows from that physical reality.

Her precision is embodied and field-practical. She is not Greg with fewer words and not Hessa transplanted outdoors.

When Greg wants a category, she often wants the consequence.

When Greg wants a model, she often wants the next usable decision.

When a trainee wants to count an outcome as success, she asks what the drill was supposed to train.

## Spoken rhythm

Compact and declarative. She can explain fully when a correction needs a reason, but usually starts with the smallest physical truth that changes the attempt.

Common shapes:
- one-word correction;
- concrete observation;
- short explanation tied to body/ground/objective;
- reset and try again.

She does not usually turn a correction into a general theory unless the scene genuinely requires it.

## Professional vocabulary / attention

Road work:
- surface;

- priority;

Training:

The shared principle is not abstraction. It is **physical consequence**.

## With Greg

Pessa does not treat Greg's analysis as final merely because it is intelligent.

She is comfortable saying `No` when he is close but wrong, then giving the narrower physical correction.

She can use Greg as an observer or extra set of eyes while retaining final authority inside her lane.

She does not need to reassure him that his interpretation was almost right.

## With Alden

More coach than peer.

She likes repeatable attempts, shorter commitments, and letting fatigue expose what speed hides.

She does not confuse winning the exchange with succeeding at the drill.

Alden's eagerness is material to work with, not a personality defect to lecture away.

## Humor

Dry and situational. She can undercut Greg or Alden without changing register into a comedy voice.

Her jokes usually come from the immediate work or from somebody pretending a physical fact does not exist.

## Voice tests — NON-CANON

- `Road still carries. Fix the wash first.`
- `You won the exchange. That wasn't the drill.`
- `Shorter. You keep more choices.`

Calibration only.

## Overlap allowed

Pessa can be concise like Hessa, Rinna, Tam, or Teren.

The distinction is source of precision:
- Hessa: safety, repeatability, body/magic evidence;
- Teren: playable stage result;
- Tam: customer use and shoe material;
- Pessa: physical route/body consequence and field priority.

## Watch list

Avoid:
- generic epistemology speeches;
- making every correction a life lesson;
- turning `No` into a catchphrase;
- making her an all-purpose combat master outside manuscript evidence;
- letting Greg's abstract explanation overwrite her embodied one;
- confusing road priority with simple severity ranking.
