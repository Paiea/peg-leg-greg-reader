# RINNA — VOICE AUTHORITY

Exact manuscript prose outranks this file.

## Core lens

Rinna thinks in terms of what the theatre needs next: bodies, payment, records, timing, substitutions, business exposure, and whether a problem actually requires more discussion.

Her authority is practical. She does not need to sound ceremonially managerial.

## Spoken rhythm

Compressed. Often gives Greg only the next necessary fact.

Questions from Greg may receive:
- the rate;
- the role;
- the number of performances;
- a name;
- `not your problem`;
- a direction to go ask the person who actually owns the technical detail.

She can speak at length when business pressure or procedure genuinely requires it, but ordinary theatre routing should stay efficient.

## Humor

Dry by consequence more than performance. She does not need to play Greg's game. A flat answer can be funnier than a crafted comeback.

## Disagreement / refusal

Rinna is comfortable deciding that Greg does not need context.

She distinguishes between:
- what he needs to perform;
- what she needs to know for records/payment;
- what belongs to somebody else's private situation.

Do not make every withheld detail mysterious.

## Information behavior

She tends to route expertise correctly rather than pretending to own it all.

Examples of shape:
- mask question goes to Sivi;
- passage/caller mechanics go to Lenn;
- business/payment/assignment stays with Rinna.

## With Greg

She increasingly trusts Greg enough to give him compressed instructions, but this should feel like reduced onboarding rather than praise.

She may also exploit his availability because the theatre is a workplace and he has proven useful.

## Canon lines / fingerprints

- **CANON LINE:** `Today.`
- **CANON LINE:** `Not your problem.`
- **CANON LINE:** `Lenn shows you once. Then you keep the passage from becoming stupid.`
- **CANON LINE:** `Masked Guest.`
- **CANON LINE:** `Seven lines. Banquet scene. Sit through most of it. Stand once. Cross once. Exit in blackout.`

## Voice tests — NON-CANON

- `If I needed you to know why, I would have started there.`
- `Two houses. Same pay. Different problem.`
- `Ask Sivi. I need the stage ready.`

Calibration only.

## Overlap allowed

Rinna can be concise like Lyssa or Hessa, but her compression comes from workflow and business throughput.

## Watch list

Avoid:
- formal boss speeches;
- universal theatre expertise;
- making her cryptic for style;
- turning every line into a command;
- Hessa-style evidence language unless the issue is specifically records or supported claims.
