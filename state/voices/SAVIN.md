# SAVIN — VOICE AUTHORITY

Exact manuscript prose outranks this file.

## Core lens

Savin is a cobbler. She notices wear, seams, leather, balance, repairability, and what a shoe has physically done under a real body.

Her caution comes from craft repetition, not formal epistemology. She has seen customers arrive with bad guesses, bad repairs, and feet that do not match the story they tell about them.

She does not need a theory before repairing what is visibly repairable.

## Cadence

Short. Often one clause. Often an answer that stops exactly where the work stops.

She is comfortable with fragments because the object is sitting between her and the other person. She can point, press, bend, or turn the shoe instead of naming every premise.

Greg tends to turn her short answers into larger questions. Savin rarely follows him all the way there.

## Register / education

Trade-literate and technically experienced rather than academically analytical.

Use concrete vocabulary:
- heel;
- seam;
- sole;
- pitch;
- nail;
- wear;
- roll;
- thread;
- leather;
- clamp;
- pull;
- hold.

She can know a great deal without translating it into abstract categories like variable, evidence strength, mechanism, causal model, or confidence level.

Do not make plain speech imply limited intelligence. Savin is frequently the person in the room who knows exactly what matters.

## Humor

Dry, sparse, usually produced by refusing Greg the framing he wants.

She is funny because she remains a cobbler while Greg keeps trying to turn the shop into a philosophy seminar.

## Disagreement / uncertainty

Savin's uncertainty is tactile and future-facing.

Useful shapes:
- `Maybe.`
- `Not yet.`
- `Come back when it wears.`
- `I see twice, maybe know more.`

She does not need to explain why one observation is insufficient unless the practical consequence matters.

If changing something prematurely could hurt Greg, she says that plainly.

## With Greg

She is not impressed by his reputation and not especially troubled by his old-life strangeness.

She will answer useful questions. She will also tell him, directly or indirectly, when he has reached his quota.

His disability is part of the shoe problem when relevant and otherwise not a moral event.

## Canon lines / fingerprints

- **CANON LINE:** `Which one?`
- **CANON LINE:** `Maybe. Maybe you always walked ugly.`
- **CANON LINE:** `Cobbler.`
- **CANON LINE:** `Face did.`
- **CANON LINE:** `Because I don't know if shoe made you roll or you made shoe roll.`
- **CANON LINE:** `Come back when this wears. Before it opens. I see pattern twice, maybe know more.`

These are evidence, not catchphrases.

## Voice tests — NON-CANON

- `Leather's fine. Stitch isn't.`
- `You want prettier answer, ask somebody selling prettier shoes.`
- `Walk on it. Then I know something.`

Calibration only.

## Overlap allowed

Savin may share words like `maybe`, `good`, or `don't know` with Hessa, Greg, Cala, or other Carrow people.

The difference is the reasoning underneath them. Hessa narrows a claim. Savin postpones a shoe decision until the shoe gives her another wear pattern.

## Watch list

Avoid:
- Hessa with leather;
- rustic caricature;
- broken grammar used to signal class;
- endless one-word replies;
- secret philosopher hidden in a cobbler shop;
- making every practical rule perfectly correct or fully explained.
