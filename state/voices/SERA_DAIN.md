# SERA DAIN — VOICE AUTHORITY

Exact manuscript prose outranks this file.

## Identity guardrail

**Sera Dain is the surgical / rehabilitation professional in the Book I injury sequence.**

She is not the later Carrow local currently covered by `SERA.md`. Do not merge these voice authorities merely because both are named Sera.

## Core lens

Sera Dain speaks from **surgical consequence and demonstrated physical progression**.

She notices:
- blood flow;
- tissue viability;
- wound margin;
- drainage;
- heat / redness;
- swelling;
- pain changes;
- whether a stumble or load is likely to threaten healing;
- what Greg's body has actually shown it can do.

Her uncertainty is clinical. It is not Hessa's magical-channel methodology and not generic caution.

## Spoken rhythm

Plain, direct, professionally bounded.

She will give a strong answer when the body supports one and `maybe` when it does not.

She dislikes Greg trying to convert a subjective threshold into a loophole-proof number when no useful number exists.

She often answers the exact medical question and refuses to become Greg's employer, life planner, or permission system for every nonmedical choice.

## Information behavior

Sera distinguishes:
- current concern from possible future concern;
- wound stability from full recovery;
- one demonstrated stair sequence from unrestricted stair use;
- pain expected from pain meaningfully worse;
- medical clearance from whether Greg should want the activity.

She can strengthen a prognosis gradually as evidence improves instead of jumping from danger to certainty.

## With Greg

She treats Greg as an intelligent patient who is still capable of using intelligence to negotiate against his own recovery.

That means she may answer him seriously and still refuse to define a number he would turn into permission.

She does not infantilize him. She also does not let him redefine the task because he can make a technically clever argument.

## With Hessa

Professional overlap without collapse.

Hessa knows Greg's magical channels/body baseline and repeatability constraints. Sera owns surgical tissue, wound progression, mobility progression, and rehabilitation consequences.

They can disagree on wrap technique or wording without becoming rivals.

## With Nerin

Nerin may relay Sera's instructions and coach immediate movement, but Sera owns the medical progression decision.

Keep that chain visible in dialogue when it matters.

## Humor

Dry, usually in response to Greg's attempts to turn medicine into a specification sheet.

She can joke without becoming casual about the wound.

- **CANON LINE:** `I am not your employer.`

Exact wording should be checked against current manuscript authority before reuse; these are evidence, not slogans.

## Voice tests — NON-CANON

- `The wound can tolerate that. Your shoulder may disagree.`
- `You proved one ascent. You did not prove a commute.`
- `If the pain changes, leave. I am not giving you a number to defeat.`

Calibration only.

## Overlap allowed

Sera Dain may share concise medical language with Hessa and may say `good`, `maybe`, or `no` frequently.

The difference is what the word is attached to:
- Sera Dain: tissue, viability, healing, mobility progression;
- Hessa: channels, casting, repeatability, magical/body safety.

## Watch list

- therapist voice;
- inspirational disability speeches;
- turning her into Greg's general permission authority;
- fake certainty about prognosis;
- making every restriction a numerical rule;
- conflating her with `SERA.md`.
