# SEVREN — VOICE AUTHORITY

Exact manuscript prose outranks this file.

## Core lens

Sevren carries the sense that roads, deliveries, fatigue, distant places, schedules, and other people's business continue beyond Greg's current frame.

He should make the world feel larger rather than functioning as a lore courier.

## Spoken rhythm

Practical, traveled, not especially interested in polishing an answer for Greg.

He may speak in:
- route fragments;
- names of places or people he assumes are known;
- complaints tied to distance/weather/work;
- stories that wander because the road mattered more than the thesis.

He does not need to share Greg's urge to classify everything.

## Humor

Can be tired, situational, worldly, or attached to the predictable stupidity of travel/work.

## Disagreement / refusal

Sevren may reject Greg's framing simply because it is not the part of the situation he considers important.

He can say what happened somewhere else without pretending to know why it happened.

## Information behavior

He knows things because he has been places, delivered things, waited on people, or heard the same argument in another town.

That knowledge can be anecdotal, practical, incomplete, or stale. It does not need Hessa-style qualification every time.

## With Greg

Greg may hear systems in Sevren's road experience that Sevren himself does not care to formalize.

Let Greg sometimes extract the pattern later rather than making Sevren explain it for him.

## Canon patterns

- **CANON PATTERN:** Roads, deliveries, fatigue, and other places remain active beyond Greg.
- **CANON PATTERN:** Sevren does not have to care about Greg's current abstraction as much as Greg does.

## Voice tests — NON-CANON

- `Road was mud past Ellin. Better after the ridge.`
- `Ask me when I've slept.`
- `Maybe the clerk knows. I carried the box.`

Calibration only.

## Overlap allowed

Sevren may share dry humor with Greg. Difference: his speech should often feel pulled by distance, work, and lived travel rather than by Greg's internal analytical loop.

## Watch list

Avoid:
- exposition wagon;
- omniscient traveler;
- generic grizzled-road voice;
- making every anecdote secretly plot-relevant;
- forcing methodological precision onto ordinary remembered experience.
