# TEREN — VOICE AUTHORITY

Exact manuscript prose outranks this file.

## Core lens

Teren sees performance problems at the scale an actor can actually use: timing, eyeline, cue, posture, intention, pace, placement, and whether the audience can read the beat.

His best corrections are local and playable.

## Spoken rhythm

Brief without being cryptic for its own sake.

He tends to give:
- one correction;
- one concrete reason when needed;
- another run.

He does not need to turn acting notes into philosophy.

## Humor

Usually secondary to work. He may be dry, but he is not there to win banter with Greg.

## Disagreement / refusal

If Greg explains instead of doing, Teren should often redirect him back into the scene.

He can reject an interpretation simply because it produces the wrong stage result.

## Information behavior

He explains enough to make the note playable. He does not owe Greg a complete theory of acting every rehearsal.

## With Greg

Greg wants to understand systems. Teren often benefits from stopping that process early and making him *do the correction* before Greg can over-model it.

As Greg improves, Teren's speech should compress further. Repeated correction should become shorthand where earned.

## Canon patterns

- **CANON PATTERN:** Teren gives brief, local, playable stage corrections tied to the exact failure.
- **CANON PATTERN:** He can correct Greg without becoming his mentor in every domain.
- **CANON PATTERN:** Later competence should reduce explanation rather than produce congratulatory speeches.

## Voice tests — NON-CANON

- `You're waiting for the cue. Listen to the scene.`
- `Don't show me that you know. Just answer her.`
- `Again. Less help.`

These are calibration lines, not manuscript history.

## Overlap allowed

Teren can sound precise. The distinction from Hessa is purpose: Teren wants a playable result now, not a claim that survives methodological scrutiny.

## Watch list

Avoid:
- aphorism machine;
- life coach;
- secret future-bluff instructor;
- universal theatre authority;
- every note becoming memorable wisdom.

If a correction could be shorter and still produce the stage behavior, prefer shorter.
