# VOICE EVIDENCE QUALITY LABELS

Status: ACTIVE EDITORIAL AID

Use with `state/voices/INDEX.md` and `state/DIALOGUE_VARIANCE_PERFORMANCE_LAYER.md`.

This file exists to prevent a feedback loop where a repeated house-style habit appears in canon, gets recorded as character evidence, then gets reinforced because the voice guide now says the character talks that way.

## CORE

Durable character behavior worth reinforcing across contexts.

Use when the evidence reflects a stable cognitive, social, professional, or emotional center.

Examples:

- Antonius returns to repayment and operational consequence.
- Arlo protects test integrity, time, tools, and authorship.
- Hessa separates evidence, body state, and permission.
- Jorren trusts embodied evidence and physical readiness.
- Alden looks for initiative and immediate usable advantage.
- Greg compresses wording, extends absurdity, and turns distinctions over.

## CONTEXTUAL

Valid in a specific relationship, role, mood, or scene pressure, but not a universal voice rule.

Examples:

- Antonius being especially dry with Greg.
- Jorren teasing more during sparring than with a stranger.
- Hessa becoming more clipped when tired or safety margin is poor.
- Alden matching Greg's speed during competitive play.
- Greg becoming less funny when something matters too much.

Do not promote CONTEXTUAL evidence into a permanent speech setting.

## SHARED HOUSE REGISTER

Project, city, workplace, friendship, or genre language that several people can naturally share.

Examples may include:

- ordinary curses;
- short practical replies;
- local trade shorthand;
- dry Carrow humor;
- common sentence shapes;
- words such as `No`, `Good`, `Maybe`, or `Probably`.

Shared register is allowed. It simply carries weak identity weight.

Do not use a SHARED HOUSE REGISTER example as the main reason a future line belongs to one character.

## CONTAMINATION WATCH

A repeated pattern that may be readable and funny but should not be reinforced as character identity because it contributes to convergence.

Common PLG candidates:

- predictive `No` ladders;
- multiple unrelated characters reading Greg's face, breath, or hidden next sentence;
- transferable `you problem` punchlines;
- specialist boundaries repeatedly converted into polished aphorisms;
- incidental strangers arriving fully calibrated to Greg;
- several supporting characters finishing Greg's joke architecture;
- generic `That is not the same thing` style reasoning when the speaker's own domain would produce a more specific answer.

CONTAMINATION WATCH does not mean automatic deletion.

It means:

1. do not cite the pattern as strong future voice evidence;
2. inspect density across the scene and neighboring chapters;
3. preserve isolated earned examples when they work;
4. prefer another response mode when the pattern is already saturated.

## Evidence promotion rule

When updating a recurring voice page, prefer CORE evidence first, then relationship-specific CONTEXTUAL evidence.

Use SHARED HOUSE REGISTER sparingly as background texture.

Never promote CONTAMINATION WATCH into a fingerprint merely because it appears often.

Frequency can be evidence of contamination rather than identity.
